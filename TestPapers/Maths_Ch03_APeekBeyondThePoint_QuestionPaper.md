**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Always compare decimals place by place from the left — more digits does NOT mean a bigger number (1.04 < 1.4). When adding or subtracting, line up the decimal POINTS, not the right-hand ends. Remember trailing zeros add nothing (0.2 = 0.20) but interior zeros shift a digit into a smaller place. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
