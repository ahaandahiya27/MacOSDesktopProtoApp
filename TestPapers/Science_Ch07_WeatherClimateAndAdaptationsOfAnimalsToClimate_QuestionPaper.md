**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Remember the key idea throughout: weather is the day-to-day state of the atmosphere, while climate is its average over many years. Some questions give a small weather-report table — read the numbers carefully before answering. Most questions need two to four reasoning steps; watch for traps such as confusing weather with climate. No calculator is needed. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. A TV presenter says, "Tomorrow Delhi will be 32 °C with light rain in the evening." This statement describes:
   (A) the altitude of Delhi
   (B) the humidity of Delhi only
   (C) the weather of Delhi
   (D) the climate of Delhi

2. Which statement correctly distinguishes weather from climate?
