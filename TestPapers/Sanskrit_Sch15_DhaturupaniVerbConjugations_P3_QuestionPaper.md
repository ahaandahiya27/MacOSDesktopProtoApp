Classic traps: लट् is PRESENT not past; parasmaipada -ति vs atmanepada -ते; Sanskrit persons are REVERSED (प्रथमपुरुष = English THIRD); the verb must AGREE with its subject in person AND number; कृ alternates a strong stem (करो-) with a weak stem (कुरु-/कुर्व-); and the अ- of अकरोत् is the past-tense augment, never the negative prefix.
