**Paper 3 — (progressively harder)**

|---|---|

| **Maximum Marks** | 240 |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: misclassifying invisible utilities or social services as 'not infrastructure'; assuming the British built the 1853 railways to help Indians rather than to serve their own extraction and control; treating infrastructure care as the government's job alone; confusing physical sub-types (transport vs utilities vs communication vs energy) with one another and with social infrastructure; mistaking renewable energy parks, living root bridges, or the Arthashastra's concern as outside the chapter's scope. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
