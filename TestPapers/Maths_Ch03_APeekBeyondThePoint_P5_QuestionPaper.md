**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This is the hardest tier: every wrong option is a fully computable slip, not a random number. Beware longer-is-bigger ordering traps, right-end alignment in add/subtract, interior-zero place shifts (0.305 is not 0.35), point-drift when multiplying or dividing by 10/100/1000, half-of-a-tenth being five hundredths, terminating-decimal tests (a fraction in lowest terms ends only when its denominator has no prime factor other than 2 and 5), and chained multi-step computations where doing the steps out of order gives a baited answer. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
