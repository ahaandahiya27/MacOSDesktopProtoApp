**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Think about the *factor* behind each climate fact — latitude, altitude, distance from the sea, winds or relief — before you choose. Watch the classic traps: confusing weather with climate, thinking the Equator is hot because it is "nearer" the Sun, calling the calm "eye" the most dangerous part of a cyclone, and assuming rain falls equally on both sides of a mountain. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. Ahaan says, "It is drizzling outside right now." His teacher says, "Cherrapunji is one of the wettest places on Earth." Which pair correctly labels the two statements?

   (D) Both describe climate
