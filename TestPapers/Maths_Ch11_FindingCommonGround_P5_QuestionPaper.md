**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Each problem may chain several ideas: decide HCF (largest divider — share/cut/tile, or 'same unknown remainder' via the HCF of pairwise differences) versus LCM (smallest common multiple — meet/ring/flash again, or LCM*k + r for a fixed remainder); always take the LOWEST powers of COMMON primes for HCF and the HIGHEST powers of ALL primes for LCM; recall HCF*LCM = product holds for exactly two numbers while LCM = product holds only for co-primes; count the co-prime factor-splits of LCM/HCF when asked how many pairs share a given HCF and LCM; and watch unit conversions, 'including/excluding the start' tallies, and which runner is fastest versus slowest. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
