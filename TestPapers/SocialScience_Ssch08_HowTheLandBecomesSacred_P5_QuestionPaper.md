**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Apex-tier traps: confusing the semantic core of 'tirtha' (a crossing toward a higher state) with surface features (water, height, age, ownership); collapsing the three networks (char dham 4, jyotirlingas 12-Shiva, Shakti pithas 51-Sati) or swapping their counts/deities; treating shared cross-faith sites, the pilgrimage-trade overlap, or sacred-grove conservation as the OPPOSITE of what the chapter argues; reading the Kumbh's 4-city, ~12-year Jupiter-Sun rotation as yearly or single-place; mistaking which religions originated in India; and treating sacred geography as dividing rather than integrating, or as unique to India. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
