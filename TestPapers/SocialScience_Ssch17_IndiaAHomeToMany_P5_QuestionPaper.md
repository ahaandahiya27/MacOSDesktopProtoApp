**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Apex traps: filing a refuge or enslaved arrival under 'trade'; collapsing Islam's peaceful 7th-century Arab-merchant arrival into the much later military invasions; reading identity-preserving integration as either total assimilation or total apartness; chaining several distinctions at once (refuge vs opportunity vs enslavement; immigrate vs emigrate; persecution vs refuge; cosmopolitan vs pluralism vs tolerance vs ethos; prophet Zarathushtra vs God Ahura Mazda; matching synagogue/fire temple/church/mosque to the right community); and treating diversity as a weakness rather than the chapter's source of strength, in the spirit of vasudhaiva kutumbakam.
