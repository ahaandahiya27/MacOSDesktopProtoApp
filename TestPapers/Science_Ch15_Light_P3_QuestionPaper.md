**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Distractors are seasoned misconceptions: 'concave mirror always magnifies' and 'convex mirror can magnify', a virtual image caught on a screen, plane-mirror images that flip top-bottom instead of left-right, image distance taken as double or half the object distance, convex/concave LENS confused with convex/concave MIRROR, a converging mirror called diverging, a prism said to 'add' or 'create' colours, red bending more than violet, and a single colour split into a spectrum. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. A pinhole camera made from a box gives an upside-down picture of a candle on its back screen. The single fact about light that best explains the inverted picture is that light:
   (A) bends as it goes through the small hole
   (B) is reflected by the inside walls of the box
   (C) is split into its colours by the hole

2. Light from a smooth mirror obeys the law of reflection. When that same beam falls on a rough wall, the wall still scatters light in many directions. In BOTH cases, what is true at each tiny point where a ray meets the surface?

   (B) the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection at that point
   (C) the light slows and bends into the surface
   (D) the colour of the light changes on reflection
