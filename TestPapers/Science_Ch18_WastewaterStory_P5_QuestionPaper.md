**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This hardest tier chains multiple plant stages and contaminant classes in one stem: it forces you to reason about what each unit physically can and cannot do, to keep aerobic aeration and anaerobic digestion (and their by-products) apart even when described by symptom rather than name, to track which of two split streams a removed material belongs to, to separate 'cleaner' from 'potable' under deceptive framings, and to place treatment events in true sequence even when listed out of order; every distractor is a precise paraphrase of a real misconception, never a giveaway.
