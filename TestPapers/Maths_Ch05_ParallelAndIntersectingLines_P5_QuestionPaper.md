**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Every item chains four or more relations, so a single slip lands you on a pre-computed wrong option: equating co-interior angles instead of supplementing them (they add to 180 degrees, they are NOT equal); treating a non-parallel crossing as parallel; applying a corresponding or alternate equality before the lines are known to be parallel; halting one linear-pair or one bisector step early; using 360 degrees where 180 degrees applies (or the reverse); and forgetting that only a perpendicular crossing yields four equal angles. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
