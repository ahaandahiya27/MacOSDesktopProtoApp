2. Two realms, P and Q, are described. In P, a single ruler's commands stop at his own frontier and there are no lesser kings beneath him. In Q, several crowned kings keep their thrones but each must obey one paramount lord and send him tribute. A student labels P the empire because it is 'more tightly unified under one will.' The flaw in this reasoning is that:
   (A) P is the empire, since an empire by definition allows no lesser kings to keep their thrones at all

   (D) neither is an empire, because an empire must be a republic that elects its ruler
