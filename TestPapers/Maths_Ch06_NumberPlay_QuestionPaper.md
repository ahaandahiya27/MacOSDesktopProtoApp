**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This chapter rewards reasoning over guessing — use parity (odd/even) to rule out impossibilities, the "add the two previous terms" rule for the Virahāṅka–Fibonacci sequence, and careful place-value logic for digit puzzles. Watch the classic traps: an ODD count of odd numbers gives an ODD sum (not even); the sequence adds the two previous terms (not a constant); and in the "taller children in front" game the first child always says 0. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
