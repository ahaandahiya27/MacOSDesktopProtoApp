**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: the Maurya empire broke up from within (not destroyed by foreign invasion), so internal weakness preceded and caused the foreign entry into the northwest; never swap Gandhara (Greco-Buddhist, wavy hair and toga-folds) with Mathura (indigenous, spotted red sandstone, fuller forms); the Satavahanas and Tamil kingdoms reached Rome by SEA while the Kushanas profited from the LAND Silk Route; foreign rulers were absorbed into a dominant Indian culture rather than erasing it; an ashvamedha or matrimonial alliance is a political act, not merely a ritual or a wedding; royal women (mother-names, land-donating queens, a queen's horse-sacrifice) held real power; a stupa is a Buddhist dome, an anicut a river-dam, a samvat a counted era; and keep the dynastic order straight (Maurya breakup, then Satavahanas, then Kanishka).
