**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This appendix tables verb conjugations. Watch the classic traps: लट्-लकार is the PRESENT tense (not past); parasmaipada verbs take -ति (पठति) while atmanepada take -ते (भाषते); Sanskrit's persons are REVERSED - प्रथमपुरुष = English THIRD person, उत्तमपुरुष = English FIRST; verbs must AGREE with the subject (अहम् करोमि 'I do', not करोति 'he does'); and the अ- in अकरोत् is the PAST-tense augment, not the negative prefix. Sanskrit is quoted in Devanagari. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
