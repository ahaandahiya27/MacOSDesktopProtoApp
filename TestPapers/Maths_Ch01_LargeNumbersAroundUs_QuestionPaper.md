**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Work in the Indian system (3-2-2-2 commas: lakh, crore, arab) unless a question names the International system (3-3-3: thousand, million, billion). Watch the classic traps — confusing one lakh with one million, miscounting zeros, putting commas in the wrong groups, and rounding the wrong way. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
