**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. More digits does NOT mean a bigger number (0.5 > 0.45); compare place by place from the left. Line up the decimal POINTS — not the right-hand ends — when adding or subtracting. Trailing zeros change nothing (0.30 = 0.3) but an interior zero pushes a digit into a smaller place (0.305 ≠ 0.35). 'Hundredths' is the second place after the point, not the hundreds place. Half of one tenth is five hundredths (half of 0.1 is 0.05). Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
