**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. National-Olympiad traps: ईशा is INSTRUMENTAL ('by the Lord'), not a nominative or vocative; the verse's vowel-sandhi joins are ईशा+वास्यम् (आ+अ→आ, savarna-dirgha) and इदम्+सर्वम् (anusvara, not visarga); भुञ्जीथाः is an OPTATIVE/imperative-mood middle 2nd-sg ('you should enjoy'), never a past or a noun; मा + गृधः is the PROHIBITIVE (मा + aorist stem, no augment अ-) meaning 'do not covet', not a future or a simple negative न; कस्यस्वित् is an indefinite GENITIVE ('of whose-ever') asking 'whose is wealth?'; त्यक्तेन is instrumental of the past-participle त्यक्त ('by renunciation'), the paradox of enjoyment-through-letting-go — NOT 'by grabbing'; detachment means non-attachment to ownership, NOT abandoning all action or treating the world as worthless.
