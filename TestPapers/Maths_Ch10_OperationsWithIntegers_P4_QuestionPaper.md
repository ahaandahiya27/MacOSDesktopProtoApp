**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. National-tier signed-integer reasoning: long add/subtract chains, deeply nested brackets under BODMAS, parity-of-negative-factors arguments, distributive manipulations, and word problems where the classic slips (subtracting a negative ADDS, two negatives MULTIPLY positive, (−2)³ ≠ −2³, a hidden 0 collapsing a product) are baked into the distractors.
