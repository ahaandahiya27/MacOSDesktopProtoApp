**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. the deadliest traps are unflipped signs when a minus sits before a nested bracket, premature left-to-right evaluation of × and ÷, half-distribution that multiplies only the first inner term, and assuming subtraction or division can be regrouped like addition and multiplication. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
