**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Distractors reward shallow rules of thumb — 'more cells always helps', 'a complete-looking loop must light', 'a permanent core makes a better bell', 'a bigger fuse is safer', 'copper out-heats nichrome', 'the field is the same above and below a wire' — and punish anyone who does not trace the branch current actually takes, the make-and-break cycle, or which single effect each device exploits.
