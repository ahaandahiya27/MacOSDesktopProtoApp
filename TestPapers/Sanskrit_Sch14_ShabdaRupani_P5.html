Worked Solutions

1. (A) In the अकारान्त MASCULINE देव, the only cell that yields देवे is the locative singular. The nom/acc dual is देवौ (not देवे) and the dual locative is देवयोः, so the locative-singular reading stands alone. (The देवे=dual collapse the distractor invokes belongs to the NEUTER stem फले, not to masculine देव.)

2. (B) For neuter फल the locative singular is फले and, by the neuter nom=acc rule, the dual nominative and accusative are also फले. The nominative plural is the distinct फलानि, so it is not a reading of फले. Both the locative singular AND the nom/acc dual are genuine, so listing only one is incomplete.

3. (C) The masculine pronoun तत् has nominative plural ते. Its nom/acc dual is तौ (not ते), its nominative singular is the suppletive सः, and its locative singular is तस्मिन्. So among the masculine cells ते is purely the nominative plural. (ते as a dual belongs to the FEMININE/NEUTER pronoun, not the masculine.)

4. (B) The feminine pronoun's ablative singular and genitive singular coincide as तस्याः ('from her' / 'of her'). The dative singular is the distinct तस्यै and the locative singular is तस्याम्, so neither belongs to तस्याः. Both the ablative and genitive readings are valid, so 'genitive only' is incomplete.

5. (C) In the इकारान्त stem the ablative and genitive singular merge to कवेः. The dative singular is the separate कवये, so it is not a reading of कवेः. Listing only the ablative or only the genitive omits the other valid reading.

6. (C) In the आकारान्त feminine paradigm of लता, two distinct sources give the form लते. First, the vocative SINGULAR is हे लते (the आ of the stem shortens to ए in direct address), so even setting the particle हे aside the bare form लते is a legitimate vocative-singular reading. Second, the dual collapses its nominative, accusative AND vocative all to लते. That is four readings together — one singular and three dual. An answer naming only the three dual cells silently drops the vocative singular; the 'vocative singular only' and 'nominative dual only' options each capture just one slice of the full set.

7. (B) For the उकारान्त stem the ablative and genitive singular share गुरोः. The genitive=locative DUAL is the distinct गुर्वोः, and the dative singular is गुरवे, so neither belongs to गुरोः. Both ablative and genitive are valid readings, so 'genitive only' is incomplete.

8. (D) The pronoun's genitive and locative DUAL coincide as तयोः (parallel to देवयोः). The genitive plural is the distinct तेषाम्, so it is not a reading. Both the genitive and locative dual are valid, so a single-case label is incomplete.

9. (D) In the आकारान्त feminine the dual nominative=accusative=vocative all give रमे. The locative singular is रमायाम्, the nominative plural रमाः, and the instrumental singular रमया, so none of those is a reading of रमे.

10. (A) The dual instrumental, dative and ablative collapse into the single -भ्याम् form देवाभ्याम् (three readings). The dative=ablative PLURAL is देवेभ्यः, and the genitive=locative dual is देवयोः, so those are different forms. Restricting to 'instrumental dual only' omits the dative and ablative readings.

11. (D) By the neuter nom=acc syncretism the plural nominative and accusative are both फलानि. The nom/acc DUAL is फले, not फलानि, so it is not a reading here. Both the nominative and accusative plural are valid, so a single-case label is incomplete.

12. (B) The उकारान्त genitive and locative DUAL coincide as गुर्वोः. The ablative=genitive SINGULAR is गुरोः (a different form), and the genitive/locative plural are गुरूणाम्/गुरुषु. So गुर्वोः carries exactly the genitive-dual and locative-dual readings.

13. (B) The feminine pronoun takes -स्यै in the dative singular, giving तस्यै. तस्याः is the merged ablative=genitive, तस्याम् the locative, and तया the instrumental — each a different feminine cell.

14. (C) Pronominal declension inserts -स्म- and the masculine locative singular is तस्मिन्. तस्मात् is the ablative, तस्मै the dative, and तस्य the genitive — all distinct -स्म-/-स्य cells.

15. (D) Pronouns take -एषाम् in the masculine genitive plural, giving तेषाम्. तेषु is the locative plural, तेभ्यः the dative=ablative plural, and तैः the instrumental plural.

16. (D) The इकारान्त FEMININE मति forms the instrumental singular as मत्या (इ→य् + आ). मतिना is the MASCULINE इ-stem instrumental (कविना-type) wrongly applied; मत्यै/मतये are dative forms. Only मत्या is the feminine instrumental singular.

17. (B) किम् declines pronominally like तत्: the masculine dative singular is कस्मै. कस्य is the genitive, कस्मात् the ablative, and केन the instrumental — each a separate -स्म-/-स्य cell.

18. (C) The उकारान्त accusative plural is गुरून् (-ऊन्). गुरवः is the nominative plural (subject), गुरूणाम् the genitive plural, and गुरुभिः the instrumental plural.

19. (A) The इकारान्त nominative plural takes -अयः, giving हरयः. हरीन् is the accusative plural, हरीणाम् the genitive plural, and हरिभिः the instrumental plural.

20. (A) The आकारान्त feminine genitive plural is रमाणाम् (with the retroflex ण after the long आ). रमानाम् with a dental न is incorrect; रमासु is the locative plural and रमाभ्यः the dative=ablative plural.

21. (C) The neuter अ-stem shares the masculine non-nom/acc endings, so the dative singular is फलाय. फलेन is the instrumental, फलात् the ablative, and फले the locative singular (also the nom/acc dual).

22. (A) The उकारान्त nominative dual lengthens the stem vowel to गुरू. गुरौ is the locative singular, गुरवः the nominative plural, and गुरुणी is not a valid masculine form (the -नी/-णी dual belongs to neuter -ु stems, not to गुरु).

23. (D) The इकारान्त vocative singular shifts -इः to -ए, giving हे कवे!. हे कविः! merely copies the nominative, हे कवो! borrows the उ-stem -ओ shift, and हे कवये! is the dative.

24. (A) किम् takes the pronominal -स्मात् in the masculine ablative singular: कस्मात्. कस्य is the genitive, कस्मिन् the locative, and कस्मै the dative.

25. (B) The accusative plural lengthens the stem vowel before -न्: अ-stem देव → देवान्, उ-stem गुरु → गुरून्. The endings therefore differ (-आन् vs -ऊन्), and neither equals its nominative plural (देवाः, गुरवः).

26. (A) गुरवे (गुरु, उ-stem) and कवये (कवि, इ-stem) are both the dative singular. The look-alike NOMINATIVE plural of गुरु is गुरवः (not गुरवे), so the pairing as datives is genuinely correct, not a case-mismatch.

27. (A) ते is the masculine nominative plural of तत्, the unaccented enclitic genitive/dative singular of युष्मद् (तव/तुभ्यम् → ते), and the feminine/neuter pronoun dual. The instrumental plural of अस्मद् is अस्माभिः, never ते — so that reading is impossible.

28. (D) तौ is the masculine nominative=accusative DUAL of तत्; तानि is the NEUTER nominative=accusative plural (the neuter takes -आनि like फलानि). The masculine plural is ते and the neuter dual is ते, so the other labels mismatch number or gender.

29. (D) The इकारान्त accusative plural lengthens to कवीन् (-ईन्); the genitive plural adds -नाम् to give कवीनाम्. The nominative plural is the distinct कवयः and the locative plural कविषु.

30. (A) In the आकारान्त feminine the ablative and genitive singular merge to रमायाः, while the dative singular alone is रमायै. The locative singular is the separate रमायाम्.

31. (C) The अ-stem masculine and neuter coincide in the instrumental singular (-एन): देवेन, फलेन. The gender-driven divergence is confined to the nominative/accusative (देवः/देवम् vs फलम्/फलम्), so both -एन forms are genuinely instrumental.

32. (B) Both are the instrumental singular of their इ/उ stems. The retroflex ण in गुरुणा is a regular sandhi after the उ-stem environment; कविना keeps the dental न. Neither is a dative (those would be कवये/गुरवे).

33. (B) रमे is the आकारान्त feminine nom/acc/voc dual, ते the feminine/neuter pronoun dual, and फले the neuter nom/acc dual. None is masculine (masculine duals are देवौ/गुरू-type or तौ), and none is a plural.

34. (A) The masculine/neuter pronoun genitive singular is तस्य; the feminine merges ablative and genitive singular into तस्याः. They are the same case (genitive) but different genders, and तस्य is not a dative (that is तस्मै).

35. (B) गुरवः is the उकारान्त nominative plural (-अवः); गुरौ is the locative singular (-औ). The dative singular look-alike is गुरवे, distinct from गुरवः by one vowel.

36. (B) The अ-stem masculine and neuter take -ऐः in the instrumental plural (देवैः, फलैः); the आकारान्त feminine takes -आभिः (रमाभिः). The -भ्यः ending is the dative=ablative plural, not the instrumental.

37. (A) The relative pronoun यत् declines like तत्: masculine instrumental singular येन. यस्य is the genitive, यस्मै the dative, and यस्मिन् the locative.

38. (C) The masculine nominative plural of यत् is ये (parallel to ते). यान् is the accusative plural, येषाम् the genitive plural, and यैः the instrumental plural.

39. (A) यत् takes the pronominal -स्मै in the masculine dative singular: यस्मै. यस्य is the genitive, यस्मात् the ablative, and येन the instrumental.

40. (C) किम् in the feminine nominative singular is का. कः is the masculine, किम् the neuter, and के the masculine nominative plural — one stem in three genders.

41. (B) The feminine pronoun's genitive plural is तासाम् (-आसाम्). तेषाम् is the MASCULINE genitive plural, तासु the feminine locative plural, and ताभिः the feminine instrumental plural.

42. (D) किम् forms the masculine locative plural केषु (-एषु, with the pronominal क-/के-). केषाम् is the genitive plural, केभ्यः the dative=ablative plural, and कैः the instrumental plural.

43. (B) The NEUTER nominative=accusative singular of the pronoun is तत् itself. सः is the masculine nominative singular (suppletive), तम् the masculine accusative singular, and ते the masculine nominative plural / neuter dual.

44. (D) The masculine accusative plural of तत् is तान् (-आन्, like देवान्). ते is the nominative plural (subject), तैः the instrumental plural, and तेषाम् the genitive plural.

45. (C) Like nouns, the pronoun's dual collapses the instrumental, dative and ablative into ताभ्याम् (-आभ्याम्). तयोः is the genitive=locative dual, ताभिः the instrumental plural, and तासाम् the genitive plural.

46. (C) यत् inserts -स्म- in the ablative singular like तत्: यस्मात्. यस्य is the genitive, यस्मिन् the locative, and यस्मै the dative.

47. (D) The neuter pronoun takes -आनि in the nominative=accusative plural, giving कानि (like तानि/फलानि). के is the masculine plural, किम् the neuter singular, and कान् the masculine accusative plural.

48. (C) The pronoun's dative and ablative plural merge as तेभ्यः (-एभ्यः). तेषाम् is the genitive plural, तेषु the locative plural, and तैः the instrumental plural.

49. (B) The suppletive nominative singular of अस्मद् is अहम् ('I'). वयम् is the nominative plural ('we'), माम् the accusative singular ('me'), and मया the instrumental singular ('by me').

50. (B) The genitive singular of अस्मद् is मम ('my'). मह्यम् is the dative singular ('to me'), मत् the ablative singular ('from me'), and अस्माकम् the genitive PLURAL ('our').

51. (D) The dative singular of अस्मद् is मह्यम् ('to me'). मम is the genitive singular ('my'), मयि the locative singular ('in me'), and अस्मभ्यम् the dative PLURAL ('to us').

52. (D) The genitive plural of अस्मद् is अस्माकम् ('our'). अस्मभ्यम् is the dative plural ('to us'), अस्माभिः the instrumental plural ('by us'), and अस्मासु the locative plural ('in us').

53. (D) The suppletive nominative plural of युष्मद् is यूयम् ('you all'). त्वम् is the nominative singular ('you'), युष्मान् the accusative plural ('you all', object), and युष्माकम् the genitive plural ('your').

54. (A) The dative singular of युष्मद् is तुभ्यम् ('to you'). तव is the genitive singular ('your'), त्वया the instrumental singular ('by you'), and त्वयि the locative singular ('in you').

55. (A) The genitive plural of युष्मद् is युष्माकम् ('your', plural). युष्मभ्यम् is the dative plural ('to you all'), युष्माभिः the instrumental plural ('by you all'), and तव the genitive SINGULAR ('your', one person).

56. (C) तस्मै is the pronoun's masculine dative singular, तेषाम् its masculine genitive plural, मह्यम् the dative singular of अस्मद्, and गुर्वोः the genitive=locative dual of गुरु. Each other row misnames at least one cell (the genitive plural is तेषाम् not तेषु; the dative=ablative plural is तेभ्यः not तेषाम्).

57. (A) तासाम् is the FEMININE pronoun genitive plural (the masculine is तेषाम्), कानि the neuter किम् nominative=accusative plural, त्वया the युष्मद् instrumental singular, and रमायै the आकारान्त feminine dative singular. The other rows mismatch gender, number or case.

58. (C) Pronominal declension uses the special -स्मै dative singular (तस्मै) and the -एषाम् genitive plural (तेषाम्), where the अकारान्त noun देव takes the ordinary देवाय and देवानाम्. तस्य is the genitive (not dative) and तेभ्यः the dative=ablative plural (not genitive).

59. (D) यस्मिन् is the relative pronoun's masculine locative singular (-स्मिन्), अस्माकम् the genitive plural of अस्मद्, तानि the neuter तत् nominative=accusative plural, and मत्या the instrumental singular of the इकारान्त feminine मति. The other rows misname the case, number or gender of at least one form (the ablative is यस्मात्, the dative plural अस्मभ्यम्, the neuter dual ते).

60. (C) कस्मात् is the masculine ablative singular of किम् (the dative would be कस्मै), तुभ्यम् the dative singular of युष्मद् (the genitive is तव, the dative plural युष्मभ्यम्), फले the neuter locative singular which is ALSO the nom/acc dual, and गुरून् the accusative plural of गुरु (the genitive plural is गुरूणाम्).
