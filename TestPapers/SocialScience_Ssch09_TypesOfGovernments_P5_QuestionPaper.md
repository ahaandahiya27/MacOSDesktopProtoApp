**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Apex traps reward students who reason from definitions through layered scenarios rather than from surface labels: a label that fits one feature can still be wrong overall, several distinct labels can describe the very same country, and the absence of any single democratic pillar can quietly hollow out an election. 1. A new state writes a constitution that lists three named bodies: one drafts and votes on statutes, one runs the daily administration and commands officials, and one hears disputes about whether actions break those statutes. A reform later merges all three into one office held by a single person. The reform most directly destroys which safeguard, and what is the chief danger?
   (A) Separation of powers; with lawmaking, enforcement and judging in one hand, nothing remains to check that single office, so power can be abused without limit
   (B) Universal adult franchise; the merger removes the right of every adult to vote in elections
   (C) The principle of accountability through a free press; the merger silences newspapers directly
   (D) Judicial review of elections; the merger ends the holding of elections rather than affecting any organ's independence
