**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Every item chains three or more relations, so a single mis-applied rule — equating co-interior angles instead of supplementing them, treating a non-parallel crossing as parallel, or halting before the last linear-pair step — lands you on a pre-computed wrong option. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. Two lines AB and CD cross at O, with rays OA, OC, OB, OD in order around the point. ∠AOC = (2x + 18)° and the adjacent angle ∠AOD = (3x − 8)° form a linear pair. Find the measure of ∠BOC.
   (A) 94°
   (B) 86°
   (C) 34°
   (D) 46°

2. Two lines intersect at O. One of the four angles is (5x − 4)° and the angle vertically opposite it is (3x + 30)°. Find the measure of the angle that forms a linear pair with these.

   (C) 99°
   (D) 162°

3. Lines AB and CD intersect at O with ∠AOC and ∠BOD vertically opposite. ∠AOC = (4x + 9)° with x = 12. Ray OE lies inside ∠BOD so that ∠BOE = 31°. Find ∠DOE.
