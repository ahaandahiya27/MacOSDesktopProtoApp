**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Distractors weaponise the plain-mean average-speed error, the wrong-direction unit conversion, slope-vs-y-value graph confusion, harmonic-mean traps over equal distances, pendulum period blamed on mass/amplitude, rest segments counted as travel, and cumulative-graph 'return' read as extra distance. 1. A spinning top stays in one place while every point on it traces circles about the central spike, and the top returns to the same orientation at regular intervals. The two descriptors that best fit a point on its rim are:

   (C) circular and oscillatory
   (D) circular and periodic
