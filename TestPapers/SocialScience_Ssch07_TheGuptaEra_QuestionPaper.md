**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic traps: Chandragupta II (Gupta) is NOT Chandragupta Maurya; the Iron Pillar resists rust because of its phosphorus-rich iron, not because 'iron cannot rust'; Faxian saw mainly the prosperous cities and must be cross-checked; the Guptas governed the north and centre directly while the Pallavas (south) and Kamarupa (northeast) ruled their own lands; and a 'Golden Age' means cultural brilliance, not equality for all. Keep the order of the rulers and their works straight. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
