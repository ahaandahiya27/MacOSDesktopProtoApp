**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: नमः/स्वाहा/स्वस्ति govern the DATIVE (चतुर्थी, -आय for a-stems, -ये for रवि), so मित्रः/सूर्यः (nominative) are wrong with नमः; सूर्यनमस्कारः is ONE compound (समास) where the first member drops its case ending, NOT the two words सूर्याय नमस्कारः; मित्र here is a Sun-name (not a friend or the moon); आचार्या (-आ) is female while आचार्यः (-ः) is male; there are exactly TWELVE Sun-names and TWELVE postures; and स्वस्थ = स्व + स्थ (self-abiding), glossed नीरोगम्.
