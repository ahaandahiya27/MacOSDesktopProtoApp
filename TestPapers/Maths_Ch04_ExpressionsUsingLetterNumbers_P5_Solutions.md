## Worked Solutions

**1. (C)** First courier: 18(d - 3) + 70 = 18d - 54 + 70 = 18d + 16. Second courier: 25d. Extra cost of the second over the first = 25d - (18d + 16) = 25d - 18d - 16 = 7d - 16. Writing 7d + 16 fails to carry the minus onto the +16 constant. Adding the two per-km rates as 43d ignores that one whole cost is being subtracted from the other. Keeping -70 forgets that the 70 was already reduced by the 18*3 covered kilometres. Seven d minus sixteen is the simplified difference.

**2. (B)** a^2 = 4 so 2a^2 = 8. ab = (-2)(5) = -10 so -3ab = -3*(-10) = +30. b^2 = 25. Total 8 + 30 + 25 = 63. Treating -3ab as -3*(+10) = -30 (dropping the negative sign on a) gives 8 - 30 + 25 = 3, and a further slip lands on -17. Using 2a^2 = (2*-2)^2 = 16 instead of 2*(a^2) gives 16 + 30 - ... an inflated total. The value sixty-three is the only one that keeps every sign on the substitution.

**3. (C)** Inner bracket first: 3x - (x - 4y) = 3x - x + 4y = 2x + 4y. Then -2[2x + 4y] = -4x - 8y. The first product 5(2x - 3y) = 10x - 15y. Sum: 10x - 15y - 4x - 8y + 7y. x-terms: 10x - 4x = 6x. y-terms: -15y - 8y + 7y = -16y. Result 6x - 16y. Distributing the -2 over only the 3x leaves the y-count short, giving -2y. Forgetting to combine the 10x and -4x gives 2x. Mis-signing the -8y gives +16y. Six x minus sixteen y is correct.

**4. (A)** Factor out -12: -12(2x) + (-12)(-3) = -24x + 36 = 36 - 24x. Check the choice: -12(2x - 3) = -24x + 36, correct. 12(3 - 24x) = 36 - 288x, which over-shrinks the x-term. -12(2x + 3) = -24x - 36, wrong constant sign. 6(6 - 24x) = 36 - 144x, again the wrong coefficient on x. Only minus-twelve times the quantity two x minus three reproduces the original.

**5. (C)** Stage n has (n + 1)^2 tiles, since stage 1 = 4 = 2^2, stage 2 = 9 = 3^2. Going to stage n+1 gives (n + 2)^2. The increase is (n + 2)^2 - (n + 1)^2 = (n^2 + 4n + 4) - (n^2 + 2n + 1) = 2n + 3. Check directly: 9 - 4 = 5 = 2(1)+3, 16 - 9 = 7 = 2(2)+3. The plain square-difference 2n + 1 fits a pattern starting at 1^2, not this one shifted up by one. n + 1 mistakes the side-length growth for the tile growth. Two n plus three is the actual difference.

**6. (C)** The term -3x has coefficient -3 (the sign stays with the number). The term 5x^2 has coefficient 5. The constant term is the number with no letter, 8. The order asked is coefficient of x, then x^2, then constant: -3, 5, 8. Reporting +3 drops the minus that belongs to the x-term. Reporting -8 sign-flips a constant that is genuinely positive. Reporting -1 for x confuses the coefficient of x with that of x^3. Minus three, five, eight is correct.

**7. (D)** Increase by 5: x + 5. Double it: 2(x + 5) = 2x + 10. Subtract 3 times the original: 2x + 10 - 3x = -x + 10. Forgetting to distribute the doubling over the 5 (writing 2x + 5) gives -x + 5. Doubling only x (and adding 5 separately as 5x) misreads the wording. Minus x plus ten keeps both the distributed 10 and the single leftover x.

**8. (D)** (a - b)^2 = (7 - 3)^2 = 4^2 = 16. a^2 - b^2 = 49 - 9 = 40. So 16 - 40 = -24. Treating (a - b)^2 as a^2 - b^2 (the classic squaring-a-difference error) would make the whole thing 0, and a partial slip lands on -8. Forgetting the leading minus on the bracket gives +24. Stopping at a^2 - b^2 reports 40. Minus twenty-four keeps the true square 16 minus 40.

**9. (C)** Innermost: 3a - (a - 5) = 3a - a + 5 = 2a + 5. Next level: [2a + 5] so 2a - (2a + 5) - 4 = 2a - 2a - 5 - 4 = -9. Now 4a - {-9} = 4a + 9. Mishandling the double minus as -9 kept negative gives 4a - 9 then a slip to 4a - 1. Dropping the -4 inside gives 4a + ... a different constant. Four a plus nine results from turning the subtracted brace, which evaluates to negative nine, into a plus nine.

**10. (B)** Test each: 15(2x - 3) = 30x - 45, equivalent. -15(3 - 2x) = -45 + 30x = 30x - 45, equivalent. 5(6x - 9) = 30x - 45, equivalent. But 5(6x - 8) = 30x - 40, which has the wrong constant (-40 not -45), so it is NOT equivalent. The trap rewards checking the constant term after distributing, not just the x-coefficient.

**11. (D)** 10th term: 3(10) + 2 = 32. 20th term: 3(20) + 2 = 62. Sum 32 + 62 = 94. Using only 3n and forgetting the +2 on each term gives 30 + 60 = 90. Reporting just the 20th term (62) ignores the word SUM. Treating the sum's index as 30 and computing one term 3(30)+2 = 92 is close but wrong logic; ninety-four is the genuine total of the two named terms.

**12. (A)** At x = 4: P = 16 - 3 = 13; Q = 12 + 1 = 13; R = 8 + 6 = 14; S = 16 - 5 = 11. The only equal pair is P and Q, both 13. R = 14 and S = 11 are the distractor values of the unmatched expressions. Computing x^2 as (4)^2 correctly is essential; a slip to 16 for P would wrongly pair it elsewhere. Thirteen is the shared value of P and Q.

**13. (B)** Add the two fixed charges: 2000 + 500 = 2500. The only per-guest charge is 60 each, giving 60g. Total 60g + 2500. Forgetting the 500 cleaning fee leaves 60g + 2000. Merging the fixed 2500 into the rate as 2560g wrongly multiplies a one-time fee by g. Dropping the 2000 base leaves 60g + 500. Sixty g plus twenty-five hundred combines both flat fees correctly.

**14. (B)** 2x - y = 6 - (-1) = 7, so (2x - y)^2 = 49. x + y = 3 + (-1) = 2. So 49 / 2 = 24.5. Computing 2x - y as 6 - 1 = 5 (sign slip) gives 25/2 = 12.5, near a wrong choice. Forgetting to square before dividing or halving twice yields 12.25. Twenty-four point five is the squared numerator over the correct denominator two.

**15. (B)** Distribute the middle minus over every term: -(x^2 - 4x - 3) = -x^2 + 4x + 3. Combine: x^2 terms 3 - 1 = 2 giving 2x^2; x terms -2 + 4 + 2 = 4 giving 4x; constants 5 + 3 - 1 = 7. Result 2x^2 + 4x + 7. Distributing the minus over only the first term leaves a +1 constant. Keeping -4x as +(-4x)... mishandling the +4x flips the x-sign. Two x-squared plus four x plus seven is the full distribution.

**16. (A)** (1/3)(9x - 6) = 3x - 2. (1/2)(4x - 10) = 2x - 5. Subtract: (3x - 2) - (2x - 5) = 3x - 2 - 2x + 5 = x + 3. Failing to flip the sign of the -5 when subtracting the bracket gives x - 7. Adding the two leading coefficients as 5x ignores that one bracket is subtracted. Mis-signing the constants gives x - 3. x plus three is the careful difference.

**17. (D)** Layer 6: 6*7 = 42. Layer 7: 7*8 = 56. Added = 56 - 42 = 14. The differences run 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 — the jump into layer 7 is the sixth difference, 14. Reading the difference pattern off by one gives 12. Reporting layer 6's total (42) or layer 7's total (56) answers a different question. Fourteen is the genuine increment.

**18. (C)** The sign always travels with the coefficient: -2x^2 gives coefficient -2; +7x gives coefficient 7; the lone number -9 is the constant term. The option claiming +2 drops the minus on the squared term. The option with -7 sign-flips the x-term, which is genuinely positive. The option with +9 sign-flips the constant. Only minus two, seven, minus nine reads every sign faithfully.

**19. (C)** Mango cost: 240n. Orange crates number 2n at 180 each: 180*2n = 360n. Subtotal 240n + 360n = 600n, plus the one-time 150 transport = 600n + 150. Forgetting to double the orange crates gives 240n + 180n = 420n. Multiplying the flat charge by something doubles the constant to 300. Treating 'twice as many' as a power inflates to n-squared. Six hundred n plus one fifty is correct.

**20. (B)** Numerator a^2 - b^2 = 81 - 16 = 65. Denominator a - b = 9 - 4 = 5. 65 / 5 = 13. (This equals a + b = 13, a useful check.) Reporting 5 gives only the denominator. Reporting 65 stops at the numerator. Dividing the wrong way (5/65... or mis-squaring) yields 3.25. Thirteen is the quotient and matches a + b.

**21. (C)** First brace: 2a - (a - b) = 2a - a + b = a + b; times 3 gives 3a + 3b. Second brace: 3b - (a + b) = 3b - a - b = -a + 2b; times -2 gives 2a - 4b. Add the trailing a. Total: (3a + 2a + a) + (3b - 4b) = 6a - b. Forgetting the minus on the second bracket flips to +b. Mis-distributing the leading 3 over only the first term loses 2a. Six a minus b is the careful sum.

**22. (D)** 2(3x - 4) = 6x - 8. -3(2x - 5) = -6x + 15. 4(x - 2) = 4x - 8. x-terms: 6x - 6x + 4x = 4x. Constants: -8 + 15 - 8 = -1. Result 4x - 1. Distributing the minus over only the 2x (leaving +5 unchanged sign) gives constant +15. Forgetting one -8 lands on -21. Adding all coefficients as 12x ignores the cancellation. Four x minus one is correct.

**23. (D)** Row 1 = 14 means at r = 1 the rule must give 14. With common difference 3 the rule is 3r + c where 3(1) + c = 14, so c = 11: rule 3r + 11. Check row 2: 3(2) + 11 = 17 = 14 + 3, correct. Row 8: 3(8) + 11 = 35. Writing 3r + 14 over-counts because it gives 17 at row 1. Swapping rate and start gives 14r + 3. Three r plus eleven, value thirty-five, fits the first row exactly.

**24. (B)** At x = 5: A = 25 - 10 = 15; B = 15 + 2 = 17; C = 10 + 1 = 11; D = 20 - 7 = 13. Ranking from greatest: B (17), A (15), D (13), C (11), so B, A, D, C. Assuming the squared expression is automatically largest puts A first wrongly. Mixing up C and D (11 vs 13) gives the orders ending C, D. B, A, D, C is the strict decreasing order.

**25. (D)** Substitute s = 7: 7(7 - 3)/2 = 7*4/2 = 28/2 = 14. Forgetting to halve gives 28. Computing 7 - 3 then ignoring the multiply-and-halve gives a stray small number. Using s(s - 3) without the division by 2 yields 28; only fourteen applies the full rule including the divide-by-two.

**26. (A)** a = -3. a^2 = 9, so -a^2 = -9. (-a)^2 = (3)^2 = 9, so -(-a)^2 = -9. 2a = -6. Sum: -9 - 9 - 6 = -24. Treating -a^2 as (+9) (mishandling the sign convention) cancels with the next term to give a wrong 0 or -6. Recognising that BOTH -a^2 and -(-a)^2 equal -9 here, plus 2a = -6, gives minus twenty-four.

**27. (D)** Note 2yx is the same like-term as xy. The xy-terms: 5xy + 2xy - xy = 6xy, coefficient 6. (The x^2 terms combine to -4x^2 and the constant is +4, but the question asks only for the xy-coefficient.) Forgetting that 2yx = 2xy and treating it as unlike gives 4. Adding the 2 twice gives 7. Missing the -xy gives 7 or leaving 5. Six is the combined xy-coefficient.

**28. (A)** The rental r covers the first 100 minutes, so only (m - 100) chargeable minutes cost 2 each: 2(m - 100) + r. Writing 2m + r charges all m minutes, double-counting the 100 free ones. Writing 2m - 100 distributes the 2 over only m and forgets to multiply the 100. Two times the quantity m minus one hundred, plus r, is correct.

**29. (B)** 3a - 2b = 3(-1) - 2(-2) = -3 + 4 = 1. a + b = -1 + (-2) = -3. Product 1 * (-3) = -3. Sign-slipping -2b as -(-2)... computing 3a - 2b = -3 - 4 = -7 (wrong sign on the -2b term) leads astray. With both brackets correct, one times minus three is minus three.

**30. (A)** A 2-by-n rectangle has length n and width 2, so perimeter = 2(length + width) = 2(n + 2) = 2n + 4. Using a single row of n squares (perimeter 2n + 2) ignores the second stacked row's effect on width. Treating it as 4 times n confuses area-like counting. Two n plus four is the perimeter of the two-high rectangle.

**31. (C)** Coefficients with signs: 5, -2, 3, -4, 1. Their sum 5 - 2 + 3 - 4 + 1 = 3. Substituting x = 1 makes every power 1, so the expression equals exactly that coefficient sum, 3. Adding magnitudes without signs gives 15. The x = 0 value is just the constant 5, not the sum. Three, equalling the value at x = 1, is the correct pairing.

**32. (C)** Ordinary pay: 40w. Overtime rate is 1.5w per hour for 6 hours: 1.5w * 6 = 9w. Total 40w + 9w = 49w. Forgetting the 1.5 multiplier and paying overtime at the plain rate gives 46w. Multiplying 6 by 1.5 to get 9 but adding it as a constant 9 rather than 9w gives 40w + 9. Forty-nine w combines both blocks at the right rates.

**33. (D)** x + y = 8.5, squared = 72.25. x - y = 3.5, squared = 12.25. Difference 72.25 - 12.25 = 60. (This matches the identity 4xy = 4*6*2.5 = 60.) Stopping at one square gives 72.25 or 12.25. Halving the result mistakenly gives 30. Sixty is the difference of the two squares and equals four x y.

**34. (A)** Inside the bracket: 3(a - b) = 3a - 3b; 2(2a - b) = 4a - 2b; so 3a - 3b - (4a - 2b) = 3a - 3b - 4a + 2b = -a - b. The whole becomes 2(a - 3b) - [-a - b] = 2a - 6b + a + b = 3a - 5b. Coefficient of a is 3. Forgetting to distribute the inner minus over -2b gives a different a-coefficient. Mishandling the outer minus on the bracket gives 1. Three is the a-coefficient after full simplification.

**35. (B)** (1/2)(4x - 6) = 2x - 3. (2/3)(9x + 3) = 6x + 2. Sum so far: 2x - 3 + 6x + 2 = 8x - 1. Subtract 5x: 8x - 1 - 5x = 3x - 1. Mis-adding the constants as +1 gives 3x + 1. Forgetting to subtract the 5x leaves 8x - 1. Dropping the +2 from the second product gives a -5 constant. Three x minus one is correct.

**36. (B)** 8th term: 8^2 - 8 = 64 - 8 = 56. 6th term: 6^2 - 6 = 36 - 6 = 30. Difference 56 - 30 = 26. **37. (A)** Gift packs: 5 packs of (2b + 3p) = 10b + 15p. Deluxe packs: 2 packs of (4b + 1p) = 8b + 2p. Total: (10b + 8b) + (15p + 2p) = 18b + 17p. Forgetting the deluxe pens (2p) gives 18b + 15p, or a near-miss 16p. Counting only one pack-type's books gives 14b. Eighteen b plus seventeen p adds both pack types fully.

**38. (B)** (2a)^2 = (6)^2 = 36. 2a^2 = 2*9 = 18. a(a - 4) = 3(3 - 4) = 3(-1) = -3. Expression: 36 - 18 - (-3) = 36 - 18 + 3 = 21. Twenty-one keeps (2a)^2 = 36 and the +3 from the subtracted negative.

**39. (A)** Inner: 3p - (p - q) = 3p - p + q = 2p + q. Times -2: -4p - 2q. Then 7p - 4p - 2q + 5q = 3p + 3q. Distributing the -2 over only the 3p (leaving the +q alone) gives 3p + 7q. Forgetting the inner sign flip on q gives 3p - q. Three p plus three q is the careful result.

**40. (D)** Perimeter = 2(length + breadth) = 2[(2x + 1) + (x - 3)] = 2[3x - 2] = 6x - 4. So 2[(2x+1)+(x-3)], 6x - 4, and 2(3x - 2) all equal the perimeter. But 6x + 4 has the wrong constant sign (the breadth's -3 should pull the constant negative), so it is NOT equivalent. The trap is dropping the minus on the breadth's constant when adding inside the bracket.

**41. (C)** Rule: 5n + 1 (since 5(1)+1 = 6, 5(2)+1 = 11, 5(3)+1 = 16). Figure 4: 5(4)+1 = 21. Total of figures 1-4: 6 + 11 + 16 + 21 = 54. Stopping at figure 3's running total or using figure 4 = 5(4) = 20 (dropping the +1) gives 53 then drifts to 44. Reporting just figure 4 (21) answers a different question. Fifty-four is the sum of the first four figures.

**42. (B)** A term whose coefficient is 0 adds 0 for every value of x, so removing 0x^2 leaves an identical expression: 4x^3 + 7x - 2. The degree (highest power present with nonzero coefficient) is still 3, set by 4x^3, so it is unchanged. The constant -2 is untouched by removing a different term. The truth of the simplification does not depend on the sign of x. The student is correct.

**43. (B)** Wheat: 20x. Rice: 15(x + 4) = 15x + 60. Subtotal 20x + 15x + 60 = 35x + 60. Subtract the 30 lost: 35x + 60 - 30 = 35x + 30. Forgetting to subtract the loss leaves 35x + 60. Sign-confusing the 60 and 30 gives 35x - 30. Thirty-five x plus thirty accounts for both crops and the loss.

**44. (D)** Expand in letters: (a + 2b)^2 = a^2 + 4ab + 4b^2; 4b(a + b) = 4ab + 4b^2. Difference = a^2. So the value is just a^2 = 5^2 = 25, regardless of b. **45. (A)** Innermost: b - (a - b) = b - a + b = -a + 2b. Next: 2a - [-a + 2b] = 2a + a - 2b = 3a - 2b. **46. (B)** 3(2x - 1) = 6x - 3. -2(x - 4) = -2x + 8. (x - 3) = x - 3. x-terms: 6x - 2x + x = 5x. Constants: -3 + 8 - 3 = 2. Result 5x + 2. **48. (A)** At x = 2: A = 2^3 = 8; B = 4*2 = 8; C = 4 + 5 = 9; D = 4 + 1 = 5. The only equal pair is A and B, both 8. C = 9 and D = 5 are the unmatched values. Reading x^3 as 3x = 6 (the cube-versus-triple confusion) would break the match. Eight is the value shared by A and B.

**49. (B)** First 5 cost 5*40 = 200. The remaining (p - 5) cost 28 each: 28(p - 5) = 28p - 140. Total 28p - 140 + 200 = 28p + 60. Adding 200 without reducing it by the 28*5 overlap gives 28p + 200. **50. (D)** At x = 3: (3*3 - 1)/2 = 8/2 = 4. (x - 5)/4 = (3 - 5)/4 = -2/4 = -0.5; subtracting it gives -(-0.5) = +0.5. Add x = 3. Total 4 + 0.5 + 3 = 7.5. **51. (C)** xy-like terms: 4xy + 3yx - xy = 4 + 3 - 1 = 6, giving 6xy (since yx = xy). x^2y-like terms: -2x^2y - x^2y = -3x^2y. **52. (C)** Joining 120. First 6 months: 6*80 = 480. Remaining (m - 6) months: 50(m - 6) = 50m - 300. Total 120 + 480 + 50m - 300 = 50m + 300. Adding 480 + 120 = 600 without the -300 reduction gives 50m + 480. Charging every month at 80 gives 80m + 120. The option 50(m - 6) + 120 forgets the 480 already-paid block. **53. (D)** (a + b)^2 = 25. 2ab = 12. So a^2 + b^2 = 25 - 12 = 13. (Indeed a, b = 2 and 3 give 4 + 9 = 13.) Reporting (a+b)^2 alone gives 25. Adding 2ab instead of subtracting gives 25 + 12 = 37. Thirteen uses the identity with the correct minus sign.

**54. (D)** Inner: 2a - (a - b) = a + b; times -3 gives -3a - 3b. First product 6(2a - b) = 12a - 6b. Last: -(a - 4b) = -a + 4b. Combine a-terms: 12a - 3a - a = 8a. b-terms: -6b - 3b + 4b = -5b. So 8a - 5b; coefficient of b is -5. Forgetting the +4b from the last bracket gives -9. Mis-signing the inner bracket gives -2. Minus five is the b-coefficient.

**55. (D)** Inside: 3(x - 1) = 3x - 3; minus (x - 4) = -x + 4; sum 3x - 3 - x + 4 = 2x + 1. Times 2: 4x + 2. Then -(4x - 2) = -4x + 2. Total: 4x + 2 - 4x + 2 = 4 (the x-terms cancel). **56. (A)** Layers: 1, 4, 9, 16. Sum = 1 + 4 + 9 + 16 = 30. Reporting only the 4th layer (16) answers a different question. Stopping at the 3rd layer's running total (1+4+9 = 14) or mis-adding gives 20/25. Thirty is the full sum of the first four square numbers.

**58. (C)** At a = -2: first pair (2*-2 - 3) = -7 and (-2 + 2) = 0, so the first product is -7 * 0 = 0. Second pair (a - 1) = -3 and (a + 4) = 2, so the second product is -3 * 2 = -6. **59. (A)** 9x^2 - 4 at x = 2: 9*4 - 4 = 36 - 4 = 32. (3x - 2)(3x + 2) = (6 - 2)(6 + 2) = 4 * 8 = 32. Both equal 32, confirming the two expressions agree (a difference-of-squares pairing). **60. (A)** Addition is commutative, so the terms of an expression may be written in any order without changing its value for any substitution: -2x^2 + 5x + 6 is the very same expression as 6 + 5x - 2x^2. The sign of the whole expression is not fixed by the leading term's sign (e.g. at x = 1 it equals 9, positive). There is no rule forcing the constant first. The equality holds for every x, not only x = 0. They are the same expression.
