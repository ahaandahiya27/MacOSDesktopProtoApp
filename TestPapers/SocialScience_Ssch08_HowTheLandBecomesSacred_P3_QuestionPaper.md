**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Sacredness in India is not limited to buildings — journeys, rivers, mountains, forests, trees and the whole land can be sacred; pilgrimage and trade routes usually overlapped rather than staying separate; and many holy sites are shared across faiths.
