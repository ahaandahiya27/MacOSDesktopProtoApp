# Olympiad Test Paper — Social Science (Class 7)
## Chapter 12: Understanding Markets

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | Social Science (NCERT Class 7) |
| **Chapter** | 12 — Understanding Markets |
| **Total Questions** | 60 (Multiple Choice, single correct) |
| **Maximum Marks** | 240 |
| **Suggested Time** | 90 minutes |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic traps: a market is ANY arrangement with buyers, sellers and a price - online counts, not just a physical shop; the cheapest price is NOT always best (too low and the seller stops selling) - the 'just right' price balances both sides; more demand against limited supply pushes price UP, not down; and middlemen (wholesalers, distributors, retailers) ADD value rather than being useless. Match the certification marks (FSSAI food, ISI/BIS goods, AGMARK farm produce, BEE Star energy) correctly. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
