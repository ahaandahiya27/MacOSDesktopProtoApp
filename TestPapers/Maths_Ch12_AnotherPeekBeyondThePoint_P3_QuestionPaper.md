**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. The two commonest traps are misplacing the decimal point in a product (counting too few or too many decimal places) and, in division by a decimal, scaling only one of the two numbers instead of both by the same power of ten.
