**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Distractors are close-but-wrong: average speed taken as a plain mean of two speeds, unit conversions multiplied the wrong way, pendulum period blamed on bob mass or amplitude, distance-time graph slope confused with the y-value, speedometer/odometer swapped, and rest segments counted as motion. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. A point on the second-hand tip of a wall clock keeps tracing the same circle, returning to the 12 mark every 60 s. The two descriptors that fit this motion best are:
   (A) rectilinear and oscillatory
   (B) oscillatory and non-periodic
   (C) circular and periodic
   (D) random and rotational

   (A) 16 m/s
