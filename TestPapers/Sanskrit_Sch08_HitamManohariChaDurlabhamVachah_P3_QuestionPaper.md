**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic traps: च is the conjunction 'AND' (both useful AND pleasing), never 'or' (वा); दुर्लभम् is RARE / hard-to-find, not impossible (अशक्यम्); विद्या is gathered क्षणशः (moment by moment) while अर्थ is gathered कणशः (particle by particle) — the adverbs do not swap; -शस् builds distributive adverbs ('by units of'), not datives; a क्रियावान् (doer) is praised over a विकत्थन (boaster), i.e. कर्म over कथन; सूक्तिः (सु+उक्ति, a good saying / सुभाषितम्) only looks like सूक्ष्म (subtle) — different roots; and through all the counsel on speech and effort the chapter's deepest value remains शीलम् (character).
