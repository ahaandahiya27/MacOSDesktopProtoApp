Watch the synthesis traps: each boy's count of nine is an OFF-BY-ONE error of self-omission, not a real loss; तीर्त्वा is a गेरुंड/ल्यबन्त (indeclinable 'having crossed'), अगणयत् the लङ्-लकार past of गण् ('count', NOT गा 'sing' → अगायत्); ordinals (दशमः 'tenth') agree in case-gender-number while cardinals (दश 'ten') need not; कतमः = which-of-MANY, कतरः = which-of-TWO; द्वितीयाविभक्ति marks the crossed-object (नदीम्) while the gerund's logical subject stays the sentence subject; and 'दशमः त्वम् असि' is तत्त्वमसि, not a queue position.
