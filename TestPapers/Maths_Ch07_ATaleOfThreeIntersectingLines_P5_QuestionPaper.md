**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. At this hardest tier every item chains several facts: the exterior angle equals the SUM of the two remote interiors (never the adjacent one); two sides must STRICTLY exceed the third (equal fails); 'acute' needs ALL three angles below 90° while a single 90° makes it right and any angle above 90° makes it obtuse; the largest angle always sits opposite the longest side; the angle between two interior bisectors of a triangle is 90° + half the third angle; an altitude is a perpendicular (not a side or median) and its foot can fall outside an obtuse triangle; and a count of integer third-side values must respect strict inequalities at both ends. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
