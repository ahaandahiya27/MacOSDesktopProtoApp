**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. The Maurya empire broke up from within rather than being destroyed by foreign invaders; the foreign rulers were largely absorbed into a dominant Indian culture rather than erasing it; a matrimonial alliance was an act of diplomatic integration; the Gandhara school is Greco-Buddhist while Mathura is the indigenous red-sandstone school; and royal women of the age could wield real power.
