**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. At this level the deadliest slips are losing a power of ten while chaining several multiplications and divisions, scaling only one number when dividing by a decimal, miscounting decimal places in a product, and confusing a place-value shift with appending zeros; estimate the order of magnitude of every answer before committing to a decimal point. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
