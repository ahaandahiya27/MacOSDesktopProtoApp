**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic slips: subtracting a negative ADDS, the sign of a product is set by the parity (odd/even) of negative factors, multiplication and division bind tighter than + and − so brackets must be cleared in the right order, and a single hidden factor of 0 collapses the whole product. 35. Which is the correct evaluation of (−10) − 6 × (−2) ÷ 3?
   (A) Apply x and ÷ before the subtraction, left to right: 6 x (−2) = −12, then −12 ÷ 3 = −4, so (−10) − (−4) = −6.
   (B) Work strictly left to right ignoring precedence: (−10) − 6 = −16, then (−16) x (−2) = 32, 32 ÷ 3 is about 10.7.
   (C) (−10) − 6 x (−2) = (−10) + 12 = 2 first, then 2 ÷ 3 is about 0.7.
   (D) 6 x (−2) ÷ 3 = −4, then read the minus as 'add': (−10) + (−4) = −14.
