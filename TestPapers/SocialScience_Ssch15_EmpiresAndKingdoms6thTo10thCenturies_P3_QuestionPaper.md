The post-Gupta age was a creative one of temples, universities, the rules of zero and Bhakti — not a 'dark age'; the Arabs conquered only Sindh in 712 CE and stayed confined to that foothold; and the contest for Kanauj was three-sided (tripartite among Palas, Pratiharas and Rashtrakutas), not a simple two-way fight.
