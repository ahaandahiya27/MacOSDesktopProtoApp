# Olympiad Test Paper — Social Science (Class 7)

| | |
|---|---|

| **Total Questions** | 60 (Multiple Choice, single correct) |
| **Maximum Marks** | 240 |
| **Suggested Time** | 90 minutes |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic traps: the government is only ONE of the state's four parts (population, territory, government, sovereignty) and changes with elections while the state endures; the majority CANNOT do whatever it wants (rights and the rule of law protect minorities); NOT all democracies are republics (the UK has a monarch); the police and army are STATE machinery the government directs, not the government itself; and the executive has TWO parts - the political executive (ministers) and the permanent executive (bureaucracy). Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. A 'state' (or nation) is best described as:
   (A) only the buildings where ministers work
