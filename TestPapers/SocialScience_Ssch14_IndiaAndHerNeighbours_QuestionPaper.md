# Olympiad Test Paper — Social Science (Class 7)
## Chapter 14: India and Her Neighbours

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | Social Science (NCERT Class 7) |
| **Chapter** | 14 — India and Her Neighbours |
| **Total Questions** | 60 (Multiple Choice, single correct) |
| **Maximum Marks** | 240 |
| **Suggested Time** | 90 minutes |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic traps: a neighbour need NOT share a land border - Sri Lanka and the Maldives are maritime neighbours; an 'open border' (India-Nepal) still IS a border, just visa-free; Indian culture spread to Thailand and Indonesia PEACEFULLY through trade, travel and religion, not by conquest (so the Ramakien is Thailand's own version of the Ramayana); and landlocked neighbours like Nepal and Bhutan still need the sea, reaching it through Indian ports. Use the pack's figures (land boundary ~15,100 km, coastline ~11,100 km). Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
