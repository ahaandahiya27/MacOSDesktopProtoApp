**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Read each diagram description carefully and label the angles yourself. Remember the three big traps: corresponding/alternate angles are equal ONLY when the lines are parallel; co-interior (same-side interior) angles are SUPPLEMENTARY (add to 180°), not equal; and only PERPENDICULAR crossings give four equal angles. Chase angles step by step using linear pairs and vertically-opposite equalities — you rarely need a protractor. Do not write on this paper.

1. Two distinct straight lines in a plane can intersect in at most how many points?

   (C) 2
   (D) infinitely many

2. Two straight lines cross at a point, forming four angles. One of them is 110°. The angle vertically opposite to it measures:
   (A) 70°
   (B) 90°
