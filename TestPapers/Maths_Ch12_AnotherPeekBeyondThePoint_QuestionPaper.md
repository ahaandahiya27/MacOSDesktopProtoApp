**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. For multiplication, multiply the digits as whole numbers and then place the point so the product has as many decimal places as the two factors together; for division by a decimal, scale BOTH numbers by the same power of ten until the divisor is whole. Estimate first — the commonest trap here is a misplaced decimal point (an answer ten or a hundred times too big or too small). Watch the difference between "multiply by 100" (shift the point) and "add two zeros".
