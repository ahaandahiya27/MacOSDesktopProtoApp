**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic traps: the post-Gupta age was CREATIVE (temples, universities, the rules of zero, Bhakti), not a 'dark age'; the Arabs conquered only SINDH in 712 CE and were held to a small foothold, not most of India; the caste (jati) system was FLEXIBLE, not rigid; a king ruled through LAYERS (samantas and self-governing village sabhas), not directly over every village; and a traveller's account (Xuanzang) is ONE source to cross-check. Match dynasties to their monuments (Rashtrakutas-Ellora, Pallavas-Mamallapuram, Palas-Nalanda). Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
