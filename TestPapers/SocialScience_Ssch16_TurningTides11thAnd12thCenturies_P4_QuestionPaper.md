**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: confusing Mahmud's loot-and-leave raiding (~17 raids, withdrew each time) with the Ghurids' staying-to-govern; reversing the Tarain battles (Ghuri LOST the First, 1191; WON the Second, 1192); assuming all India fell when the south (Cholas, Kakatiyas, Hoysalas) and much of the north stayed free; reading the age as 'only war' when al-Biruni, Bhaskaracharya, Ramanuja, Basava and great temples flourished; treating learning as one-way when al-Biruni studied India and Indian sine tables travelled outward; mislabelling terms (vimana=shrine tower, torana=gateway, vachana=Kannada poem, al-jabr=algebra/'reunion of broken parts', Mount Meru=cosmic mountain).
