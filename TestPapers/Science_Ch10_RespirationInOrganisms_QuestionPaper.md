**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Remember the word equation for cellular respiration: glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy. Keep "breathing" (the exchange of air) separate from "respiration" (the release of energy inside cells). No calculator is needed. Most questions need two to four reasoning steps; watch for traps. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
