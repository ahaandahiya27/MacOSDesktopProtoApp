**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Apex traps: distinguish CAUSE from mere SEQUENCE (internal Mauryan breakup preceded and enabled the foreign entry into the northwest, so a later event cannot be the earlier collapse's cause); never swap Gandhara (Greco-Buddhist, wavy hair and toga-folds, the visible confluence) with Mathura (indigenous red sandstone, fuller forms); SEA routes carried Satavahana and Tamil trade to Rome while the Kushanas profited from the overland LAND Silk Route; foreign rulers were absorbed into a dominant Indian culture (contributors, not destroyers); an ashvamedha or a matrimonial alliance is a political-sovereignty act, not merely ritual or a wedding; royal women (mother-names, land-granting queens, a queen's horse-sacrifice) wielded real power; weigh sources by their strengths (inscription = fixed-wording deed/gift, coin = name/title/portrait, poem = value that may flatter); a stupa is a Buddhist dome, an anicut a river-dam, a samvat a counted era; and keep the dynastic order straight (Maurya breakup, then Satavahanas, then Kanishka). Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
