**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: treating warm air as heavier, believing fast-moving air pushes outward (Bernoulli reversal), thinking wind blows from low to high pressure, swapping land/sea heating rates, calling the calm eye the most dangerous part, ranking a tornado as larger than a cyclone, and reading the can-crush as the metal folding itself rather than an outside-vs-inside pressure imbalance.
