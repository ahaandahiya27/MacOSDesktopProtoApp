**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Decide HCF (largest divider — share/cut/tile) vs LCM (smallest common multiple — meet/ring again); take LOWEST powers of common primes for HCF and HIGHEST powers of all primes for LCM; for 'same unknown remainder' use HCF of pairwise differences, for 'leaves remainder r' use LCM·k + r, and remember HCF·LCM = product only for two numbers while LCM = product only for co-primes. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
