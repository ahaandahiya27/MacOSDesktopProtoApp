**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: a desert means low rainfall not heat (cold Ladakh qualifies), the towering Himalayas are young and still rising while the worn-low Aravallis are ancient, the tall unbroken Western Ghats sit on the WEST coast while the low river-cut Eastern Ghats sit on the EAST, coral low-lying Lakshadweep (Arabian Sea) must not be swapped with volcanic forested Andaman & Nicobar (Bay of Bengal), and a sediment-built fertile delta is not a funnel-shaped estuary nor a bar-enclosed lagoon.
