**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. National-Olympiad traps: परमः is a superlative of RANK (highest), never of TIME (first/earliest), and the comparative भाव it implies is 'outranks all others', not 'is the only one'; हि is a confirming/causal emphatic (इव/एव-family 'verily, indeed'), never interrogative, negative, or a conjunction, and dropping it leaves a grammatical but flatter assertion; the सन्धि परमः+धर्मः→परमो धर्मः is विसर्ग→ओ before a voiced soft consonant (ध्), governed by the FOLLOWING sound being voiced — not by धर्मः being a noun; सेव् is आत्मनेपदी (सेवते) governing the accusative object, so the server is the doctor (subject) and the served are रोगिणः (acc. pl., ण by णत्व); चिकित्सकः is an अ-stem AGENT in -क, रोगिन् an इन्-stem CHARACTERISED-BY noun whose nom. sg. masks the न् (रोगी) revealed obliquely; -वत् in यन्त्रवत् is the इव/'like' तद्धित (manner of tireless steady action), not the possessive 'having'; -ईय in मानवीय makes a 'befitting-a-human' adjective that must agree in gender/number with its noun; कषाय flips between क्वाथ (decoction/medicine) and the astringent रस purely by CONTEXT, never by gender or number; and सेवा (time+body+presence) is categorically distinct from दान (transfer of money/goods at a distance) even when both help.
