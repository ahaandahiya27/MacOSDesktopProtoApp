**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Paper 3 is the first hard tier: every item is multi-step. Do brackets first, then × and ÷, then + and −; never strictly left-to-right. A minus sign before a bracket flips every inner term's sign; distributing means multiplying every term inside; only addition and multiplication may be freely reordered or regrouped — subtraction and division may not. Watch for sign/term-splitting slips, premature left-to-right evaluation, and modelling a situation with the wrong bracketing. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
