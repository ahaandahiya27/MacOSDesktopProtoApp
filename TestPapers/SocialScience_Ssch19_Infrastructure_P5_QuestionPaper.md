**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Apex traps: the physical/social split is by FUNCTION (move-and-supply vs build-human-capability), never by cost, visibility, size, age or material; a facility's TYPE never changes just because something else depends on it (a power grid stays physical even when a hospital needs it; a hospital stays social even though it draws power); buried utilities and people-serving institutions are fully infrastructure; the British built the 1853 railways for extraction, trade and troop control and only LATER were they repurposed as a 20-million-a-day national lifeline; care is a shared duty of citizens AND government; J.C. Bose (c.1895) was a communication scientist who improved the coherer and declined patents — he built no railway or highway; tele+communicare and infra+structura roots; aviation=avis; TEU is a container unit, not speed/height/distance; renewable energy parks, living root bridges (Khasi/Jaintia, Meghalaya) and Kautilya's Arthashastra all sit INSIDE the chapter. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
