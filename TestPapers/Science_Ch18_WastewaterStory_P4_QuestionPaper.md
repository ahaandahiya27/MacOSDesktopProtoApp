**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Distractors exploit reversed aerobic/anaerobic roles, conflating cleaner with potable, swapping sludge/scum/grit, treating screening or settling as removing dissolved or microscopic load, mismatching by-products with their source process, and mistaking nutrient pollution and bacterial poisoning for harmless events.
