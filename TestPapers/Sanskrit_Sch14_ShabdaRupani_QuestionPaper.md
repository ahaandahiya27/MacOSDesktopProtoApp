**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This appendix tables the masculine declensions. Watch the classic traps: Sanskrit has SEVEN cases plus the vocative (EIGHT forms), not Latin's six; there are THREE numbers, and the DUAL (द्विवचन) is for exactly TWO (देवौ = 'two gods', not 'many'); each case has its OWN ending and ONE job (देवस्य 'of', देवाय 'to/for', देवात् 'from'); the vocative is NOT always the nominative (हे देव!, हे गुरो!, हे कवे!); and Sanskrit has no Hindi-style 'oblique'. Match देव/अकारान्त, कवि/इकारान्त, गुरु/उकारान्त. Sanskrit in Devanagari. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
