**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. National-olympiad traps: लट् present vs लङ् past vs लृट् future vs लोट् imperative vs विधिलिङ् optative of the SAME root; the अ- augment belongs ONLY to लङ् (अपठत्) — adding it to a present/optative or confusing it with the negative prefix अ- is wrong; Sanskrit persons are REVERSED (प्रथमपुरुष = English THIRD, उत्तमपुरुष = English FIRST); parasmaipada -ति/-तः/-न्ति vs atmanepada -ते/-ेते/-न्ते; कृ alternates strong करो- and weak कुरु-/कुर्व-; the verb must agree in BOTH person AND number; distractors swap one feature at a time (wrong person, wrong number, wrong लकार, missing/extra augment). 1. एका पङ्क्तिः अधोलिखिता — 'सः उद्याने ___, अधुना तु गृहं गच्छति।' रिक्तस्थाने 'सः पूर्वम् क्रीडति स्म' इति न लिख्यते, अपितु लङ्-लकारेण पूर्वकालः बोध्यः। 'सः अपठत्' इति वाक्ये कः लकारः, का च व्यक्ति-वचनम्?
