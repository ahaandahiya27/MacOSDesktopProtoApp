**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic traps: a country with a king CAN be a democracy (the UK is a constitutional monarchy and a parliamentary democracy); India is a PARLIAMENTARY democracy even though it has a President - real executive power is with the prime minister and council of ministers; elections ALONE do not make a democracy (it also needs equality, rights and an independent judiciary); 'democracy' and 'republic' are NOT identical; and the majority cannot simply vote away anyone's fundamental rights. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
