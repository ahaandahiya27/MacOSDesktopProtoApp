**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. the hardest traps tempt you to use Earth's shadow for ordinary phases, to read 'crescent/gibbous' off the wrong orbit position, to confuse waxing-left with waning, to forget that the same near-side fact comes from one rotation per orbit, and to mix up apparent size (angular) with real size.
