**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This supplementary chapter teaches vowel-quantity (mātrā). Watch the classic traps: Sanskrit has THREE vowel quantities - ह्रस्व (short, 1 mātrā), दीर्घ (long, 2), प्लुत (prolonged, 3) - not two; a मात्रा is a unit of TIME (the duration of a short vowel); रौति is SINGULAR ('it crows') while रुवन्ति is PLURAL; and वर्ण here means a sound/letter (phonetics), not caste. Match the Sanskrit bird/animal names correctly. Sanskrit is quoted in Devanagari. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
