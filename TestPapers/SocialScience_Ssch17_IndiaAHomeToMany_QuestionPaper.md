# Olympiad Test Paper — Social Science (Class 7)
## Chapter 17: India, a Home to Many

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | Social Science (NCERT Class 7) |
| **Chapter** | 17 — India, a Home to Many |
| **Total Questions** | 60 (Multiple Choice, single correct) |
| **Maximum Marks** | 240 |
| **Suggested Time** | 90 minutes |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic traps: not all communities came to TRADE - many came as refugees fleeing persecution, and the Siddis were brought ENSLAVED; Islam first came PEACEFULLY through Arab merchants from the 7th century (India's oldest mosque), separately from the later invasions; newcomers KEPT their identities (synagogues, fire temples, churches) while joining Indian society; a refugee (fleeing danger) differs from an immigrant (seeking opportunity); and diversity ENRICHED Indian culture, in the spirit of vasudhaiva kutumbakam. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
