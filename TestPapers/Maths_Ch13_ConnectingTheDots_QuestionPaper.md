**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Remember that mean = total ÷ count, the median needs the data SORTED first, and the mode is the most frequent value — they are three different summaries, not the same "average." Watch the classic traps: comparing totals when group sizes differ, forgetting to sort before taking a median, mistaking the mode for the middle, and reading a bar graph's shape without checking its scale (especially a truncated axis).
