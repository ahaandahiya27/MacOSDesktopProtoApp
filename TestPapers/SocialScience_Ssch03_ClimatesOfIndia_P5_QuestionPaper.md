The classic traps are treating one day's reading as climate, blaming equatorial heat on nearness to the Sun rather than ray-angle, forgetting that altitude and the sea both moderate temperature, reversing which monsoon is wet, mistaking the calm cyclone eye for safety, and assuming the windward and leeward sides of a range share the same rainfall. 1. A town keeps thirty years of records. Year 18 was unusually rainy and warm; year 25 was unusually dry and cold. A researcher building the town's climate profile should treat these two odd years by:

   (B) redefining the climate twice, once for the warm-wet pattern and once for the dry-cold pattern
