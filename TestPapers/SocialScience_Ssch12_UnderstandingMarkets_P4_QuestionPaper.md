**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. A market is any buyer-seller-price arrangement (online and weekly haats count, not just shop buildings); the cheapest price is not 'best' because a price below the seller's cost makes supply dry up, so the just-right clearing price is what matters; more demand against limited supply pushes price UP while plentiful supply pushes it DOWN; intermediaries (wholesalers, distributors, retailers, aggregators) ADD real value by bulk-buying, transporting, storing and breaking bulk into small amounts; and the certification marks must be matched to the right domain - FSSAI for food safety, ISI/BIS for manufactured goods, AGMARK for graded farm produce, BEE Star for appliance energy use - while MRP is the maximum a consumer may be charged, not a minimum. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
