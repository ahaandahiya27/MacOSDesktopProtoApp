**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Apex traps: reading compound growth as flat simple interest across MANY years; mis-pricing a bank's net interest position when only PART of deposits are lent; assuming a benchmark-rate RISE makes loans cheaper; treating the RBI as a place individuals bank rather than the banker-to-banks, currency issuer and regulator; believing shares are a guaranteed, risk-free saving; thinking UPI or a cheque mints new money rather than moving existing rupees; and imagining a genuine bank would phone for your OTP, PIN or password (report fraud on 1930). One option is correct; the rest are expert misconceptions. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
