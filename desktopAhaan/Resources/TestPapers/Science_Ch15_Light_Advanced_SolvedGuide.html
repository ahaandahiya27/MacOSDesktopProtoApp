Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

Light travels in:

  • Acurved paths
  • Bzig-zag paths
  • Cstraight lines✓
  • Dcircles

Working

✓

Light travels in straight lines (rectilinear propagation).

Q2

A shadow forms mainly because light:

  • Acannot pass through an opaque object✓
  • Bbends around objects
  • Cis absorbed by air
  • Dchanges colour

Working

✓

A shadow appears because light cannot pass through an opaque object and travels straight, leaving a dark region behind it.

Q3

The image formed by a pinhole camera is:

  • Aerect and large
  • Berect and small
  • Cvirtual
  • Dinverted✓

Working

✓

A pinhole camera forms an inverted image (the rays cross at the hole).

Q4

An object that lets no light pass through it is:

  • Atransparent
  • Bopaque✓
  • Ctranslucent
  • Dluminous

Working

✓

An opaque object lets no light through.

Q5

An object through which light passes only partially (giving a blurred view) is:

  • Atransparent
  • Bopaque
  • Ctranslucent✓
  • Dshiny

Working

✓

A translucent object lets light pass only partly, giving a blurred view.

Q6

An object through which we can see clearly is:

  • Atransparent✓
  • Bopaque
  • Ctranslucent
  • Ddark

Working

✓

A transparent object lets light through clearly.

Q7

The colour of a shadow shows:

  • Athe object's colour
  • Bthe light's colour
  • Ca mix of colours
  • Donly the object's shape, as a dark patch✓

Working

✓

A shadow shows only the outline (shape) of the object as a dark patch, not its colour.

Q8

A pinhole camera's image is inverted because light:

  • Abends at the hole
  • Btravels in straight lines that cross at the hole✓
  • Cchanges speed
  • Dis reflected twice

Working

✓

Straight-line rays from the top and bottom of the object cross at the pinhole, so the image is inverted.

Q9

A shadow is cast on the side of an object that is:

  • Afacing the light
  • Bbrightest
  • Caway from the light source✓
  • Dcoloured

Working

✓

The shadow falls on the side away from the light source.

Q10

During a solar eclipse, a shadow is cast when the:

  • AMoon comes between the Sun and the Earth✓
  • BEarth comes between the Sun and the Moon
  • CSun is switched off
  • Dsky is cloudy

Working

✓

In a solar eclipse the Moon comes between the Sun and the Earth, casting its shadow on the Earth.

Set 2 · Q11–Q20

Q11

The bouncing back of light from a surface is called:

  • Arefraction
  • Bdispersion
  • Cabsorption
  • Dreflection✓

Working

✓

Light bouncing back from a surface is reflection.

Q12

In reflection, the angle of incidence is always equal to the angle of:

  • Athe mirror
  • Breflection✓
  • Crefraction
  • Dthe normal alone

Working

✓

The law of reflection: angle of incidence = angle of reflection.

Q13

The image in a plane mirror is:

  • Areal and inverted
  • Breal and smaller
  • Cvirtual, erect and the same size✓
  • Dvirtual and magnified

Working

✓

A plane mirror gives a virtual, erect image of the same size as the object.

Q14

The image in a plane mirror is laterally inverted, meaning:

  • Aleft and right appear swapped✓
  • Bit is upside down
  • Cit is smaller
  • Dit is coloured

Working

✓

Lateral inversion means left and right appear interchanged.

Q15

For a plane mirror, the image distance behind the mirror compared with the object distance in front is:

  • Atwice as much
  • Bhalf as much
  • Czero
  • Dequal✓

Working

✓

The image is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front — equal distances.

Q16

"AMBULANCE" is painted reversed on the front of the vehicle so it reads correctly in a driver's:

  • Awindscreen
  • Brear-view mirror✓
  • Cheadlamp
  • Dside window

Working

✓

Reversed lettering reads the right way round in the rear-view mirror of the vehicle ahead.

Q17

A plane-mirror image cannot be caught on a screen because it is:

  • Atoo bright
  • Btoo small
  • Cvirtual✓
  • Dcoloured

Working

✓

A plane-mirror image is virtual, so it can't be caught on a screen.

Q18

Standing 2 m in front of a plane mirror, the distance between you and your image is:

  • A4 m✓
  • B2 m
  • C1 m
  • D0 m

Working

✓

Image is 2 m behind the mirror, you are 2 m in front, so the separation is 2 + 2 = 4 m.

Q19

When you raise your right hand before a plane mirror, the image appears to raise its:

  • Aright hand
  • Bhead
  • Cboth hands
  • Dleft hand✓

Working

✓

Because of lateral inversion, the raised right hand appears as the image's left hand.

Q20

The size of the image in a plane mirror compared with the object is:

  • Alarger
  • Bthe same✓
  • Csmaller
  • Dzero

Working

✓

A plane mirror gives an image the same size as the object.

Set 3 · Q21–Q30

Q21

Reflection from a smooth, polished surface is called:

  • Adiffuse reflection
  • Bscattering
  • Cregular reflection✓
  • Ddispersion

Working

✓

A smooth surface gives regular (specular) reflection.

Q22

Reflection from a rough surface, which scatters light in many directions, is called:

  • Adiffuse (irregular) reflection✓
  • Bregular reflection
  • Crefraction
  • Dabsorption

Working

✓

A rough surface scatters light — diffuse (irregular) reflection.

Q23

A ray strikes a mirror making 30° with the mirror surface. Its angle of incidence (measured from the normal) is:

  • A30°
  • B90°
  • C15°
  • D60°✓

Working

✓

The normal is perpendicular to the mirror, so a ray 30° from the surface is 90° − 30° = 60° from the normal.

Q24

If the angle of incidence is 40°, the angle of reflection is:

  • A50°
  • B40°✓
  • C80°
  • D20°

Working

✓

Angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence: 40°.

Q25

A ray that hits a mirror exactly along the normal reflects:

  • Aat 90°
  • Bsideways
  • Cstraight back along the same path✓
  • Dat 45°

Working

✓

A ray along the normal reflects straight back along the same path.

Q26

A mirror that curves inward (like the inside of a spoon) is:

  • Aconcave✓
  • Bplane
  • Cconvex
  • Dflat

Working

✓

An inward-curving mirror is concave.

Q27

A mirror that bulges outward is:

  • Aplane
  • Bconcave
  • Cflat
  • Dconvex✓

Working

✓

An outward-bulging mirror is convex.

Q28

A concave mirror can form an image that is:

  • Aalways virtual and small
  • Breal (and even magnified, depending on distance)✓
  • Cnever real
  • Dalways the same size

Working

✓

A concave mirror can form a real image, which may be magnified depending on the object's distance.

Q29

A convex mirror always forms an image that is:

  • Areal and large
  • Breal and inverted
  • Cvirtual, erect and smaller✓
  • Dthe same size

Working

✓

A convex mirror always gives a virtual, erect, diminished image.

Q30

The rear-view mirror of a car is:

  • Aconvex✓
  • Bconcave
  • Cplane
  • Dcoloured

Working

✓

A car's rear-view mirror is convex (wide field of view).

Q51

A plane-mirror image cannot be shown on a screen because:

  • Ait is too dim
  • Bthe mirror is flat
  • Clight is absorbed
  • Dthe light only appears to come from behind the mirror (it is virtual)✓

Working

✓

The image is virtual — light only appears to come from behind the mirror, so nothing real lands on a screen.

Q52

The shortest plane mirror in which a person 1.7 m tall can see their whole image has a height of:

  • A1.7 m
  • B0.85 m✓
  • C3.4 m
  • D1.0 m

Working

✓

A plane mirror needs to be only half the person's height: 1.7 ÷ 2 = 0.85 m.

Q53

As you walk toward a plane mirror at 1 m/s, your image approaches you at:

  • A0.5 m/s
  • B1 m/s
  • C2 m/s✓
  • Dit stays still

Working

✓

The image moves toward the mirror as fast as you do, so relative to you it approaches at 1 + 1 = 2 m/s.

Q54

Two plane mirrors placed at 90° to each other form, of a single object:

  • A3 images✓
  • B1 image
  • Cinfinite images
  • D2 images

Working

✓

Two mirrors at 90° give 360°/90° − 1 = 3 images.

Q55

  • Cthe same size
  • Dmagnified and erect✓

Held close, the concave (inner) side of a spoon shows a magnified, erect face.

Q56

  • Amagnified

  • Cinverted
  • Dthe same size

✓

The convex (outer) side always shows a small, erect image.

✓
