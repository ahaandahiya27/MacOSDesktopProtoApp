**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: what a state is and its four elements, how the state differs from the government, democracy and the rule of law, the three organs of government, and the government's roles in your life. Reason about how a country is organised and serves its people. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
