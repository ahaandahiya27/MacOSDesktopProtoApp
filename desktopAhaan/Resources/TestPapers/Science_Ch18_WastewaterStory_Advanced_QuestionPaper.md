**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: what sewage is and what it carries, the journey from drain to treatment plant, each stage of a Wastewater Treatment Plant in order, what becomes of the sludge, and the home sanitation choices that protect public health. Reason through the process step by step. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
