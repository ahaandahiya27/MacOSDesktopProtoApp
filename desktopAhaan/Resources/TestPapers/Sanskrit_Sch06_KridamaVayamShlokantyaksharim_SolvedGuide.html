Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी is a game in which:

  • Ashlokas are sung backwards
  • Bthe first letters are all removed
  • Ceach new shloka begins with the last letter of the previous one✓
  • Donly one shloka is recited

Working

✓

In श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी each new verse begins with the last letter of the previous one. It is not singing backwards, removing first letters, or reciting one verse.

Q2

श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी breaks into श्लोक + अन्त्य + अक्षर + ई, meaning:

  • A'first letter of a verse'
  • B'a verse without letters'
  • C'a single letter'
  • D'verse' + 'last' + 'letter' + (game-name) = the last-letter shloka game✓

Working

✓

श्लोक ('verse') + अन्त्य ('last') + अक्षर ('letter') + ई names the last-letter shloka game. The other readings are wrong.

Q3

The -ई suffix in श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी:

  • Amakes it past tense
  • Bturns the phrase into a feminine action/game-name✓
  • Cmarks the dative
  • Dnegates the word

Working

✓

The -ई suffix forms a feminine action/game-name. It does not mark past tense, the dative, or a negation.

Q4

अन्त्य means:

  • Afirst
  • Bmiddle
  • Cnone
  • Dlast / final✓

Working

✓

अन्त्य means 'last / final'. It does not mean first, middle, or none.

Q5

अक्षर means:

  • Averse
  • Bgame
  • Cfragrance
  • Dletter / syllable✓

Working

✓

अक्षर means 'letter / syllable'. A verse is श्लोक, a game क्रीडा, and fragrance गन्ध.

Q6

Reading श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी as merely 'last letter' misses that it names:

  • Aa single shloka
  • Ba fragrance
  • Cthe GAME (the last-letter game), not just the letter✓
  • Da wicked person

Working

✓

श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी names the GAME, not just a letter. It is not a single shloka, a fragrance, or a wicked person.

Q7

The verse विद्याहीना न शोभन्ते means:

  • Athe knowledgeable never shine
  • Bflowers always shine
  • Cthose without knowledge do not shine (look good)✓
  • Donly the wealthy shine

Working

✓

It means those without knowledge do not truly shine. It does not say the knowledgeable never shine, that flowers always shine, or that only the wealthy shine.

Q8

विद्याहीनाः means people who are:

  • Awithout wealth
  • Bwithout fragrance
  • Cwithout knowledge / unlearned✓
  • Dwithout fame

Working

✓

विद्याहीनाः are those without knowledge (विद्या + हीन). Not without wealth, fragrance, or fame.

Q9

According to the verse, even handsome people without विद्या:

  • Ashine the most
  • Bdo not truly shine✓
  • Cbecome wealthy
  • Dgain fame automatically

Working

✓

Even the handsome, without knowledge, do not truly shine. They do not shine most, gain wealth, or gain automatic fame.

Q10

The verse says the विद्याहीनाः are like:

  • Afragrant roses
  • Bgolden coins
  • Cfragrance-less किंशुक flowers✓
  • Dtall mountains

Working

✓

They are likened to fragrance-less किंशुक flowers. Not fragrant roses, coins, or mountains.

It teaches that one may be outwardly attractive yet lack worth without knowledge. The point is not fragrance, wealth, or wisdom (which is the missing thing).

Q17

Like colour without scent, the unlearned has good looks but no real worth from knowledge. The other pairings reverse or distort the simile.
