Q1

The sum of any seven consecutive odd numbers is always:

  • Aeven
  • Bodd✓
  • Ca multiple of 7 only if the first is odd
  • Ddivisible by 14

Working

✓

Seven is an odd count of odd numbers, and an odd count of odds always sums to an odd number.

Q2

The sum of any ten consecutive whole numbers is always:

  • Aodd✓
  • Ba multiple of 10
  • Ca multiple of 5 only
  • Deven

Working

✓

The sum of ten consecutive whole numbers starting at n is 10n + (0 + 1 + … + 9) = 10n + 45. Since 10n is even and 45 is odd, the total is always odd.

Q3

The value of (odd × odd) + (odd × odd) is always:

  • Aeven✓
  • Bodd
  • Cprime
  • Da multiple of 4

Working

✓

odd × odd = odd, and odd + odd = even. So the whole expression is even.

Q4

  • Dare equal

Working

✓

A sum is odd only when one addend is odd and the other even — they must have different parity.

Q9

✓

  • A48
  • B54✓

  • D36

Working

✓

Q24

In the cryptarithm T + T + T = "UT" (a two-digit number whose units digit is T), the digit T is:
