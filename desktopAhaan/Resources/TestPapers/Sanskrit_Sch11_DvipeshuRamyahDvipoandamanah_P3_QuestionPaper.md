Classic traps this paper: the title-sandhi द्वीपः + अण्डमानः → द्वीपोऽण्डमानः is विसर्ग-सन्धि giving ओ + अवग्रह (ऽ), NOT a dropped letter or a compound of 'two'; द्वीपेषु is सप्तमी बहुवचन (locative plural, 'among/in the islands'), not nominative; द्वि + अप् yields द्वीप by lengthening, so the इ becomes long ई — it is not अ + अ; सोढवान् is the क्तवतु past-active participle of root सह् (he-who-bore), agreeing in liṅga-vacana with Savarkar, NOT a finite future or the gerund सोढ्वा/-त्वा; दश वर्षाणि is नपुंसकलिङ्ग बहुवचन in the accusative of DURATION (for ten years), not a subject or instrument; कालापानी is the prison's NAME (exile over the seas), not a literal black river; कारागार (कारा 'cell' + गार/अगार 'house') is a prison, not the Hindi कारखाना 'factory'; जनजाति is a Scheduled/indigenous TRIBE, not सामान्यजन 'common folk'; the Sentinelese are uncontacted and law-protected, not extinct.
