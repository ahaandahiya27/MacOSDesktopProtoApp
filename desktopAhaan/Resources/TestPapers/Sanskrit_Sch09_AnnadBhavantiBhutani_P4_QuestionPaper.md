National-olympiad traps: the ablative (पञ्चमी, -आत्) marks अपादान — the source a thing 'arises FROM', never an agent that 'makes' it, so अन्नात् ≠ a maker and ≠ the dative अन्नाय ('for food'); भवन्ति governs भूतानि (नपुंसकलिङ्ग nom. pl., identical to acc. pl.) which agrees in being 3rd-person plural, NOT because भूतानि is the source; in अन्नाद्भवन्ति the त् of अन्नात् voices to द् before voiced भ् (case unchanged); the wheel भूतानि←अन्न←पर्जन्य←यज्ञ←कर्म is read by ablatives, so 'X from Y' means Y is the ablative; भवन्ति/सम्भवः/समुद्भवः all share √भू ('become'), making the chain one of coming-into-being not manufacture; जानीयात् is विधिलिङ् (optative, 'should know') of √ज्ञा (the -ईय- marker), distinct from present जानाति, future ज्ञास्यति, imperative जानीहि; ज्ञेयः is the -य gerundive ('to be known'), parallel to कार्य, distinct from past participle ज्ञात and agent ज्ञाता; ब्रह्म (नपुंसक, सर्वव्याप्तः परमेश्वरः, impersonal) ≠ ब्रह्मा (masc. creator) ≠ ब्रह्मन् (priest); ओषधीनाम् is genitive plural of the ई/इ-feminine ओषधि; रसायन is an Ayurvedic word re-coined for modern chemistry; उत्पत्तिक्रमम् ('order of arising') is the कर्म (object) of जानीयात्.
