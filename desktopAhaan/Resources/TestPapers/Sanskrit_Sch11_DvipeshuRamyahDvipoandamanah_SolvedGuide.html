Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

The Andaman Islands (अण्डमानद्वीपः) lie to the ____ of the Indian mainland.

  • Awest
  • Bnorth
  • Csouth
  • Deast (in the Bay of Bengal)✓

Working

✓

The Andamans lie to the east, in the Bay of Bengal. Not to the west, north, or south of the mainland.

Q7

द्वीपः means:

  • Aan island✓
  • Ba prison
  • Ca tribe
  • Da coral reef

Working

✓

द्वीपः means 'an island'. A prison is कारागारम्, a tribe जनजातिः, and a coral reef प्रवालप्रस्तरः.

Q8

द्वीपः breaks into द्वि + अप्, literally meaning:

  • A'two' + 'house'
  • B'black' + 'water'
  • C'two' + 'water' = surrounded by water (an island)✓
  • D'cell' + 'house'

Working

✓

द्वि ('two') + अप् ('water') = 'two-watered', i.e. surrounded by water (an island). The other splits are wrong.

Q9

द्वीप is called 'two-watered' because an island is:

  • Aland with water around it✓
  • Btwo rivers joined
  • Ca prison at sea
  • Da mountain peak

Working

✓

द्वीप is 'two-watered' because an island is land with water around it. Not two joined rivers, a sea-prison, or a peak.

Q10

Knowing द्वि = 'two' helps, but द्वीप names:

  • Aone piece of land surrounded by water✓
  • Btwo separate lands
  • Ca pair of rivers
  • Dtwo prisons

Working

✓

द्वीप names one piece of land surrounded by water. Not two lands, a pair of rivers, or two prisons.
