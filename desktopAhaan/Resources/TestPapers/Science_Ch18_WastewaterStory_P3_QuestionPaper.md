**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Traps target the most common Wastewater-Story confusions: treating 'cleaner' as 'drinkable'; swapping sludge (sinks) with scum (floats); mixing up aerobic aeration with anaerobic digestion; assuming bar screens or settling remove dissolved/microscopic impurities; thinking grit removal and sludge settling are the same step; believing pouring oil/chemicals/medicines down the drain is harmless; and confusing which by-product (biogas vs manure) comes from which process. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
