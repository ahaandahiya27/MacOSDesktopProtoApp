**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it drills the INSTRUMENTAL of means (विद्यया, वपुषा, वस्त्रेण, विनयेन — 'honoured BY ...'), the GERUNDIVE / future-passive-participle (हातव्य 'to be abandoned', कर्तव्य 'to be done'), the NEUTER declension of सुभाषितम् (plural सुभाषितानि), and the precise counts — exactly FIVE 'va' qualities and SIX faults, with आलस्यम् (laziness) ≠ दीर्घसूत्रता (procrastination). Remember the intellect (बुद्धिः) is purified by ज्ञानेन (knowledge), not water. Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
