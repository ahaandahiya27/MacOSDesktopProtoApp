Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

In the parable, how many boys cross the river?

  • Anine
  • Bseven
  • Cten✓
  • Dtwelve

Working

✓

Ten boys (दश बालकाः) cross the river. The whole parable depends on the number being ten, not nine, seven, or twelve.

Q2

दशमः कः? means:

  • AWhere is the river?
  • BHow many boys?
  • CWho is the leader?
  • DWho is the tenth?✓

Working

✓

दशमः कः? means 'Who is the tenth?'. It does not ask where the river is, how many boys, or who the leader is.

Q3

What do the ten boys do at the start of the story?

  • Across a river (by swimming)✓
  • Bclimb a mountain
  • Cbuild a house
  • Dlight a fire

Working

✓

The boys cross a river by swimming. They do not climb a mountain, build a house, or light a fire.

Q4

नदीं तीर्त्वा पारं गताः means:

  • Ahaving crossed the river, they went to the other bank✓
  • Bthey built a river
  • Cthey drank the river
  • Dthey sang by the river

Working

✓

It means 'having crossed the river, they went to the other bank'. The other readings are wrong.

Q5

After crossing, each boy wants to make sure that:

  • Athe river is deep
  • Bthe bank is far
  • Cthe sun has set
  • Dall ten are safe (none drowned)✓

Working

✓

Each boy wants to be sure all ten are safe. The depth of the river, distance of the bank, and time of day are not the concern.

Q6

The whole parable depends on the number of boys being:

  • Aany number
  • Bunknown
  • Calways changing
  • Dten✓

Working

✓

The parable depends on there being exactly ten boys. Not any number, an unknown, or a changing one.

Q7

When each boy counts, he counts only:

  • Anine (नव)✓
  • Bten
  • Ceight
  • Deleven

Working

✓

Each boy counts only nine (नव). Not ten, eight, or eleven.

Q8

Each boy counts only nine because he:

  • Aforgets to count himself✓
  • Bcannot count past nine
  • Closes one boy in the river
  • Dcounts too fast

Working

✓

Each counts nine because he forgets to count himself. It is not an inability to count, a lost boy, or counting too fast.

Q9

Counting nine, each boy wrongly concludes that:

  • Aall are safe
  • Bthe tenth has drowned✓
  • Cthere were only nine
  • Done boy ran away

Working

✓

Counting nine, each wrongly concludes the tenth drowned. The truth is all are safe; there were ten, and none ran away.

Q10

मग्नः, the word for the supposedly lost boy, means:

  • Alost in a forest
  • Bsubmerged / drowned✓
  • Casleep
  • Dhidden

Working

✓

मग्नः means 'submerged / drowned'. It does not mean lost in a forest, asleep, or hidden.

Q11

The boys' mistake is that the 'missing tenth' is actually:

  • Areally drowned
  • Ban eleventh boy
  • Cthe traveler
  • Dthe counter himself (each forgot to count himself)✓

Working

✓

The 'missing tenth' is the counter himself, whom each boy forgot to count. He is not really drowned, an eleventh boy, or the traveler.

Q12

  • Amade by only one boy

✓

The error is shared by all - each forgets himself. It is not one boy's, the river's, or the traveler's fault.

✓

The passing पथिकः counts correctly. Not the youngest boy, the leader alone, or no one.

The 'lost tenth' was never truly lost because:

Q24

The Sanskrit ordinals प्रथमः, द्वितीयः, तृतीयः mean:

  • Aone, two, three (cardinals)
  • Btenth, ninth, eighth
  • Cfirst, second, third✓
  • Dthis, that, the other

Working

✓

प्रथमः, द्वितीयः, तृतीयः are the ordinals 'first, second, third'. They are not cardinals, the high ordinals, or demonstratives.

Q25

दशमः means:

  • Aten (the count)
  • Bsecond
  • Ctenth✓
  • Dninth

Working

✓

दशमः is the ordinal 'tenth'. The cardinal 'ten' is दश; 'second' is द्वितीयः and 'ninth' नवमः.

Q26

Ordinal numbers (प्रथमः ... दशमः) tell us a thing's:

  • Aweight
  • Bcolour
  • Cposition in order (first, second, ...)✓
  • Dprice

Working

✓

Ordinals tell position in order. They do not tell weight, colour, or price.

Q27

'Second' is द्वितीयः. प्रथमः is first, तृतीयः third, and दशमः tenth.

Set 4 · Q31–Q40

Q31

तीर्त्वा is a:

  • Apresent-tense verb
  • Bgerund (an indeclinable 'having done X')✓
  • Cordinal number
  • Dnoun

Working

✓

तीर्त्वा is a gerund ('having done X'). It is not a present-tense verb, an ordinal, or a noun.

Q32

In नदीं तीर्त्वा पारं गताः, the gerund तीर्त्वा marks the action that:

  • Ahappens last
  • Bnever happens
  • Chappens first (crossing), before they reach the bank✓
  • Dis in the future

Working

✓

The gerund marks the crossing as happening first, before reaching the bank. It is not last, non-occurring, or future.

Q33

A gerund like तीर्त्वा links two actions by showing one is:

  • Adone at the same instant only
  • Bcompleted before the next ('having crossed, they went')✓
  • Cnever completed
  • Din the future

Working

✓

A gerund shows one action completed before the next. Not simultaneous-only, never completed, or future.

Q34

अगणयत् means:

  • Ahe will count
  • Blet him count
  • Che counted✓
  • Dhe sang

Working

✓

अगणयत् means 'he counted' (past). Not 'will count', 'let him count', or 'he sang'.

Q35

The tense of अगणयत् is:

  • Aimperfect past (laN-lakara)✓
  • Bpresent
  • Cfuture
  • Doptative

Working

✓

अगणयत् is the imperfect past (लङ्-लकार). It is not present, future, or optative.

Q36

अगणयत् comes from the root गण्, meaning:

  • Ato sing
  • Bto count / enumerate✓
  • Cto swim
  • Dto drown

Working

✓

अगणयत् comes from गण् ('to count'). गा is 'to sing'; swimming and drowning are other roots.

Q37

गण् ('to count') must not be confused with गा, which means:

  • Ato sing✓
  • Bto count
  • Cto cross
  • Dto drown

Working

✓

गा means 'to sing', distinct from गण् ('to count'). It does not mean count, cross, or drown.

Q38

'He counts' (present) and 'he counted' (past) of गण् are:

  • Aगणयति and अगणयत्✓
  • Bगायति and अगायत्
  • Cतरति and अतरत्
  • Dजानाति and अजानात्

Working

✓

गण् gives गणयति (present) and अगणयत् (past). गायति/अगायत् is from गा (sing); तरति/अतरत् from तृ (cross); जानाति/अजानात् from ज्ञा (know).

Q39

Listening for the consonant tells गण् (count) from गा (sing); गणयति means:

  • Ahe sings
  • Bhe counts✓
  • Che swims
  • Dhe knows

Working

✓

गणयति means 'he counts'. 'He sings' is गायति, 'he swims' तरति, and 'he knows' जानाति.

Q40

कतमः means:

  • Awhich (of two)?
  • Bwhich (of many)?✓
  • Cwho?
  • Dwhat?

Working

✓

कतमः asks 'which of many?'. 'Which of two?' is कतरः, 'who?' कः, and 'what?' किम्.
