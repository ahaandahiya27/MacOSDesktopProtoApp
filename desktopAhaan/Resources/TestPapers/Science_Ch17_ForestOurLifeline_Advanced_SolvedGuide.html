Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

A forest is best described as a:

  • Acollection of unrelated trees
  • Bman-made garden of one crop
  • Cself-sustaining ecosystem of plants, animals and microbes living together✓
  • Dbare patch of land

Working

✓

A forest is a **self-sustaining ecosystem of plants, animals and microbes living together**, not just scattered trees.

Q2

The topmost layer of a forest, formed by the interlocking branches of the tallest trees, is the:

  • Acanopy✓
  • Bforest floor
  • Cshrub layer
  • Dunderstorey

Working

✓

The topmost interlocking-branch layer of the tallest trees is the **canopy**.

Q3

Shorter trees that grow in the shade beneath the tallest trees form the:

  • Acanopy
  • Bsoil layer
  • Ccloud layer
  • Dunderstorey✓

Working

✓

Shorter trees growing in the shade below the canopy form the **understorey**.

Q4

In a forest food chain, green plants are the:

  • Aconsumers
  • Bproducers✓
  • Cdecomposers
  • Dpredators

Working

✓

Green plants make their own food and start the food chain, so they are the **producers**.

Q5

  • Acarnivore
  • Bherbivore✓
  • Cdecomposer
  • Dproducer

Working

✓

An animal that eats only plants is a **herbivore**.

Q6

In the chain grass → deer → tiger, the tiger is a:

  • Aproducer
  • Bherbivore (first-order consumer)
  • Cdecomposer
  • Dcarnivore (higher-order consumer)✓

Working

✓

In grass → deer → tiger, the tiger eats other animals, so it is a **carnivore (higher-order consumer)**.

Q7

Many interconnected food chains in a forest together form a:

  • Afood web✓
  • Bsingle straight line
  • Cwater cycle
  • Dcanopy

Working

✓

Many food chains linked together make a **food web**.

Q8

  • Bherbivores
  • Cdecomposers✓
  • Dpredators

Working

✓

Fungi and bacteria that break down dead matter into simpler substances are **decomposers**.

Q9

  • Ahumus✓
  • Bsand
  • Cclay
  • Dgravel

Working

✓

The dark, crumbly, nutrient-rich material from decomposed matter is **humus**.

Q10

The main role of decomposers in a forest is to:

  • Ahunt large animals
  • Bmake the canopy taller
  • Creturn nutrients from dead matter back to the soil for plants to reuse✓
  • Dproduce oxygen by photosynthesis

✓

Decomposers **return nutrients from dead matter back to the soil for plants to reuse** — that is their key role.
