**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Paper 5 (hardest tier). Expert traps: reading a chain of indicator results where some indicators (phenolphthalein, turmeric) cannot separate acid from neutral and only a second indicator settles the family; tracking a drop-by-drop neutralisation past the endpoint into overshoot, then reasoning what every indicator shows afterwards; choosing the correct member of an acid-base pair to fix a sting, soil, effluent, antacid or pond when the scenario is layered or reversed; never confusing the colour an indicator displays with the family it names (china rose magenta = acid, turmeric red = base, methyl orange red = acid, phenolphthalein pink = base); knowing that the salt of a neutralisation depends on the particular acid and base and is rarely common salt; and never letting strength or amount of sourness flip a substance out of its chemical family. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
