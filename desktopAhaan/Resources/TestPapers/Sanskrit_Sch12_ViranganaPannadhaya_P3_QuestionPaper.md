**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the Paper-3 traps: the dead boy is Panna's OWN son, not the prince and not Panna herself; मारयितुम् (-तुम्) is the infinitive 'to kill', while अमारयत् is past, मारयिष्यति future, मारयतु imperative; the cardinal 'sixteen' is षोडश but the ordinal 'sixteenth' is षोडशम्; दुष्ट means wicked (moral) while मूर्ख means foolish (intellect); राजगृह is a place (palace) but राजपुत्र is a person (prince); छलेन / स्वर्णिमाक्षरैः / वीराङ्गनासु carry case-endings that change the meaning (by-means-of, in, among) — do not read them as simple nominatives.
