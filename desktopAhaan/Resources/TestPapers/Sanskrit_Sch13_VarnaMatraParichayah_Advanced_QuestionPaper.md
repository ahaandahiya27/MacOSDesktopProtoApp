**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper applies the three vowel quantities rather than just naming them: classify individual vowels as ह्रस्व (short, 1 mātrā) or दीर्घ (long, 2), COUNT the total mātrās in a word, recognise that ए, ऐ, ओ, औ are ALWAYS long (they have no short form), read the प्लुत's ३-mark (राम३, ओ३म्), and pick रौति (singular) vs रुवन्ति (plural) by subject-number agreement. Count vowel duration only (a bare consonant with halant carries no vowel). Sanskrit is quoted in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
