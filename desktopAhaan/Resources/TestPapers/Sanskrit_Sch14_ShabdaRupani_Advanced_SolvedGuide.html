Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

The instrumental PLURAL of देव ('by the gods') is:

  • Aदेवेन
  • Bदेवेभ्यः
  • Cदेवैः✓
  • Dदेवानाम्

Working

✓

The instrumental plural of देव ('by the gods') is **देवैः** (-ऐः). देवेन is the instrumental *singular*; देवेभ्यः is dative/ablative plural; देवानाम् is genitive plural.

Q2

देवान् is the:

  • Aaccusative plural ('the gods', as object)✓
  • Bnominative plural
  • Cgenitive plural
  • Daccusative singular

Working

✓

देवान् is the **accusative plural** — the gods as the object of the verb. The nominative plural is देवाः.

Q9

  • Cinstrumental
  • Dgenitive

Working

✓

In रामः देवम् नमति, देवम् is the **accusative (object)** — what the bowing is directed at; रामः is the nominative subject.

Q10

  • Aदेवाय
  • Bदेवात्

Working

✓

'By means of the god' (instrumental singular) is **देवेन**. देवाय is 'to/for', देवात् is 'from', देवस्य is 'of'.
