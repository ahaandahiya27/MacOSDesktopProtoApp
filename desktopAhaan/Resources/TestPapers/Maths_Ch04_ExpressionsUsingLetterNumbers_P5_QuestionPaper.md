**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps at this level: charging an 'included first item' twice, distributing a coefficient (or a leading minus) over only the first term of a bracket, merging unlike terms or terms of different powers, dropping or sign-flipping a constant, squaring a sum as if it were the sum of squares, applying a negative sign before versus after squaring, mistaking 2n for n+2 or 3a for a-cubed, and reading a pattern's first value as its common difference. Keep every sign exactly as given; only LIKE terms combine; this chapter builds and simplifies expressions and reads off rules — it never solves equations. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. A courier charges a fixed 70 rupees that already covers the first 3 kilometres, then 18 rupees for each kilometre after the third.    (D) 7d - 70
