**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: what a constitution is and why a nation needs one, how India's Constitution was written, what shaped it, its key features, and the Preamble. Reason about how a "rulebook" organises a whole country. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
