**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it analyses the chapter's compounds and etymologies (द्वीप = द्वि + अप् 'two-water'; कारागार = कारा + गार 'cell-house'; त्रितलात्मक 'three-storied'), drills the past active participle (क्तवतु: सोढवान् 'he bore', कृतवान् 'he did', गतवान् 'he went') and contrasts it with finite past tense, and tests the geography, history and precise vocabulary. Remember: द्वीप is an ISLAND, not a prison; कारागार (prison) ≠ कारखाना (factory); कालापानी NAMES the Cellular Jail and the exile-over-the-seas it meant, not a literal black river; जनजाति is an indigenous TRIBAL community, not 'common people'; Savarkar bore दश वर्षाणि (ten years). Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
