**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it distinguishes the TWO senses of -वत् — 'like' (यन्त्रवत् 'like a machine' = यन्त्रम् इव) versus 'possessing' (धनवत्/धनवान् 'wealthy'); it drills the agent/possessor suffixes -क (चिकित्सकः 'healer'), -तृ (कर्तृ 'doer', दातृ 'giver') and -इन् (रोगिन् 'one with disease'); the emphatic particle हि; and the सेवा-vs-दान and परमः ('highest in rank', not 'first in time') distinctions. Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
