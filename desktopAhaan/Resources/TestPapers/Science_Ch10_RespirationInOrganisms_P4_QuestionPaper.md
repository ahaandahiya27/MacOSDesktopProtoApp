**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: equating breathing with cellular respiration, assuming exhaled air has zero oxygen or fresh air zero carbon dioxide, believing plants stop respiring by day or do not respire at all, treating anaerobic respiration as releasing as much energy as aerobic, reversing diaphragm and rib movements or the pressure logic of inhalation/exhalation, swapping the breathing organs of different organisms (gills/spiracles/tracheae/skin/stomata/lenticels), and reading breathing-rate or gas-volume data backwards.
