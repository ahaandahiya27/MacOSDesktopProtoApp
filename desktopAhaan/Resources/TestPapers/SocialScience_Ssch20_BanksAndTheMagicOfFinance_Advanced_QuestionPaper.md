**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: reason from cause to effect through the flow of money — from a depositor, through the bank's spread and reserves, to a borrower; from the RBI's benchmark rate to the prices in a shop; from a saver's idle rupee to a share in a company. Several questions need two or three steps of arithmetic (interest, the spread, compounding, the Rule of 72). Watch for the recurring traps: the RBI is the *banker to banks*, not a bank for people; shares can fall as well as rise; and no genuine bank ever asks for your PIN, password or OTP. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
