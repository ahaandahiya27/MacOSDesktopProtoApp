This is history — read each stem carefully and reason from cause and effect, chronology, and the evidence described in the chapter. Watch the classic traps: confusing the FIRST Urbanisation (Harappan) with the SECOND (Ganga plains); assuming ALL mahaajanapadas were monarchies (Vajji and Malla were republics); thinking the Harappans used iron (they used copper and bronze); and treating the gana-sangha republics as full modern democracies.
