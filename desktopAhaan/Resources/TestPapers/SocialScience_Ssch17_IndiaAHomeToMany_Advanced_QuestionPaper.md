**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: why and how many communities made India their home, those who came seeking refuge and those who came for trade, and the values of welcome and pluralism that mark Indian society. Reason from history to the idea of a shared home. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
