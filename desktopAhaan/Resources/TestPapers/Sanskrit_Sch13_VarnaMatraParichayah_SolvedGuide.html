Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

वर्णमात्रा refers to a vowel's:

  • Acolour
  • Bcaste
  • Cduration (how long it is sounded)✓
  • Dspelling

Working

✓

वर्णमात्रा refers to a vowel's duration (how long it is sounded). It is not about colour, caste, or spelling.

Q2

A मात्रा is a unit of:

  • Aweight
  • Bdistance
  • Cmoney
  • Dtime (duration)✓

Working

✓

A मात्रा is a unit of time (duration). It is not weight, distance, or money.

Q3

The मात्रा is defined as the duration of a:

  • Ashort vowel✓
  • Blong vowel
  • Cprolonged vowel
  • Dconsonant

Working

✓

One मात्रा is the duration of a short vowel. Long and prolonged vowels take two and three; a consonant is not the measure.

Q4

The root meaning of मात्रा is:

  • A'colour'
  • B'measure'✓
  • C'caste'
  • D'sound'

Working

✓

मात्रा's root meaning is 'measure' (cf. mensuration). It does not mean colour, caste, or sound.

Q5

वर्णमात्रा (sound-duration) belongs to the study of:

  • Ahistory
  • Bphonetics (the sounds of Sanskrit)✓
  • Cmathematics
  • Dgeography

Working

✓

वर्णमात्रा belongs to phonetics, the study of sounds. Not history, mathematics, or geography.

Q6

How many vowel quantities (मात्राः) does Sanskrit recognise?

  • Atwo
  • Bfour
  • Cone
  • Dthree✓

Working

✓

Sanskrit recognises three vowel quantities. Not two, four, or one.

Q7

The three vowel quantities are:

  • Ashort and long only
  • Bhigh, middle and low pitch
  • Cpast, present and future
  • Dह्रस्व (short), दीर्घ (long), प्लुत (prolonged)✓

Working

✓

They are ह्रस्व (short), दीर्घ (long) and प्लुत (prolonged). Not just two, nor pitches, nor tenses.

Q8

The most common mistake about Sanskrit vowel-length is:

  • Aadding a fourth length
  • Bforgetting प्लुत and assuming only two lengths✓
  • Cignoring short vowels
  • Dcounting consonants

Working

✓

The common mistake is forgetting प्लुत and assuming only two lengths. Not adding a fourth, ignoring short vowels, or counting consonants.

Q9

Unlike many languages with two vowel-lengths, Sanskrit has:

  • Aa third, the प्लुत (prolonged)✓
  • Bonly one length
  • Cno vowel lengths
  • Dten lengths

Working

✓

Sanskrit adds a third length, the प्लुत. It does not have only one, none, or ten.

Q10

The three quantities differ in their:

  • Aduration (how long the vowel is held)✓
  • Bcolour
  • Cspelling
  • Dcaste

Working

✓

They differ in duration. Not colour, spelling, or caste.

Set 2 · Q11–Q20

Q11

Ordering by length from shortest to longest, the three are:

  • Aप्लुत, दीर्घ, ह्रस्व
  • Bह्रस्व, दीर्घ, प्लुत✓
  • Cदीर्घ, ह्रस्व, प्लुत
  • Dह्रस्व, प्लुत, दीर्घ

Working

✓

Shortest to longest: ह्रस्व (1), दीर्घ (2), प्लुत (3). The other orders are wrong.

Q12

ह्रस्व means:

  • Along (2 matras)
  • Bprolonged (3 matras)
  • Cshort (1 matra)✓
  • Dsilent

Working

✓

ह्रस्व means 'short' (1 mātrā). Long is दीर्घ (2), prolonged प्लुत (3); it is not silent.

Q13

The descriptive name अल्पकालिकः ('short-time') refers to which class?

  • Aदीर्घ (long)
  • Bप्लुत (prolonged)
  • Cह्रस्व (short)✓
  • Dconsonants

Working

✓

अल्पकालिकः ('short-time') is ह्रस्व. दीर्घ is लम्बकालिकः and प्लुत अतिविलम्बकालिकः; consonants are not meant.

Q14

How many matras does a ह्रस्व vowel last?

  • Atwo
  • Bthree
  • Cone✓
  • Dzero

Working

✓

A ह्रस्व vowel lasts one mātrā. Two is दीर्घ, three प्लुत; it is not zero.

Q15

Which are examples of ह्रस्व (short) vowels?

  • Aअ, इ, उ✓
  • Bआ, ई, ऊ
  • Cthe calls भो-3, राम-3
  • Dक, ख, ग (consonants)

Working

✓

अ, इ, उ are short (ह्रस्व) vowels. आ, ई, ऊ are long, the drawn-out calls are प्लुत, and क, ख, ग are consonants.

Q16

दीर्घ means:

  • Along (2 matras)✓
  • Bshort (1 matra)
  • Cprolonged (3 matras)
  • Dsilent

Working

✓

दीर्घ means 'long' (2 mātrās). Short is ह्रस्व (1), prolonged प्लुत (3); it is not silent.

Q17

The descriptive name लम्बकालिकः ('long-time') refers to which class?

  • Aह्रस्व (short)
  • Bप्लुत (prolonged)
  • Cदीर्घ (long)✓
  • Dconsonants

Working

✓

लम्बकालिकः ('long-time') is दीर्घ. ह्रस्व is अल्पकालिकः and प्लुत अतिविलम्बकालिकः; consonants are not meant.

Q18

How many matras does a दीर्घ vowel last?

  • Aone
  • Bthree
  • Ctwo✓
  • Dfour

Working

✓

A दीर्घ vowel lasts two mātrās. One is ह्रस्व, three प्लुत; it is not four.

Q19

Which are examples of दीर्घ (long) vowels?

  • Aअ, इ, उ
  • Bthe drawn-out call भो-3
  • Cआ, ई, ऊ✓
  • Dconsonants only

Working

✓

आ, ई, ऊ are long (दीर्घ) vowels. अ, इ, उ are short, the drawn-out call is प्लुत, and consonants are not vowels.

Q20

प्लुत means:

  • Aprolonged (3 matras)✓
  • Bshort (1 matra)
  • Clong (2 matras)
  • Dsilent

Working

✓

प्लुत means 'prolonged' (3 mātrās). Short is ह्रस्व (1), long दीर्घ (2); it is not silent.

Q23

प्लुत vowels are mostly used in:

  • Aeveryday quick speech
  • Bchanting and dramatic calls (e.g. भो-3 राम!)✓
  • Cwriting numbers
  • Dsilent reading only

Working

✓

Q24

A प्लुत vowel should be:

  • Asaid as quickly as possible
  • Bskipped entirely
  • Cwhispered
  • Ddrawn out (held three times as long as a short vowel)✓

Working

✓

A प्लुत vowel is drawn out, held three times as long as a short vowel. It is not said quickly, skipped, or whispered.

Q25

If a प्लुत vowel is said quickly, it wrongly becomes:

  • Aa short vowel only
  • Ba consonant
  • Ca whole word
  • Da long (दीर्घ) vowel, not a प्लुत✓

Working

✓

Said quickly, a प्लुत collapses into a long (दीर्घ) vowel. It does not become merely short, a consonant, or a word.

कुक्कुटः रौति means 'the rooster crows'. It is not about a crow flying, a peacock dancing, or a mongoose running.

Q27

  • Aa rooster / cock✓
  • Ba crow
  • Ca sparrow
  • Da pigeon

Working

✓

कुक्कुटः means a rooster / cock. A crow is वायसः, a sparrow चटका, and a pigeon कपोतः.

कुक्कुटः is onomatopoeic, mimicking the rooster's call. It is not an English loan, a number, or a verb.

The crow naturally spans short, long and prolonged sounds, illustrating the three mātrās. It is not soundless, silent, or consonant-only.
