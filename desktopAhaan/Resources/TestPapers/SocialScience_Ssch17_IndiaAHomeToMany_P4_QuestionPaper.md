**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic P4 traps: filing a refuge or enslaved community under 'trade'; fusing Islam's peaceful 7th-century arrival via Arab merchants with the much later military invasions; reading identity-preserving integration as either total assimilation or total apartness; swapping cosmopolitan/pluralism/tolerance/ethos, refuge/persecution, and immigrate/emigrate; treating diversity as a weakness rather than the chapter's source of strength; and confusing prophet (Zarathushtra) with God (Ahura Mazda), or one community's house of worship with another's. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
