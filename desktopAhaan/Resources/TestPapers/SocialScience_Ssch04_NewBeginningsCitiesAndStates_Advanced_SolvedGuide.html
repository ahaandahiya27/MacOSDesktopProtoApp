Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

India's "First Urbanisation" refers to the cities of the:

  • AMaurya empire
  • BMughal empire
  • CGupta age
  • DHarappan (Indus / Sindhu-Sarasvati) civilisation✓

Working

✓

India's "First Urbanisation" was the **Harappan (Indus / Sindhu-Sarasvati) civilisation** — the Subcontinent's earliest cities, long before the Mauryas, Guptas or Mughals.

Q2

After the Harappan cities declined in the early 2nd millennium BCE, organised urban life in the Subcontinent largely disappeared for about:

  • Aa thousand years✓
  • Ba hundred years
  • Cten years
  • Dit never disappeared

Working

✓

After the Harappan cities declined, organised urban life largely vanished for about **a thousand years** — the long gap before the Second Urbanisation.

Q3

The "Second Urbanisation" began in the 1st millennium BCE chiefly in the fertile plains of the:

  • AIndus
  • BGanga✓
  • CNarmada
  • DKaveri

Working

✓

The Second Urbanisation grew chiefly in the fertile **Ganga** plains, where rich soil and iron tools supported dense farming and new towns.

*Jana* ("people") + *pada* ("foot") gives the sense of **a place where the people have set foot / settled** — a clan's territory.

A mahaajanapada ("great janapada") formed when **smaller janapadas merged into a bigger, more powerful state**.
