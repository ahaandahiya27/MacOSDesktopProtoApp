**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. A market is any buyer-seller-price arrangement (online counts too); the cheapest price is not always best because a price set too low makes the seller stop selling, so the 'just right' price that clears the market is what matters; and the retailer who hands you the good is not the producer who made it - the intermediaries in between add real value. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

   (B) a market only exists if the buyer and seller stand face to face
