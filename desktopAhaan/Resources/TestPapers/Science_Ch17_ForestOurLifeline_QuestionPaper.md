**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. A forest is a living system — trees, shrubs, herbs, climbers, animals, birds, insects and micro-organisms all linked together. Two short forest food-web descriptions appear in the paper; read them carefully and answer the questions that follow. Most questions need two to four reasoning steps; watch for traps (a forest is not just trees, and decomposers are helpful, not harmful). Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. A forest is best described as:

9. On the forest floor, dead leaves, twigs and animal droppings are broken down mainly by:

    (C) decomposers only live in ponds, not forests

12. Imagine every decomposer in a forest suddenly vanished. Over time the forest would most likely:

    (B) become buried under dead matter and starved of recycled nutrients

> **Forest food web (for Q18–Q22).** In one forest: grass and leaves are eaten by grasshoppers and by deer. Grasshoppers are eaten by frogs; frogs are eaten by snakes; snakes are eaten by hawks. Deer are eaten by tigers. Hawks also catch and eat small birds, and those small birds eat grasshoppers too.

18. In this web, the grasshopper is eaten by both:
    (A) grass and frogs
    (B) tigers and hawks
    (C) deer and snakes
    (D) frogs and small birds

19. If a disease killed almost all the **frogs** in this web, the most direct effect would be:
    (A) the grass would disappear
    (B) grasshopper numbers would tend to rise and snake food would fall
    (C) tigers would immediately die out
    (D) nothing would change anywhere

20. In this web, which animal eats prey from **two different** food chains?
    (A) the hawk (it eats both snakes and small birds)
    (B) the grasshopper
    (C) the deer
    (D) the frog

21. The ultimate source of energy for **every** organism in this forest web is:

22. If hunters removed all the **tigers** from this web, the most likely first effect is that:
    (A) the deer population would tend to increase
    (B) the grass would vanish at once

23. Forests are often called the "green lungs" of the Earth because their plants:

Rabbits are eaten by foxes. Snakes are also eaten by foxes. On the floor, fungi and bacteria break down everything that dies.
