Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

Infrastructure is best defined as:

  • Aonly the roads and buildings of a country
  • Bthe money a country keeps in its banks
  • Cthe basic facilities and systems a country needs to function✓
  • Dthe laws passed by a country's government

Working

✓

Infrastructure is the **basic facilities and systems a country needs to function** — far more than just roads and buildings.

Q2

The two broad types of infrastructure are:

  • Aphysical and social✓
  • Burban and rural
  • Cpublic and private
  • Dancient and modern

Working

✓

Its two broad types are **physical** (transport, utilities, communication, energy) and **social** (schools, hospitals, courts, parks).

Q3

Of the following, the one that belongs to **social** infrastructure is:

  • Aa national highway
  • Ba power grid
  • Ca sea port
  • Da government hospital✓

Working

✓

A **government hospital** is social infrastructure; highways, power grids and ports are physical.

Q4

  • Asocial infrastructure
  • Bphysical infrastructure✓
  • Cfacilities that aid wellbeing but not the economy
  • Dprivate property with no public role

Working

✓

A telecom tower, water pipeline and solar park are all **physical infrastructure** (communication, utilities, energy).

Q5

  • Cis visible from far away

Working

✓

Infrastructure is the "backbone" because it **supports and connects all other activity** — moving goods, people, power and information.

Q6

✓

The tomato reaches the city through a *chain*: **roads, cold storage, the internet and trucks together** link production to the market — no single piece does it alone.

Q8
