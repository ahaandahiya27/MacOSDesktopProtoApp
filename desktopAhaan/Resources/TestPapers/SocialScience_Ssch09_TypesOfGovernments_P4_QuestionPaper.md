**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. A king need not block democracy (constitutional monarchy = parliamentary democracy); India is parliamentary despite having a President because real power sits with the PM and Council of Ministers; elections alone never equal democracy (equality, rights, rule of law and an independent judiciary are also required); democracy and republic are not synonyms; and a winning majority can never vote away anyone's fundamental rights. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. A country abolishes its hereditary throne and, from then on, elects a head of state for a fixed term, yet it still lets only a tiny landowning elite vote and silences all critics. Using only the textbook definitions, which single label is now unquestionably accurate?
   (A) Republic, because the head of state is elected rather than inheriting the office
   (B) Democracy, because abolishing the throne automatically establishes rule by the people
   (C) Constitutional monarchy, because it retains an elected head of state in place of a king
   (D) Direct democracy, because the citizens themselves now choose the head of state

2. Which statement most precisely captures the relationship between 'democracy' and 'republic'?
   (A) They are exact synonyms, so every democracy is a republic and every republic is a democracy
   (B) A republic is a kind of monarchy in which the king is chosen by the people for a fixed term
   (C) Democracy describes an elected head of state, while a republic describes rule by the whole people
   (D) They overlap but are not identical: democracy concerns who rules (the people), while a republic specifies that the head of state is elected, not hereditary
