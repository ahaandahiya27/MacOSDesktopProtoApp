**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Beware: dividing by a fraction below 1 enlarges, multiplying by it shrinks; chained 'of the remaining' fractions act on the leftover, not the whole; mixed numbers must become improper before multiplying or dividing; and 'flip the divisor, not the dividend' when dividing. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
