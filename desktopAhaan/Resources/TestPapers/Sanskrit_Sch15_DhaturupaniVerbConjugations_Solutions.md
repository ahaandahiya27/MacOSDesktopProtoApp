## Worked Solutions

**1. (D)** A धातु is a verb root. It is not a noun ending, a vowel quantity, or a case.

**2. (A)** पठति, पठसि, पठामि are built from पठ् ('to read'). अस्, कृ and भाष् are different roots.

**3. (B)** The appendix tables eight roots. Not three, ten, or one.

**4. (C)** Knowing the root lets you build its many tense and person forms. Verbs are conjugated, not declined like nouns, and they have no case.

**5. (A)** The लकार system has ten tense-moods. Seven is the number of noun cases; not three or one.

**6. (A)** लकाराः are Sanskrit's ten tenses and moods. The seven cases, three genders and declensions are noun features.

**7. (D)** लट् marks the present tense. लङ् is the imperfect past, लृट् the future, and लोट् the imperative.

**8. (D)** लट्-लकार shows the present tense. Not the past, the future, or no time.

**9. (A)** The appendix conjugates the roots in लट् (the present). Not only the future, imperative, or optative.

**10. (B)** लङ्-लकार marks the imperfect past. The present is लट्, future लृट्, and optative विधिलिङ्.

**11. (C)** लृट्-लकार marks the future. The present is लट्, the past लङ्, and the imperative लोट्.

**12. (B)** लोट् is the imperative and विधिलिङ् the optative. The other pairings are wrong.

**13. (C)** Sanskrit verbs fall into ten गण (classes). Not three, seven, or one.

**14. (A)** पठ् belongs to the first class (भ्वादि), the most common. It is not the tenth, none, or the eighth (which is कृ's class).

**15. (B)** Multiple examples show that different गण classes conjugate differently. Verbs do not all conjugate identically, do change, and पठ् is not the only root.

**16. (D)** अस्, कृ and श्रु belong to other classes with different, often irregular forms. They are verbs (not nouns), do conjugate, and are not class 1.

**17. (B)** The two pada-classes are परस्मैपदम् and आत्मनेपदम्. The others are numbers, tenses, or genders.

**18. (B)** Parasmaipada 3rd-singular present ends in -ति (पठति). -ते is atmanepada, -स्य a future marker, and -आय a dative ending.

**19. (D)** Atmanepada 3rd-singular present ends in -ते (भाषते). -ति is parasmaipada, -तुम् an infinitive, and -स्य a future marker.

**20. (A)** पठति and भाषते differ by pada-class (parasmaipada vs atmanepada). They are the same tense, number and (verbs have no) gender.

**21. (C)** A root taking both classes is उभयपद. परस्मैपद and आत्मनेपद name a single class each, and अव्यय is an indeclinable.

**22. (D)** The lexicon tells you a root's pada-class. It is not decided by the noun, the time of day, or vowel length.

**23. (A)** Sanskrit's persons are numbered in reverse: प्रथम = English 'third person'. Not the same, random, or absent.

**24. (B)** प्रथमपुरुष corresponds to English third person (he/she/it). Not first or second person.

**25. (A)** उत्तमपुरुष corresponds to English first person (I/we). Not third or second person.

**26. (C)** मध्यमपुरुष corresponds to English second person (you). Not first or third person.

**27. (B)** Sanskrit's प्रथमपुरुष ('first person') is what Western books call the third person. Not the English first or second person.

**28. (D)** पठति is प्रथमपुरुष (Sanskrit 'first', English third person). Not उत्तमपुरुष or मध्यमपुरुष.

**29. (D)** Do not translate प्रथमपुरुष as English 'first person' (I); it actually means the English third person (he/she).

**30. (C)** The 3rd-singular present of पठ् is पठति. पठतः is dual, पठन्ति plural, and पठामि first-person singular.

**31. (C)** The 3rd-dual present is पठतः. पठति is singular, पठन्ति plural, and पठसि second-person singular.

**32. (D)** The 3rd-plural present is पठन्ति. पठति is singular, पठतः dual, and पठामि first-person singular.

**33. (B)** पठति, पठतः, पठन्ति are all 3rd-person present parasmaipada, differing only in number. Not in tense, person, or pada-class.

**34. (B)** अस् means 'to be'. 'To read' is पठ्, 'to do' कृ, and 'to speak' भाष्.

**35. (B)** The 3rd-singular present of अस् is अस्ति. अस्मि is first-singular, असि second-singular, and सन्ति third-plural.

**36. (C)** अहम् अस्मि means 'I am'. 'He is' is सः अस्ति, 'you are' त्वम् असि, and 'they are' ते सन्ति.

**37. (A)** अस्मि is the उत्तमपुरुष (first-person) singular, 'I am'. प्रथमपुरुष is अस्ति, मध्यमपुरुष असि; it is singular, not plural.

**38. (C)** The 3rd-plural present of अस् is सन्ति. अस्ति is singular, अस्मि first-singular, and स्तः dual.

**39. (D)** कृ means 'to do / make'. 'To be' is अस्, 'to read' पठ्, and 'to speak' भाष्.

**40. (A)** The 3rd-singular present of कृ is करोति. कुरुतः is dual, कुर्वन्ति plural, and करोमि first-person singular.

**41. (C)** The 3rd-plural present of कृ is कुर्वन्ति. करोति is singular, कुरुतः dual, and करोमि first-person singular.

**42. (B)** कृ alternates: strong करो- in करोति, weak कुर्व- in कुर्वन्ति. करो- is the strong stem, and पठ-/भाष- are other roots.

**43. (B)** 'I do' is करोमि (first-person singular). करोति is 'he does', कुर्वन्ति 'they do', and कुरुतः 'the two do'.

**44. (B)** करोति, कुरुतः, कुर्वन्ति are the 3rd-person singular, dual and plural of कृ. They are one tense, one person, and one verb.

**45. (C)** भाष् means 'to speak'. 'To be' is अस्, 'to do' कृ, and 'to read' पठ्.

**46. (D)** भाष् is atmanepada, so its 3rd-singular present is भाषते (-ते). भाषति (-ति) would be parasmaipada; भाषामि is first-singular and भाषन्ति a wrong-pada plural.

**47. (D)** The 3rd-plural present of भाष् is भाषन्ते. भाषन्ति is a parasmaipada-style ending, भाषते is singular, and भाषेते dual.

**48. (C)** भाषते (-ते) shows भाष् is atmanepada. Parasmaipada takes -ति; it is not a past tense or a first-person marker.

**49. (B)** अहम् ('I') requires करोमि ('I do'). करोति is 'he does', कुर्वन्ति 'they do', and कुरुतः 'the two do'.

**50. (A)** अहम् करोति is wrong because करोति is third person ('he does'), while अहम् is first person (needing करोमि). करोति is not correct here, a past tense, or a noun.

**51. (C)** Sanskrit verbs agree with their subject in person and number. Not colour, case/gender, or vowel length.

**52. (A)** वयम् ('we') takes कुर्मः ('we do'). करोति is 'he does', कुरुतः 'the two do', and करोमि 'I do'.

**53. (D)** अकरोत् means 'he did' (past). 'He does' is करोति, 'he will do' करिष्यति, and 'let him do' करोतु.

**54. (D)** The अ- in अकरोत् is the past-tense augment (लङ्-लकार). It is not the negative prefix, a case ending, or a length mark.

**55. (C)** The अ- in अधर्मः is the negative prefix ('non-'). It is not the past-tense augment, a present marker, or a dative ending.

**56. (D)** The two अ- (augment vs negative prefix) are distinguished by context. Not by colour, time, or vowel quantity alone.

**57. (A)** कृ = to do and भाष् = to speak. अस् is 'to be', पठ् 'to read'; the other pairings are wrong.

**58. (A)** Parasmaipada uses -ति (पठति) and atmanepada -ते (भाषते). The reversal and 'both same' options are wrong.

**59. (C)** प्रथमपुरुष = English third person and उत्तमपुरुष = English first person. The reversal and the other options are wrong.

**60. (A)** लट् is the present tense, and करोति means 'he/she does' (अहम् करोमि is 'I do'). The other options keep one or both errors.
