Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

सूर्यनमस्कारः means:

  • Athe moon salutation
  • Ba wise saying
  • Can enemy
  • Dthe Sun Salutation✓

Working

✓

सूर्यनमस्कारः is the Sun Salutation. It is not a moon salutation, a wise saying, or an enemy.

Q2

सूर्यनमस्कारः is a combination (समाहारः) of how many postures (आसनानि)?

  • Afive
  • Bsix
  • Ctwelve✓
  • Dten

Working

✓

The Sun Salutation is a combination of twelve postures (द्वादश आसनानि). It is not five, six, or ten.

Q3

Each posture of the Sun Salutation is paired with:

  • Aa fault to give up
  • Ba wise saying about pots
  • Ca mantra saluting the Sun by one of its twelve names✓
  • Da month's name only

Working

✓

Each of the twelve postures is paired with a mantra saluting the Sun by one of its twelve names. It is not a fault, a pot-saying, or merely a month-name.

Q4

  • Arivers of India
  • Bnames of the Sun (and the twelve months)✓
  • Cfaults to give up
  • Dva-qualities

Working

✓

The twelve postures mirror the twelve Sun-names (and the twelve months). Not rivers, faults, or the va-qualities.

Q7

✓

मित्राय (dative) + नमः = 'salutation to the Sun (Mitra)'. It is not 'the Sun salutes me', a plural, or a vocative call.

Q9

  • Afriend
  • Bposture
  • Csalutation / bow✓
  • Dwisdom

Working

✓

Q10

नमः takes its object in which case?

  • Anominative
  • Baccusative
  • Cdative (चतुर्थी विभक्ति)✓
  • Dinstrumental

Working

✓

नमः idiomatically governs the dative (चतुर्थी), so the one saluted goes into the dative (मित्राय). Not nominative, accusative, or instrumental.

Set 2 · Q11–Q20

Q11

Using नमः मित्रः (nominative) instead of नमः मित्राय is wrong because नमः governs the:

  • Adative, so मित्राय (-आय) is needed✓
  • Bnominative
  • Cgenitive
  • Dvocative

Working

✓

नमः governs the dative, so मित्राय (-आय) is correct, not the nominative मित्रः. It is not genitive or vocative.

Q12

The चतुर्थी विभक्ति (dative) generally means:

  • A'with / by'
  • B'of'
  • C'to / for'✓
  • D'from'

Working

✓

The dative (चतुर्थी) means 'to / for'. 'With/by' is instrumental, 'of' genitive, and 'from' ablative.

Q13

The dative ending that turns मित्र into मित्राय is:

  • A-एन
  • B-स्य
  • C-आय✓
  • D-अम्

Working

✓

The dative singular ending -आय gives मित्राय. -एन is instrumental, -स्य genitive, and -अम् accusative.

Q14

To say 'salutation to Ravi (the Sun)', the correct form is:

  • Aरविः नमः
  • Bरवये नमः✓
  • Cरविम् नमः
  • Dरवेः नमः

Working

✓

नमः needs the dative, so रवये नमः (dative of रवि). रविः is nominative, रविम् accusative, and रवेः genitive/ablative.

Q15

  • Caccusative
  • Dinstrumental

Working

✓

सूर्यः is nominative; नमः needs the dative सूर्याय. It is not already correct, accusative, or instrumental.
