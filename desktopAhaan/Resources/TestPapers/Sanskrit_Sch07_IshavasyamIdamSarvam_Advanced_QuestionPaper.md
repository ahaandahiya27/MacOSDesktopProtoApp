**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it drills the FUTURE tense (लृट्, the -स्य-/-इष्य- marker: दास्यन्ति 'they shall give', गमिष्यति, करिष्यति) and tells it from present and past; the INSTRUMENTAL case of means (ध्यानेन 'by meditation', नामकीर्तनेन); the possessive suffix -मत्/-वत् (सर्वशक्तिमान् 'all-powerful', बुद्धिमान्); the etymology of ईश/ईश्वर (root ईश् 'to rule'); and the rank of the semi-divine यक्ष/गन्धर्व/किन्नर. Remember ब्रह्म (neuter) is the Absolute, distinct from ब्रह्मन् (priest) and ब्रह्मा (creator). Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
