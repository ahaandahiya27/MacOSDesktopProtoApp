Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

The phrase "the product of 1/2 and 1/3" equals:

  • A5/6
  • B1/6✓
  • C2/3
  • D1/5

Working

✓

"Product of 1/2 and 1/3" = 1/2 × 1/3 = (1×1)/(2×3) = 1/6. (Adding the two, 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6, is the trap (A).)

Q2

3 × 1/4 equals:

  • A3/12
  • B1/12
  • C4/3
  • D3/4✓

Working

✓

3 × 1/4 = 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 = 3/4. Multiply the whole number into the numerator only; the denominator (the piece size) does not change. (3/12 wrongly multiplies the denominator too.)

Q3

Which expression correctly represents "2/5 of 30"?

  • A2/5 ÷ 30
  • B2/5 + 30
  • C2/5 × 30✓
  • D30 ÷ 2/5

Working

✓

The word "of" means multiply, so "2/5 of 30" = 2/5 × 30. (Division and addition are the standard distractors.)

Q4

The reciprocal of 3/7 is:

  • A7/3✓
  • B−3/7
  • C3/7
  • D1/3

Working

✓

The reciprocal swaps numerator and denominator: 3/7 → 7/3. (The reciprocal is not the negative.)

Q5

Compute 7 × 3/5 and write it as a mixed number.

  • A21/35
  • B10/5
  • C4 1/5✓
  • D3 2/5

Working

✓

7 × 3/5 = 21/5. As a mixed number, 21 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 1, so 4 1/5. (21/35 wrongly multiplies the denominator.)

Q6

What is 2/3 of 3/5?

  • A5/8
  • B6/8
  • C2/5✓
  • D2/15

Working

✓

"2/3 of 3/5" = 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5 in lowest terms. (Leaving it as 6/15-style unreduced or adding gives the distractors.)

Q7

Compute 5/6 ÷ 2/3.

  • A5/9
  • B10/18
  • C4/5
  • D5/4✓

Working

✓

5/6 ÷ 2/3 = 5/6 × 3/2 (flip the divisor) = 15/12 = 5/4. (5/9 comes from multiplying instead of flipping; 10/18 is the unreduced wrong product.)

Q8

How many 1/2-cup servings can be poured from 3 cups of juice?

  • A6✓
  • B3/2
  • C1 1/2
  • D3

Working

✓

3 ÷ 1/2 asks "how many halves fit in 3?" = 3 × 2 = 6. Dividing by a number below 1 gives a result larger than 3. (3/2 flips the wrong fraction.)

Q9

Which of these multiplications gives a result SMALLER than 8?

  • A8 × 5/4
  • B8 × 1
  • C8 × 3/4✓
  • D8 × 9/8

Working

✓

Multiplying by a fraction LESS than 1 shrinks a number. Only 8 × 3/4 = 6 < 8; 8 × 5/4 = 10 and 8 × 9/8 = 9 are larger, and 8 × 1 = 8.

Q10

The reciprocal of the whole number 5 is:

  • A5
  • B1/5✓
  • C−5
  • D0

Working

✓

A whole number 5 = 5/1, so its reciprocal is 1/5 (their product is 1). (5 itself is not its own reciprocal.)

Set 2 · Q11–Q20

Q11

Dividing a number by which of the following is the same as multiplying it by 4/5?

  • A4/5
  • B5
  • C5/4✓
  • D20

Working

✓

Dividing by 5/4 = multiplying by its reciprocal 4/5. So dividing by 5/4 matches multiplying by 4/5. (Dividing by 4/5 would equal multiplying by 5/4 — the reversed trap.)

Q12

A rectangle has length 2/3 m and breadth 3/4 m. Its area (in m²) is:

  • A1/2✓
  • B5/7
  • C17/12
  • D6/7

Working

✓

Area = length × breadth = 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2 m². (5/7 and 6/7 come from adding numerators/denominators.)

Q13

Convert the mixed number 2 1/2 to an improper fraction.

  • A4/2
  • B5/2✓
  • C3/2
  • D21/2

Working

✓

2 1/2 = (2×2 + 1)/2 = 5/2. (4/2 forgets the +1; 21/2 mis-places digits.)

Q14

Compute 2 1/2 × 1 1/3 by first converting to improper fractions.

  • A2 1/6
  • B3 1/3✓
  • C2 1/2
  • D3 2/3

Working

✓

Convert first: 2 1/2 = 5/2, 1 1/3 = 4/3. Product = 5/2 × 4/3 = 20/6 = 10/3 = 3 1/3. (Multiplying whole parts separately is the classic mixed-number error.)

Q15

A 4 1/2-acre plot is divided into 3/4-acre shares. How many shares are there?

  • A3
  • B12
  • C6✓
  • D4 1/2

Working

✓

4 1/2 ÷ 3/4 = 9/2 ÷ 3/4 = 9/2 × 4/3 = 36/6 = 6 shares. Dividing by a fraction below 1 yields more pieces than the acreage.

Q16

Which statement is TRUE?

  • AMultiplying any number by 2/3 makes it larger.
  • BDividing 6 by 1/2 gives 3.
  • CDividing a number by 1/4 multiplies it by 4.✓
  • DThe reciprocal of 1 is 0.

Working

✓

Dividing by 1/4 means multiplying by 4 (its reciprocal) — TRUE. (B) 6 ÷ 1/2 = 12, not 3; (A) ×2/3 shrinks a number; (D) reciprocal of 1 is 1, not 0.

Q17

Half OF a third equals:

  • A5/6
  • B1/6✓
  • C2/3
  • D1/3

Working

✓

"Half of a third" = 1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6 (of means multiply, not add). 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6 is the trap (A).

Q18

Ahaan spends 1/3 of his money on a book, then 1/4 of what remains on a pen. What fraction of his original money is left?

  • A5/12
  • B1/4
  • C2/3
  • D1/2✓

Working

✓

After the book, 1 − 1/3 = 2/3 remains. The pen costs 1/4 of 2/3 = 1/6. Left = 2/3 − 1/6 = 4/6 − 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2. (5/12 = 1/4 + 1/6 confuses "spent" with "left".)

Q19

A recipe needs 1 1/2 cups of flour per cake. How many cups are needed for 2 2/3 cakes?

  • A4✓
  • B4 1/2
  • C3
  • D4 1/6

Working

✓

Flour = 1 1/2 × 2 2/3 = 3/2 × 8/3 = 24/6 = 4 cups. (4 1/2 and 4 1/6 come from not converting both mixed numbers.)

Q20

Compute 3/4 × 20.

  • A15✓
  • B80
  • C60
  • D5

Working

✓

3/4 × 20 = (3 × 20)/4 = 60/4 = 15. (60 forgets to divide by 4; 5 divides by 4 but drops the 3.)

Set 3 · Q21–Q30

Q21

Which product is the LARGEST?

  • A12 × 1/2
  • B12 × 2/3
  • C12 × 3/4
  • D12 × 5/6✓

Working

✓

All share the multiplier 12, so the largest fraction wins: 5/6 > 3/4 > 2/3 > 1/2. Thus 12 × 5/6 = 10 is the largest.

Q22

The value of 4/9 × 3/8, in lowest terms, is:

  • A12/72
  • B1/6✓
  • C7/17
  • D1/12

Working

✓

4/9 × 3/8 = 12/72. Reduce by 12: 1/6. (12/72 is correct but not in lowest terms; 7/17 adds across.)

Q23

A tortoise walks 1/4 km each hour. How far does it walk in 3 1/2 hours?

  • A3 1/2 km
  • B14 km
  • C1 1/8 km
  • D7/8 km✓

Working

✓

Distance = 1/4 × 3 1/2 = 1/4 × 7/2 = 7/8 km. (3 1/2 ignores the rate; 14 multiplies wrongly.)

Q24

1 ÷ 2/3 equals:

  • A2/3
  • B3/2✓
  • C1/3
  • D2

Working

✓

1 ÷ 2/3 = 1 × 3/2 = 3/2. Flip the divisor and multiply; the answer exceeds 1 because you divide by less than 1.

Q25

Two-thirds of the students in a class of 30 wear glasses, and 1/5 of those who wear glasses also wear caps. How many students wear both?

  • A20
  • B6
  • C4✓
  • D5

Working

✓

Glasses = 2/3 × 30 = 20. Both glasses and caps = 1/5 × 20 = 4. (20 stops at the first step; 6 = 1/5 of 30 skips the chaining.)

Q26

Which of the following equals 2/3 ÷ 4?

  • A8/3
  • B2/12
  • C2/7
  • D1/6✓

Working

✓

2/3 ÷ 4 = 2/3 × 1/4 = 2/12 = 1/6. Dividing by a whole number = multiplying by its reciprocal. (8/3 multiplies by 4 instead.)

Q27

A pole 6 m long is painted; 2/3 of it is red and 1/2 of the rest is blue. What length (in m) is blue?

  • A4
  • B3
  • C2
  • D1✓

Working

✓

Red = 2/3 × 6 = 4 m, so 2 m remain. Blue = 1/2 of that rest = 1/2 × 2 = 1 m. (Taking 1/2 of the whole pole gives 3 — the trap.)

Q28

Which pair of fractions are reciprocals of each other?

  • A2/5 and 5/2✓
  • B3/4 and 3/4
  • C2/3 and 3/4
  • D1/2 and 2/4

Working

✓

Reciprocals multiply to 1: 2/5 × 5/2 = 10/10 = 1. So 2/5 and 5/2. (1/2 and 2/4 are equal, not reciprocal.)

Q29

A 3/4 m ribbon is cut into pieces each 1/8 m long. How many pieces are obtained?

  • A3/32
  • B6✓
  • C8
  • D4

Working

✓

3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8 = 24/4 = 6 pieces. Many small 1/8 m pieces fit in 3/4 m. (3/32 multiplies instead of dividing.)

Q30

Without computing exactly, which is true about 15 × 4/5?

  • AIt is more than 15.
  • BIt equals 15.
  • CIt is less than 15.✓
  • DIt is less than 4/5.

Working

✓

4/5 is less than 1, so 15 × 4/5 = 12 is LESS than 15. Multiplying by a proper fraction shrinks the number. (It is certainly more than 4/5, so (D) is false.)

Q31

Compute (2/3) × (9/10) × (5/6).

  • A1/2✓
  • B16/19
  • C90/180
  • D3/5

Working

✓

(2/3) × (9/10) × (5/6) = (2×9×5)/(3×10×6) = 90/180 = 1/2. (90/180 is correct but unreduced.)

Q32

A jug holds 2 1/4 litres. How many 3/8-litre glasses can be filled from it?

  • A6✓
  • B8
  • C5
  • D27/32

Working

✓

2 1/4 ÷ 3/8 = 9/4 ÷ 3/8 = 9/4 × 8/3 = 72/12 = 6 glasses. (27/32 multiplies instead of dividing.)

Q33

Three-quarters of a number is 18. The number is:

  • A13 1/2
  • B72
  • C27
  • D24✓

Working

✓

3/4 of the number = 18, so number = 18 ÷ 3/4 = 18 × 4/3 = 72/3 = 24. Check: 3/4 × 24 = 18. ✓ (13 1/2 = 3/4 × 18 multiplies instead of dividing; 72 multiplies by 4 only.)

Q34

The expression "5 ÷ 1/3" gives the same result as:

  • A5 × 1/3
  • B5 + 3
  • C5 × 3✓
  • D1/3 × 5

Working

✓

Dividing by 1/3 = multiplying by its reciprocal 3, so 5 ÷ 1/3 = 5 × 3 = 15. (5 × 1/3 multiplies by the divisor itself.)

Q35

Which of these is equal to 1?

  • A7/8 × 8/7✓
  • B3/4 + 1/4 × 0
  • C2/3 ÷ 2/3 × 1/2
  • D5/6 × 6/5 × 1/2

Working

✓

7/8 × 8/7 = 56/56 = 1 (a number times its reciprocal). (B) = 3/4; (C) = 1 × 1/2 = 1/2; (D) = 1 × 1/2 = 1/2.

Q36

1/2 of 1/3 of 1/4 equals:

  • A3/9
  • B3/24
  • C1/9
  • D1/24✓

Working

✓

1/2 × 1/3 × 1/4 = (1×1×1)/(2×3×4) = 1/24. (3/24 = 3/24 adds numerators; 1/9 adds denominators-style.)

Q37

A car covers 3/5 of a 200 km journey before lunch. How many kilometres remain after lunch?

  • A120
  • B40
  • C60
  • D80✓

Working

✓

Travelled 3/5 of 200 = 120 km, so remaining = 200 − 120 = 80 km. (Equivalently 2/5 × 200 = 80.) (120 answers the wrong part.)

Q38

Compute 2 1/3 ÷ 1 1/6.

  • A14/18
  • B2✓
  • C49/18
  • D1 1/2

Working

✓

2 1/3 ÷ 1 1/6 = 7/3 ÷ 7/6 = 7/3 × 6/7 = 42/21 = 2. (49/18 multiplies the fractions; 14/18 mishandles the reciprocal.)

Q39

Which value makes the sentence true: 3/8 × ___ = 3/8?

  • A0
  • B8/3
  • C1✓
  • D3/8

Working

✓

A number times 1 is itself, so 3/8 × 1 = 3/8. The multiplicative identity is 1. (×0 gives 0; ×3/8 gives 9/64.)

Q40

A wall is painted at 2/5 of a wall per hour. How long, in hours, to paint the whole wall?

  • A2/5
  • B2
  • C7/5
  • D5/2✓

Working

✓

Whole wall ÷ rate = 1 ÷ 2/5 = 1 × 5/2 = 5/2 hours. Dividing by a rate below 1 takes more than 1 hour. (2/5 and 7/5 misuse the reciprocal.)

  • D1/4

Working

✓

After Riya, 1 − 1/4 = 3/4 remains. Sam ate 2/3 of that: 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2 of the whole cake. (2/3 forgets it is "of the remainder".)

Q42

The product 5/12 × 8 equals:

  • A5/96
  • B40/96
  • C13/12
  • D10/3✓

Working

✓

5/12 × 8 = (5 × 8)/12 = 40/12 = 10/3. (40/96 wrongly multiplies the denominator by 8 too.)

Q43

Which statement about reciprocals is FALSE?

  • AThe reciprocal of a proper fraction is greater than 1.
  • BZero has no reciprocal.
  • CThe reciprocal of 1 is 1.
  • DEvery fraction is larger than its reciprocal.✓

Working

✓

FALSE statement: a fraction is NOT always larger than its reciprocal — 1/2's reciprocal is 2, which is bigger. (A), (B), (C) are all true: a proper fraction flips to >1, 0 has no reciprocal, and 1's reciprocal is 1.)

Q44

A tank fills 3/4 of its volume in 1 hour. At the same rate, what fraction fills in 1/3 hour?

  • A9/4
  • B3/7
  • C13/12
  • D1/4✓

Working

✓

Fraction filled = 3/4 × 1/3 = 3/12 = 1/4 in 1/3 hour. (9/4 divides instead of multiplying; 13/12 adds.)

Q45

Solve: (1/2 + 1/3) × 6/5.

  • A1✓
  • B5/6
  • C6/5
  • D11/30

Working

✓

1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6; then 5/6 × 6/5 = 30/30 = 1 (reciprocal pair). (Forgetting to add inside the bracket first gives the distractors.)

Q46

A garden is 5/6 of an acre; 3/10 of it is planted with roses. The rose area (in acres) is:

  • A8/16
  • B4/5
  • C1/4✓
  • D15/16

Working

✓

Rose area = 3/10 of 5/6 = 3/10 × 5/6 = 15/60 = 1/4 acre. (8/16 and 15/16 mis-multiply or fail to reduce.)

Q47

If 2/3 of a number equals 1/2 of another number, and the first number is 9, the second number is:

  • A6
  • B12✓

  • D27/4

Working

✓

First number 9: 2/3 × 9 = 6. This equals 1/2 of the second, so 1/2 × x = 6 → x = 12. Check: 1/2 × 12 = 6. ✓ (6 stops at the first value; 27/4 mishandles the reciprocal.)

Q48

A piece of cloth 7/8 m long is shared equally among 7 children. Each child gets (in m):

  • A7/8
  • B1/8✓
  • C49/8
  • D1/7

Working

✓

Share each = 7/8 ÷ 7 = 7/8 × 1/7 = 7/56 = 1/8 m. (7/8 forgets to divide; 49/8 multiplies by 7.)

Q49

Which division gives the LARGEST result?

  • A6 ÷ 1/2✓
  • B6 ÷ 2
  • C6 ÷ 3
  • D6 ÷ 1

Working

✓

Dividing by a number below 1 gives the biggest result: 6 ÷ 1/2 = 12, while 6 ÷ 1 = 6, 6 ÷ 2 = 3, 6 ÷ 3 = 2. So (A).

A worker paints 2/7 of a fence in a day. What fraction is left unpainted after 2 days?

In 2 days the worker paints 2 × 2/7 = 4/7, so unpainted = 1 − 4/7 = 3/7. (2/7 is one day's work; 4/7 is the painted part, not the leftover.)

Q57

Q58

✓

Q59

✓
