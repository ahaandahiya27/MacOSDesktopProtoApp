**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Recall the five steps of nutrition (ingestion → digestion → absorption → assimilation → egestion) and the path of food through the human alimentary canal, plus the special cases of ruminants and Amoeba. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
