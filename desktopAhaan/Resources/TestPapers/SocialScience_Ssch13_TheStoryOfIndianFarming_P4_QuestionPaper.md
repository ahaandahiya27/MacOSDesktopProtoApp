**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: most rural farm work is done by women not men; the Green Revolution had a MIXED record (more grain but degraded soil, depleted groundwater, pollution, health harm, debt); rabi is the dry winter crop that usually needs irrigation while kharif lives on the monsoon; intensive/extensive is about effort per area, not purpose; subsistence/commercial is about purpose, not plot size; organic farming cuts chemicals rather than abandoning food; hydroponics shows plants can grow without soil.
