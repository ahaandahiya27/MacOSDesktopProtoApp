**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. National-olympiad traps: count मात्रा by adding each VOWEL's value (ह्रस्व 1, दीर्घ 2, प्लुत 3) — never count letters/consonants; a syllable is गुरु if its vowel is दीर्घ (long by nature) OR a short vowel is closed by a conjunct/अनुस्वार/विसर्ग or word-final consonant (long by position) — forgetting position-guru is the classic slip; अनुस्वार and विसर्ग add no मात्रा of their own yet still make the preceding short vowel गुरु; ए ऐ ओ औ are all दीर्घ (2 मात्रा), never short; sandhi conserves vowel-time only sometimes (अ+आ→आ drops 3→2); मात्रा counts duration not pitch/weight; वर्ण = sound/letter not caste; रौति singular vs रुवन्ति plural is number not tense.
