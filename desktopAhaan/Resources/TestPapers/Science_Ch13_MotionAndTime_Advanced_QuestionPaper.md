**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Use the conversions 1 km/h = 5/18 m/s and 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h. Most questions need three or more reasoning steps; some blend two ideas (e.g. average speed + unit conversion). Watch for traps in the distractors — close-but-wrong is the rule, not the exception. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.

1. A ceiling fan spinning at constant speed has each blade-tip moving in a circle once every 0.4 s. Which two motion descriptors fit the tip best?
   (A) circular and oscillatory
   (B) rectilinear and periodic
   (C) random and periodic
   (D) circular and periodic

2. A child swings from rest, rises to the same height on the other side, and stops there forever (no further push). The motion shown is:
   (A) periodic, because it crossed the centre once
   (B) uniform circular motion
   (C) oscillatory only — not periodic, because it didn't repeat
   (D) random motion

3. A car covers 50 km in the 1st hour, 70 km in the 2nd hour, and 90 km in the 3rd hour. Its motion is best described as:
   (A) uniform
   (B) at rest
   (C) non-uniform, speeding up
   (D) non-uniform, slowing down

4. A train 200 m long crosses a 300 m platform completely in 25 s. The train's speed is:
   (A) 12 m/s
   (B) 4 m/s
   (C) 8 m/s
   (D) 20 m/s

5. A man walks **half** of his journey at 4 km/h and the **other half** at 6 km/h. His average speed for the whole journey is:
   (A) 5 km/h
   (B) 5.2 km/h
   (C) 4.8 km/h
   (D) 2.4 km/h

6. A boat moves at 12 km/h in still water. In a river flowing at 4 km/h, what are the boat's speeds **downstream** and **upstream**?
   (A) 12 km/h both ways
   (B) 8 km/h and 16 km/h
   (C) 16 km/h and 8 km/h
   (D) 4 km/h and 12 km/h

7. A pendulum's length is **doubled**. By what factor does its time period change? (Use *T* ∝ √*L*.)
   (A) ×2
   (B) ×4
   (C) ÷2
   (D) ×√2

8. A pendulum's length is **quartered** (cut to one-fourth). Its time period:
   (A) doubles
   (B) halves
   (C) is one-fourth as long

9. Two cyclists start at the same point. P moves east at 5 m/s, Q moves west at 3 m/s. Their **rate of separation** is:
   (A) 8 m/s
   (B) 2 m/s
   (C) 15 m/s
   (D) 0 m/s

10. A car travelling at 20 m/s overtakes a truck moving at 15 m/s in the same direction. The relative speed of the car with respect to the truck is:
    (A) 35 m/s
    (B) 20 m/s
    (C) 0 m/s
    (D) 5 m/s

11. A satellite circles the Earth at 7.5 km/s. Expressed in km/h, its speed is:
    (A) 2700 km/h
    (B) 270 km/h
    (C) 27 000 km/h
    (D) 270 000 km/h

12. A car maintains an average speed of 60 km/h for a 240 km trip. If the first 120 km were covered at 40 km/h, what speed must the car average over the second 120 km?
    (A) 80 km/h
    (B) 100 km/h
    (C) 120 km/h
    (D) 90 km/h

13. The Moon's gravity is about *g*/6 of Earth's. A pendulum that has a time period of 1 s on Earth will, on the Moon, have a time period of about:
    (A) 1/√6 s
    (B) 6 s
    (C) 1 s — gravity doesn't matter
    (D) √6 s ≈ 2.45 s

14. A cricket ball is bowled at 144 km/h toward a batsman 18 m away. How long does the ball take to reach the batsman? (Assume constant speed.)
    (A) 0.45 s
    (B) 0.18 s
    (C) 1.0 s
    (D) 0.05 s

15. A pendulum-clock that loses 6 minutes per day has a period that is approximately:
    (A) 0.4% too long
    (B) 0.4% too short
    (C) 6% too long
    (D) 6% too short

16. A car covers a journey in three equal **time** segments at 30, 50, and 70 km/h. Its average speed for the whole journey is:
    (A) 50 km/h

    (D) 60 km/h

17. A car covers a journey in three equal **distance** segments at 20, 30, and 60 km/h. Its average speed for the whole journey is:
    (A) 30 km/h

    (D) 50 km/h

18. On a distance–time graph the line is straight from (0 s, 0 m) to (10 s, 60 m), then horizontal from (10 s, 60 m) to (15 s, 60 m), then straight from (15 s, 60 m) back to (25 s, 0 m). At t = 20 s the object is:
    (A) at rest
    (B) at the starting point

19. In the previous question, the **total distance travelled** in 25 s is:
