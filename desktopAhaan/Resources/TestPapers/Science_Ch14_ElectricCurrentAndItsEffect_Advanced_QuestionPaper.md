**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. A bulb glows only when the circuit is a complete (closed) loop. Recall the symbols (cell = one long thin + one short thick line; battery = cells in a row; bulb = a circle with a cross; closed switch completes the loop). Current has a heating effect and a magnetic effect. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
