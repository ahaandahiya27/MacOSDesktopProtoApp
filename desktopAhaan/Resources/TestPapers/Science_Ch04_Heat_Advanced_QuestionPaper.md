**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Temperatures are in degrees Celsius (°C). Recall that heat flows from hotter to colder, that conduction needs a solid path, convection needs a moving fluid, and radiation needs no medium at all. Most questions need two to four reasoning steps; watch for traps. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
