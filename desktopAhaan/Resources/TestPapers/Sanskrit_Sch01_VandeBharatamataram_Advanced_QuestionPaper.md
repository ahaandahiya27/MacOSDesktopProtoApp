**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it declines the kinship र-stem मातृ (माता, मातरम्, मातुः) and its siblings पितृ/भ्रातृ; it contrasts the CASES the two salutation-verbs take — वन्दे takes the ACCUSATIVE (वन्दे मातरम्) while नमः takes the DATIVE (मात्रे नमः); it splits the poem's compounds (पर्वतराजः 'king of mountains', रत्नाकरः 'jewel-mine = ocean', देशभक्तः 'country-devotee'); reads the personification of Bharat as Mother (Himalaya = crown, ocean = washing the feet चरणौ, a dual); and tests vowel-length meaning-shifts (आनन्द vs अनन्द). वन्दे means 'I bow/salute', not 'I love'. Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
