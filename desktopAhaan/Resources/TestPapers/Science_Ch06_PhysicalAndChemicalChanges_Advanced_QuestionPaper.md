**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. A physical change makes no new substance and is usually reversible (melting, dissolving, tearing). A chemical change makes a new substance and is usually irreversible (burning, rusting, cooking, curdling), often with heat/light, gas, colour change or smell. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
