Q4

  • A23, 2, 4, 16
  • B23, −2, ×4, +16
  • C23 − 2, 4 + 16

Working

✓

Terms are the parts separated by + after turning subtraction into adding the inverse, and a product stays whole. So 23 − 2 × 4 + 16 has terms 23, −(2 × 4), 16. (Splitting 2 × 4 into separate terms is the trap.)

Q24
