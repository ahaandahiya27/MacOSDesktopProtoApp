**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the chapter's classic traps: the Maurya Empire broke up from WITHIN (it was not destroyed by foreign invaders); the foreign rulers were largely absorbed into Indian culture rather than erasing it; a matrimonial alliance was an act of diplomacy, not just a wedding; queens could hold real power; and never swap the Gandhara (Greco-Buddhist) and Mathura (indigenous, red-sandstone) schools of art. Keep the dates and the order of dynasties straight. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
