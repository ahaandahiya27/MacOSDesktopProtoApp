**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Treating corresponding/alternate angles as equal when the lines are NOT parallel; treating co-interior (same-side interior) angles as equal when they are actually supplementary; confusing alternate (equal) with co-interior (sum 180°); skipping a linear-pair step before applying a parallel-line relation; stopping the angle chase one step early; using 360° where 180° applies (and the reverse). 1. Two lines AB and CD cross at O. Angle AOC = (4x)° and the adjacent angle BOC = (5x)° form a linear pair. Find angle AOC.
   (A) 100°
   (B) 72°
   (C) 20°
   (D) 80°

2. Two lines intersect at O. Angle AOC = (3x + 12)° and its vertically opposite angle BOD = (5x − 18)°. Find angle AOC.
   (A) 57°
   (B) 123°
   (C) 15°
   (D) 78°

3. At an intersection of two lines, one angle is (4x − 6)° and its adjacent angle (linear pair) is (2x + 6)°. Find the larger of these two angles.

   (D) 120°

4. A transversal cuts two parallel lines p and q. A corresponding angle at p is (2x + 30)° and the matching corresponding angle at q is (4x − 10)°. Find that common corresponding-angle measure.

   (C) 110°
   (D) 50°
