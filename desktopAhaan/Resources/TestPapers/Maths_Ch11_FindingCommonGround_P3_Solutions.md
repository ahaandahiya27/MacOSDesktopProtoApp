**9. (A)** A factor 2^a x 3^b x 5^c is a perfect square when a, b, c are all even. a in {0,2} (2 ways), b in {0,2} (2 ways), c in {0} (1 way, since exponent of 5 is only 1). Total = 2 x 2 x 1 = 4: namely 1, 4, 9 and 36. Counting c=1 as allowed gives 6 (wrong, 5 is not squared); counting all factors loosely gives 8; forgetting 1 gives 3. Answer 4.

**10. (C)** The number of factors is found by adding 1 to each exponent and multiplying: (4+1)(2+1)(1+1) = 5 x 3 x 2 = 30. Adding the exponents (4+2+1=7) and adding the primes is the common error giving 11 or similar; 15 forgets the +1 on one prime; 24 = 4 x 3 x 2 forgets all the +1s. Answer 30.

**11. (B)** HCF takes lowest powers: 2^2 x 3 x 5 = 60. LCM takes highest powers: 2^3 x 3^2 x 5^2 = 1800. Ratio = 1800/60 = 30. In general LCM/HCF = product of (prime^|difference of exponents|) = 2^(3-2) x 3^(2-1) x 5^(2-1) = 2 x 3 x 5 = 30. Picking 6 uses only two primes; 15 drops the 2; 60 is the HCF itself. Ratio = 30.
