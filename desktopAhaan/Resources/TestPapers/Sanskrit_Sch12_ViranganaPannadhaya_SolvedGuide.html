Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

The heroine of this chapter is:

  • APanna Dhai (पन्नाधाया)✓
  • BRani Lakshmi Bai
  • CPanna's own son
  • Dthe assassin

Working

✓

The heroine is Panna Dhai (पन्नाधाया). Not Lakshmi Bai, her son, or the assassin.

Q2

पन्नाधाया is described as a वीराङ्गना, meaning:

  • Aa queen
  • Ba wet-nurse only
  • Ca brave woman / heroine✓
  • Da soldier

Working

✓

वीराङ्गना means 'a brave woman / heroine'. She was a heroic wet-nurse, but the word means 'heroine', not 'queen' or 'soldier'.

Q9

धाया (with long आ) must not be confused with धैर्य, which means:

  • Aa wet-nurse
  • Bpatience / courage✓
  • Ca bedroom
  • Da plot

Working

✓

धैर्य means 'patience / courage', distinct from धाया (wet-nurse). It is not a bedroom or a plot.

Q10

The difference between धाया and धैर्य shows that in Sanskrit:

  • Avowel length and quality (आ vs ऐ) distinguish words✓
  • Ball vowels sound the same
  • Cspelling never matters
  • Dwords have no genders

Working

✓

धाया vs धैर्य shows that vowel length and quality (आ vs ऐ) distinguish words. Vowels are not all the same, spelling does matter, and words do have genders.

✓
