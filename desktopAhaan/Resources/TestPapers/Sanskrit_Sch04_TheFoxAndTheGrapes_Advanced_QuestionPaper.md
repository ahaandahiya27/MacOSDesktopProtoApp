**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it drills prefixes (उपसर्ग: उत्- 'up' in उत्पतति, नि- 'down', आ- 'towards', प्र- 'forth') and how they change a root's meaning; the उत्-prefix vs the अ-past-augment (उत्पतति present vs उदपतत् past); the conditional चेत्/यदि ('if'); the तसिल् direction-adverbs in -तः (वामतः 'on the left', दक्षिणतः 'on the right'); and the wish-nouns पिपासा (from पा 'drink') vs बुभुक्षा (from भुज् 'eat'). Remember the moral is about the dishonest 'sour-grapes' EXCUSE, not the failed jump. Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
