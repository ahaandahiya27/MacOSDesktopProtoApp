**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. "Congruent" means same shape AND same size; "similar" means same shape, any size. The valid triangle congruence rules are SSS, SAS (the included angle), ASA (the included side) and RHS (for right triangles). AAA and SSA are NOT congruence rules. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
