Worked Solutions

1. (B) The space from 2 to 3 is split into 10 tenths; the 7th mark past 2 is 2 and 7 tenths = 2.7 cm. (2.07 would be 7 hundredths — the "put the digit in the wrong place" trap.)

2. (B) 34 tenths = 30 tenths + 4 tenths. Since 10 tenths = 1 whole, 30 tenths = 3 wholes. So 34/10 = 3 wholes and 4 tenths. (Reading the digits straight off as "4 wholes 3 tenths" reverses them — trap C.)

3. (C) There is no "oneths" place. Immediately right of the point is the TENTHS place, then hundredths, then thousandths.

4. (D) A unit holds 10 tenths and each tenth holds 10 hundredths, so a whole holds 10 × 10 = 100 hundredths. (10 is hundredths-in-a-tenth — the trap.)

5. (D) Half of 8 9/10 = 8.9 ÷ 2 = 4.45 units. It falls between 4.4 and 4.5, exactly at 4 and 45 hundredths. (4.5 is the careless "round up" answer.)

6. (B) In 47.305 the 3 is in the tenths place, so its value is 3 tenths = 0.3. (0.03 mistakes it for hundredths.)

7. (A) Compare from the left. Tenths digits: 0.4 → 4, 0.39 → 3, 0.198 → 1, 0.04 → 0. The largest tenths digit wins: 0.4. (Picking 0.39 because "more digits" is the classic length trap.)

8. (C) 7/100 means 7 hundredths = 0.07. (0.7 = 7 tenths = 70/100; 0.007 = 7 thousandths.)

9. (C) Pad to thousandths: 0.500, 0.450, 0.405, 0.054. Increasing: 0.054 < 0.405 < 0.45 < 0.5. So (C).

10. (A) Trailing zeros never change a value: 4.5 = 4.50 = 4.500 (all four and five tenths). In (B) the digits sit in different places (4.05, 4.005 differ); (C) has 0.05 ≠ 0.5; (D) mixes 0.45 in. So (A).

11. (D) Align points: 12.70 + 3.45 + 0.80. Hundredths: 0+5+0=5; tenths: 7+4+8=19 → write 9, carry 1; ones: 2+3+0+1=6; tens: 1. Total 16.95. (16.15 drops the tenths carry; 25.5 is a misalignment slip.)

12. (C) 2.5 − 0.85 − 1.2 = 2.50 − 0.85 = 1.65; 1.65 − 1.20 = 0.45 m. (0.55 mis-borrows; 1.45 forgets one cut.)

13. (B) 0.08 = 8/100. Divide top and bottom by 4: 2/25. (8/10 misreads the place; 1/8 = 0.125.)

14. (D) "4 ones, 0 tenths, 7 hundredths, 2 thousandths" = 4.072. Adding 0.5 (= 5 tenths): 4.072 + 0.500 = 4.572. (4.122 wrongly adds 0.5 into the hundredths; 9.072 adds 5 instead of 0.5.)

15. (D) 2.745 = 2 ones + 7 tenths + 4 hundredths + 5 thousandths = 2 + 7/10 + 4/100 + 5/1000. (Option A scrambles the tenths and thousandths digits.)

16. (A) 1.04 = 1 unit + 0 tenths + 4 hundredths, so it sits between 1.0 and 1.1 (just past 1, at the 4th hundredth-mark). It does NOT reach the 4th tenth-mark — that would be 1.4.

17. (C) Halfway between 0.30 and 0.40 is their average: (0.30 + 0.40) ÷ 2 = 0.70 ÷ 2 = 0.35. (0.34 is not the midpoint; 0.7 is the sum, not the midpoint.)

18. (A) 6.305 = 6 ones and 305 thousandths, read "six and three hundred five thousandths". (B) just names digits; (C) "thirty-five hundredths" = 0.35, wrong.

19. (C) Compare 0.6 and 0.59: tenths 6 vs 5, so 0.6 > 0.59. (0.6 = 0.60 > 0.59 by one hundredth.) The other statements are false.

20. (A) 2 7/10 + 3 6/10 = 2.7 + 3.6. Tenths 7+6 = 13 → 3 tenths, carry 1; ones 2+3+1 = 6. Total 6.3 units. (5.3 forgets the carry.)

21. (B) 7.2 − 3.45 = 7.20 − 3.45. Hundredths 0−5 borrow → 10−5=5; tenths 1−4 borrow → 11−4=7; ones 6−3=3. Result 3.75. (4.75 forgets a borrow.)

22. (C) 0.25 = 25/100 = 1/4. (1/2 = 0.5, 2/5 = 0.4, 1/25 = 0.04.)

23. (A) Pad to thousandths: 0.009, 0.100, 0.011, 0.020. Smallest is 0.009. (Picking 0.011 over 0.02 by "more digits" is the trap; comparing place by place, 0.009 < 0.011 < 0.02 < 0.1.)

24. (C) ₹96.726 → look at the third decimal (6 ≥ 5), so round the hundredths up: 96.73. (96.72 truncates instead of rounding; 96.7 rounds too far.)

25. (D) 5 − 3.6 = 5.0 − 3.6 = 1.4. Check: 3.6 + 1.4 = 5.0. ✓ (2.4 = 6 − 3.6 slip; 1.6 = 5 − 3.4 slip.)

26. (D) In 5.182 the 8 is in the hundredths place → 8 hundredths. (Tenths would be the 1; thousandths the 2.)

27. (B) Cost = 4.75 + 2.50 = 7.25. Change = 10.00 − 7.25 = 2.75. (3.25 = 10 − 6.75 slip; 2.25 mis-borrows.)

28. (C) 4.50, 4.500, 04.50 all equal 4.5 (trailing/leading zeros add nothing). But 4.05 = 4 and 5 hundredths ≠ 4.5. So the one NOT equal is (C).

29. (B) From 0.7 to 1.2 is a rise of 0.5; in steps of 0.1 that is 0.5 ÷ 0.1 = 5 steps (0.7→0.8→0.9→1.0→1.1→1.2). So 5.

30. (B) Largest is 0.66, smallest is 0.06. 0.66 − 0.06 = 0.60 = 0.6. (0.606 is not the extreme; 0.546 = 0.606 − 0.06 mis-picks the largest.)

31. (A) 0.45 + 1.2 + 0.8 = 0.45 + 1.20 + 0.80. Hundredths 5; tenths 4+2+8=14 → 4, carry 1; ones 0+1+0+1=2. Total 2.45 m. (2.05 drops the tenths carry.)

32. (B) 3/5 = 6/10 = 0.6. (0.35 reads the digits literally; 0.3 confuses 3/5 with 3/10.)

33. (B) Counting by hundredths, after 4.99 comes 4.99 + 0.01 = 5.00. (4.991 jumps to thousandths; 5.99 jumps by a whole.)

34. (A) "Add 0.3 + 0.4" → tenths 3 + 4 = 7 tenths = 0.7. (0.07 puts it in hundredths; the others misplace the digit.)

35. (D) Place value 4/100 means a digit worth 4 hundredths. In 2.745 the hundredths digit is 4. So the digit is 4.

36. (A) 1.5 − 0.75 = 1.50 − 0.75 = 0.75 L. Check: 0.75 + 0.75 = 1.50. ✓ (1.25 mis-borrows; 0.5 subtracts only 1.0.)

37. (A) 2.3 = 23 tenths (2 wholes = 20 tenths, plus 3 tenths). So 23. (5 = 2+3 careless; 230 = number of hundredths.)

38. (C) 1 1/4 = 1 + 0.25 = 1.25. (1.4 confuses 1/4 with 4 tenths; 1.14 places the 1 wrongly.)

39. (A) 5 − 0.5 − 0.05 = 5.00 − 0.50 = 4.50; 4.50 − 0.05 = 4.45. (4.5 forgets the hundredths subtraction; 4.95 subtracts only 0.05.)

40. (D) 2.5 m ÷ 5 = 0.5 m each. Check: 0.5 × 5 = 2.5. ✓ (0.05 misplaces the decimal; 5 forgets to divide.)

41. (A) Need a number with tenths digit 5 and more than 0.50: 0.56 (between 0.5 and 0.6). 0.49 < 0.5; 0.65 > 0.6; 0.06 far below. So (A).

42. (D) Going 0.2 → 0.02 → 0.002, the 2 shifts one place smaller each time, so each number is 10 times the one after it; equivalently 0.2 is 10 times larger than 0.02. So "10 times larger". (100 times confuses two shifts; "same size" is the zero-dilemma misconception.)

43. (B) Contents = full − empty = 8.1 − 3.25 = 8.10 − 3.25 = 4.85 kg. (5.85 / 5.15 are borrow slips; 11.35 adds instead of subtracts.)

44. (D) 0.405 = 405 thousandths = 405/1000. (45/1000 drops the 0; 405/100 = 4.05 wrong place; 4050/1000 = 4.05 too.)

45. (C) Distances to 3: |3.09−3| = 0.09, |2.85−3| = 0.15, |3.2−3| = 0.2, |2.7−3| = 0.3. Smallest is 0.09, so 3.09 is closest. (3.2 looks "just past 3" but 0.2 > 0.09.)

46. (B) 85.50 × 2 = 171.00. (Tenths/hundredths: 85.50 + 85.50 = 171.00.) (₹170.00 forgets the 50 paise twice; ₹170.50 doubles only the rupees.)

47. (D) Pad to thousandths and order: 0.002, 0.020, 0.200, 0.220 → ascending 0.002, 0.02, 0.2, 0.22. So (D). (Option A is descending-tail confusion.)

48. (C) Align: 75.345 + 86.691. Thousandths 5+1=6; hundredths 4+9=13 → 3 carry 1; tenths 3+6+1=10 → 0 carry 1; ones 5+6+1=12 → 2 carry 1; tens 7+8+1=16. Total 162.036. (161.936 mishandles a carry.)

49. (D) "3 hundredths extra" = 0.03 added to 0.70: 0.70 + 0.03 = 0.73 kg. (0.703 puts the 3 in thousandths; 0.37 swaps the digits.)

50. (C) 0.9 − 0.85 = 0.90 − 0.85 = 0.05. (0.15 mis-borrows; 0.5 ignores the hundredths.)

51. (D) A fraction terminates only if its denominator (in lowest terms) uses just the primes 2 and 5. 1/2 = 0.5, 1/4 = 0.25, 1/5 = 0.2 all terminate; but 1/3 = 0.333… never ends (3 is neither 2 nor 5). So (D).

52. (C) Pad to hundredths: 100.05, 99.90, 100.50. Compare whole parts: 100 > 99, so 99.9 is out. Between 100.05 and 100.50, tenths 0 vs 5 → 100.5 is larger. So (C).

53. (B) 6 tenths + 0 hundredths + 8 thousandths = 0.608. (0.68 drops the hundredths place and shifts the 8; 0.680 = 6 tenths 8 hundredths.)

54. (B) 1.35 + 1.6 = 1.35 + 1.60. Hundredths 5; tenths 3+6=9; ones 1+1=2. Total 2.95 m. (1.51 mis-aligns the ends instead of the points — the alignment trap.)

55. (C) The decimal point separates the whole (units and up) part from the fractional (tenths and down) part. (It is not between tens/units or tenths/hundredths.)

56. (A) Spent = 12.50 + 6.75 + 0.80 = 20.05. Left = 25.00 − 20.05 = 4.95. (5.05 mis-borrows; 4.05 drops a carry in the sum.)

57. (A) A number between 4.4 and 4.5 must keep 4.4… with the next digit 0–9: 4.45 fits (4.40 < 4.45 < 4.50). 4.04 and 4.54 are outside; 4.5 is the endpoint, not between. So (A).

58. (A) Greatest value: put the biggest digit before the point and arrange the rest in descending order → 5.30. (5.03 is smaller in the hundredths; 3.50 has a smaller whole part; 0.53 < 1.)

59. (B) 0.95 + 0.1 = 0.95 + 0.10 = 1.05. (0.96 adds only 0.01; 0.105 mis-shifts the place; 1.5 adds 0.55.)

60. (D) 0.125 + 0.875 + 0.5: 0.125 + 0.875 = 1.000; 1.000 + 0.5 = 1.5. (1.25 leaves the 0.5 as 0.05; 0.15 mis-aligns.)
