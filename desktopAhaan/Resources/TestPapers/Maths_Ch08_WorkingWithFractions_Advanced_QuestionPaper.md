**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. "of" means multiply; to divide by a fraction, flip the DIVISOR and multiply; turn mixed numbers into improper fractions first; and ALWAYS reduce the final answer. Remember: multiplying by a fraction below 1 makes a number smaller, dividing by it makes it bigger. Do brackets, then × and ÷, then + and −. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
