**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Rewrite every subtraction as "add the opposite", and track signs ruthlessly: subtracting a negative ADDS, two negatives MULTIPLY to a positive, and the sign of a long product is positive when the count of negative factors is even, negative when it is odd. Work brackets and × ÷ before + −. Watch the distractors — they are the answers you get from the most common single slip. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
