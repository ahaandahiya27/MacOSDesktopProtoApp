**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: confusing raiding-for-plunder (Mahmud, ~17 raids, withdrew each time) with conquering-to-govern (the Ghurids, Aibak's Sultanate); reversing the Tarain battles (Ghuri LOST the First, 1191; WON the Second, 1192); assuming all of India fell when the south (Cholas, Kakatiyas, Hoysalas) and much of the north stayed free; treating the age as 'only war' when al-Biruni, Bhaskaracharya, Ramanuja, Basava and great temples flourished; and forgetting al-Biruni's learning and India's sine-tables made it two-way. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
