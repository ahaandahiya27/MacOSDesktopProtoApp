**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it drills the infinitive (-तुम्, as in मारयितुम् 'to kill', गन्तुम् 'to go') and contrasts it with the past tense (अमारयत्); it splits the chapter's compounds (राजगृह = 'king's house' = palace; दुष्टबुद्धि = 'wicked-intellect'; सर्वस्वत्याग = 'giving-up of everything'); it tests the prefixes कु- ('bad') and सु- ('good'); and it asks you to reason about the history and the moral. Remember: Panna sacrificed her own SON (she herself survived); धाया (nurse) ≠ धैर्य (courage); दुष्ट (wicked) ≠ मूर्ख (foolish); राजगृह (a place) ≠ राजपुत्र (a person); the events are षोडशे शतके (16th century, the 1500s) in Mewar. Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
