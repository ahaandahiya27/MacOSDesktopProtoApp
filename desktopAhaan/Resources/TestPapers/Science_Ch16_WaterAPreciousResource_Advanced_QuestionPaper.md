**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: how little of Earth's water is usable fresh water, the water cycle, the water table and aquifers, what makes the table rise or fall, efficient irrigation and rainwater harvesting, and why every drop must be conserved. Reason from cause to effect. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
