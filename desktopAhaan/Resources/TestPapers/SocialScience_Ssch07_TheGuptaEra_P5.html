Instructions: Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Apex traps: fusing Chandragupta II (Gupta) with Chandragupta Maurya across a centuries-wide gap; reading the Iron Pillar as proof iron 'cannot rust' rather than as a phosphorus-rich passive-layer marvel; mis-crediting the Prayaga Prashasti or treating its court authorship as either flawless proof or worthless flattery instead of partial evidence to cross-check; collapsing the Gupta directly-ruled north-centre into a Mauryan whole-subcontinent model; mistaking 'Golden Age' for social equality when the chandalas were excluded; assuming contemporaries like the Pallavas, Kamarupa or Vakatakas were Gupta subordinates; and confusing which scholar or work belongs to which strand (math/astronomy vs poetry vs medicine vs metallurgy).
