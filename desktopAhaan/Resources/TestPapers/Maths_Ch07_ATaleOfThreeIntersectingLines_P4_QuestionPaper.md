## Chapter 7: A Tale of Three Intersecting Lines

**Paper 4 — (progressively harder)**

| | |
|---|---|

| **Total Questions** | 60 (Multiple Choice, single correct) |
| **Maximum Marks** | 240 |
| **Suggested Time** | 90 minutes |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. An exterior angle equals the SUM of the two remote interior angles (never the adjacent one); a sum of two sides must strictly EXCEED the third; 'acute' needs all three angles below 90°; and the largest angle always sits opposite the longest side. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
