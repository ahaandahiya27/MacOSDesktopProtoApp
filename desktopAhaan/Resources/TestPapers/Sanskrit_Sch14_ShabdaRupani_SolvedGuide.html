Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

How many विभक्ति (cases) does Sanskrit have?

  • Asix
  • Bseven✓
  • Ceight
  • Dthree

Working

✓

Sanskrit has seven cases (विभक्ति). With the vocative counted separately there are eight noun-forms; six is the Latin count.

Q2

Counting the vocative (सम्बोधन) separately, how many noun-forms are there in all?

  • Asix
  • Bseven
  • Cten
  • Deight✓

Working

✓

Seven cases plus the vocative make eight forms. Six is Latin's count, seven omits the vocative, and ten is too many.

Q3

सप्तविभक्तयः means:

  • Athe seven vowels
  • Bthe seven days
  • Cthe seven cases✓
  • Dthe seven sages

Working

✓

सप्तविभक्तयः means 'the seven cases'. Not the vowels, days, or sages.

Q4

The seven cases run from प्रथमा to:

  • Aचतुर्थी
  • Bसप्तमी✓
  • Cपञ्चमी
  • Dअष्टमी

Working

✓

The seven cases run from प्रथमा (1st) to सप्तमी (7th). चतुर्थी and पञ्चमी are in between, and there is no अष्टमी case.

Q5

Each noun is declined across the seven cases and how many numbers?

  • Atwo
  • Bone
  • Cseven
  • Dthree (singular, dual, plural)✓

Working

✓

Each noun is declined in three numbers - singular, dual and plural. Not two, one, or seven.

Q6

The vocative (सम्बोधन) is usually:

  • Athe first case
  • Bnot used at all
  • Cshown alongside the cases but counted separately✓
  • Dthe same as the genitive

Working

✓

The vocative is shown alongside the seven cases but counted separately. It is not the first case, unused, or the genitive.

Q7

प्रथमा (the nominative) marks the:

  • Asubject (the doer)✓
  • Bobject
  • C'by' (the means)
  • D'of' (possession)

Working

✓

प्रथमा marks the subject. The object is द्वितीया, the means तृतीया, and possession षष्ठी.

Q8

द्वितीया (the accusative) marks the:

  • Aobject (what the action affects)✓
  • Bsubject
  • C'from'
  • D'in / on'

Working

✓

द्वितीया marks the object. The subject is प्रथमा, 'from' पञ्चमी, and 'in/on' सप्तमी.

Q9

तृतीया (the instrumental) means:

  • A'to / for'
  • B'by / with' (the means)✓
  • C'of'
  • D'in / on'

Working

✓

तृतीया means 'by / with' (the means). 'To/for' is चतुर्थी, 'of' षष्ठी, and 'in/on' सप्तमी.

Q10

The case meaning 'by/with' (the means) is the:

  • Aप्रथमा (nominative)
  • Bषष्ठी (genitive)
  • Cसप्तमी (locative)
  • Dतृतीया (instrumental)✓

Working

✓

'By/with' (the means) is the तृतीया (instrumental). The others are nominative, genitive and locative.

✓

पञ्चमी means 'from'. 'To/for' is चतुर्थी, 'of' षष्ठी, and 'in/on' सप्तमी.

षष्ठी (genitive) means 'of', and सप्तमी (locative) means:

  • A'by'
  • B'in / on'✓
  • C'to'

Working

✓

सप्तमी (locative) means 'in / on'. 'By' is तृतीया, 'to' चतुर्थी, and 'from' पञ्चमी.

Q14

The nominative singular of देव is:

  • Cदेवः✓
  • Dदेवाय

Working

✓

The nominative singular of देव is देवः. देवम् is accusative, देवेन instrumental, and देवाय dative.

Q15

The accusative singular of देव is:

  • Aदेवम्✓
  • Bदेवः
  • Cदेवात्
  • Dदेवे

Working

✓

The accusative singular is देवम्. देवः is nominative, देवात् ablative, and देवे locative.

Q16

The instrumental singular of देव is:

  • Aदेवाय
  • Bदेवस्य
  • Cदेवः
  • Dदेवेन✓

Working

✓

The instrumental singular is देवेन ('by the god'). देवाय is dative, देवस्य genitive, and देवः nominative.

Q17

The dative singular of देव is:

  • Aदेवाय✓
  • Bदेवात्
  • Cदेवेन
  • Dदेवम्

Working

✓

The dative singular is देवाय ('to/for the god'). देवात् is ablative, देवेन instrumental, and देवम् accusative.

Q18

The ablative singular of देव is:

  • Aदेवाय
  • Bदेवस्य
  • Cदेवे
  • Dदेवात्✓

Working

✓

The ablative singular is देवात् ('from the god'). देवाय is dative, देवस्य genitive, and देवे locative.

Q19

The genitive singular of देव is:

  • Aदेवाय
  • Bदेवात्
  • Cदेवेन
  • Dदेवस्य✓

Working

✓

The genitive singular is देवस्य ('of the god'). देवाय is dative, देवात् ablative, and देवेन instrumental.

Q20

The locative singular of देव is:

  • Aदेवः
  • Bदेवे✓
  • Cदेवम्
  • Dदेवस्य

Working

✓

The locative singular is देवे ('in/on the god'). देवः is nominative, देवम् accusative, and देवस्य genitive.

Set 3 · Q21–Q30

Q21

The vocative singular of देव is:

  • Aहे देवः!
  • Bहे देवाय!
  • Cहे देव!✓
  • Dहे देवस्य!

Working

✓

The vocative singular drops the -ः: हे देव!. हे देवः! keeps the nominative ending, and the others are dative/genitive forms.

Q22

Sanskrit's three numbers are:

  • Asingular and plural only
  • Bmasculine, feminine, neuter
  • Cpast, present, future
  • Dsingular, dual and plural (एक/द्वि/बहु-वचन)✓

Working

✓

The three numbers are singular, dual and plural. Not two numbers, the genders, or the tenses.

Q23

The द्विवचन (dual) is used for:

  • Aexactly two✓
  • Bthree or more
  • Cone only
  • Dany number

Working

✓

The dual is for exactly two. Three or more take the plural, and one the singular.

Q24

देवौ means:

  • Athe two gods (dual)✓
  • Bthe gods (plural)
  • Ca god (singular)
  • DO god! (vocative)

Working

✓

देवौ is the dual, 'the two gods'. The plural is देवाः, the singular देवः, and the vocative हे देव!.

Q25

For 'a few gods' (three or more), the correct number is:

  • Aद्विवचन (dual)
  • Bबहुवचन (plural)✓
  • Cएकवचन (singular)
  • Dthe vocative

Working

✓

Three or more take the बहुवचन (plural). The dual is for two, the singular for one.

Q26

English has lost the dual, but Sanskrit keeps it for:

  • Apairs and exactly-two contexts (eyes, hands, two brothers)✓
  • Ball groups of people
  • Csingle objects
  • Dpast events

Working

✓

Sanskrit's dual is for pairs and exactly-two contexts. It is not for all groups, single objects, or tenses.

Q27

Using देवौ for 'many gods' is wrong because देवौ is:

  • Asingular
  • Bplural
  • Cdual (exactly two), not plural✓
  • Dvocative

Working

✓

देवौ is dual (exactly two), so it cannot mean 'many'. It is not singular, plural, or vocative.

Q28

कवि means 'poet'. Its nominative plural is:

  • Aकवयः✓
  • Bदेवाः
  • Cकविम्
  • Dकविना

Working

✓

The nominative plural of कवि is कवयः. देवाः is the अकारान्त plural, कविम् accusative singular, and कविना instrumental singular.

Q29

The इकारान्त nominative-plural ending -अयः appears in:

  • Aकवयः, हरयः, मुनयः✓
  • Bदेवाः, रामाः
  • Cगुरवः
  • Dदेवान्

Working

✓

The -अयः plural appears in कवयः, हरयः, मुनयः (इकारान्त). देवाः/रामाः are अकारान्त, गुरवः उकारान्त, and देवान् is accusative plural.

Q30

कवयः differs from देवाः because they belong to different:

  • Adeclension classes (इकारान्त vs अकारान्त)✓
  • Btenses
  • Cgenders
  • Dnumbers

Working

✓

कवयः and देवाः differ by declension class (इकारान्त vs अकारान्त), not tense, gender, or number (both are nominative plural).

Set 4 · Q31–Q40

Q31

हरि ('Hari') and मुनि ('sage') decline like:

  • Aदेव (अकारान्त)
  • Bगुरु (उकारान्त)
  • Cnone of them
  • Dकवि (इकारान्त)✓

Working

✓

हरि and मुनि are इकारान्त, declining like कवि. They do not follow देव (अकारान्त) or गुरु (उकारान्त).

Q32

गुरु belongs to which declension class?

  • Aअकारान्त (ending in अ)
  • Bइकारान्त (ending in इ)
  • Cfeminine
  • Dउकारान्त (ending in उ)✓

Working

✓

गुरु is उकारान्त (ending in उ). देव is अकारान्त, कवि इकारान्त; गुरु here is masculine, not feminine.

Q33

The vocative singular of गुरु is:

  • Aहे गुरुः!
  • Bहे गुरुम्!
  • Cहे गुरवे!
  • Dहे गुरो!✓

Working

✓

The vocative singular of गुरु is हे गुरो!. हे गुरुः! keeps the nominative, हे गुरुम्! is accusative-like, and हे गुरवे! dative-like.

Q34

Forming हे गुरो! from गुरु, the ending -ुः shifts to:

  • A-आय
  • B-स्य
  • C-ओ✓
  • D-एन

Working

✓

The उकारान्त vocative shifts -ुः to -ओ (हे गुरो!). -आय is dative, -स्य genitive, and -एन instrumental.

Q35

Like हे गुरो!, the vocative of साधु is:

  • Aहे साधुः!
  • Bहे साधो!✓
  • Cहे साधाय!
  • Dहे साधुना!

Working

✓

साधु, like गुरु, has the vocative हे साधो!. हे साधुः! keeps the nominative, and the others are dative/instrumental-like.

Q36

विष्णु, like गुरु, has the vocative singular:

  • Aहे विष्णुः!
  • Bहे विष्णवे!
  • Cहे विष्णो!✓
  • Dहे विष्णुम्!

Working

✓

विष्णु has the vocative हे विष्णो!. हे विष्णुः! keeps the nominative, हे विष्णवे! is dative-like, and हे विष्णुम्! accusative-like.

Q37

The vocative singular of an अकारान्त noun like देव is formed by:

  • Aadding -ओ
  • Bchanging to -ए
  • Cdropping the -ः (हे देव!)✓
  • Dadding -स्य

Working

✓

For अकारान्त the vocative drops the -ः (हे देव!). -ओ is for उकारान्त, -ए for इकारान्त, and -स्य is the genitive.

Q38

The vocative singular of an उकारान्त noun like गुरु is formed by:

  • Adropping the -ः only
  • Bchanging -ुः to -ओ (हे गुरो!)✓
  • Cchanging to -ए
  • Dadding -आय

Working

✓

For उकारान्त the vocative changes -ुः to -ओ (हे गुरो!). Dropping -ः is अकारान्त, -ए इकारान्त, and -आय the dative.

Q39

The vocative singular of an इकारान्त noun like कवि is:

  • Aहे कविः!
  • Bहे कवो!
  • Cहे कवे! (-एः to -ए)✓
  • Dहे कवाय!

Working

✓

For इकारान्त the vocative is हे कवे!. हे कविः! keeps the nominative, हे कवो! is उकारान्त-like, and हे कवाय! dative-like.

Q40

The claim 'the vocative always looks exactly like the nominative' is:

  • Aalways true
  • Bfalse - it changes (हे देव!, हे गुरो!, हे कवे!)✓
  • Ctrue only for plurals
  • Dtrue only for the dual

Working

✓

It is false: the vocative changes (हे देव!, हे गुरो!, हे कवे!). It is not always like the nominative, nor only for plurals or the dual.

Q41

The vocative case is used to:

  • Ashow possession
  • Bshow the means
  • Ccall or address someone ('O ...!')✓
  • Dshow the object

Working

✓

The vocative calls or addresses someone ('O ...!'). Possession is genitive, the means instrumental, and the object accusative.

Q42

कवि and हरि are इकारान्त. देव/राम are अकारान्त and गुरु/शम्भु उकारान्त.

Working

✓

Q47
