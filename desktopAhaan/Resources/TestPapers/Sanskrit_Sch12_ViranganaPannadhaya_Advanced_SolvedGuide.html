Q6

  • A'he did X' (past)
  • B'let him X' (command)
  • C'he will X' (future)
  • D'to X' (purpose)✓

Working

✓

The infinitive translates as **'to X'** and usually expresses purpose ('in order to ...').

Q7

  • Apurpose ('in order to kill')✓
  • Bsuccess
  • Cpast failure
  • Dname

Working

✓

मारयितुम् expresses the assassin's **purpose** — he came 'in order to kill' — not that he succeeded.

Q8

  • Bमारयति
  • Cकर्तुम्✓

Working

✓

**कर्तुम्** ('to do') is the infinitive; अमारयत्, मारयति and करोति are all finite verbs.

Q9

  • Cमारयतु
  • Dमारयेत्

Working

✓

'To kill' (infinitive) is **मारयितुम्** (matching the past अमारयत् 'he killed').

Q10

अमारयत् and मारयितुम् differ because the first is a ____ and the second an ____.

  • Ainfinitive ... past tense
  • Bnoun ... verb
  • Cfinite past tense ... infinitive✓
  • Dfuture ... command

Working

✓

अमारयत् is a **finite past tense** ('he killed'); मारयितुम् is the **infinitive** ('to kill').
