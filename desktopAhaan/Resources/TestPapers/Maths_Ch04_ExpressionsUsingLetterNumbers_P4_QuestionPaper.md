# Olympiad Test Paper — Mathematics (Class 7) — Paper 4
## Chapter 4: Expressions Using Letter-Numbers

**Paper 4 — (progressively harder)**

|---|---|

| **Total Questions** | 60 (Multiple Choice, single correct) |
| **Maximum Marks** | 240 |
| **Suggested Time** | 90 minutes |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch order of operations under awkward substitutions, never merge unlike terms or drop a constant, distribute a coefficient over every inside term (including subtraction signs), and generalise a pattern from its actual differences rather than guessing. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. A photo studio charges a sitting fee of 90 rupees that already includes the very first print, then 24 rupees for every print after the first. If a customer orders p prints (p ≥ 1), the total charge in rupees is:
   (A) 24p + 90
   (B) 90(p − 1) + 24
   (C) 24(p − 1) + 90
   (D) 114p

2. Evaluate 2a² − ab − b² when a = −3 and b = 2.
   (A) 20
   (B) 8
   (C) 32
   (D) −4

3. Simplify 3(2x − 5y) − 2(4x − 3y) + y.
   (A) −2x − 14y
   (B) −2x − 8y
   (C) 2x − 8y
   (D) −2x + y

4. Which expression is EQUIVALENT to 18 − 12m?
