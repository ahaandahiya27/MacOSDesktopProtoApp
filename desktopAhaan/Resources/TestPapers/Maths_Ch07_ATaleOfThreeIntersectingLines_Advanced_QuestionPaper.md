**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Your main tools are the angle sum (180°), the exterior-angle theorem (an exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles), and the triangle inequality (any two sides sum to MORE than the third). A triangle is acute only if ALL three angles are under 90°. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
