**1. (C)** In श्लोक + अन्त्य + अक्षर + ई, अन्त्य means **last / final**.

**2. (A)** अक्षर means **a letter / syllable**.

**3. (D)** So श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी names **the last-letter shloka game**.

**4. (B)** The final -ई turns the phrase into a feminine **action / game** name.

**5. (B)** क्रीडाम वयम् ('let us play') is first-person plural in the **imperative/hortative** ('let us ...').

**6. (D)** विद्याहीन = विद्या + हीन = **'knowledge-lacking' (unlearned)**.

**7. (A)** -हीन means **'lacking / without'**.

**8. (C)** -हीन is the opposite of the possessive suffix **-वत्/-मत् ('possessing')**.

**9. (A)** धनहीन (धन + हीन) means **poor** (wealth-lacking).

**10. (C)** निर्गन्ध = निर् + गन्ध = **'scent-less / without fragrance'**.

**11. (D)** The prefix निर्- (निस्-) generally means **'without / -less'**.

**12. (B)** निर्जल (निर् + जल) means **waterless**.

**13. (D)** निर्भय (निर् + भय) means **fearless**.

**14. (B)** विद्याहीन (-हीन) and निर्गन्ध (निर्-) both express **lacking / being without something**.

**15. (C)** किंशुक is **the Flame of the Forest tree (Butea monosperma)**.

**16. (A)** The किंशुक flower is brilliantly coloured but **scent-less (निर्गन्ध)**.

**17. (C)** The unlearned do not shine, like **निर्गन्धाः किंशुकाः (scent-less Flame-of-Forest flowers)**.

**18. (A)** The simile maps 'colour without scent' onto **good looks without the worth that knowledge brings**.

**19. (B)** The किंशुक is the **उपमान** (the standard the unlearned person is compared TO).

**20. (D)** विवादाय (dative) means **'for quarrel'**.

**21. (A)** The -आय ending marks the **dative** (here of purpose: 'for ...').

**22. (D)** दानाय means **'for giving'**.

**23. (B)** रक्षणाय means **'for protection'**.

**24. (C)** सेवा (ā-stem fem) has the dative **सेवायै** ('for service').

**25. (B)** In the wicked, विद्या/धन/शक्ति turn to **quarrel, pride and oppression (विवाद, मद, परिपीडन)**.

**26. (C)** In the good, they turn to **sharing-of-learning, giving (दान) and protection (रक्षण)**.

**27. (A)** The structure is summed up by **खलस्य साधोः विपरीतम्** ('for the wicked and the good, it is opposite').

**28. (D)** विपरीतम् means **opposite / reversed**.

**29. (D)** The worth of the gifts depends on **the character (शीलम्) of the holder**.

**30. (A)** शीलम् means **character / conduct**.

**31. (C)** खल means **a wicked person**.

**32. (B)** मूर्ख means **foolish / stupid** (a fault of intellect, not morals).

**33. (C)** साधु means **a good / virtuous person**.

**34. (B)** The opposite of खल in the verse is **साधु**.

**35. (D)** खलस्य and साधोः are both in the **genitive** ('of').

**36. (A)** परिपीडन means **oppression / tormenting**.

**37. (A)** मद means **pride / arrogance**.

**38. (B)** विवाद means **quarrel / dispute**.

**42. (C)** The same विद्या leading to विवाद OR sharing shows the outcome **depends on the user's character**.

**46. (D)** The correct elements: **निर्- = 'without'; -हीन = 'lacking'**.

**47. (C)** The correct cases: **दानाय = 'for giving' (dative); खलस्य = 'of the wicked' (genitive)**.

**48. (A)** The correct character-words: **खल = wicked; साधु = good** (मूर्ख is 'foolish').

**53. (C)** In the wicked, wealth turns from दान to **मद (pride)**.

**54. (D)** विद्याहीनाः न शोभन्ते teaches that **the unlearned do not truly shine**.

**55. (B)** शीलम् (character) — not birth or looks — gives a person **real worth**.
