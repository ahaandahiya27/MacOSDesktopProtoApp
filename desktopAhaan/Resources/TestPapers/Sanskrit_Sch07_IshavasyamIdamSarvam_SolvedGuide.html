Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

ईशावास्यम् इदं सर्वम् means:

  • Athe Lord is only in temples
  • Bnothing is divine
  • Call this is pervaded by the Lord✓
  • Dthe world has no creator

Working

✓

It means 'all this is pervaded by the Lord'. It does not confine God to temples, deny the divine, or deny a creator.

Q2

ईशावास्यम् is glossed as ईश्वरेण व्याप्तम्, meaning:

  • Apervaded by the Lord✓
  • Bruled by a king
  • Cmade of gold
  • Dwithout God

Working

✓

ईशावास्यम् = ईश्वरेण व्याप्तम् = 'pervaded by the Lord'. Not ruled by a king, made of gold, or godless.

Q3

इदं सर्वम् in the verse means:

  • Athis one thing
  • Bnothing at all
  • Conly the temple
  • Dall this (the whole world)✓

Working

✓

इदं सर्वम् means 'all this' (the whole world). Not one thing, nothing, or only the temple.

Q4

The verse's central claim is that God:

  • Aexists only in the sky
  • Bpervades everything, everywhere✓
  • Cis found only in idols
  • Ddoes not exist

Working

✓

The claim is that God pervades everything, everywhere. It is not sky-only, idol-only, or a denial of God.

Q5

  • ARamayana
  • BArthashastra

✓

It opens the Ishopanishad. It is not the Ramayana, the Arthashastra, or a fox fable.

Q6

  • Areverence and care✓
  • Bcontempt
  • Cgreed
  • Dindifference

Working

✓

If the Lord pervades all, one should treat the world with reverence and care - not contempt, greed, or indifference.

Q7

ईश here means:

  • Athe Lord (the Supreme)✓
  • Bthe English suffix '-ish'
  • Ca fox
  • Da flower

Working

✓

ईश means 'the Lord'. It has nothing to do with English '-ish', and is not a fox or flower.

Q8

ईश comes from the root ईश्, meaning:

  • B'to drink'
  • C'to sleep'
  • D'to rule, to own'✓

Working

✓

ईश is from the root ईश् ('to rule, to own'). It does not mean eat, drink, or sleep.

Q9

  • Aकिंशुक
  • Bशृगालः
  • Cईश्वर ('Lord')✓
  • Dघटः

Working

✓

ईश्वर ('Lord') is a derivative of ईश. किंशुक (a flower), शृगालः (a fox) and घटः (a pot) are unrelated.

Q10

Treating ईश like the English '-ish' suffix is wrong because ईश is:

  • Aan English ending
  • Ba Sanskrit word meaning 'Lord', from the root ईश्✓

✓

ईश is a Sanskrit word for 'Lord' (root ईश्), not an English suffix, a fox, or a number.

Q19

सर्वशक्तिमान् breaks into सर्व + शक्ति + -मत्/-मान्, meaning:

  • A'having no power'
  • B'one power only'
  • C'possessing all power'✓
  • D'all knowledge'

Working

✓

सर्व ('all') + शक्ति ('power') + -मत्/-मान् ('possessing') = 'possessing all power'. The other readings are wrong.

Q20

The suffix -मत्/-वत् (as in -मान्) means:

  • A'lacking'
  • B'like'
  • C'to / for'
  • D'possessing'✓

Working

✓

The -मत्/-वत् suffix means 'possessing'. 'Like' is -वत् in a different sense (समासे); 'lacking' and 'to/for' are wrong here.
