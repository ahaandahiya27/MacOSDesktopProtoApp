Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

A सुभाषितम् is:

  • Aa long novel
  • Ba mountain
  • Can enemy
  • Da wise, well-spoken saying✓

Working

✓

सुभाषितम् is a wise / well-spoken saying. It is not a novel, a mountain, or an enemy.

Q2

सुभाषितम् breaks into सु + भाषितम्, meaning:

  • A'bad' + 'spoken'
  • B'well' + 'spoken' = well-spoken (saying)✓
  • C'six' + 'fault'
  • D'water' + 'drop'

Working

✓

सु ('well') + भाषितम् ('spoken') = a well-spoken saying. The other splits are wrong.

Q3

The grammatical gender of सुभाषितम् is:

  • Aneuter (नपुंसकलिङ्ग), declining like ज्ञानम्✓
  • Bfeminine
  • Cmasculine
  • Dit has no gender

Working

✓

सुभाषितम् is neuter (ends in -अम्, declines like ज्ञानम्). Sanskrit gender is grammatical; this word is neuter, not feminine or masculine.

Q4

The plural of सुभाषितम् is:

  • Aसुभाषितानि✓
  • Bसुभाषिताः
  • Cसुभाषिता
  • Dसुभाषितम्

Working

✓

Being neuter, सुभाषितम् has the plural सुभाषितानि. सुभाषिताः would be a masculine plural; the others are singular or stem forms.

Q5

Saying सुभाषिताः for the plural is wrong because सुभाषितम् is:

  • Afeminine
  • Ba verb
  • Cneuter, so its plural is सुभाषितानि✓
  • Dsingular only

Working

✓

सुभाषितम् is neuter, so its plural is सुभाषितानि, not the masculine-looking सुभाषिताः. It is not feminine, a verb, or singular-only.

Q6

By how many 'va' (व) qualities is a person honoured?

  • Afive✓
  • Bsix
  • Cthree
  • Dten

Working

✓

A person is honoured by five 'va' qualities (पञ्चभिः वकारैः). It is not six, three, or ten.

Q7

The five 'va' qualities are listed in the ____ case (the means of being honoured).

  • Anominative
  • Binstrumental✓
  • Caccusative
  • Dvocative

Working

✓

The qualities are the means of being honoured, so they take the instrumental (विद्यया, वपुषा, etc.). Not nominative, accusative, or vocative.

Q8

Which list correctly names the five 'va' qualities?

  • Aनिद्रा, तन्द्रा, भयम्, क्रोधः, आलस्यम्
  • Bजल, सत्य, तपस्, ज्ञान, घट
  • Cचैत्र, वैशाख, ज्येष्ठ, आषाढ, श्रावण
  • Dविद्या, वपुस्, वाणी, वस्त्र, विनय (knowledge, body, speech, dress, humility)✓

Working

✓

The five va-qualities are knowledge, body, speech, dress and humility. The second list is the six faults, the third the purification means, and the fourth months.

Q9

All five honouring qualities share the feature that they:

  • Abegin with the letter स
  • Bare all faults to give up
  • Care all rivers
  • Dbegin with the letter व✓

Working

✓

All five begin with व (विद्या, वपुस्, वाणी, वस्त्र, विनय). They do not start with स, are not faults, and are not rivers.

Q10

विद्यया, one of the five va-qualities, means 'by ____'.

  • Asleep
  • Bfear
  • Cknowledge✓
  • Danger

Working

✓

विद्यया means 'by knowledge'. Sleep, fear and anger are among the faults, not the honouring qualities.

Q22

आलस्यम् and दीर्घसूत्रता are:

  • Athe same single fault
  • Bboth meaning 'fear'
  • Ctwo different faults - general laziness vs delaying specific tasks✓

Working

They are two distinct faults: आलस्यम् is general laziness, दीर्घसूत्रता is procrastination (delaying specific tasks). They are not the same, nor mean fear or sleep.

✓

दीर्घसूत्रता ('long-thread-ness') is procrastination. General laziness is आलस्यम्, long life दीर्घायुष्, and anger क्रोधः.

Q24

  • Aprocrastination of specific tasks

Working

✓

आलस्यम् is general laziness. Procrastination is दीर्घसूत्रता, fear भयम्, and drowsiness तन्द्रा.
