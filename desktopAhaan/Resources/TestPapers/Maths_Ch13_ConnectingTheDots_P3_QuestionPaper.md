# Olympiad Test Paper — Mathematics (Class 7) — Paper 3
## Chapter 13: Connecting the Dots

**Paper 3 — (progressively harder)**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | Mathematics (NCERT Class 7) |
| **Chapter** | 13 — Connecting the Dots |
| **Total Questions** | 60 (Multiple Choice, single correct) |
| **Maximum Marks** | 240 |
| **Suggested Time** | 90 minutes |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Compare per-item means not raw totals when group sizes differ, always sort before taking a median, and keep mean, median and mode distinct — an outlier shifts the mean far more than the median. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. A student's six test scores have a mean of 72. After a seventh test the mean rises to 74. What did the student score in the seventh test?
   (A) 74
   (B) 76
   (C) 86
   (D) 2

2. The mean age of a family of 5 is 28 years. The mean age of the same family 4 years later (everyone alive) will be:
   (A) 28 years
   (B) 48 years
   (C) 32 years
   (D) 30 years

3. A list of seven numbers is 4, 4, 6, 7, 9, 9, 9. After one more 4 is added, what is the mode of the eight numbers?
   (A) 4 only
   (B) 9 only
   (C) There are now two modes, 4 and 9 (each appears three times)
   (D) 7, the middle value

4. A data set is 3, 5, 6, 8, 10. The value 10 is replaced by 50. How do the mean and the median change?
   (A) Both the mean and the median increase by 8
   (B) The mean increases by 8 but the median stays at 6
   (C) The median increases but the mean stays the same
   (D) Neither changes because only one value moved

5. The mean of 12 numbers is 9. The mean of the first 7 of them is 8 and the mean of the last 4 is 11. What is the remaining (8th when counted) number — the one not in either group?
