**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Reason from *cause* to *effect* — the five climate factors (latitude, altitude, distance from the sea, winds, topography) and how unequal heating of land and sea drives the monsoon. Watch the traps: weather is hourly, a season returns yearly, climate is the decades-long pattern; the *windward* slope is wet and the *leeward* rain-shadow dry; the *southwest* monsoon brings the summer rains while the *northeast* monsoon waters the Tamil coast in winter. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
