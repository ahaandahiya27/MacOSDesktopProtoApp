**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it tests the *chemistry* of fibres (protein vs cellulose), the full sequence of wool processing, the silkworm life cycle and sericulture, and the trade-offs between natural and synthetic fibres. Read every option — the distractors are designed to catch a half-remembered fact. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
