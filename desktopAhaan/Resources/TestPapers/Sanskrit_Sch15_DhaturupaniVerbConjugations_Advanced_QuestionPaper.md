**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes beyond Paper 1: it asks for the FULL nine-cell present (लट्) table across all three persons and numbers, the ACTUAL forms of the other लकाराः (लङ् past, लृट् future, लोट् imperative, विधिलिङ् optative), the class-specific stems of दा, ज्ञा, क्री and श्रु, and multi-step subject–verb agreement, tense-spotting and error-correction. Remember: प्रथमपुरुष = English THIRD person; उत्तमपुरुष = English FIRST; parasmaipada ends -ति, atmanepada ends -ते; the अ- augment in अपठत्/अकरोत् marks the past (लङ्), it is NOT the negative अ-.
