# Sanskrit — Chapter 8: हितं मनोहारि च दुर्लभं वचः · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** च joins the two qualities, so the rare speech must be **beneficial AND pleasing together**.

**2. (A)** With वा the sense would weaken to **'beneficial OR pleasing'** — an easier standard.

**3. (D)** वा means **'or'**.

**4. (B)** तु means **'but / however'**.

**5. (B)** एव means **'only / indeed'** (emphasis).

**6. (D)** रामः एव गच्छति means **'Rama only / it is Rama who goes'**.

**7. (A)** च demands **two qualities at once, which is harder than either alone** — hence दुर्लभ.

**8. (C)** दुर्लभ = दुर् + लभ ('obtain') = **hard to obtain (rare)**.

**9. (A)** The prefix दुर्- means **'hard / bad / difficult'**.

**10. (C)** The सु- opposite of दुर्लभ is **सुलभ (easy to get)**.

**11. (D)** दुर्बल (दुर् + बल) means **weak** (poor in strength).

**12. (B)** दुर्गम (दुर् + गम) means **hard to traverse / inaccessible**.

**13. (D)** अशक्य means **impossible** (whereas दुर्लभ is rare but possible).

**14. (B)** Calling it दुर्लभ (not अशक्य) tells the student to **keep striving, since it is rare but attainable**.

**15. (C)** Knowledge should be gathered क्षणशः — **moment by moment**.

**16. (A)** Wealth should be gathered कणशः — **particle by particle**.

**17. (C)** The -शस् suffix forms adverbs meaning **'by units of / one X at a time'**.

**18. (A)** शतशः (शत + शस्) means **'by hundreds'**.

**19. (B)** सहस्रशः means **'by thousands'**.

**20. (D)** एकशः means **'one by one'**.

**21. (A)** क्षणशः and कणशः are **two adverbs (indeclinables), one per noun**.

**22. (D)** क्षणे नष्टे कुतो विद्या means **'if the moment is wasted, where is knowledge?'**.

**23. (B)** कणे नष्टे कुतो धनम् makes the parallel point about **wasting each small particle and losing wealth**.

**24. (C)** Together the lines teach that knowledge and wealth are built by **patient, incremental effort**.

**25. (B)** क्रियावान् = क्रिया + -वत्/-वान् = **'action-possessing' = a doer**.

**26. (C)** धन + -वत् gives **धनवान् ('wealthy')**.

**27. (A)** बुद्धि + -मत् gives **बुद्धिमान् ('intelligent')**.

**28. (D)** बल + -वत् gives **बलवान् ('strong')**.

**29. (D)** बुद्धि takes the **-मान्** form (बुद्धिमान्), since the -वान् form follows अ/आ-ending bases.

**30. (A)** गुण + suffix gives **गुणवान् ('virtuous')**.

**31. (C)** The verse contrasts क्रियावान् with विकत्थन, the **boaster**.

**32. (B)** The chapter prefers the **doer (one of action)** over the boaster.

**33. (C)** विकत्थन means **boasting / a boaster**.

**34. (B)** 'Prefer the doer to the boaster' values **deeds over empty words**.

**35. (D)** हितम् and मनोहारि describe speech that **helps the listener / wins the heart**, respectively.

**36. (A)** मनोहारि (मनस् + हारि) literally means **'heart-stealing' (captivating)**.

**37. (A)** सूक्ति (सु + उक्ति) means **a good / well-spoken saying**.

**38. (B)** सूक्ष्म means **subtle / fine** — not to be confused with सूक्ति.

**39. (D)** A सुभाषितम् (सु + भाषित) is a **'well-spoken' wise saying**.

**40. (C)** हितम्-but-harsh speech lacks **the pleasing quality** (मनोहारि).

**41. (D)** मनोहारि-but-harmful speech lacks **the beneficial quality** (हितम्).

**42. (C)** Demanding both qualities makes the ideal **rare (दुर्लभ)**.

**43. (A)** विद्यां च धनं च means **'knowledge AND wealth (both)'**.

**44. (B)** 'Tea OR coffee' uses **वा**.

**45. (B)** क्षणशः tells **how (in what increments)** the gathering is done.

**46. (D)** The correct pair is **क्रियावान् = a doer**. दुर्लभ = rare, विकत्थन = boaster, मनोहारि = pleasing.

**47. (C)** The correct particles: **च = 'and'; वा = 'or'**.

**48. (A)** The correct forms: **धन → धनवान्; बुद्धि → बुद्धिमान्**.

**49. (A)** The correct adverbs: **क्षणशः = moment by moment; कणशः = particle by particle**.

**50. (D)** The correct prefixes: **दुर्- = 'hard/bad'; सु- = 'easy/good'**.

**51. (C)** Using दुर्लभ (not अशक्य) keeps the goal **attainable though rare**.

**52. (B)** 'Knowledge grows क्षणशः' warns most against **wasting time / each moment**.

**53. (C)** Studying a little every day follows the principle of **क्षणशः (moment by moment)**.

**54. (D)** Both contrasts share the theme that **substance (good deeds, good-and-kind words) beats empty show**.

**55. (B)** बलवान् बुद्धिमान् च means a person who is **strong and intelligent (both)**.

**56. (A)** -वत्/-मत् ('possessing') is the opposite of -हीन, which means **'lacking / without'**.

**57. (B)** The chapter is a set of सुभाषितानि on **speech, effort and action**.

**58. (A)** The best summary: **speech that is both useful and kind is rare and worth striving for**.

**59. (C)** The chapter pairs its sayings with lessons on **the particles (च/वा), the -शस् adverbs, the possessive -वत्/-मत्, and the दुर्- prefix**.

**60. (D)** च means 'and' (both qualities), and दुर्लभ means **'rare but attainable'** (अशक्य is 'impossible') — the student is wrong on both.
