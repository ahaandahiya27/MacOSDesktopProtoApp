# Sanskrit — Chapter 5: सेवा हि परमो धर्मः · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** In यन्त्रवत् ('like a machine'), -वत् means **'like / resembling'**.

**2. (A)** यन्त्रम् इव uses इव, the particle meaning **'like / as'**.

**3. (D)** The -वत्/-मत् in धनवत् ('wealthy') means **'possessing / having'** — a different use of -वत्.

**4. (B)** धनवान् is 'wealthy', but सिंहवत् means **'like a lion'** (comparison).

**5. (B)** 'He fought like a lion' uses **सिंहवत्** (like a lion).

**6. (D)** 'A virtue-possessing man' uses the possessing -वत्: **गुणवान्**.

**7. (A)** The two -वत् senses are told apart by **context** (a comparison 'like X' vs an attribute 'having X').

**8. (C)** चिकित्सकः is an agent noun formed with the suffix **-क (the agent / doer)**.

**9. (A)** An agent noun names **the doer of the action**.

**10. (C)** From कृ, the -तृ agent noun is **कर्तृ (कर्ता, 'doer')**.

**11. (D)** From दा, the -तृ agent noun 'giver' is **दातृ (दाता)**.

**12. (B)** From नी, the -तृ agent noun 'leader' is **नेतृ (नेता)**.

**13. (D)** रोगिन् ('one who has disease') is formed with the possessor suffix **-इन् ('having / possessing')**.

**14. (B)** धनिन् (धनी) means **wealthy** (one having wealth).

**15. (C)** ज्ञानिन् (ज्ञानी) and बलिन् (बली) mean **knowledgeable and strong**.

**16. (A)** The healer is चिकित्सकः (-क); the sick one is **रोगिन् (-इन्)**.

**17. (C)** हि is **emphatic ('verily / indeed')**.

**18. (A)** परमः here means **highest / supreme (in rank)**.

**19. (B)** परमः means **highest in importance, not first in time** — so 'the first duty of the morning' is wrong.

**20. (D)** परम, उत्तम and श्रेष्ठ all express **the highest / best**.

**21. (A)** सेवा is **service through one's time, body and presence**.

**22. (D)** दान is **giving money or goods (often from a distance)**.

**23. (B)** A donor who only writes a cheque does दान but not **सेवा (hands-on service)**.

**24. (C)** In चिकित्सकः रोगिणः सेवते, रोगिणः is the **accusative object** ('serves the sick').

**26. (C)** कषायम् means **a herbal decoction (medicine)**.

**27. (A)** उपाधिः means **a degree / qualification**.

**28. (D)** पदवी means **a position / post / title**.

**31. (C)** यन्त्र means **a machine**.

**39. (D)** कर्ता, दाता and नेता are all **-तृ agent nouns**.
