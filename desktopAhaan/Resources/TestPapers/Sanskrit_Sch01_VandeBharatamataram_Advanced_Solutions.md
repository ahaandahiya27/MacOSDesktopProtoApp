# Sanskrit — Chapter 1: वन्दे भारतमातरम् · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** वन्दे governs the accusative, so 'I salute the Mother' is **वन्दे मातरम्**.

**2. (A)** The accusative singular of मातृ is **मातरम्**.

**3. (D)** The nominative singular of मातृ is **माता**.

**5. (B)** The genitive singular of मातृ is **मातुः**.

**6. (D)** भारतमातुः चरणौ uses भारतमातुः in the **genitive** ('of the Motherland').

**7. (A)** The accusative of पितृ ('father') is **पितरम्** (declining like मातृ).

**8. (C)** The nominative singular of भ्रातृ is **भ्राता**.

**9. (A)** वन्दे takes the accusative, but नमः takes the **dative (मात्रे नमः)**.

**10. (C)** 'Salutation to the Mother' with नमः is **मात्रे नमः** (dative).

**11. (D)** The contrast: वन्दे + **accusative**, but नमः + **dative**.

**12. (B)** वन्दे means **'I salute / I bow / I praise'**.

**13. (D)** Sanskrit uses a different verb for love, such as **कामये ('I desire/love')** — so वन्दे ≠ 'I love'.

**14. (B)** पर्वतराजः = पर्वत + राजः = **'king of mountains' (the Himalaya)**.

**15. (C)** पर्वतराजः is a तत्पुरुष expressing a hidden **genitive** relation ('king OF mountains').

**16. (A)** The Himalaya is placed at Bharat's **head / top (north)**, like a crown.

**17. (C)** रत्नाकरः = रत्न + आकर = **'jewel-mine' (a name for the ocean)**.

**18. (A)** The ocean is imagined **washing (प्रक्षालयति)** Bharat's feet.

**19. (B)** चरणौ ('feet') is a **dual** form (exactly two feet).

**20. (D)** Himalaya = crown at the head; ocean = washer of the **feet (चरणौ)**.

**21. (A)** मुकुटरूपेण (instrumental) means **'in the form of / as' (manner)**.

**22. (D)** देशभक्तः = देश + भक्त = **'country-devotee' (a patriot)**.

**23. (B)** देशभक्तः and धर्मभक्तः differ in their first member: **देश 'country' vs धर्म 'duty/religion'**.

**24. (C)** The poem treats भारत as a **Mother (माता) to be revered**.

**25. (B)** भारतमाता = भारत + माता = **'Mother India / India-as-Mother'**.

**26. (C)** वन्दे मातरम् appears in the novel **आनन्दमठः (Anandamath)**.

**27. (A)** The author is **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**.

**28. (D)** आनन्दमठः is dated to **1882**.

**29. (D)** An उपन्यासः is a **novel**.

**30. (A)** जिज्ञासा means **curiosity / desire to know**.

**31. (C)** जिज्ञासा (from ज्ञा) is parallel to पिपासा, built on **पा ('drink')**.

**32. (B)** आनन्द (दीर्घ आ) means 'joy'; अनन्द would mean **'no-joy / joylessness'**.

**33. (C)** The pair आनन्द / अनन्द shows that swapping a दीर्घ for a ह्रस्व vowel can **reverse or change a word's meaning**.

**34. (B)** The accusative मातरम् answers **'whom do I salute?'**.

**35. (D)** With नमः the case would switch to the **dative (मात्रे)**.

**36. (A)** The dative singular of मातृ is **मात्रे**.

**38. (B)** रत्नाकरः names the ocean because the sea **holds pearls, corals and gems**.

**39. (D)** 'Himalaya crowns the head, ocean washes the feet' depicts Bharat as a **living body / person (the Mother)**.

**40. (C)** 'We salute' (वन्द्, atmanepada) is **वन्दामहे**.

**43. (A)** The correct declension (nom/acc/gen sg) is **माता / मातरम् / मातुः**.

**44. (B)** The correct split: **पर्वतराजः = mountain (पर्वत) + king (राजः)**.

**50. (D)** पितृ, मातृ and भ्रातृ are all **kinship र-stem (ऋकारान्त) nouns that decline alike**.
