**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it splits the chapter's compounds (श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी = श्लोक + अन्त्य + अक्षर + ई; विद्याहीन = विद्या + हीन 'knowledge-lacking'; निर्गन्ध = निर् + गन्ध 'scent-less'), drills the 'lacking' elements निर्- and -हीन against the possessive -वत्/-मत्, the DATIVE of purpose (दानाय 'for giving', रक्षणाय 'for protection', सेवायै 'for service'), the structure of the simile (उपमा), and the character-words खल (wicked) vs मूर्ख (foolish) vs साधु (good). Remember the niti-shlokas' point: the SAME विद्या/धन/शक्ति serve opposite ends in the wicked and the good, and worth comes from शीलम् (character). Sanskrit is in Devanagari.
