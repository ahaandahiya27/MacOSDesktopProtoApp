Q1

The chapter's thesis, सेवा हि परमो धर्मः, means:

  • Aduty is the highest service
  • Bmoney is the highest duty

Working

✓

सेवा हि परमो धर्मः means 'service is verily the highest duty'. The other readings reverse or distort it.

  • Aa degree
  • B(selfless) service✓
  • Ca machine
  • Da decoction

Working

✓

सेवा is (selfless) service. A degree is उपाधिः, a machine यन्त्रम्, and a decoction कषायम्.

Working

✓

परमो धर्मः means 'the highest duty'. It is not a morning task, a medicine, or a designation.

Q4

  • Aa negation ('not')
  • Ba question marker
  • Cemphatic ('verily / indeed')✓

✓

हि is an emphatic particle ('verily / indeed'). It is not a negation, a question marker, or a plural ending.

Q6
