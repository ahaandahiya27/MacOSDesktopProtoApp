Q1

Which of these statements is a "statistical" statement — a likely conclusion drawn from data, not a certainty?

  • AA person 6 ft tall is more likely to be an adult than a toddler.✓
  • BA triangle has exactly three sides.
  • CThe sum of 12 and 8 is 20.
  • DA square has four equal sides.

Working

✓

A statistical statement reasons from data to a *likely* (not certain) conclusion. "A person 6 ft tall is more likely to be an adult than a toddler" is a likelihood claim from a pattern. The others are definitions/arithmetic facts — certain, not statistical.

Q2

Shubman scored 23, 7, 10, 52, 18 in 5 matches (total 110); Yashasvi scored 26, 53, 2, 15 in 4 matches (total 96). Why is comparing only the totals unfair here?

  • ATotals are never useful in cricket.
  • BShubman's highest score is the only thing that matters.
  • CYashasvi's scores are all even.
  • DThey played different numbers of matches, so more matches allow a larger total — compare the per-match mean instead.✓

Working

✓

Shubman played 5 matches, Yashasvi 4. More innings give more chances to pile up runs, so the raw total is biased toward whoever batted more often. A fair comparison divides by the number of matches (the mean). The "bigger total = better" reflex is exactly the trap.

✓

Q19

Five test scores are 60, 60, 70, 80, 80. Which statement is TRUE?

  • AThe data has a single mode.
  • BThe mode is 70.
  • CThe mean is 70 and the median is 70.✓
  • DThe range is 80.

Working

✓

Q26

A bar graph's vertical axis starts at 90 instead of 0. A value rises from 92 to 96. Why does the bar look like it nearly doubled?

  • ABecause 96 is double 48.
  • BBecause 96 − 92 = 4 is a large jump.
  • CBecause the eye reads bar heights as if measured from zero, so a truncated axis exaggerates the difference.✓
  • DBecause bar graphs always exaggerate.

Working

✓

The eye judges a bar's value by its height as if it grew from zero. With the axis truncated at 90, the visible part of the 96-bar is far longer than that of the 92-bar, so a 4-unit change looks huge. The real change is only about 4%.

Q27

✓

The mean uses every value, so the outlier 100 drags it far above the cluster around 4. The mode (4) and median (4) ignore the extreme. So the mean is most distorted.
