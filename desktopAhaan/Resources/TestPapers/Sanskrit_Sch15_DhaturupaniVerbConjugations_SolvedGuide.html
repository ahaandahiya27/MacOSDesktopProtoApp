Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

A धातु is:

  • Aa noun ending
  • Ba vowel quantity
  • Ca case
  • Da verb root (the base from which verb forms are built)✓

Working

✓

A धातु is a verb root. It is not a noun ending, a vowel quantity, or a case.

Q2

Verb forms like पठति, पठसि, पठामि are all built from the धातु:

  • Aपठ् ('to read')✓
  • Bअस् ('to be')
  • Cकृ ('to do')
  • Dभाष् ('to speak')

Working

✓

पठति, पठसि, पठामि are built from पठ् ('to read'). अस्, कृ and भाष् are different roots.

Q3

The appendix tables how many common roots?

  • Athree
  • Beight (पठ्, अस्, कृ, श्रु, दा, ज्ञा, क्री, भाष्)✓
  • Cten
  • Done

Working

✓

The appendix tables eight roots. Not three, ten, or one.

Q4

Knowing a धातु lets you:

  • Adecline it like a noun
  • Bcount its vowels
  • Cbuild its many tense and person forms✓
  • Dfind its case

Working

✓

Knowing the root lets you build its many tense and person forms. Verbs are conjugated, not declined like nouns, and they have no case.

Q5

Sanskrit's लकार system has how many tense-moods?

  • Aten✓
  • Bseven
  • Cthree
  • Done

Working

✓

The लकार system has ten tense-moods. Seven is the number of noun cases; not three or one.

Q6

  • Bseven noun cases
  • Cthree genders
  • Deight declensions

Working

✓

लकाराः are Sanskrit's ten tenses and moods. The seven cases, three genders and declensions are noun features.

Q7

The लकार that marks the present tense is:

  • Aलङ् (past)
  • Bलृट् (future)
  • Cलोट् (imperative)
  • Dलट् (वर्तमानकाल)✓

Working

✓

लट् marks the present tense. लङ् is the imperfect past, लृट् the future, and लोट् the imperative.

Q8

लट्-लकार shows forms of which time?

  • Athe past
  • Bthe future
  • Cno particular time
  • Dthe present (वर्तमानकाल)✓

Working

✓

लट्-लकार shows the present tense. Not the past, the future, or no time.

Q9

The appendix conjugates its eight roots in which लकार?

  • Aलट् (the present)✓
  • Bलृट् (future) only
  • Cलोट् (imperative) only
  • Dविधिलिङ् (optative) only

Working

✓

The appendix conjugates the roots in लट् (the present). Not only the future, imperative, or optative.

Q10

लङ्-लकार marks the:

  • Apresent
  • Bimperfect past✓
  • Cfuture
  • Doptative

Working

✓

लङ्-लकार marks the imperfect past. The present is लट्, future लृट्, and optative विधिलिङ्.

Q16

अस्, कृ and श्रु differ from पठ् in that they:

✓

Q17

The two pada-classes (voice-classes) are:

  • Cpast and present

Working

✓

The two pada-classes are परस्मैपदम् and आत्मनेपदम्. The others are numbers, tenses, or genders.

Q18

A parasmaipada verb's 3rd-singular present ends in:

  • A-ते (e.g. भाषते)
  • B-ति (e.g. पठति)✓
  • C-स्य
  • D-आय

Working

✓

Parasmaipada 3rd-singular present ends in -ति (पठति). -ते is atmanepada, -स्य a future marker, and -आय a dative ending.

Q19

An atmanepada verb's 3rd-singular present ends in:

  • A-ति (e.g. पठति)
  • B-तुम्
  • C-स्य
  • D-ते (e.g. भाषते)✓

Working

✓

Atmanepada 3rd-singular present ends in -ते (भाषते). -ति is parasmaipada, -तुम् an infinitive, and -स्य a future marker.

Q20

पठति vs भाषते differ because they belong to:

  • Adifferent pada-classes (parasmaipada vs atmanepada)✓
  • Bdifferent tenses
  • Cdifferent numbers
  • Ddifferent genders

Working

✓

पठति and भाषते differ by pada-class (parasmaipada vs atmanepada). They are the same tense, number and (verbs have no) gender.
