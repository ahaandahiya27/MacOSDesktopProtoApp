Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

What does the phrase वन्दे मातरम् (Vande Mataram) mean?

  • AThe Mother loves me
  • BMothers of the nation
  • CI bow to / salute the Mother✓
  • DSalute, O mothers!

Working

✓

वन्दे (root वन्द्, 'to salute') + मातरम् (accusative of मातृ) = 'I salute the Mother'. It is not 'the Mother loves me', a plural, or a vocative address.

Q2

The verb वन्दे most precisely means:

  • AI salute / I bow / I praise✓
  • BI love (romantically)
  • CI see
  • DI give

Working

✓

वन्दे (root वन्द्) means 'I salute, bow, praise' - reverence, not romantic love (कामये), seeing (दृश्), or giving (दा).

Q3

Translating वन्दे as 'I love' is wrong because:

  • Aवन्दे actually means 'I hate'
  • Bवन्दे has no meaning at all
  • Clove and salutation are the same word in Sanskrit
  • DSanskrit uses different verbs for love (e.g. कामये) and for salutation (वन्दे)✓

Working

✓

वन्दे specifically means salutation/reverence; love has its own verbs like कामये/स्निह्यामि. It does not mean hate, is meaningful, and is distinct from love.

Q4

In वन्दे मातरम्, the word मातरम् is in which case?

  • Anominative (the subject)
  • Bgenitive (possession)
  • Caccusative (the object being saluted)✓
  • Dvocative (direct address)

Working

✓

वन्दे takes its object in the accusative, so मातृ becomes मातरम् (accusative singular). It is not the subject (nominative), possession (genitive), or address (vocative).

Q5

'I bow to the Mother' requires मातरम् rather than माता because:

  • Aमाता is a plural form
  • Bमातरम् is the subject doing the bowing
  • Cthe Mother is the object of वन्दे, which takes the accusative✓
  • Dमाता means 'ocean'

Working

✓

The Mother is the object saluted, needing the accusative मातरम्; माता is nominative singular (the subject), not plural, and means 'mother', not 'ocean'.

The difference between देशभक्तः and धर्मभक्तः lies in the:

Q38

Sanskrit compounds like देशभक्तः are best understood by:

  • Bguessing from English

✓

Q39

In आनन्दमठः, the first syllable has a long आ. Shortening it to अ would be wrong because:

  • Avowel length (ह्रस्व/दीर्घ) changes meaning in Sanskrit✓
  • Blong and short vowels mean the same thing
  • CSanskrit has no long vowels
  • Dअ is always longer than आ

Working

✓

Vowel length matters in Sanskrit: आनन्द ('bliss') depends on the long आ. Long and short are not the same, Sanskrit has long vowels, and आ (two matras) is longer than अ.

Q40

आ (as in आनन्द) is a ____ vowel, while अ is a ____ vowel.

  • Ashort ... long
  • Bsilent ... loud
  • Clong (दीर्घ) ... short (ह्रस्व)✓
  • Dnasal ... oral

Working

✓

आ is दीर्घ (long, two matras) and अ is ह्रस्व (short, one matra). The other pairings are wrong.
