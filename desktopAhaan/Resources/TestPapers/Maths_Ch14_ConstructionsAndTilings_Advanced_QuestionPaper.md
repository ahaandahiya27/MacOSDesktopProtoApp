**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Recall that compass-and-straightedge constructions rest on equal radii (equal arcs); a regular polygon tiles the plane on its own only when its interior angle divides 360° exactly; and the angles meeting at any vertex of a tiling sum to 360°. Use the interior-angle formula (n − 2) × 180° ÷ n. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
