# Sanskrit — Chapter 15: धातुरूपाणि (Verb Conjugations) · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** पठसि carries the ending **-सि**, the मध्यमपुरुष एकवचन (second-person singular) marker, so it means 'you (one person) read'. The -ति ending would be third person, -मि would be first person.

**2. (A)** The first-person (उत्तमपुरुष) dual of पठ् is **पठावः** ('we two read') — ending -वः. पठामः is the plural ('we all read') and पठामि is the singular ('I read').

**3. (D)** पठथः ends in -थः, the मध्यमपुरुष द्विवचन (second-person dual) ending: 'you two read'.

**4. (B)** The first-person plural ('we all read') is **पठामः**, ending -मः. पठन्ति is third-person plural ('they read').

**5. (B)** The मध्यमपुरुष (second-person) row in एकवचन/द्विवचन/बहुवचन order is **पठसि, पठथः, पठथ** (-सि, -थः, -थ). The पठति/पठतः/पठन्ति row is third person; पठामि/पठावः/पठामः is first person.

**6. (D)** Both are second person; the difference is number. **पठथ** (no visarga) is बहुवचन, 'you all read'; पठथः (with visarga, -थः) is द्विवचन, 'you two read'.

**7. (A)** Addressing one person ('you read') needs the second-person singular **पठसि**. पठति would be 'he reads'.

**8. (C)** The मध्यमपुरुष (second-person) personal endings for singular/dual/plural are **-सि, -थः, -थ**. The -ति/-तः/-अन्ति set is third person; -मि/-वः/-मः is first person; -ते/-एते/-अन्ते is atmanepada.

**9. (A)** The initial अ- augment plus the ending -त् marks **लङ् (imperfect past)**: अपठत् = 'he read'. The plain present would be पठति.

**10. (C)** The past-tense (लङ्) third-person plural 'they read' is **अपठन्** — augment अ- + plural ending -अन्. पठन्ति is the present 'they read'.

**11. (D)** The future (लृट्) third-singular of पठ् is **पठिष्यति** ('he will read'), formed with the future infix -ष्य-.

**12. (B)** The infix **-ष्य-** (as in पठिष्यति, पठिष्यन्ति) is the सार्वधातुक future marker — it builds the **future tense (लृट्)**.

**13. (D)** पठतु ('let him read') is the third-singular of the **imperative, लोट्-लकार** — used for commands and wishes.

**14. (B)** A command to one person ('Read!') is the bare imperative second-singular **पठ**. पठतु would be 'let him read' (third person).

**15. (C)** पठेत् ('he should/may read') is the third-singular of the **optative, विधिलिङ्-लकार**, recognised by the -ए- mood-sign before the ending.

**16. (A)** The optative (विधिलिङ्) expresses **'should / would / may'** — advice, possibility, or a polite wish — not a plain fact or a finished action.

**17. (C)** The four sample third-singulars line up as **पठति (लट्, present), अपठत् (लङ्, past), पठिष्यति (लृट्, future), पठतु (लोट्, imperative)**.

**18. (A)** अपठन् combines the past augment अ- with the plural ending -अन्, so it is **लङ् (past), third-person plural** — 'they read'.

**19. (B)** The past (लङ्) first-singular 'I read' is **अपठम्** (augment अ- + first-singular -अम्). पठामि is the present 'I read'.

**20. (D)** पठिष्यामि carries the future infix -ष्य- with the first-singular -आमि, so it means **'I shall read' (future, first person)**.

**21. (A)** Present → past → future for the third-singular of पठ् is **पठति → अपठत् → पठिष्यति**.

**22. (D)** 'Let us read' is the imperative (लोट्) first-person plural **पठाम**. पठामः would be the plain present 'we read'.

**23. (B)** The third-person dual of अस् ('the two are') is **स्तः**. अस्ति is singular ('he/it is'); सन्ति is plural ('they are').

**24. (C)** The first-person plural of अस् ('we are') is **स्मः**. अस्मि is singular ('I am'); स्वः is the dual ('we two are').

**25. (B)** वयम् ('we', plural) needs the first-person plural **स्मः**: वयम् छात्राः स्मः = 'We are students'.

**26. (C)** The second-person singular of अस् ('you are') is **असि**.

**27. (A)** ते बालकाः ('those boys', plural) takes the third-person plural **सन्ति**: 'those boys are'.

**28. (D)** The first-person dual of अस् ('we two are') is **स्वः**. स्मः is the plural.

**29. (D)** The second-person plural of अस् ('you all are') is **स्थ**. स्थः is the dual.

**30. (A)** The third-person row of अस् in एक/द्वि/बहु order is **अस्ति, स्तः, सन्ति**. अस्मि/स्वः/स्मः is the first-person row; असि/स्थः/स्थ is the second-person row.

**31. (C)** कृ uses the strong stem करो- in the singular: the second-person singular ('you do') is **करोषि**. कुरुतः (dual) and कुर्मः (plural) use the weak stem कुरु-/कुर्.

**32. (B)** The first-person plural of कृ ('we do') is **कुर्मः** (weak stem कुर्). कुर्वः is the dual.

**33. (C)** The first-person dual of कृ ('we two do') is **कुर्वः**. कुर्मः is the plural.

**34. (B)** The second-person dual of कृ ('you two do') is **कुरुथः**. कुरुथ (no visarga) is the plural.

**35. (D)** कृ keeps the strong stem करो- only in the **three singular parasmaipada forms — करोमि, करोषि, करोति**; every dual and plural form switches to the weak stem कुरु-/कुर्.

**36. (A)** The second-person plural of कृ ('you all do') is **कुरुथ**. कुरुथः is the dual.

**37. (A)** The analogy is past : present. अकरोत् ('he did') is the past of the present करोति ('he does'); likewise अपठत् ('he read', past) is the past of the present **पठति** ('he reads'). So अपठत् pairs with पठति.

**38. (B)** भाष् is आत्मनेपदी, so its endings are the -से/-से series. The second-person singular ('you speak') is **भाषसे**.

**39. (D)** The first-person singular of भाष् ('I speak') is **भाषे** (atmanepada -ए ending).

**40. (C)** The third-person dual of भाष् ('the two speak') is **भाषेते**. भाषन्ते is the plural.

**41. (D)** The second-person plural of भाष् ('you all speak') is **भाषध्वे** (atmanepada -ध्वे ending).

**42. (C)** The first-person plural of भाष् ('we speak') is **भाषामहे**. भाषावहे is the dual.

**43. (A)** The atmanepada third-person endings (एक/द्वि/बहु) are **-ते, -एते, -अन्ते**, as in भाषते, भाषेते, भाषन्ते. The -ति/-तः/-अन्ति set is parasmaipada.

**44. (B)** दा is a reduplicating (class-3) root; its third-singular present is **ददाति** ('he gives'), not the simple दाति.

**45. (B)** The third-person plural of दा ('they give') is **ददति** — the reduplicated stem with the plural ending, with no inserted nasal.

**46. (D)** ज्ञा takes the class-9 present stem जाना-/जानी-; 'he knows' is **जानाति**.

**47. (C)** The third-person plural of ज्ञा ('they know') is **जानन्ति**.

**48. (A)** क्री (class 9) forms 'he buys' as **क्रीणाति**, using the -णा- present infix.

**49. (A)** श्रु (class 5) builds its present on शृणो-/शृणु-; 'he hears' is **शृणोति**.

**50. (D)** The third-person plural of श्रु ('they hear') is **शृण्वन्ति** (weak stem शृणु- → शृण्व् before the plural ending).

**51. (C)** छात्रौ is dual ('the two students'), so the verb must be the third-person dual **पठतः**. पठति (singular) and पठन्ति (plural) would not agree.

**52. (B)** 'You and I' together = 'we two', a first-person dual, so the verb is **पठावः** ('we two read').

**53. (C)** ते ('they', plural) demands the third-person plural; करोति (singular) is wrong, so the correct verb is **कुर्वन्ति** ('they do').

**54. (D)** भाष् is atmanepada, and वयम् ('we') is first-person plural, so the correct form is **भाषामहे** ('we speak'). भाषामः would wrongly use parasmaipada endings.

**55. (B)** The shared initial अ- in अकरोत्, अपठत्, अगच्छत् is the **past-tense augment of लङ्** — it is NOT the negative अ-.

**56. (A)** अपठत् carries the past augment अ- (लङ्), so it is **not present tense**. पठति, करोति and भाषते are all present (लट्).

**57. (B)** A conditional 'if he were to read' uses the **optative, विधिलिङ्** — पठेत्.

**58. (A)** The wrong pair is **भाष् → भाषति**: भाष् is atmanepada, so its third-singular is भाषते (-ते), not भाषति. The others — कृ → करोति, अस् → अस्ति, श्रु → शृणोति — are correct.

**59. (C)** The first-person (उत्तमपुरुष) set of पठ् in एक/द्वि/बहु order is **पठामि, पठावः, पठामः**. पठति/पठतः/पठन्ति is third person; पठसि/पठथः/पठथ is second person.

**60. (D)** लट् is the present tense, and करोति is the third-person form 'he/she does'. To say 'you do', use the second-person **करोषि**; 'I do' is करोमि. So the correct fix is: **त्वम् करोषि means 'you do', and करोति is present third-person ('he does')**.
