**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Apex traps: classification axes are independent and stack (purpose = subsistence/commercial; effort-per-area = intensive/extensive; water = rain-fed/irrigated; season = kharif/rabi/zaid) so a single farm carries several labels at once; the Green Revolution's gains and harms are CAUSALLY linked through the same intensive inputs, not separate; rabi lives in the dry winter and usually needs irrigation while kharif rides the southwest monsoon (Jun-Sep) and the northeast monsoon (Oct-Dec) only soaks the southeast; women do most hands-on field work yet are undercounted as unpaid family labour; organic/sustainable farming CUTS chemicals without abandoning food; hydroponics proves soil is not strictly required; antiquity (7th-8th millennium BCE rice) shows continuity, not a foreign or modern origin.
