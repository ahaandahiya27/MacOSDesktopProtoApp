**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: treating a bank as a passive locker rather than a lender that re-circulates most deposits at a higher rate than it pays; reading compound growth as flat simple interest; mistaking the RBI for a place ordinary people bank rather than the banker-to-banks, currency-issuer and regulator; assuming a benchmark-rate RISE makes borrowing cheaper; believing shares are a guaranteed, risk-free saving like a fixed deposit; thinking UPI or a cheque creates new money rather than moving existing rupees; and imagining a genuine bank would ever phone for your OTP, PIN or password (report fraud on 1930).
