**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Always do brackets first, then × and ÷, then + and −; never evaluate strictly left-to-right. Watch the classic traps: a minus sign in front of a bracket flips every inner sign, distributing means multiplying every term inside, and only addition/multiplication may be reordered freely — subtraction and division may not. Many items can be compared or simplified without full computation. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
