**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Reason from *cause* to *effect* — what each weather element measures, which instrument reads it, and why. Watch the traps: weather is the *moment*, climate is the *long average*; warm air rises and lowers the pressure beneath it; the rain gauge measures depth (mm), not volume; relative humidity rises as air cools even when no water is added; and "red sky at night" is folklore that often works for a physical reason. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
