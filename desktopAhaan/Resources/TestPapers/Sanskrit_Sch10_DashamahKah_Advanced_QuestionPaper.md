**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it drills the gerund (क्त्वा: तीर्त्वा 'having crossed', गत्वा 'having gone', कृत्वा 'having done') and its 'completed-first' sense, the full ordinal series (प्रथमः … दशमः) versus cardinals (दश), the imperfect past अगणयत् (root गण् 'count', not गा 'sing'), and the interrogatives कतमः ('which of many?') vs कतरः ('which of two?'). It also reads the Vedantic point — दशमः त्वम् असि / तत्त्वमसि: the missing tenth is the counter himself. Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
