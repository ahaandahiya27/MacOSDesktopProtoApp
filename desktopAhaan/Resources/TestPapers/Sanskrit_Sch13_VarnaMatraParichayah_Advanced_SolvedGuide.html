Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

Which vowel is ह्रस्व (short, 1 mātrā)?

  • Aआ
  • Bई
  • Cइ✓
  • Dऊ

Working

✓

**इ** is a simple short (ह्रस्व) vowel, 1 mātrā. आ, ई and ऊ are all long (दीर्घ), 2 mātrās.

Q2

Which vowel is दीर्घ (long, 2 mātrās)?

  • Aआ✓
  • Bअ
  • Cइ
  • Dउ

Working

✓

**आ** is long (दीर्घ), 2 mātrās. अ, इ and उ are short, 1 mātrā each.

Q3

The short–long pair that is correctly matched is:

  • Aआ (short) — अ (long)
  • Bई (short) — इ (long)
  • Cऊ (short) — उ (long)
  • Dइ (short) — ई (long)✓

Working

✓

The correctly matched short–long pair is **इ (short) — ई (long)**. The other rows reverse the lengths.

Q4

The complete set of simple short vowels is:

  • Aआ, ई, ऊ, ॠ
  • Bअ, इ, उ, ऋ✓
  • Cए, ऐ, ओ, औ
  • Dक, च, ट, त

Working

✓

The simple short vowels are **अ, इ, उ, ऋ** (each 1 mātrā). आ/ई/ऊ/ॠ are their long counterparts; ए/ऐ/ओ/औ are long; क/च/ट/त are consonants.

Q5

The vowel ए (as in देव) is:

  • Ashort (1 mātrā)
  • Blong (2 mātrās), always✓
  • Csometimes short
  • Da consonant

Working

✓

**ए is always long (2 mātrās)** — it has no short form. So the ए in देव counts as 2 mātrās.

Q6

A special feature of ए, ऐ, ओ and औ is that they:

  • Aare always short
  • Bare consonants
  • Chave three mātrās each
  • Dare always दीर्घ (long) — they have no short form✓

Working

✓

ए, ऐ, ओ and औ **are always दीर्घ (long); they have no short version**, so they always count 2 mātrās.

Q7

There is no 'short ओ' in standard Sanskrit because ओ is:

  • Ainherently long (2 mātrās)✓
  • Binherently short
  • Ca consonant
  • Dsilent

Working

✓

There is no 'short ओ' because **ओ is inherently long (2 mātrās)** in standard Sanskrit.

Q8

Counting only simple vowels, अ and आ differ in that:

  • Aआ is 1 mātrā, अ is 2 mātrās
  • Bboth are 1 mātrā
  • Cअ is 1 mātrā, आ is 2 mātrās✓
  • Dboth are 3 mātrās

Working

✓

**अ is 1 mātrā (short); आ is 2 mātrās (long)** — the same vowel-place, twice the duration.

Q9

ऋ (as in ऋषि) is a:

  • Ashort vowel (1 mātrā)✓
  • Blong vowel
  • Cconsonant
  • Dप्लुत

Working

✓

**ऋ is a short vowel (1 mātrā)**, as in ऋषि. It is a true vowel, not a consonant.

Q10

The long counterpart of ऋ is:

  • Aऌ
  • Bए
  • Cॠ✓
  • Dऔ

Working

✓

The long counterpart of ऋ is **ॠ** (rarely used, 2 mātrās).
