**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper probes the difference between weather and climate, the factors that shape climate, the elements of weather and how they are measured, and how polar, tropical and migratory animals are adapted to their climate — including the modern threats of climate change and coral bleaching. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
