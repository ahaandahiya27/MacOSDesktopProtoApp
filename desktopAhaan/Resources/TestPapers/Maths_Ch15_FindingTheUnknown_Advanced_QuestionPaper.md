**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Most questions need three or more steps — translate carefully, clear denominators first, then transpose. Watch for sign traps when removing brackets, "average of two rates" traps where the harmonic mean is correct, and "off-by-one" digit traps. Always check by substituting back when in doubt.
