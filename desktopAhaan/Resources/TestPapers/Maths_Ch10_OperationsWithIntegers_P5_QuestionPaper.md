**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Expert-tier signed-integer reasoning: long mixed chains under strict BODMAS, deeply nested brackets and braces, parity-of-negative-factors arguments, distributive splits, powers where (-2) cubed is not minus 2 cubed, and word problems where every classic slip (subtracting a negative ADDS, two negatives MULTIPLY to a positive, a single hidden factor of 0 collapses the product, and division by the wrong sign) is deliberately baked into the distractors.
