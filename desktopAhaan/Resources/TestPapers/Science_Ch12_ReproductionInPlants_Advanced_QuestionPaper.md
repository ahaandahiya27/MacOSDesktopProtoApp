**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: the parts of a flower and which part becomes which, self- vs cross-pollination and their agents, the pollen-tube journey of fertilisation, the four routes of seed dispersal, and the many forms of asexual reproduction — vegetative propagation, budding, fragmentation and spore formation. Reason from structure to function, and watch the "ovule → seed, ovary → fruit" trap. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
