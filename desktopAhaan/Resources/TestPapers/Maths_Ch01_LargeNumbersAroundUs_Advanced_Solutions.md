**1. (C)** One crore is 10⁷ and one billion is 10⁹. So 1 billion ÷ 1 crore = 10⁹ ÷ 10⁷ = 10² = 100. One billion = 100 crore. The trap is to confuse a billion with an arab (which is also 100 crore) — here they agree, but always count zeros: 1,00,00,00,000 has 9 zeros after the 1.

**2. (A)** In 4,73,16,028 the 7 sits in the ten-lakhs place, so its place value is 70,00,000; its face value is just 7. Difference = 70,00,000 − 7 = 69,99,993. Place value minus face value is always (place value − the single digit).

**3. (D)** Per day: 2,500 × 16 = 40,000 bolts. April has 30 days, so 40,000 × 30 = 12,00,000. The "12,50,000" trap assumes 31 days; April has 30.

**4. (B)** Rounding to the nearest lakh means looking at the part below one lakh: 36,49,712 has 49,712 beyond 36,00,000, and 49,712 < 50,000, so round DOWN to 36,00,000.
