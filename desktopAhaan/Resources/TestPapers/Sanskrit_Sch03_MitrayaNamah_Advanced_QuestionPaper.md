**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it drills the DATIVE (चतुर्थी विभक्ति, -आय) that नमः governs and forms it across stems (मित्राय, रवये, गुरवे), the words that take the dative (नमः, स्वस्ति, स्वाहा), the twelve Sun-names as synonyms (एकार्थकशब्दाः) versus antonyms (विलोम), the compound (समास) सूर्यनमस्कारः versus the two words सूर्याय नमस्कारः, and feminine formation (आचार्य → आचार्या). Remember: नमः मित्राय (dative), NOT नमः मित्रः; there are exactly TWELVE Sun-names and postures. Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
