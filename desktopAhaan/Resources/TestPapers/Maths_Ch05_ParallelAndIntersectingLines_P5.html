Worked Solutions

1. (C) A linear pair sums to 180, so (3x+14)+(2x+16)=180, giving 5x+30=180 and x=30. Then angle AOC = 3(30)+14 = 104. Its bisector OE splits it into two equal halves, so angle EOC = 104/2 = 52 degrees. Reporting the whole angle 104 ignores the bisector; 38 bisects the adjacent angle (76); 26 halves twice. Angle EOC = 52 degrees.

2. (C) Vertically opposite angles are equal: 4x-6 = 2x+30, so 2x = 36 and x = 18. The angle itself is 4(18)-6 = 66. Its linear-pair neighbour is its supplement, 180 - 66 = 114 degrees. Stopping at the vertical angle gives 66; 18 reports x; 33 halves the original. The neighbour is 114 degrees.

3. (D) Opposite angles are equal and the four total 360: 2(2x)+2(x+30)=360, so 6x+60=360 and x=50. The two distinct angles are 2x=100 and x+30=80. OF bisects a 100° angle into two equal halves, so the angle to the nearer arm is 100/2 = 50 degrees. Choosing 100 forgets the bisector; 40 halves the 80° angle; 80 reports the other angle. Angle to the nearer arm is 50 degrees.

4. (D) Linear pair: (5x+8)+(4x+10)=180, so 9x+18=180 and x=18. The angles are 5(18)+8 = 98 and 4(18)+10 = 82. The larger is 98; half the smaller is 82/2 = 41; the difference is 98 - 41 = 57 degrees. Taking the difference of the two angles gives 16; 98 stops too early; 41 reports the half itself. The difference is 57 degrees.

5. (B) Vertically opposite angles are equal, so 7x-5 = 3x+47, giving 4x = 52 and x = 13. Choosing 23 mis-handles the constants; 26 doubles x; 6.5 halves it. x = 13.

6. (B) Angles around a point sum to 360: 2y + (3y+20) + (4y-20) = 360, so 9y = 360 and y = 40. The angles are 80, 140, and 140. The largest is 140, whose supplement is 180 - 140 = 40 degrees. Choosing 140 forgets to supplement; 100 supplements the smallest; 20 mis-subtracts. The supplement of the largest is 40 degrees.

7. (D) Corresponding angles on parallels are equal: 5x-12 = 3x+8, so 2x = 20 and x = 10. The corresponding angle is 3(10)+8 = 38. Its bisector splits it into two halves of 19, so the acute angle with the transversal is 19 degrees. Choosing 38 forgets the bisector; 71 bisects the supplement; 76 doubles. The bisector makes 19 degrees.

8. (B) Alternate interior angles are equal: 3x-5 = x+41, so 2x = 46 and x = 23. The angle is 3(23)-5 = 64. Its co-interior partner at the same crossing forms a linear pair, so it is 180 - 64 = 116 degrees. Stopping at the angle itself gives 64; 23 reports x; 46 doubles x. The co-interior partner is 116 degrees.

9. (A) Angle BOD is vertically opposite angle AOC, so angle BOD = 70. Its bisector OE splits it into two 35° halves, so the angle between OE and OB is 35 degrees. Choosing 70 forgets the bisector; 110 is the supplement; 55 mis-halves. Angle EOB = 35 degrees.

10. (C) Vertically opposite: 6x+4 = 8x-30, so 2x = 34 and x = 17. Angle AOC = 6(17)+4 = 106. Its linear-pair neighbour is 180 - 106 = 74 degrees. Choosing 106 stops early; 17 reports x; 53 halves. The neighbour is 74 degrees.

11. (B) The four angles sum to 360: 4a + (18+36+54) = 360, so 4a + 108 = 360, 4a = 252 and a = 63. The angles are 63, 81, 99, 117. Largest minus smallest is 117 - 63 = 54 degrees. Choosing 18 takes one gap; 36 takes two gaps; 108 is the constant total. The difference is 54 degrees.

12. (D) The ratio parts total 2+3+5 = 10, filling 360, so one part is 36. The angles are 72, 108, and 180. The largest is the straight angle 180, whose supplement is 180 - 180 = 0 degrees. Choosing 30, 60, or 90 supplements a smaller angle or mis-totals. The supplement of the largest (180°) is 0 degrees.

13. (D) Sum is 360: 3x + (2x+10) + (x+20) + (4x-30) = 360, so 10x + 0 = 360 and x = 36. The four angles are 108, 82, 56, 114. The largest is 114, half of which is 57 degrees. Choosing 114 forgets to halve; 54 halves 108; 36 reports x. Half the largest is 57 degrees.

14. (B) The two (2x+10)° marks are vertically opposite, and (3x)° is adjacent, forming a linear pair: (2x+10)+(3x)=180, so 5x+10=180, 5x=170 and x=34. 15. (B) Co-interior angles are supplementary: (4x+10)+(5x-1)=180, so 9x+9=180, 9x=171 and x=19. The two are 4(19)+10 = 86 and 5(19)-1 = 94. Choosing 86 picks the smaller; 90 assumes perpendicular; 85 mis-computes. The answer is 94 degrees.
