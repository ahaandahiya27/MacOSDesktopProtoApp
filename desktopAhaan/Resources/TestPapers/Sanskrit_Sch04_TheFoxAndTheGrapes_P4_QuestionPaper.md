National-Olympiad traps: the moral indicts only the dishonest EXCUSE, never the failed effort (vain striving can even be noble); न लभ्यते is PASSIVE ('is not obtained') with लभ् in the saving-the-agent voice, so the fox dodges naming himself; चेत् and यदि both mean 'if' but चेत् sits inside/after the clause while यदि fronts it; उत्पतति is PRESENT (उत्=उपसर्ग 'up'), past is उदपतत् where prefix+augment उत्+अ→उद; the तसिल् -तः makes indeclinable direction ADVERBS (वामतः), not noun cases, and pairs as opposites वामतः/दक्षिणतः, अग्रतः/पृष्ठतः; पिपासा (←पा 'drink') and बुभुक्षा (←भुज् 'eat') are reduplicated DESIDERATIVES named by the action desired; द्राक्षालता=the whole VINE (द्राक्षा+लता तत्पुरुष), द्राक्षा=the single grape, द्राक्षाफलम्=the grape-fruit; आम्ल=sour, opposite of मधुर sweet.
