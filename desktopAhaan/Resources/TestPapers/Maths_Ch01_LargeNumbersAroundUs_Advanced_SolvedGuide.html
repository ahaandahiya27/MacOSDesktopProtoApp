Q1

One billion equals how many crores?

  • A10
  • B1,000
  • C100✓
  • D10,000

Working

✓

One crore is 10⁷ and one billion is 10⁹. So 1 billion ÷ 1 crore = 10⁹ ÷ 10⁷ = 10² = 100. One billion = 100 crore. The trap is to confuse a billion with an arab (which is also 100 crore) — here they agree, but always count zeros: 1,00,00,00,000 has 9 zeros after the 1.

Q2

The place value of 7 in 4,73,16,028 exceeds its face value by:

  • A69,99,993✓
  • B70,00,000
  • C7,99,993
  • D7,00,00,000

Working

✓

In 4,73,16,028 the 7 sits in the ten-lakhs place, so its place value is 70,00,000; its face value is just 7. Difference = 70,00,000 − 7 = 69,99,993. Place value minus face value is always (place value − the single digit).
