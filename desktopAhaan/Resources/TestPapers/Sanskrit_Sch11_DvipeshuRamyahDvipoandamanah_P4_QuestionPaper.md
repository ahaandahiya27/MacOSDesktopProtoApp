10. द्वि + अप् yields द्वीप. A grammarian objects: 'joining अ-final द्वि-... wait, द्वि ends in इ, so इ + अ should give a glide य (यण्), making द्व्यप्, not द्वीप.' The best response is:
   (A) द्वीप is a fixed, traditionally lengthened formation in which the इ becomes long ई; the etymology ('water on two sides') is preserved even though the surface form does not follow ordinary यण्-sandhi
