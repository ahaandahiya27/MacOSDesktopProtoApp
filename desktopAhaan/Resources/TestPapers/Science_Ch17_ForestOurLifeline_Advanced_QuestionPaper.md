**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: the layers of a forest, food chains and webs, how decomposers recycle nutrients through the soil, the many services a forest provides — oxygen, climate control, erosion and flood control, biodiversity — and the chain of harm that deforestation sets off. Reason from how the parts connect. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
