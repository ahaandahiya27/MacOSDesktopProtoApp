**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Barter is goods-for-goods with NO money (the instant money appears it is no longer barter); money arrived AFTER barter to cure its problems; and a note's worth comes from trust and Reserve Bank backing, not the paper or any precious metal, while UPI/cards merely move the same rupees electronically rather than creating a new currency. 1. At a village fair, Reena hands a basket of mangoes to a potter and walks away with a clay pot; no coins, notes, cards, or rupee figures are mentioned anywhere in the deal. The single feature that makes this a barter exchange rather than an ordinary purchase is that:
   (A) the exchange happened at a fair instead of inside a shop
   (B) the two items did not have the same weight
   (C) the trade was between a farmer and a craftsperson
   (D) goods were traded directly for other goods, with no money taking part in the deal

2. Three friends describe a deal. Asha says she gave a book and got a pen 'and no money changed hands'. Brij says he gave a book and got change from a 50-rupee note. Cyrus says he scanned a QR code to pay for a pen. Which describes barter, and why?
   (A) Asha's deal, because a good was exchanged straight for another good with no money at any stage
   (B) Brij's deal, because change was handed back in coins
   (C) Cyrus's deal, because no physical cash was touched
   (D) all three, because in each a pen was obtained

   (B) barter can only ever take place outdoors and never inside a shop
