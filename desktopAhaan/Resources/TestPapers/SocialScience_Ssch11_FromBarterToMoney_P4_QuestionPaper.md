**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Classic traps: barter is goods-for-goods with zero money (any rupee, card, cheque, or UPI rupee instantly disqualifies it); money was invented AFTER barter to cure its faults, not before; a note's worth is trust plus Reserve Bank of India backing, never the paper or any precious metal, so modern coins and notes are fiat money; cheques, cards, and UPI merely move the SAME existing rupees between accounts and create no new currency; and a single named barter defect (double coincidence of wants, no common measure of value, indivisibility, perishability/poor store of value, poor portability) must be matched to exactly the symptom described.
