Q1

The HCF of 2³ × 3² × 5 and 2² × 3³ × 7 is:

  • A2³ × 3³
  • B2² × 3³
  • C2³ × 3² × 5 × 7
  • D2² × 3²✓

Working

✓

HCF takes the LOWEST power of each *common* prime. Common primes are 2 and 3: lowest powers are 2² and 3². So HCF = 2² × 3² = 36. (5 and 7 are not common, so they're excluded.)

Q2

The LCM of 2³ × 3² × 5 and 2² × 3³ × 7 is:

  • A36
  • B7560✓
  • C1080
  • D2520

Working

✓

LCM takes the HIGHEST power of *every* prime present: 2³, 3³, 5, 7. So LCM = 8 × 27 × 5 × 7 = 7560.

Q3

The HCF of 60, 72 and 90 is:

  • A6✓
  • B12
  • C18
  • D30

Working

✓

60 = 2²·3·5, 72 = 2³·3², 90 = 2·3²·5. Common primes 2 and 3 at lowest powers: 2¹ × 3¹ = 6.

Working

Highest powers across 60, 72, 90: 2³ (from 72), 3² (from 72/90), 5 (from 60/90). LCM = 8 × 9 × 5 = 360.

Q11

The largest number that divides 70 and 125 leaving remainders 5 and 8 respectively is:

  • A5
  • B25
  • C13✓
  • D65

Working

✓

Subtract the remainders: 70 − 5 = 65 and 125 − 8 = 117. The divisor must divide both, so take HCF(65, 117). 65 = 5·13, 117 = 9·13 → HCF = 13.

Q12

The greatest number that divides 1251, 9377 and 15628 leaving remainders 1, 2 and 3 respectively is:

  • A625✓
  • B125
  • C25
  • D5

Working

✓

Reduce by the remainders: 1250, 9375, 15628 − 3 = 15625. 1250 = 2·5⁴, 9375 = 3·5⁵, 15625 = 5⁶. The only common prime power is 5⁴ = 625.

Q13

Working

✓

Greatest equal length with no waste = HCF. 320 = 2⁶·5, 480 = 2⁵·3·5, 560 = 2⁴·5·7. Lowest common: 2⁴ × 5 = 80 cm.

Q14

  • D21

Working

✓

Total length ÷ piece length = (320 + 480 + 560) ÷ 80 = 1360 ÷ 80 = 17 pieces.

Q21

Three bells toll at intervals of 9 s, 12 s and 15 s. If they toll together now, they next toll together after:

  • A36 s
  • B90 s
  • C180 s✓
  • D540 s

Working

✓

"Toll together again" → LCM. 9 = 3², 12 = 2²·3, 15 = 3·5. LCM = 2² × 3² × 5 = 180 s (= 3 minutes).

Q31

The LCM of two co-prime numbers is equal to:

  • A1
  • Btheir product✓
  • Cthe larger number
  • Dtheir sum

Working

✓

Co-prime numbers share no prime, so the LCM uses every factor of both → LCM = their product.

  • A75

  • D135

Working

✓

Other number = (HCF × LCM) ÷ one number = (15 × 180) ÷ 45 = 2700 ÷ 45 = 60.

✓

Consecutive numbers share no factor other than 1, so their HCF is always 1 (they are co-prime).
