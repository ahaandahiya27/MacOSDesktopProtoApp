# Sanskrit — Chapter 11: द्वीपेषु रम्यः द्वीपोऽण्डमानः (The Andaman Islands) · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** In द्वीप = द्वि + अप्, the अप् element means **'water'** (द्वि = 'two').

**2. (A)** So द्वीप ('two-watered') describes **land surrounded by water — an island**.

**3. (D)** द्वीपेषु is the locative plural, **'among the islands'**.

**4. (B)** कारागार = कारा ('cell') + गार ('house') = **a prison**.

**5. (B)** कारागार (prison) must not be confused with the Hindi कारखाना, which means a **factory**.

**6. (D)** त्रितलात्मक means **three-storied** (the Cellular Jail's structure).

**7. (A)** त्रितल = त्रि ('three') + तल ('floor/storey').

**8. (C)** प्रवालप्रस्तर = प्रवाल ('coral') + प्रस्तर ('rock') = **coral rock / reefs**.

**9. (A)** सोढवान् ('he bore/endured') comes from the root **सह् ('to bear/endure')**.

**10. (C)** सोढवान् is a **past active participle** ('having borne / who bore'), not a finite verb.

**11. (D)** The past active participle of कृ is **कृतवान्** ('he did / having done').

**12. (B)** The past active participle of गम् is **गतवान्** ('he went / having gone').

**13. (D)** The past active participle of श्रु is **श्रुतवान्** ('he heard').

**14. (B)** The past active participle of दृश् is **दृष्टवान्** ('he saw').

**15. (C)** सोढवान्, कृतवान्, गतवान्, श्रुतवान् all carry the participle suffix **-वान् (the क्तवतु suffix)**.

**16. (A)** सोढवान् दश वर्षाणि कष्टम् means Savarkar **bore the suffering for ten years**.

**17. (C)** Unlike a finite past verb, the participle सोढवान् can also act as an **adjective describing the doer** ('the one who bore').

**18. (A)** दश वर्षाणि means **ten years**.

**19. (B)** वर्षाणि is a **neuter plural** (here the accusative of duration — 'for ten years').

**20. (D)** कल्पनातीत = कल्पना ('imagination') + अतीत ('gone beyond') = **beyond imagination**.

**21. (A)** कल्पनातीतं घोरं कष्टम् describes the suffering as **terrible and beyond imagination**.

**22. (D)** स्वातन्त्र्यवीर = स्वातन्त्र्य ('freedom') + वीर ('hero') = **a freedom-hero**.

**23. (B)** Savarkar endured the jail for the sake of **the Motherland's freedom**.

**24. (C)** The Andamans lie in the **Bay of Bengal**, south-east of the mainland.

**25. (B)** The islands grouped with the Andamans are the **Nicobar Islands**.

**26. (C)** कालापानी NAMES **the Cellular Jail and the exile-across-the-seas it symbolised**.

**27. (A)** Read literally as 'black water', कालापानी misses that it stands for **the dreaded prison and banishment**.

**28. (D)** The English 'Cellular' is kept because **Sanskrit has no native synonym for the cell-by-cell design**.

**29. (D)** The cell-by-cell design isolated each prisoner to **prevent prisoners from communicating**.

**30. (A)** The Cellular Jail was built by the **British**.

**31. (C)** जनजाति means **an indigenous tribal community**.

**32. (B)** जनजाति differs from सामान्यजन because it is **an officially recognised indigenous group (a Scheduled Tribe)**.

**33. (C)** The four tribes named are the Andamanese, Onge, Jarawa and **Sentinelese**.

**34. (B)** The Sentinelese are notable as **one of the world's last uncontacted peoples, protected by law**.

**35. (D)** Protecting the Sentinelese shows India's concern for **indigenous peoples and their way of life**.

**37. (A)** The beauty-imagery includes white sand, coconut palms and **coral reefs**.

**39. (D)** The participle in 'Savarkar, who bore ...' agrees with Savarkar as **masculine singular**.

**40. (C)** The past active participle of पठ् is **पठितवान्** ('he read').

**41. (D)** The past active participle of दा is **दत्तवान्** ('he gave').

**42. (C)** A person described as सोढवान् is one who **endured / bore hardship**.

**43. (A)** द्वीप and 'peninsula' ('almost-island') share the idea of **land related to surrounding water**.

**46. (D)** The कारागार-vs-रम्यता contrast shows the Andamans as a place of **both natural beauty and solemn history**.
