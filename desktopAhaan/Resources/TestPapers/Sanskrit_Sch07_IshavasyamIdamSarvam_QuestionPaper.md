| **Maximum Marks** | 240 |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Questions test the verse's meaning and grammar. Watch the classic traps: ईश means 'Lord' (root ईश्), nothing to do with English '-ish'; ब्रह्म (neuter, the Absolute) differs from ब्रह्मन् (a priest) and ब्रह्मा (the creator-god); सर्वशक्तिमान् uses the -मत्/-वत् suffix ('possessing') to mean ALL-POWERFUL; दास्यन्ति is FUTURE tense ('they shall give', the -स्य- marker), not present or past; and यक्ष/गन्धर्व/किन्नर are SEMI-divine, below the gods. Sanskrit is quoted in Devanagari. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
