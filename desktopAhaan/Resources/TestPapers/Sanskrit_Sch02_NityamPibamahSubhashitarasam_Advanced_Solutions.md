# Sanskrit — Chapter 2: नित्यं पिबामः सुभाषितरसम् · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** विद्यया means **'by knowledge'** (instrumental).

**2. (A)** The -या ending (from the ā-stem विद्या) marks the **instrumental ('by/with')**.

**3. (D)** वपुषा ('by the body') is the instrumental of वपुस्, an irregular **-उस् (consonant) stem** noun.

**4. (B)** वस्त्रेण (a-stem neuter) takes the instrumental ending **-एण/-एन**.

**5. (B)** विनयेन means **'by humility'**.

**6. (D)** The five va-qualities, in the instrumental, are **विद्यया, वपुषा, वाण्या, वस्त्रेण, विनयेन**.

**7. (A)** They use the instrumental because the person is honoured **BY / THROUGH** these qualities.

**8. (C)** हातव्याः is a gerundive meaning **'to be abandoned / given up'**.

**9. (A)** A gerundive (future passive participle) expresses **what SHOULD / MUST be done**.

**10. (C)** The gerundive of कृ is **कर्तव्य** ('to be done / ought to be done').

**11. (D)** The gerundive of गम् is **गन्तव्य** ('to be gone to / worth going to').

**12. (B)** The gerundive of दृश् is **द्रष्टव्य** ('to be seen / worth seeing').

**13. (D)** हातव्य, कर्तव्य and द्रष्टव्य carry the gerundive suffix **-तव्य (also -अनीय / -य)**.

**14. (B)** षड् दोषाः हातव्याः means 'six faults are **to be given up**'.

**15. (C)** The faults are given up by one who desires भूतिम् — **prosperity / well-being**.

**16. (A)** इच्छता (instrumental of the present participle) means **'by (the one) desiring'**.

**17. (C)** सुभाषितम् is **neuter (नपुंसकलिङ्ग), like ज्ञानम्**.

**18. (A)** The neuter nominative plural is **सुभाषितानि**.

**19. (B)** सुभाषिताः is wrong because -आः is **masculine, not neuter** (neuter plural is -आनि).

**20. (D)** Like सुभाषितम्, neuter ज्ञानम् has the plural **ज्ञानानि**.

**21. (A)** सुभाषितम् = सु + भाषितम् = **'well-spoken' (a good saying)**.

**22. (D)** दुर्भाषितम् (दुर् + भाषितम्) means **badly-spoken / harsh speech**.

**23. (B)** सुभाषितरसम् means the **nectar / essence** of good sayings.

**24. (C)** नित्यम् means **daily / always** — 'let us daily drink the nectar of subhashitas'.

**25. (B)** पिबामः ... रसम् is used **metaphorically** ('drink in / imbibe' wisdom).

**26. (C)** A person is honoured by **five (पञ्च)** 'va' qualities.

**27. (A)** **Six (षट्/षड्)** faults are to be given up.

**28. (D)** आलस्यम् and दीर्घसूत्रता are **two DIFFERENT faults** (general laziness vs delaying tasks).

**29. (D)** दीर्घसूत्रता (दीर्घ + सूत्र + ता) means **procrastination** (drawing tasks out long).

**30. (A)** आलस्यम् means **general laziness / inactivity**.

**31. (C)** निद्रा is sleep; तन्द्रा is **drowsiness / lethargy**.

**32. (B)** The sixth fault (after निद्रा, तन्द्रा, भयम्, क्रोधः, आलस्यम्) is **दीर्घसूत्रता**.

**33. (C)** क्रोधः and भयम् are listed among the **six faults to abandon**.

**34. (B)** In the purification verse, the body is cleaned by **water (अद्भिः)**.

**35. (D)** The mind is purified by **truth (सत्येन)**.

**36. (A)** The intellect is purified by **knowledge (ज्ञानेन)**.

**37. (A)** ज्ञानेन is in the **instrumental** (the means of purifying).

**38. (B)** Different things are cleaned by different **means (water, truth, knowledge)**.

**39. (D)** The intellect is cleaned by **knowledge (ज्ञानेन)**, not water.

**40. (C)** विद्यया, वपुषा, ज्ञानेन and सत्येन are all in the **instrumental ('by')**.

**41. (D)** The chapter calls आलस्यम् the great **enemy (रिपुः)** of those who wish to achieve.

**42. (C)** The correct pair: **हातव्य = 'to be given up'; भूति = prosperity**.

**43. (A)** The correct count: **five va-qualities and six faults**.

**44. (B)** सुभाषितम् is **neuter; plural सुभाषितानि**.

**45. (B)** **विद्यया = instrumental ('by knowledge')**.

**46. (D)** The correct pair: **आलस्यम् = laziness; दीर्घसूत्रता = procrastination**.

**47. (C)** The correct pairing: **बुद्धिः → ज्ञानेन (intellect by knowledge); गात्राणि → अद्भिः (body by water)**.

**48. (A)** The correct gerundive: **कृ → कर्तव्य ('to be done')**.

**49. (A)** विद्यया and हातव्य express the **MEANS ('by knowledge') and an OBLIGATION ('to be given up')**.

**50. (D)** 'This book is to be read' uses the gerundive **पठनीय / पठितव्य**.

**51. (C)** The five va-qualities teach that a person is honoured by **a rounded set of inner and outer virtues**.

**52. (B)** The six faults are all **obstacles to success, to be abandoned**.

**53. (C)** नित्यम् ('daily') tells us the 'drinking' of wisdom should be **a daily, regular habit**.

**54. (D)** पिबामः ... रसम् compares subhashitas to **a sweet, nourishing drink for the mind**.

**55. (B)** सुभाषितानि (neuter -आनि) is parallel to **ज्ञानानि, फलानि (other neuter plurals)**.

**56. (A)** The chapter pairs its subhashitas with lessons on **the instrumental, the gerundive (-तव्य), and neuter declension**.

**57. (B)** The deeper message is to **cultivate virtues and abandon self-defeating habits**.

**58. (A)** The best summary: **let us daily imbibe the nectar of wise sayings**.

**59. (C)** The central theme is **subhashitas (wise sayings) on virtues, faults and self-improvement**.

**60. (D)** सुभाषितम् is neuter (plural सुभाषितानि), and आलस्यम् (laziness) ≠ दीर्घसूत्रता (procrastination) — the student is wrong on both.
