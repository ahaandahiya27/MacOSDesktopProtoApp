# Sanskrit — Chapter 7: ईशावास्यम् इदं सर्वम् · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** दास्यन्ति ('they shall give') is in the **future tense (लृट्)**.

**2. (A)** The future is signalled by the marker **-स्य-** (here दा + स्य + अन्ति).

**3. (D)** The future of गम् ('go') is **गमिष्यति** ('he will go').

**4. (B)** The future of कृ ('do') is **करिष्यति** ('he will do').

**5. (B)** The future of भू ('be') is **भविष्यति** ('it will be').

**6. (D)** The future of स्मृ ('remember') is **स्मरिष्यति** ('he will remember').

**7. (A)** दास्यति vs दास्यन्ति differ in **number** ('he will give' vs 'they will give').

**8. (C)** 'They shall give a boon' uses the future **दास्यन्ति**.

**9. (A)** ध्यानेन means **'by / through meditation'** (the means).

**10. (C)** The -एन ending marks the **instrumental** ('by/through').

**11. (D)** ध्यानेन and नामकीर्तनेन tell us **the means by which the wise remember God**.

**12. (B)** 'He writes with the hand' puts हस्त in the instrumental: **हस्तेन**.

**13. (D)** ईशावास्यम् (गloss ईश्वरेण व्याप्तम्) means **'pervaded by the Lord'**.

**14. (B)** ईश and ईश्वर both come from the root ईश्, meaning **'to rule / to own'**.

**15. (C)** Therefore ईश means **the Lord / ruler** — nothing to do with the English '-ish'.

**16. (A)** ईश्वरेण is the instrumental, **'by the Lord'**.

**17. (C)** व्याप्तम् ('pervaded') tells us the Lord is **present throughout everything**.

**18. (A)** सर्वशक्तिमान् = सर्व + शक्ति + -मान् = **'possessing all power' (omnipotent)**.

**19. (B)** The suffix -मत्/-वत् (-मान्) means **'possessing / having'**.

**20. (D)** 'All-knowing' is rendered **सर्वज्ञः** (the -ज्ञ form).

**21. (A)** सर्वशक्तिमान्, बुद्धिमान् and बलवान् all mean **'possessing (the quality)'**.

**22. (D)** आराधनम् means **worship**.

**23. (B)** ध्यानम् means **meditation**.

**24. (C)** नामकीर्तनम् (नाम + कीर्तनम्) means **chanting / singing the Name in praise**.

**25. (B)** The wise respond with **worship, meditation and Name-chanting**.

**26. (C)** यक्ष/गन्धर्व/किन्नर are **semi-divine beings (attendants of the gods)**.

**27. (A)** They rank **below the gods** (semi-divine), not as full gods.

**28. (D)** गन्धर्वाः are associated with **celestial music and song**.

**29. (D)** ईशावास्यमिदं सर्वम् opens the **Isha Upanishad (Ishopanishad)**.

**30. (A)** Since all is pervaded by the Lord, the verse counsels **reverence and care**.

**31. (C)** तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथाः counsels enjoying the world through **renunciation / non-attachment**.

**32. (B)** मा गृधः ('do not covet') warns against **greed for others' wealth**.

**33. (C)** इदम् सर्वम् refers to **the whole world / everything**.

**34. (B)** The future of स्मृ ('they will remember') is **स्मरिष्यन्ति**.

**35. (D)** The future 'will give' (from दा) is **दास्यति**.

**36. (A)** The future tense answers **'what will happen?'**.

**37. (A)** ईश्वरेण (instrumental) shows the Lord as **the one BY whom all is pervaded (the agent)**.

**38. (B)** बुद्धिमन्तः जनाः is the **nominative plural** ('the wise', as subject).

**39. (D)** The chapter pairs the verse with lessons on **the future tense, the instrumental, and the possessive suffix**.

**40. (C)** ब्रह्म (neuter) names **the all-pervading impersonal Absolute**.

**41. (D)** ब्रह्म differs from ब्रह्मन् and ब्रह्मा by **gender (and meaning)**.

**42. (C)** आराधनेन and ध्यानेन are both **instrumentals of means**.

**43. (A)** The future सेविष्यन्ति again shows the marker **-इष्य-/-स्य-**.

**44. (B)** 'God pervades all' matches **ईशावास्यमिदं सर्वम्**.

**45. (B)** **करिष्यति ('he will do')** is the future-tense verb; करोति is present, अकरोत् past, कुरु imperative.

**46. (D)** The correct pair is **ईश = the Lord (root ईश्)**.

**47. (C)** The correct grammar: **दास्यन्ति = future ('they shall give'); ध्यानेन = instrumental ('by meditation')**.

**48. (A)** The correct suffix: **-मत्/-वत् = 'possessing'; सर्वशक्तिमान् = all-powerful**.

**49. (A)** The correct ranking: **यक्ष/गन्धर्व/किन्नर = semi-divine (below the gods)**.

**50. (D)** The correct pair: **ब्रह्म (neuter) = the Absolute; ब्रह्मा (masc) = creator-god**.

**51. (C)** The instrumental names **the means / instrument of an action ('by/with')**.

**52. (B)** 'By meditation' is **ध्यानेन (instrumental)**.

**53. (C)** The future 'they will remember' is **स्मरिष्यन्ति**.

**54. (D)** 'All is pervaded by the Lord' should make a person **reverent and non-greedy**.

**55. (B)** ईश्वर is a fuller form built on the same **root-word** (both from ईश् 'to rule').

**56. (A)** सर्वज्ञः and सर्वशक्तिमान् describe God as **omniscient and omnipotent**.

**57. (B)** The central theme is **the all-pervading presence of the Divine and the reverence it calls for**.

**58. (A)** ईशावास्यम् इदं सर्वम् means **'all this (the whole world) is pervaded by the Lord'**.

**59. (C)** दास्यन्ति tells us the giving of the boon is **yet to come (future)**.

**60. (D)** दास्यन्ति is future ('they shall give'), and ध्यानेन is instrumental ('by meditation') — the student is wrong on both.
