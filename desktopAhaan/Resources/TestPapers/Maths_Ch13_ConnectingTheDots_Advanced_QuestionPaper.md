**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Mean = total ÷ count; the median needs the data SORTED first (and is the average of the two middle values when the count is even); the mode is the most frequent value; the range is max − min. Watch the traps: sort before taking a median, don't confuse mode with middle, and check a bar graph's scale. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
