✓

  • Ahigh pressure
  • Blow pressure✓
  • Cno pressure
  • Dequal pressure

✓

  • Dstops moving

Working

✓

Heated air expands, becomes lighter and rises.

  • Adecreases✓

  • Dbecomes zero

✓

Faster-moving air exerts lower pressure, so increasing wind speed lowers the pressure.
