Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

हितं मनोहारि च दुर्लभं वचः means:

  • Aall speech is useless
  • Bpleasing speech is common
  • Cuseful speech is impossible
  • Drare is speech that is both useful and pleasing✓

Working

✓

The verse means rare is speech that is both useful (हितम्) and pleasing (मनोहारि). The other readings distort it.

Q2

वचः in the verse means:

  • Aaction
  • Bspeech / words✓
  • Cwealth
  • Dcuriosity

Working

✓

वचः means 'speech / words'. Action is क्रिया, wealth अर्थ, and curiosity कुतूहलम्.

Q3

हितम् means:

  • Apleasing
  • Brare
  • Cbeneficial / useful✓
  • Dboastful

Working

✓

हितम् means 'beneficial / useful'. Pleasing is मनोहारि, rare दुर्लभम्, and boastful विकत्थन.

Q4

मनोहारि means:

  • Apleasing (heart-stealing)✓
  • Buseful
  • Crare
  • Dharmful

Working

✓

मनोहारि means 'pleasing' (heart-stealing). Useful is हितम्, rare दुर्लभम्, and harmful अहितम्.

Q5

The verse says such speech (both useful and pleasing) is:

  • Acommon
  • Bimpossible
  • Cuseless
  • Dदुर्लभम् (rare)✓

Working

✓

Such speech is दुर्लभम् (rare). It is not common, impossible, or useless.

Q6

The particle च in हितं मनोहारि च means:

  • A'and' (both qualities together)✓
  • B'or' (either one)
  • C'not'
  • D'why'

Working

✓

च means 'and', demanding both qualities together. It is not 'or', 'not', or 'why'.

Q7

Mistranslating च as 'or' weakens the verse because it would:

  • Amake no difference
  • Bmake the verse harder
  • Clower a near-impossible 'both' standard to an easy 'either' one✓
  • Dchange हितम् into मनोहारि

Working

✓

Reading च as 'or' would lower the demanding 'both' standard to an easy 'either' one. It does make a difference and does not make the verse harder.

Q8

In Sanskrit, का, वा and च mean, respectively:

  • A'and', 'or', 'what'
  • B'or', 'and', 'what'
  • Cinterrogative ('what/which'), 'or', and 'and'✓
  • D'what', 'and', 'or'

Working

✓

का is interrogative, वा is 'or', and च is 'and'. The other orderings are wrong.

Q9

Because of च, the rare speech must be:

  • Aboth beneficial AND pleasing at once✓
  • Beither beneficial or pleasing
  • Conly beneficial
  • Donly pleasing

Working

✓

च requires the speech to be both beneficial and pleasing at once. Not either-or, only useful, or only pleasing.

Q10

Speech that is pleasing but harmful, or useful but harsh, is:

  • Aexactly the rare ideal
  • Balways impossible
  • CNOT the rare ideal the verse praises✓
  • Dthe same as boasting

Working

✓

Pleasing-but-harmful or useful-but-harsh speech is NOT the rare both-and-both ideal. It is not the ideal, impossible, or boasting.
