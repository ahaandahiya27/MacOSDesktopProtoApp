Classic traps: swapping percolation rate with water-holding capacity, confusing the A horizon (loses washed-down material) with the B horizon (gains it), assuming 'more water always helps the crop' regardless of aeration, and treating a faster drain time as proof of coarser texture when depth or volume differs.
