**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Recall that green plants are autotrophs that make food by photosynthesis (chlorophyll traps sunlight; carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen), while parasites, saprotrophs and insectivorous plants get nourishment in special ways. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
