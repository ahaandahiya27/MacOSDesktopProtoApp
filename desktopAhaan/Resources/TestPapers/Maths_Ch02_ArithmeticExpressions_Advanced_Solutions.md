# Mathematics — Chapter 2: Arithmetic Expressions · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (B)** × and ÷ before +, left to right: 6 ÷ 2 = 3, then 3 × 3 = 9. So 12 + 9 = 21. (Strict left-to-right wrongly gives 27.)

**2. (D)** Innermost first: 3 + 2 = 5; then 8 − 5 = 3; then 20 − 3 = 17.

**3. (A)** Each bracket first: 15 − 3 = 12 and 2 + 2 = 4; then 12 ÷ 4 = 3.

**4. (C)** Innermost 4 + 1 = 5; then 9 − 5 = 4; then 2 × 4 = 8; finally 5 + 8 = 13.

**5. (B)** Inner 10 − 5 = 5; then 2 × 5 = 10; then 100 ÷ 10 = 10.

**6. (D)** × and ÷ first: 7 × 4 = 28 and 6 ÷ 2 = 3. Then 28 − 3 + 1 = 26.

**7. (A)** Inner 6 + 4 = 10; × 2 = 20; − 5 = 15.

**8. (C)** 4 × 5 = 20 first; then 3 + 20 − 2 = 21.

**9. (D)** Division left to right: 48 ÷ 6 = 8; then 8 ÷ 2 = 4. (Not 48 ÷ 3.)

**10. (B)** Innermost 5 − 3 = 2; then 4 × 2 = 8; then 3 + 8 = 11; then 2 × 11 = 22.

**11. (A)** Bracket 5 + 3 − 2 = 6; then 18 − 6 = 12.

**12. (C)** Bracket 10 − 4 = 6; then 25 − 6 = 19.

**13. (D)** Innermost 6 − 2 = 4; then 12 − 4 = 8; then 40 − 8 = 32.

**14. (B)** The minus flips the bracket: −(3 − 8) = −(−5) = 5.

**15. (A)** Bracket 5 − 8 = −3; then 2 × (−3) = −6; then 50 − (−6) = 56.

**16. (C)** Innermost 10 − 3 = 7; then 5 − 7 = −2; then 30 − (−2) = 32.

**17. (D)** Innermost 4 + 1 = 5; 10 − 5 = 5; 20 − 5 = 15; 100 − 15 = 85.

**18. (B)** Subtraction left to right: 12 − 3 = 9, 9 − 4 = 5, 5 − 2 = 3.

**19. (A)** The minus flips the bracket: 7 − (2 − 9) = 7 − (−7) = 14.

**20. (C)** Work outward: −(−6) = 6 inside, then the outer minus gives −(6) = −6.

**21. (D)** 7 × (100 − 2) = 700 − 14 = 686 (distributive shortcut).

**22. (B)** Factor out 25: 25 × (16 + 4) = 25 × 20 = 500.

**23. (A)** Factor out 47: 47 × (99 + 1) = 47 × 100 = 4700.

**24. (A)** 6 × (50 + 3) = 300 + 18 = 318.

**25. (A)** Multiplying a sum term-by-term, a × (b + c) = a×b + a×c, is the distributive property.

**26. (B)** Regrouping the *same* numbers without reordering them is the associative property of addition.

**27. (D)** Reorder as (25 × 4) × 8 = 100 × 8 = 800.

**28. (C)** Pair to tens: (17 + 23) + (38 + 12) = 40 + 50 = 90.

**29. (A)** 12 − 7 = 5. Subtraction is not commutative, so 7 − 12 = −5 ≠ 5. The other three all equal 5.

**30. (D)** 35 × 101 = 35 × 100 + 35 = 3500 + 35 = 3535.

**31. (B)** Thrice 8 is 24; five less is 24 − 5 = 19.

**32. (D)** 6 × (4 + 9) = 6 × 13 = 78. (Do the bracket first, then multiply.)

**33. (A)** Difference 15 − 7 = 8; double it: 2 × 8 = 16.

**34. (C)** 4 × 12 + 3 × 25 = 48 + 75 = ₹123.

**35. (B)** (20 − 6) ÷ (3 + 4) = 14 ÷ 7 = 2.

**36. (D)** 100 − 3 × 18 = 100 − 54 = ₹46.

**37. (A)** Product 5 × 4 = 20; seven more: 20 + 7 = 27.

**38. (C)** 5 × 45 − 25 = 225 − 25 = ₹200.

**39. (B)** Sum 18 + 26 = 44; half of it: 44 ÷ 2 = 22.

**40. (D)** 36 ÷ 4 = 9 per team; 9 − 2 = 7 after two leave.

**41. (C)** Values: 2 + 3×4 = 14; 2×3 + 4 = 10; 2×3×4 = 24; (2+3)×4 = 20. Greatest is 24.

**42. (A)** Try +: 8 + (4 ÷ 2) = 8 + 2 = 10. ✓ (× gives 16, − gives 6, ÷ gives 4.)

**44. (D)** 48 × (100 − 1) = 4800 − 48 = 4752.

**45. (C)** (12 + 8) ÷ (12 − 8) = 20 ÷ 4 = 5.

**46. (A)** Brackets: 4 + 5 = 9 and 6 − 1 = 5. Then 3 × 9 − 2 × 5 = 27 − 10 = 17.

**47. (B)** Inner 4 − 1 = 3; then 3 + 3 = 6; then 2 × 6 = 12; then 10 − 12 = −2.

**49. (B)** Distributing: 4 × (10 − 3) = 4 × 10 − 4 × 3. (Both terms get the ×4 — the classic trap is to multiply only the first.)

**51. (A)** Innermost 10 − 5 = 5; then 20 − 5 = 15; then 50 − 15 = 35; finally 35 ÷ 5 = 7.

**52. (C)** × and ÷ first: 2 × 2 = 4 and 2 ÷ 2 = 1. Then 2 + 4 − 1 = 5.

**53. (D)** Brackets: 7 + 3 = 10 and 7 − 3 = 4; then 10 × 4 = 40.

**54. (B)** 144 ÷ 12 = 12 and 12 × 0 = 0; then 12 + 0 = 12.

**55. (A)** Brackets: 8 − 3 = 5 and 2 × 3 = 6; then 5 + 6 = 11; then 5 × 11 = 55.

**59. (A)** Inner 8 − 2 = 6; then 24 ÷ 6 = 4; then 4 × 5 = 20; then 6 + 20 = 26.
