|---|---|
| **Subject** | Science (NCERT Class 7) |

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Use the conversions 1 km/h = 5/18 m/s and 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h. No calculator is needed — all numbers are designed to work by hand. Most questions need two to four reasoning steps; watch for traps. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.

1. Which of the following is an example of **periodic** motion that is **not** oscillatory?
   (A) A swinging pendulum
   (B) A vibrating guitar string
   (C) The Earth revolving around the Sun
   (D) A child going to-and-fro on a swing

2. A bus covers the first 100 m in 5 s and the next 100 m in 4 s. Its motion is:
   (A) uniform, because the distances are equal
   (B) non-uniform, because equal distances take unequal times
   (C) uniform, because it moves in a straight line
   (D) at rest throughout

3. A snail crawls 6 m in 2 minutes. Its speed is:
   (A) 0.05 m/s
   (B) 0.5 m/s
   (C) 3 m/s
   (D) 0.1 m/s

4. Convert 72 km/h into m/s.
   (A) 25 m/s
   (B) 200 m/s
   (C) 18 m/s
   (D) 20 m/s

5. Convert 15 m/s into km/h.
   (A) 45 km/h
   (B) 54 km/h
   (C) 540 km/h
   (D) 4.17 km/h

6. A car goes from town A to town B at 60 km/h and returns along the same road at 40 km/h. Its **average speed for the whole trip** is:
   (A) 50 km/h
   (B) 100 km/h
   (C) 24 km/h
   (D) 48 km/h

7. Anya and Ravi both leave home for a town 60 km away. Anya drives at a steady 40 km/h the whole way. Ravi drives the first 30 km at 30 km/h and the last 30 km at 60 km/h. Who reaches the town first?
   (A) Anya arrives first
   (B) Ravi arrives first
   (C) they arrive at the same time
   (D) it cannot be determined

8. A cyclist's distance–time graph has three parts: 0–10 s a straight slanted line, 10–20 s a horizontal line, 20–30 s a steeper slanted line. The cyclist:
   (A) moves, then stops, then moves faster
   (B) speeds up the whole time
   (C) moves, then slows down, then stops
   (D) stays at rest throughout

9. A pendulum completes 20 oscillations in 40 s. Its time period is:
   (A) 2 s
   (B) 0.5 s
   (C) 20 s
   (D) 800 s

10. The SI unit of time is the:
    (A) minute
    (B) hour
    (C) second
    (D) day

11. A train moves at 90 km/h. How far does it travel in 10 seconds?
    (A) 900 m
    (B) 150 m
    (C) 25 m
    (D) 250 m

12. A cyclist rides 30 km in 2 h, rests for 1 h, then rides 20 km in 1 h. The average speed for the whole journey is:
    (A) 16.7 km/h
    (B) 12.5 km/h
    (C) 25 km/h
    (D) 10 km/h

13. Increasing the length of a simple pendulum will:
    (A) decrease its time period
    (B) leave its time period unchanged
    (C) stop it from swinging
    (D) increase its time period

14. Which ancient device tells time using the position of the Sun's shadow?
    (A) sundial
    (B) water clock
    (C) sand clock
    (D) pendulum clock

15. Two objects P and Q are plotted as straight lines from the origin on a distance–time graph. P is steeper than Q. Which object is faster?
    (A) Q
    (B) P
    (C) both equal
    (D) cannot be decided

16. Object X moves at 36 km/h; object Y moves at 12 m/s. Which is faster?
    (A) X
    (B) both equal
    (C) Y
    (D) cannot compare different units

17. A car travels at 54 km/h for 20 minutes. The distance covered is:
    (A) 27 km
    (B) 1080 km
    (C) 18 km
    (D) 9 km

18. How long does a 240 km journey take at a steady 80 km/h?
    (A) 3 h
    (B) 320 h
    (C) 2 h
    (D) 0.33 h

19. Which of these timekeepers would **fail** to work on a cloudy night?
    (A) water clock
    (B) sand clock
    (C) pendulum clock
    (D) sundial

20. A stone tied to a string and whirled around in a circle shows:
    (A) rectilinear motion
    (B) circular motion
    (C) oscillatory motion
    (D) random motion

21. A distance–time line that curves upward (its slope keeps increasing) indicates:
    (A) uniform motion
    (B) non-uniform motion with increasing speed
    (C) rest
    (D) backward motion

22. A car travels at 40 km/h for 2 h and then at 60 km/h for 2 h. Its average speed for the whole trip is:
    (A) 48 km/h
    (B) 100 km/h
    (C) 50 km/h
    (D) 52 km/h

23. Two pendulums have the **same length** but **different bob masses**. Their time periods are:
    (A) equal
    (B) larger for the heavier bob
    (C) larger for the lighter bob
    (D) zero for the heavier bob

24. A table shows time (s): 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and distance (m): 0, 5, 10, 15, 20. The motion is:
    (A) 20 m/s, uniform
    (B) 5 m/s, non-uniform
    (C) speeding up
    (D) 5 m/s, uniform

25. A table shows time (s): 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 and distance (m): 0, 10, 20, 20, 20. Between t = 2 s and t = 4 s the object is:
    (A) moving at 10 m/s
    (B) moving backward
    (C) speeding up
    (D) at rest

26. A pendulum makes 30 oscillations in 60 s. Its string is then lengthened so that its time period becomes 3 s. How many oscillations will it now make in 60 s?
    (A) 30
    (B) 20
    (C) 45
    (D) 90

27. Three runners move at these speeds: P at 5 m/s, Q at 21.6 km/h, R covers 300 m in 1 minute. Who is the fastest?
    (A) P
    (B) R
    (C) Q
    (D) all three are equal

28. A car covers 360 km: the first 180 km at 90 km/h and the next 180 km at 60 km/h. Its average speed is:
    (A) 72 km/h
    (B) 75 km/h
    (C) 70 km/h
    (D) 80 km/h

29. Arrange these three speeds in increasing order: 10 m/s, 30 km/h, 720 m/min.
    (A) 30 km/h < 10 m/s < 720 m/min
    (B) 10 m/s < 30 km/h < 720 m/min
    (C) 720 m/min < 10 m/s < 30 km/h
    (D) all three are equal

30. Pendulum X is longer than pendulum Y. In one minute, which one makes **more** oscillations?
    (A) X
    (B) both equal
    (C) it depends on the bob mass
    (D) Y

31. A sprinter runs 100 m in 12.5 s. The average speed is:
    (A) 12.5 m/s
    (B) 8 m/s
    (C) 80 m/s
    (D) 0.125 m/s

32. A car moves at a steady 54 km/h. How many **metres** does it cover in 2 minutes?
    (A) 108 m
    (B) 6480 m
    (C) 1800 m
    (D) 900 m

33. A ball rolling **up** a slope covers 5 m in the 1st second, 3 m in the 2nd second and 1 m in the 3rd second. Its motion is:
    (A) uniform
    (B) non-uniform, speeding up
    (C) non-uniform, slowing down
    (D) at rest

34. Two cars start from the same point. On a distance–time graph their lines cross at t = 3 s. At that instant the two cars:
    (A) have the same speed
    (B) both stop
    (C) reverse direction
    (D) are at the same distance from the start

35. Two cars cover the same distance: car A takes 2 h and car B takes 3 h. Which is faster, and in what ratio are their speeds (A : B)?

    (B) B is faster, 2 : 3
    (C) A is faster, 2 : 3
    (D) they are equally fast

    (A) speed
    (B) total distance travelled
    (C) time
    (D) fuel level

37. On a distance–time graph, a curve that becomes **less steep** (flattens out) over time means the object is:
    (A) speeding up
    (B) slowing down
    (C) at rest
    (D) moving at constant speed

38. A train runs 120 km in 2 h, halts 20 minutes at a station, then runs another 90 km in 1 h 40 min. Its average speed for the whole trip is:
    (A) 52.5 km/h
    (B) 60 km/h
    (C) 70 km/h
    (D) 63 km/h

39. Which statement about motion types is correct?
    (A) every periodic motion is also oscillatory
    (B) "periodic" and "oscillatory" mean exactly the same thing
