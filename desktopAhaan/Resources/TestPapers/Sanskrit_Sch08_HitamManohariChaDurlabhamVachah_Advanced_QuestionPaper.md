**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it drills the particle च ('and') against वा ('or'), तु ('but') and एव ('only'); the distributive -शस् adverbs (क्षणशः 'moment by moment', कणशः 'particle by particle', शतशः 'by hundreds'); the possessive suffix -वत्/-मत् (क्रियावान् 'action-possessing = a doer', धनवान्, बुद्धिमान्); the prefix दुर्- ('hard / bad', दुर्लभ vs the contrast सुलभ) and the दुर्लभ-vs-अशक्य distinction; and the subhashitas' moral (gradual effort; the doer over the boaster). Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
