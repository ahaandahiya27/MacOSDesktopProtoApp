**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Reason from *how* landforms come to be and *what* they do — plate movement, rivers, rainfall and rain-shadow. Watch the traps: a "desert" means low rainfall (Ladakh is a cold desert), young Himalayas still rise while the Aravallis are worn stubs, west-coast rivers make estuaries (not deltas), and coral Lakshadweep is nothing like the tectonic Andamans. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
