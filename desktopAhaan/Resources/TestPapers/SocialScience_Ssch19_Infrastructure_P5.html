Worked Solutions

1. (C) Physical infrastructure covers transport, utilities, communication and energy. The fibre-optic backbone is physical (communication) and the sewage-treatment plant is physical (utility) — two items. The children's park and the magistrate's court are people-serving social infrastructure (recreation and justice), so the count is two, not three. The 'only the sewage plant' answer wrongly demotes the buried network for being invisible, but the split is by function, not visibility, so a communication network still counts. 'All four' confuses funding source with category — sharing a budget does not make a court or a park physical.

2. (C) Infra ('below') + structura ('a structure') give the figurative sense of the foundational base supporting everything built on top — it is not a literal demand that the thing sit under the soil. Highways (above ground) and schools (people-serving) are still infrastructure. The 'only physically below ground' reading takes the metaphor literally and wrongly excludes roads. 'Shared between distant places' misdefines infra as 'between' (that is the tele/inter idea, not infra). 'Tallest structures' inverts the meaning entirely, since infra is 'below', not 'above'.

3. (C) Classification follows what a facility does: a hospital serves people's health (social), and a power grid supplies energy (physical). Dependence does not transfer category — the hospital draws on energy infrastructure yet remains social itself. Saying the hospital 'becomes' energy infrastructure confuses using power with generating it. Saying the grid 'becomes social' inverts the same error in the other direction. Treating only roads as infrastructure ignores both arms — social services and energy systems are both basic facilities a country needs.

4. (A) An expressway moves people and goods, so it is physical transport; a hospital serves health, so it is social; a piped-water main supplies a basic service to homes, so it is a physical utility. The order is therefore physical transport, social, physical utility. The option that ends 'utility, social' swaps the hospital and the water main.
