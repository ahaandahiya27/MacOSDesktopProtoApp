**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Factor into primes first: for the **HCF** take the LOWEST power of each *common* prime; for the **LCM** take the HIGHEST power of *every* prime that appears. Use HCF × LCM = product of the two numbers, but only for **two** numbers. For "leaves a remainder" problems, subtract the remainder first (HCF of the reduced numbers) or add it last (LCM + remainder). This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
