**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Questions test the Gita 3.14 cycle and its grammar. Watch the classic traps: भूतानि means 'beings / creatures' (root भू), NOT 'ghosts'; अन्नात् is the ABLATIVE ('from food', a source not a maker), and अन्नाद् in अन्नाद्भवन्ति is the same case after sandhi (त् -> द् before भ्); जानीयात् is the OPTATIVE ('one should know'), not past tense; ब्रह्म (neuter) is the all-pervading Absolute, not the creator-god ब्रह्मा or the priest ब्रह्मन्. Keep the cycle order straight: action -> yajna -> rain -> food -> beings. Sanskrit in Devanagari. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
