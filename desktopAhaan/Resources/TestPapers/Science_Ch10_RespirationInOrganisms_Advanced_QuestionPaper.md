**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Remember that respiration releases energy by breaking down glucose; aerobic respiration uses oxygen (giving carbon dioxide + water + energy) while anaerobic respiration does not (yeast → alcohol + CO₂, muscles → lactic acid). Breathing is only the taking-in and giving-out of air. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
