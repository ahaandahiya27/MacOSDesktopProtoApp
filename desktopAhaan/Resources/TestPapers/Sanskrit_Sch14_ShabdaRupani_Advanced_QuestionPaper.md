**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes beyond Paper 1: it asks for the FULL paradigm of देव (अकारान्त), कवि (इकारान्त) and गुरु (उकारान्त) across all eight forms AND all three numbers (एक/द्वि/बहु), for the case+number behind a given form, and for the right form to slot into a Sanskrit sentence. Remember: the dual (द्विवचन) is for exactly two; each case has its own ending and one job (देवेन 'by', देवाय 'to/for', देवात् 'from', देवस्य 'of', देवे 'in'); the genitive+locative DUAL share one form (देवयोः); the instrumental, dative and ablative DUAL share one form (देवाभ्याम्); and the vocative is not always the nominative (हे देव!, हे गुरो!, हे कवे!). Sanskrit is quoted in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
