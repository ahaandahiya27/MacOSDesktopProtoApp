# Sanskrit — Chapter 4: न लभ्यते चेत् आम्लं द्राक्षाफलम् (The Fox and the Grapes) · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** In उत्पतति, the prefix उत्- means **'up'**.

**2. (A)** A prefix (उपसर्ग) attaches to a root and **can change or colour the root's meaning**.

**3. (D)** पत् ('fall') + नि- ('down') gives निपतति = **falls down**.

**4. (B)** गम् ('go') + आ- ('towards') gives आगच्छति = **comes (towards)**.

**5. (B)** गम् + प्र- ('forth') gives प्रगच्छति = **goes forth / sets out**.

**6. (D)** उत्पतति, निपतति and आपतति differ only in their **prefix (उत् 'up', नि 'down', आ 'towards')**.

**7. (A)** उत्पतति is in the **present** tense ('he leaps up').

**8. (C)** In उदपतत्, the अ- is the **past-tense augment (लङ्)**.

**9. (A)** उत्- is a **prefix**; the extra अ- (उद-अ-पतत्) is the **past-tense augment**.

**10. (C)** Both keep the prefix उत्-, so both are about **upward** motion.

**11. (D)** In द्राक्षा + लता, लता means **a creeper / climbing plant (vine)**.

**12. (B)** द्राक्षालता is the whole **grape-vine**; द्राक्षाफलम् is the **grapes (fruit)**.

**13. (D)** Using द्राक्षालता for one English word shows Sanskrit's love of **compounds (joining words)**.

**14. (B)** चेत् means **'if'**.

**15. (C)** चेत् (and यदि) introduces a **condition ('if ...')**.

**16. (A)** न लभ्यते चेत् means **'if (it) is not obtained'**.

**17. (C)** लभ्यते with न means **'is not obtained'** (passive present).

**18. (A)** यदि pairs with **तर्हि ('then')**.

**19. (B)** वामतः means **on the left**.

**20. (D)** The -तः (तसिल्) suffix forms adverbs of **direction / place ('on the ... side')**.

**21. (A)** दक्षिणतः means **on the right**.

**22. (D)** पुरतः (अग्रतः) means **in front**.

**23. (B)** पृष्ठतः means **behind**.

**24. (C)** The -तः adverbs answer **'in which direction / on which side?'**.

**25. (B)** पिपासा means **thirst (the wish to drink)**.

**26. (C)** पिपासा (पातुम् इच्छा) comes from the root **पा ('to drink')**.

**27. (A)** बुभुक्षा means **hunger (the wish to eat)**.

**28. (D)** बुभुक्षा comes from the root **भुज् ('to eat')**.

**29. (D)** पिपासा and बुभुक्षा are 'wish-to-X' nouns on the roots **पा (drink) and भुज् (eat)**.

**30. (A)** जिज्ञासा (from ज्ञा) means **the wish to know (curiosity)**.

**31. (C)** श्रान्तः / क्लान्तः both mean **tired / weary**.

**32. (B)** खिन्नः means **sad / dejected**.

**33. (C)** शृगालः is glossed लोमशा, pointing to its **furry / hairy body**.

**34. (B)** The opening shows a fox that is **tired, sad and thirsty**.

**35. (D)** एकवारम्, द्विवारम्, पुनः पुनः means **once, twice, again and again**.

**36. (A)** पुनः पुनः उत्पतति means the fox **leaps up again and again**.

**37. (A)** Failing to reach them, the fox calls the grapes आम्लम् = **sour**.

**38. (B)** आम्लम् (sour) contrasts with मधुरम् = **sweet**.

**39. (D)** The verdict आम्लम् is a reaction to **failing to reach the grapes**.

**40. (C)** The moral is: **do not belittle ('call sour') what you fail to get**.

**41. (D)** The fox's real mistake is **the dishonest excuse, not the failure to jump**.

**42. (C)** Calling it 'sour' because you couldn't get it illustrates **self-deception / a false excuse**.

**43. (A)** सः वने भ्रमति puts वन in the **locative (वने)** to mean 'in'.

**44. (B)** The correct prefixes: **उत्- = 'up'; नि- = 'down'**.

**45. (B)** Correct: **उत्- is a prefix ('up') and the verb is present tense**.

**46. (D)** The correct split: **द्राक्षालता = grape (द्राक्षा) + creeper (लता)**.

**47. (C)** The correct adverbs: **वामतः = on the left; दक्षिणतः = on the right**.

**48. (A)** The correct wish-nouns: **पिपासा = thirst (पा); बुभुक्षा = hunger (भुज्)**.

**49. (A)** The correct conditional: **चेत् / यदि = 'if'**.

**50. (D)** The correct tastes: **आम्लम् = sour; मधुरम् = sweet**.

**51. (C)** The chapter pairs the fable with lessons on **prefixes (उपसर्ग), the conditional चेत्, direction-adverbs (-तः), and wish-nouns**.

**52. (B)** सः गृहम् आगच्छति uses आ- to turn 'go' into **'come' (motion towards)**.

**53. (C)** The fox calling unreachable grapes 'sour' is the origin of the idiom **'sour grapes'**.

**54. (D)** The chapter says the failed jump is **no shame — the dishonest excuse is the fault**.

**55. (B)** The 'if ... then ...' uses **यदि ... तर्हि (conditional)**.

**56. (A)** Both वने and वामतः answer **a 'where?' question**.

**57. (B)** श्रान्तः, क्लान्तः and खिन्नः paint the fox as **weary and dejected**.

**58. (A)** The best summary: **do not dishonestly belittle what you cannot get**.

**59. (C)** The central theme is **the Fox-and-Grapes fable and its moral against self-deceiving excuses**.

**60. (D)** उत्पतति is present ('leaps up'; उत्- is the prefix 'up'), and वामतः means 'on the left' (दक्षिणतः is 'on the right') — the student is wrong on both.
