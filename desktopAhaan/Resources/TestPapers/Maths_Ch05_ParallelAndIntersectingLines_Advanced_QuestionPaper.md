**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Chase angles step by step using linear pairs (sum 180°) and vertically-opposite equalities. Corresponding and alternate angles are equal ONLY when the lines are parallel; co-interior (same-side interior) angles are SUPPLEMENTARY, not equal.
