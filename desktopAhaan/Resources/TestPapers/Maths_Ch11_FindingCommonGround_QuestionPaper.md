**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Decide first whether the problem asks for the largest number that DIVIDES the given numbers (HCF — for sharing/cutting/tiling) or the smallest number they DIVIDE INTO (LCM — for bells/lights/cycles meeting again). Beware the classic traps: HCF is never bigger than the smaller number, LCM equals the product only for co-prime pairs, and for the HCF you take the LOWEST powers of the common primes while the LCM takes the HIGHEST powers of all primes. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
