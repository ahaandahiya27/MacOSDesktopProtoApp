Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

In the present (लट्) table of पठ्, the form पठसि is the:

  • Aप्रथमपुरुष एकवचन ('he reads')
  • Bउत्तमपुरुष एकवचन ('I read')
  • Cमध्यमपुरुष एकवचन ('you read', one person)✓
  • Dमध्यमपुरुष बहुवचन ('you all read')

Working

✓

पठसि carries the ending **-सि**, the मध्यमपुरुष एकवचन (second-person singular) marker, so it means 'you (one person) read'. The -ति ending would be third person, -मि would be first person.

Q2

The उत्तमपुरुष द्विवचन (first-person dual, 'we two read') of पठ् is:

  • Aपठावः✓
  • Bपठामः
  • Cपठामि
  • Dपठथः

Working

✓

The first-person (उत्तमपुरुष) dual of पठ् is **पठावः** ('we two read') — ending -वः. पठामः is the plural ('we all read') and पठामि is the singular ('I read').

Q3

पठथः is the present-tense:

  • Aप्रथमपुरुष बहुवचन
  • Bउत्तमपुरुष एकवचन
  • Cप्रथमपुरुष एकवचन
  • Dमध्यमपुरुष द्विवचन ('you two read')✓

Working

✓

पठथः ends in -थः, the मध्यमपुरुष द्विवचन (second-person dual) ending: 'you two read'.

Q4

The उत्तमपुरुष बहुवचन ('we all read') of पठ् is:

  • Aपठथ
  • Bपठामः✓
  • Cपठन्ति
  • Dपठतः

Working

✓

The first-person plural ('we all read') is **पठामः**, ending -मः. पठन्ति is third-person plural ('they read').

Q5

Which row of the लट् table is correctly ordered (एकवचन, द्विवचन, बहुवचन) for मध्यमपुरुष?

  • Aपठति, पठतः, पठन्ति
  • Bपठसि, पठथः, पठथ✓
  • Cपठामि, पठावः, पठामः
  • Dपठसि, पठावः, पठन्ति

Working

✓

The मध्यमपुरुष (second-person) row in एकवचन/द्विवचन/बहुवचन order is **पठसि, पठथः, पठथ** (-सि, -थः, -थ). The पठति/पठतः/पठन्ति row is third person; पठामि/पठावः/पठामः is first person.

Q6

पठथ (without visarga) differs from पठथः (with visarga) only in:

  • Atense (present vs past)
  • Bperson
  • Cpada-class
  • Dnumber (बहुवचन 'you all' vs द्विवचन 'you two')✓

Working

✓

Both are second person; the difference is number. **पठथ** (no visarga) is बहुवचन, 'you all read'; पठथः (with visarga, -थः) is द्विवचन, 'you two read'.

Q7

To say 'You (one friend) read well', the verb पठ् must take the form:

  • Aपठसि✓
  • Bपठति
  • Cपठामि
  • Dपठन्ति

Working

✓

Addressing one person ('you read') needs the second-person singular **पठसि**. पठति would be 'he reads'.

Q8

The full मध्यमपुरुष singular/dual/plural of पठ् has the personal endings:

  • A-ति, -तः, -अन्ति
  • B-मि, -वः, -मः
  • C-सि, -थः, -थ✓
  • D-ते, -एते, -अन्ते

Working

✓

The मध्यमपुरुष (second-person) personal endings for singular/dual/plural are **-सि, -थः, -थ**. The -ति/-तः/-अन्ति set is third person; -मि/-वः/-मः is first person; -ते/-एते/-अन्ते is atmanepada.

Q9

अपठत् ('he read') belongs to which लकार?

  • Aलङ् (imperfect past)✓
  • Bलट् (present)
  • Cलृट् (future)
  • Dलोट् (imperative)

Working

✓

The initial अ- augment plus the ending -त् marks **लङ् (imperfect past)**: अपठत् = 'he read'. The plain present would be पठति.

Q10

The प्रथमपुरुष बहुवचन of पठ् in लङ् (past, 'they read') is:

  • Aपठन्ति
  • Bअपठत्
  • Cअपठन्✓
  • Dअपठताम्

Working

✓

The past-tense (लङ्) third-person plural 'they read' is **अपठन्** — augment अ- + plural ending -अन्. पठन्ति is the present 'they read'.

Q11

'He will read' (पठ्, future, लृट्, 3rd-singular) is:

  • Aपठति

  • Cपठतु
  • Dपठिष्यति✓

Working

✓

The future (लृट्) third-singular of पठ् is **पठिष्यति** ('he will read'), formed with the future infix -ष्य-.

Q12

The -ष्य- inserted in पठिष्यति, पठिष्यन्ति marks the:

  • Apast tense
  • Bfuture tense (लृट्)✓
  • Cimperative
  • Dplural number

Working

✓

The infix **-ष्य-** (as in पठिष्यति, पठिष्यन्ति) is the सार्वधातुक future marker — it builds the **future tense (लृट्)**.

Q13

पठतु ('let him read') is the प्रथमपुरुष एकवचन of which लकार?

  • Aलट् (present)
  • Bलङ् (past)
  • Cलृट् (future)
  • Dलोट् (imperative)✓

Working

✓

पठतु ('let him read') is the third-singular of the **imperative, लोट्-लकार** — used for commands and wishes.

Q14

'Read!' addressed to one friend (पठ्, लोट्, मध्यमपुरुष एकवचन) is simply:

  • Aपठतु
  • Bपठ✓
  • Cपठसि
  • Dपठानि

Working

✓

A command to one person ('Read!') is the bare imperative second-singular **पठ**. पठतु would be 'let him read' (third person).

Q15

पठेत् ('he should read / may he read') is the 3rd-singular of which mood?

  • Aलट् (present)
  • Bलृट् (future)
  • Cविधिलिङ् (optative)✓
  • Dलङ् (past)

Working

✓

पठेत् ('he should/may read') is the third-singular of the **optative, विधिलिङ्-लकार**, recognised by the -ए- mood-sign before the ending.

Q16

The optative (विधिलिङ्) is used to express:

  • A'should / would / may' (advice, possibility)✓
  • Ba completed past action
  • Ca plain present fact
  • Da question

Working

✓

The optative (विधिलिङ्) expresses **'should / would / may'** — advice, possibility, or a polite wish — not a plain fact or a finished action.

Q17

  • Cपठति, अपठत्, पठिष्यति, पठतु✓
  • Dपठतु, पठिष्यति, अपठत्, पठति

Working

✓

The four sample third-singulars line up as **पठति (लट्, present), अपठत् (लङ्, past), पठिष्यति (लृट्, future), पठतु (लोट्, imperative)**.

Q18

In अपठन् the final न् and the initial अ- together signal:

  • Apast tense (लङ्), third-person plural✓

  • Cfuture tense, dual

अपठन् combines the past augment अ- with the plural ending -अन्, so it is **लङ् (past), third-person plural** — 'they read'.

Q19

Q20
