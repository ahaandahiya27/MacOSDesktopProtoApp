**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Reason rather than guess: use parity to rule out impossibilities (an odd count of odd numbers gives an odd sum), the "add the two previous terms" rule for the Virahāṅka–Fibonacci sequence, digit-sum tests for divisibility, and place-value logic for digit puzzles.
