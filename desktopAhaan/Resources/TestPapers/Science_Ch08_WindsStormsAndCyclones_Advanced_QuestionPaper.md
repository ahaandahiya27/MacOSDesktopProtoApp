**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Remember the key ideas: air exerts pressure; warm air is lighter and rises; wind blows from high pressure to low pressure; faster-moving air has lower pressure; and uneven heating of land, water and the Earth drives all winds. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
