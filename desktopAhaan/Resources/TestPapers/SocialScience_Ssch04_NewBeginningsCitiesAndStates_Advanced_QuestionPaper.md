**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Reason from cause to effect — how iron tools and farming surplus fed the "Second Urbanisation," how janapadas grew into mahaajanapadas, and how monarchies differed from the gana-sanghas. Watch the traps: the *First* Urbanisation was Harappan, the *Second* was in the Ganga plains a thousand years later; a *gana-sangha* is a republic where the assembly even chose the rajaa, unlike a hereditary monarchy; punch-marked coins were *silver* with stamped symbols. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
