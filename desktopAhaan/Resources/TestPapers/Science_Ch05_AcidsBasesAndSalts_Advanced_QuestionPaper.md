**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Recall that acids are sour and turn blue litmus red; bases are bitter, feel soapy and turn red litmus blue; a neutral substance changes neither. Acid + base → salt + water (neutralisation, which releases heat). This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
