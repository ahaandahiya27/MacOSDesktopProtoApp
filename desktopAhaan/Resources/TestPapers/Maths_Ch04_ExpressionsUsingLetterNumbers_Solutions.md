## Worked Solutions

**1. (B)** Shabnam's age is always Aftab's age + 3. With Aftab's age = a, Shabnam's age = a + 3. (3a would mean three times Aftab's age; 3 − a reverses the relation.)

**2. (C)** By convention 4n is shorthand for 4 × n — the multiplication sign is simply dropped. It does NOT mean 4 + n; with n = 5, 4n = 20, not 9.

**3. (A)** Substitute m = 2: 5m + 3 = 5 × 2 + 3 = 10 + 3 = 13. (Reading 5m as 5 + m gives 10 — the trap; 5(m+3) gives 25.)

**4. (C)** Dropping the × sign and writing the number before the letter gives 7k. (k7 violates the number-first convention; 7 + k changes the operation.)

**5. (D)** The letter carries its whole sign: 10 − a = 10 − (−4) = 10 + 4 = 14. (Ignoring the negative sign gives the trap 6; 10 × (−4) would give −40.)

**6. (D)** Perimeter of a square = 4 × side = 4q. (q + 4 adds instead of multiplies; q⁴ raises to a power.)

**7. (D)** l + b + l + b = (l + l) + (b + b) = 2l + 2b. The like terms l + l make 2l, not l²; and l and b are unlike, so they stay separate (not 2lb).

**8. (A)** The nth term is 4n, so the 29th term is 4 × 29 = 116. (33 comes from 29 + 4; "429" mistakenly writes the digits side by side.)

**9. (D)** Collect like terms: a-terms 5a − 2a = 3a; b-terms 3b + 7b − b = 9b. Total 3a + 9b. (3a + 11b forgets to subtract the −b; 12ab wrongly merges unlike terms.)

**10. (C)** Substitute n = 10 into 3n + 1: 3 × 10 + 1 = 30 + 1 = 31. (Stopping at 3n = 30 gives the trap 30; "301" writes the digits together.)

**11. (C)** 2pq − p² with p = 3, q = 5: 2 × 3 × 5 − 3² = 30 − 9 = 21. (Forgetting to square, 30 − 3 = 27 isn't offered; 2 × 3 × 5 + 3² = 39 adds wrongly; 30 + ... gives 51.)

**12. (A)** Perimeter = 2(length + breadth) = 2((x + 5) + x) = 2(2x + 5) = 4x + 10. (2x + 5 forgets to double; x² + 5x multiplies instead of adding.)

**13. (B)** In 8y the number multiplying the letter-number is the coefficient, so it is 8. (y is the letter-number itself; 1 would be the coefficient of a bare y.)

**14. (C)** Each L uses 2 sticks, so n L-shapes use 2 × n = 2n sticks. (n + 2 adds rather than multiplies; n² squares.)

**15. (C)** Like terms have the same letter part. 7m and 2m share the letter m, so they are like. (3x & 3y, 4p & 4q have different letters; 5a & 5a² have different powers.)

**16. (D)** Perimeter = 5q; with q = 6, 5 × 6 = 30 cm. (5 + 6 = 11 adds; "56" writes digits together; 25 = 5².)

**17. (D)** Collect like terms: x-terms 6x − 4x = 2x; numbers 9 − 2 = 7. Result 2x + 7. (Merging everything into 9x or 11x wrongly adds unlike terms; 2x + 11 adds the numbers wrongly.)

**18. (C)** Three consecutive numbers about a middle n are (n − 1), n, (n + 1); their sum is (n − 1) + n + (n + 1) = 3n. (3n + 1 / 3n − 1 keep a leftover that actually cancels.)

**19. (A)** Substitute t = 6: 4t − 9 = 4 × 6 − 9 = 24 − 9 = 15. (24 forgets to subtract; reading 4t as 4 + t gives 1.)

**20. (B)** "Twice a number" = 2x; "5 more than" it = 2x + 5. (2(x + 5) doubles after adding 5; 5x + 2 swaps the roles.)

**21. (A)** All three terms are like (letter a), so 3a + 4a + 5a = (3 + 4 + 5)a = 12a. (12a³ wrongly multiplies the powers; 60a multiplies the coefficients.)

**22. (A)** 3a + 2b with a = 2, b = 5: 3 × 2 + 2 × 5 = 6 + 10 = 16. (Reading 3a as 3 + a etc. gives 10; "25" comes from 5².)

**23. (D)** 7x, −3x and x are all like terms (letter x); 7 has no letter, so it is the odd one out — a constant, unlike the others.

**24. (D)** Fixed 50 plus 12 for each of n units = 12 × n + 50 = 12n + 50. (50n + 12 swaps which quantity is multiplied; 62n adds the two numbers first.)

**25. (D)** 9 − 2k with k = −3: 9 − 2 × (−3) = 9 − (−6) = 9 + 6 = 15. (Dropping the sign of k gives 9 − 6 = 3; mis-signing gives −15.)

**26. (A)** All like terms (m): 10m − 3m + m = (10 − 3 + 1)m = 8m. (7m forgets the final + m; 6m subtracts it.)

**27. (B)** 5 pencils at c plus 4 erasers at d cost 5c + 4d; c and d are unlike, so the terms stay separate (not 9cd or 20cd). (4c + 5d swaps the counts.)

**28. (B)** x² + 3x with x = 4: 4² + 3 × 4 = 16 + 12 = 28. (Forgetting to square, 4 + 12 = 16 gives the trap 16; 14 = ... mis-reads 3x.)

**29. (B)** l + l + l is three l's added = 3l. (l³ would be l × l × l; l + 3 / 3 + l add a number instead of counting the l's.)

**30. (A)** The pattern's nth term is nx, so after 5x come 6x and 7x. (Dropping the letter gives 6 and 7; squaring the position gives 36x for the 6th.)

**31. (B)** Add like terms across the three days. c-terms: 5c + 3c + 10c = 18c; d-terms: 4d + 6d + d = 11d. Total 18c + 11d. (28cd / 18cd + 11 wrongly merge unlike terms; 11c + 18d swaps the totals.)

**32. (D)** a + bc with a = 3, b = 4, c = 0 means a + (b × c) = 3 + 4 × 0 = 3 + 0 = 3. (Adding all three, 3 + 4 + 0 = 7, is the trap; (a + b)c = 0.)

**33. (A)** 4(2x) = 4 × 2 × x = 8x. (6x adds the coefficients 4 + 2; 2x + 4 / 8 + x treat the product as a sum.)

**34. (D)** Cost of n books at 15 each = 15n; "20 taken off" = 15n − 20. (15(n − 20) subtracts inside; 20 − 15n reverses the order; 15n + 20 adds.)

**35. (B)** In 6x − 7 the number 6 multiplies the letter x, so it is the coefficient of x. (−7 is the constant term; x is the variable.)

**36. (C)** Two expressions are equal when they give the same value for every substitution (e.g. l + b + l + b and 2l + 2b). Looking alike or sharing a first letter is irrelevant.

**37. (A)** Collect like terms: l-terms 2l + 4l = 6l; b-terms 3b − b = 2b. Result 6l + 2b. (8lb / 9lb wrongly merge unlike terms.)

**38. (B)** Perimeter of an equilateral triangle = 3s; with s = 12, 3 × 12 = 36 cm. (3 + 12 = 15 adds; "312" writes digits together; 27 = 3³.)

**39. (C)** 2(x + 3) with x = 5: 2 × (5 + 3) = 2 × 8 = 16. (2x + 3 = 13 forgets to distribute the 2 over the 3; 2 × 5 + ... mis-steps to 11; 5² = 25.)

**40. (C)** 2a + 2b = 2(a + b), which is 2 times a whole number, hence even. The factored form shows divisibility by 2. (2ab / 4ab wrongly multiply; a + b drops the factor 2.)

**41. (D)** 7p − p means 7p − 1p = (7 − 1)p = 6p. (Treating −p as −1 of nothing gives 7 or 0; 7p ignores the subtraction.)

**42. (A)** 100 ÷ n with n = 4: 100 ÷ 4 = 25. (400 multiplies; 96 subtracts; 104 adds.)

**43. (B)** "The sum of a number and 5" = (x + 5); "twice" it = 2(x + 5). (2x + 5 doubles only the number; x + 10 mis-distributes.)

**44. (A)** Group like terms: x-terms 3x + 2x = 5x; numbers 5 + 7 = 12. Result 5x + 12. (17x / 6x + 12 wrongly merge x's and numbers; 5x + 35 multiplies the numbers.)

**45. (B)** m² + 3 with m = −2: (−2)² + 3 = 4 + 3 = 7. (Squaring a negative gives +4; mistakenly writing −4 gives −1 or 1.)

**46. (C)** 1 year = 12 months, so y years = 12 × y = 12y months. (y + 12 adds; y/12 divides; y − 12 subtracts.)

**47. (A)** Collect like terms: a-terms 9a − 3a = 6a; b-terms −4b + 4b = 0. Result 6a. (6a + 8b / 6a − 8b mishandle the b-terms that actually cancel; 6ab merges unlike terms.)

**48. (C)** 3a² has the letter part a² while 5a has the letter part a; different powers make them unlike terms, so they cannot be combined.

**49. (D)** Perimeter = 4 × side. With side 2s, perimeter = 4 × (2s) = 8s. (4s keeps the old side; 6s adds 4s + 2s; 16s squares the doubling.)

**50. (D)** (a + b)² with a = 1, b = 2: (1 + 2)² = 3² = 9. (Computing a² + b² = 1 + 4 = 5 is the classic trap; 1 + 2 = 3 forgets to square; 6 = 2 × 3.)

**51. (C)** Tiles for the nth shape = 2n + 3. 5th shape: 2 × 5 + 3 = 13; 4th shape: 2 × 4 + 3 = 11. Extra added = 13 − 11 = 2 (the coefficient of n). (3 is the constant; 5 / 13 misread the question.)

**52. (B)** 5(x + 2) + 3x = (5x + 10) + 3x = (5x + 3x) + 10 = 8x + 10. (8x + 2 mishandles the 10; 5x + 12 forgets to distribute the 5; 8x + 12 adds wrongly.)

**53. (B)** The n body-segments give a length of n units, and the head adds 2 units, so the total length is n + 2. (2n doubles the body; 3n / 2n + 2 over-count.)

**54. (D)** With x = 10: (A) 10 + 9 = 19; (B) 2 × 10 − 5 = 15; (C) 10² ÷ 10 = 100 ÷ 10 = 10; (D) 3 × 10 − 8 = 30 − 8 = 22. The greatest is (D) 22.

**55. (B)** (n − 1) + n + (n + 1) = 3n: the −1 and +1 cancel, leaving 3n — exactly 3 times the middle number. (3n + 3 / 3n − 3 keep a leftover that actually cancels.)

**56. (A)** Collect like terms: a-terms 4a − a − 2a = a; numbers 7 + 3 = 10. Result a + 10. (a + 4 mishandles the numbers; 7a + 10 wrongly adds a coefficient.)

**57. (C)** Perimeter of a square = 4 × side = 4(n + 1) = 4n + 4. (n + 4 forgets to multiply the n; 4n + 1 distributes only over the n; n² + 1 squares.)

**58. (A)** 3x with x = 2 is 3 × 2 = 6; 3 + x is 3 + 2 = 5. So the values are 6 and 5 respectively. (Swapping them gives 5 and 6 — the 3x = 3 + x confusion.)

**59. (B)** Output = 4 × input − 1. With input 7: 4 × 7 − 1 = 28 − 1 = 27. (28 forgets the −1; 4 × (7 − 1) = 24 subtracts before multiplying; 4 + 7 = 11 adds.)

**60. (C)** 3 books at price b cost 3b; 2 pens at price p cost 2p; b and p are unlike terms, so the total stays 3b + 2p (it cannot collapse to 5bp or 6bp). (2b + 3p swaps the counts.)
