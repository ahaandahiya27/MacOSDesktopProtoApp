**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: the barter system and its problems, why money came into being and what it does, the journey from commodity money to coins and paper, and the new digital forms of money. Reason from the problems of exchange to the solutions money provides. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
