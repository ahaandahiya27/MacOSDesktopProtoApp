**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. National-Olympiad traps: च joins हितम् with मनोहारि as a logical conjunction ('both...and'), so it raises the bar — it never weakens it to a disjunctive 'or' (वा); दुर्लभम् ('dur-' = difficulty, not absolute negation) is the contradictory of सुलभम् yet only the contrary, not the negation, of अशक्यम् — rare-but-attainable, never impossible; all three of हितम्, मनोहारि, दुर्लभम् are neuter-singular adjectives agreeing with the neuter noun वचः (नपुंसकलिङ्ग, एकवचन), not nouns in apposition or indeclinables; मनोहारि resolves by sandhi as मनः + हारि (visarga → 'o' before a voiced sound) = 'heart-stealing', not 'mind-harming'; विद्या is gathered क्षणशः (units of TIME) while अर्थः is gathered कणशः (physical UNITS) — the -शस् suffix builds gender-indifferent distributive adverbs, never datives, and the two never swap; a क्रियावान् (कृ → क्रिया + possessive -वत्/-वान्) embodies कर्म over the विकत्थन's कथन; सूक्तिः (सु+उक्ति, like सुभाषितम्) merely resembles सूक्ष्म ('subtle', no उक्ति); and through every counsel शीलम् remains the deepest value.
