**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Read source-style and "best explanation" questions carefully — the chapter's classic traps are confusing a *language* (Prakrit) with a *script* (Brahmi), thinking Alexander conquered all of India, assuming an emperor ruled every village directly, and reversing cause and effect in the Kalinga story. Watch dates and the order of rulers. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
