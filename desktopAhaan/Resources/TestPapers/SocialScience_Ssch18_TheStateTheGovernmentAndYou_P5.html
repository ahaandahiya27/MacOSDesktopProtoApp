Apex traps: treating one part (government) as the enduring whole (state); smuggling an organ, role, or tier into the four PARTS; mistaking the holders of force (police/army) or the exam-selected bureaucracy for 'the government'; assuming democracy and republic are the same axis (UK = democracy, not republic; a one-party state can be a republic, not a democracy); believing a numerical majority overrides constitutional rights and the rule of law; confusing matsya nyaya with equality; swapping who-makes vs who-enforces vs who-interprets; treating judicial review as law-making; reading accountability (RTI/media/voting/grievance redressal) as judicial review or sovereignty; and mistaking decentralisation/federalism for centralised, foreign, or abolished power.
