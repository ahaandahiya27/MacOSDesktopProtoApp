# Sanskrit — Chapter 10: दशमः कः? (Who Is the Tenth?) · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** तीर्त्वा (from तॄ 'cross') is a gerund meaning **'having crossed'**.

**2. (A)** The gerund of गम् is **गत्वा** ('having gone').

**3. (D)** The gerund of कृ is **कृत्वा** ('having done').

**4. (B)** The gerund of दृश् is **दृष्ट्वा** ('having seen').

**5. (B)** The gerund of श्रु is **श्रुत्वा** ('having heard').

**6. (D)** Every gerund here ends in **-त्वा (the क्त्वा suffix)**.

**7. (A)** In नदीं तीर्त्वा पारं गताः, the gerund shows the crossing is **completed FIRST, before they reach the bank**.

**8. (C)** A gerund is indeclinable — it **does not change its form** for case or number.

**9. (A)** 'Having read the book' uses the gerund **पठित्वा**.

**10. (C)** The gerund of पा ('drink') is **पीत्वा** ('having drunk').

**11. (D)** तीर्त्वा is 'having crossed' (gerund); तर्तुम् would be the infinitive **'to cross'**.

**12. (B)** अगणयत् is the imperfect past of गण्: **'he counted'**.

**13. (D)** The initial अ- in अगणयत् is the **past-tense augment (लङ्)**, not a negative.

**14. (B)** The present 'he counts' (from गण्) is **गणयति**.

**15. (C)** गण् ('count') must not be confused with गा, which means **'to sing'**.

**16. (A)** गायति is 'he sings', so अगायत् means **'he sang'**.

**17. (C)** Each boy counted only **nine** boys — one short.

**18. (A)** The count fell short because the speaker (अहम्, 'I') was **not included in his own count**.

**19. (B)** दशम is the ordinal **'tenth'**.

**20. (D)** दश is to दशम as 'ten' is to **'tenth'** (cardinal vs ordinal).

**21. (A)** The ordinal 'first' is **प्रथमः**.

**22. (D)** The ordinal 'fifth' is **पञ्चमः**.

**23. (B)** प्रथमः, द्वितीयः, तृतीयः, चतुर्थः mean **first, second, third, fourth**.

**24. (C)** The ordinal just before दशमः is **नवमः (ninth)**.

**25. (B)** कतमः asks **'which (of many)?'**.

**26. (C)** कतरः asks **'which (of two)?'**.

**27. (A)** 'Which one of the ten boys drowned?' uses **कतमः (which of many)**.

**28. (D)** Choosing between two banks uses **कतरः (which of two)**.

**29. (D)** कति asks **'how many?'**.

**30. (A)** मग्नः means **submerged / drowned**.

**31. (C)** The boys feared the tenth was मग्नः — **drowned in the river**.

**32. (B)** विषण्णाः describes the boys as **sad / dejected**.

**33. (C)** प्रहृष्टाः describes the boys as **delighted / overjoyed**.

**34. (B)** Their mood moves from विषण्णाः to प्रहृष्टाः — **from sad to delighted**.

**35. (D)** पथिकः is a common noun, **a wayfarer / traveler** (not a name).

**36. (A)** पथिकः, यायावरः and अध्वगः are synonyms for **a traveler / wanderer**.

**37. (A)** The पथिकः counts correctly because he **stands outside the group and counts all of them, missing no one**.

**38. (B)** दशमः त्वम् असि means **'you are the tenth'**.

**39. (D)** The 'missing tenth' was the **counter himself** — each boy forgot to count himself.

**40. (C)** The Vedantic teaching paired with the parable is **तत्त्वमसि ('you are That')**.

**41. (D)** 'You are the tenth' points to **the Self that was present all along but overlooked**.

**42. (C)** The deeper point: **what we search for (the Self) is the very one searching**.

**43. (A)** The error was shared because **each in turn forgot to count himself**.

**44. (B)** हर्ष is joy; आश्चर्य and विस्मय mean **surprise and wonder**.

**45. (B)** Both नदीं तीर्त्वा and बालकान् गणयित्वा use **the gerund (क्त्वा)**.

**46. (D)** The gerund of ज्ञा is **ज्ञात्वा** ('having known/realised').

**47. (C)** **नवमः (ninth)** is an ordinal; नव, दश and सप्त are cardinals.

**48. (A)** **दश (ten)** is a cardinal; दशमः, प्रथमः and तृतीयः are ordinals.

**49. (A)** The correct pair is **मग्नः = drowned**. पथिकः is a traveler, अगणयत् is 'he counted', प्रहृष्टाः is 'delighted'.

**50. (D)** The correct grammar: **तीर्त्वा = gerund ('having crossed'); अगणयत् = imperfect past ('he counted')**.

**51. (C)** The correct interrogatives: **कतमः = which of many; कतरः = which of two**.

**52. (B)** The correct roots: **गण् = to count; गा = to sing**.

**53. (C)** The correct pairing: **दश = ten (cardinal); दशम = tenth (ordinal)**.

**54. (D)** नदीं तीर्त्वा पारं गताः tells the order **crossing first, then reaching the bank**.

**55. (B)** The miscount warns against **trusting a count made from inside the problem, without an outside check**.

**56. (A)** दशमः त्वम् असि points the count at **the speaker himself — the very one counting**.

**57. (B)** The पथिकः plays the role of **a teacher-figure who reveals the truth**.

**58. (A)** The chapter pairs the parable with lessons on **ordinals, the gerund (तीर्त्वा), the imperfect past (अगणयत्) and कतमः**.

**59. (C)** दशमः त्वम् असि teaches that **the tenth was never lost — each boy forgot himself, and the Self was present all along**.

**60. (D)** अगणयत् is past ('he counted'), and कतमः asks **'which of many'** (कतरः is 'which of two'); the student is wrong on both.
