**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Always do brackets first (innermost outward), then × and ÷ (left to right), then + and − (left to right) — never strictly left to right. A minus in front of a bracket flips every inner sign; distributing means multiplying *every* term inside. Only addition and multiplication may be freely reordered.
