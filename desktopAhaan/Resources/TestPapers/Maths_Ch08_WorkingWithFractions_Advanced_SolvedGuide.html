Q24

In a class, 2/5 play football and 1/3 play cricket (no one plays both). The fraction playing neither is:

  • A11/15
  • B3/8
  • C4/15✓
  • D1/15

Working

✓

Football + cricket = 2/5 + 1/3 = 6/15 + 5/15 = 11/15. Neither = 1 − 11/15 = 4/15.

Q25

A tank is 3/4 full; then 1/3 of the water in it is used. The fraction of the whole tank now filled is:

  • A5/12
  • B1/2✓
  • C1/4
  • D2/3

Working

✓

After using 1/3, two-thirds of the water remains: 3/4 × 2/3 = 6/12 = 1/2 of the tank.

Q26

  • A7/12

  • C1/12
  • D5/12✓

Working

✓

Spent = 1/4 + 1/3 = 3/12 + 4/12 = 7/12. Left = 1 − 7/12 = 5/12.

Q27
