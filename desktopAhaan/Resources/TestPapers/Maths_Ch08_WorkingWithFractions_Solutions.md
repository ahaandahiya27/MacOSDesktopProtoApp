## Worked Solutions

**1. (B)** "Product of 1/2 and 1/3" = 1/2 × 1/3 = (1×1)/(2×3) = 1/6. (Adding the two, 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6, is the trap (A).)

**2. (D)** 3 × 1/4 = 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 = 3/4. Multiply the whole number into the numerator only; the denominator (the piece size) does not change. (3/12 wrongly multiplies the denominator too.)

**3. (C)** The word "of" means multiply, so "2/5 of 30" = 2/5 × 30. (Division and addition are the standard distractors.)

**4. (A)** The reciprocal swaps numerator and denominator: 3/7 → 7/3. (The reciprocal is not the negative.)

**5. (C)** 7 × 3/5 = 21/5. As a mixed number, 21 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 1, so 4 1/5. (21/35 wrongly multiplies the denominator.)

**6. (C)** "2/3 of 3/5" = 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5 in lowest terms. (Leaving it as 6/15-style unreduced or adding gives the distractors.)

**7. (D)** 5/6 ÷ 2/3 = 5/6 × 3/2 (flip the divisor) = 15/12 = 5/4. (5/9 comes from multiplying instead of flipping; 10/18 is the unreduced wrong product.)

**8. (A)** 3 ÷ 1/2 asks "how many halves fit in 3?" = 3 × 2 = 6. Dividing by a number below 1 gives a result larger than 3. (3/2 flips the wrong fraction.)

**9. (C)** Multiplying by a fraction LESS than 1 shrinks a number. Only 8 × 3/4 = 6 < 8; 8 × 5/4 = 10 and 8 × 9/8 = 9 are larger, and 8 × 1 = 8.

**10. (B)** A whole number 5 = 5/1, so its reciprocal is 1/5 (their product is 1). (5 itself is not its own reciprocal.)

**11. (C)** Dividing by 5/4 = multiplying by its reciprocal 4/5. So dividing by 5/4 matches multiplying by 4/5. (Dividing by 4/5 would equal multiplying by 5/4 — the reversed trap.)

**12. (A)** Area = length × breadth = 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2 m². (5/7 and 6/7 come from adding numerators/denominators.)

**13. (B)** 2 1/2 = (2×2 + 1)/2 = 5/2. (4/2 forgets the +1; 21/2 mis-places digits.)

**14. (B)** Convert first: 2 1/2 = 5/2, 1 1/3 = 4/3. Product = 5/2 × 4/3 = 20/6 = 10/3 = 3 1/3. (Multiplying whole parts separately is the classic mixed-number error.)

**15. (C)** 4 1/2 ÷ 3/4 = 9/2 ÷ 3/4 = 9/2 × 4/3 = 36/6 = 6 shares. Dividing by a fraction below 1 yields more pieces than the acreage.

**16. (C)** Dividing by 1/4 means multiplying by 4 (its reciprocal) — TRUE. (B) 6 ÷ 1/2 = 12, not 3; (A) ×2/3 shrinks a number; (D) reciprocal of 1 is 1, not 0.

**17. (B)** "Half of a third" = 1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6 (of means multiply, not add). 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6 is the trap (A).

**18. (D)** After the book, 1 − 1/3 = 2/3 remains. The pen costs 1/4 of 2/3 = 1/6. Left = 2/3 − 1/6 = 4/6 − 1/6 = 3/6 = 1/2. (5/12 = 1/4 + 1/6 confuses "spent" with "left".)

**19. (A)** Flour = 1 1/2 × 2 2/3 = 3/2 × 8/3 = 24/6 = 4 cups. (4 1/2 and 4 1/6 come from not converting both mixed numbers.)

**20. (A)** 3/4 × 20 = (3 × 20)/4 = 60/4 = 15. (60 forgets to divide by 4; 5 divides by 4 but drops the 3.)

**21. (D)** All share the multiplier 12, so the largest fraction wins: 5/6 > 3/4 > 2/3 > 1/2. Thus 12 × 5/6 = 10 is the largest.

**22. (B)** 4/9 × 3/8 = 12/72. Reduce by 12: 1/6. (12/72 is correct but not in lowest terms; 7/17 adds across.)

**23. (D)** Distance = 1/4 × 3 1/2 = 1/4 × 7/2 = 7/8 km. (3 1/2 ignores the rate; 14 multiplies wrongly.)

**24. (B)** 1 ÷ 2/3 = 1 × 3/2 = 3/2. Flip the divisor and multiply; the answer exceeds 1 because you divide by less than 1.

**25. (C)** Glasses = 2/3 × 30 = 20. Both glasses and caps = 1/5 × 20 = 4. (20 stops at the first step; 6 = 1/5 of 30 skips the chaining.)

**26. (D)** 2/3 ÷ 4 = 2/3 × 1/4 = 2/12 = 1/6. Dividing by a whole number = multiplying by its reciprocal. (8/3 multiplies by 4 instead.)

**27. (D)** Red = 2/3 × 6 = 4 m, so 2 m remain. Blue = 1/2 of that rest = 1/2 × 2 = 1 m. (Taking 1/2 of the whole pole gives 3 — the trap.)

**28. (A)** Reciprocals multiply to 1: 2/5 × 5/2 = 10/10 = 1. So 2/5 and 5/2. (1/2 and 2/4 are equal, not reciprocal.)

**29. (B)** 3/4 ÷ 1/8 = 3/4 × 8 = 24/4 = 6 pieces. Many small 1/8 m pieces fit in 3/4 m. (3/32 multiplies instead of dividing.)

**30. (C)** 4/5 is less than 1, so 15 × 4/5 = 12 is LESS than 15. Multiplying by a proper fraction shrinks the number. (It is certainly more than 4/5, so (D) is false.)

**31. (A)** (2/3) × (9/10) × (5/6) = (2×9×5)/(3×10×6) = 90/180 = 1/2. (90/180 is correct but unreduced.)

**32. (A)** 2 1/4 ÷ 3/8 = 9/4 ÷ 3/8 = 9/4 × 8/3 = 72/12 = 6 glasses. (27/32 multiplies instead of dividing.)

**33. (D)** 3/4 of the number = 18, so number = 18 ÷ 3/4 = 18 × 4/3 = 72/3 = 24. Check: 3/4 × 24 = 18. ✓ (13 1/2 = 3/4 × 18 multiplies instead of dividing; 72 multiplies by 4 only.)

**34. (C)** Dividing by 1/3 = multiplying by its reciprocal 3, so 5 ÷ 1/3 = 5 × 3 = 15. (5 × 1/3 multiplies by the divisor itself.)

**35. (A)** 7/8 × 8/7 = 56/56 = 1 (a number times its reciprocal). (B) = 3/4; (C) = 1 × 1/2 = 1/2; (D) = 1 × 1/2 = 1/2.

**36. (D)** 1/2 × 1/3 × 1/4 = (1×1×1)/(2×3×4) = 1/24. (3/24 = 3/24 adds numerators; 1/9 adds denominators-style.)

**37. (D)** Travelled 3/5 of 200 = 120 km, so remaining = 200 − 120 = 80 km. (Equivalently 2/5 × 200 = 80.) (120 answers the wrong part.)

**38. (B)** 2 1/3 ÷ 1 1/6 = 7/3 ÷ 7/6 = 7/3 × 6/7 = 42/21 = 2. (49/18 multiplies the fractions; 14/18 mishandles the reciprocal.)

**39. (C)** A number times 1 is itself, so 3/8 × 1 = 3/8. The multiplicative identity is 1. (×0 gives 0; ×3/8 gives 9/64.)

**40. (D)** Whole wall ÷ rate = 1 ÷ 2/5 = 1 × 5/2 = 5/2 hours. Dividing by a rate below 1 takes more than 1 hour. (2/5 and 7/5 misuse the reciprocal.)

**41. (A)** After Riya, 1 − 1/4 = 3/4 remains. Sam ate 2/3 of that: 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2 of the whole cake. (2/3 forgets it is "of the remainder".)

**42. (D)** 5/12 × 8 = (5 × 8)/12 = 40/12 = 10/3. (40/96 wrongly multiplies the denominator by 8 too.)

**43. (D)** FALSE statement: a fraction is NOT always larger than its reciprocal — 1/2's reciprocal is 2, which is bigger. (A), (B), (C) are all true: a proper fraction flips to >1, 0 has no reciprocal, and 1's reciprocal is 1.)

**44. (D)** Fraction filled = 3/4 × 1/3 = 3/12 = 1/4 in 1/3 hour. (9/4 divides instead of multiplying; 13/12 adds.)

**45. (A)** 1/2 + 1/3 = 5/6; then 5/6 × 6/5 = 30/30 = 1 (reciprocal pair). (Forgetting to add inside the bracket first gives the distractors.)

**46. (C)** Rose area = 3/10 of 5/6 = 3/10 × 5/6 = 15/60 = 1/4 acre. (8/16 and 15/16 mis-multiply or fail to reduce.)

**47. (B)** First number 9: 2/3 × 9 = 6. This equals 1/2 of the second, so 1/2 × x = 6 → x = 12. Check: 1/2 × 12 = 6. ✓ (6 stops at the first value; 27/4 mishandles the reciprocal.)

**48. (B)** Share each = 7/8 ÷ 7 = 7/8 × 1/7 = 7/56 = 1/8 m. (7/8 forgets to divide; 49/8 multiplies by 7.)

**49. (A)** Dividing by a number below 1 gives the biggest result: 6 ÷ 1/2 = 12, while 6 ÷ 1 = 6, 6 ÷ 2 = 3, 6 ÷ 3 = 2. So (A).

**50. (A)** 1 1/4 × 4/5 = 5/4 × 4/5 = 20/20 = 1. (5/4 and 4/5 forget to multiply the whole; 25/16 squares the wrong term.)

**51. (C)** In 2 days the worker paints 2 × 2/7 = 4/7, so unpainted = 1 − 4/7 = 3/7. (2/7 is one day's work; 4/7 is the painted part, not the leftover.)

**52. (C)** Area of a square = side² = (2/3)² = 2/3 × 2/3 = 4/9 cm². (8/3 and 4/3 mishandle the squaring; 4/6 fails to square the denominator.)

**53. (A)** We need 3/5 × ? = 1, so ? is the reciprocal 5/3 (since 3/5 × 5/3 = 1). (3/5 and 5 are tempting but only 5/3 works.)

**54. (B)** Poured out = 2/3 of 3/4 = 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2 litre. (1/4 is the part LEFT; 3/8 mis-multiplies.)

**55. (B)** 9 ÷ 3/4 = 9 × 4/3 = 36/3 = 12. Dividing by a fraction below 1 gives more than 9. (27/4 multiplies by 3/4; 27 multiplies by 3 only.)

**56. (D)** Half of a half of a half = (1/2)³ = 1/2 × 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/8 of the pizza. (1/6 adds the halves; 3/2 adds them as wholes.)

**57. (B)** Repaired = 2/3 of 3/5 = 2/3 × 3/5 = 6/15 = 2/5 km, so unrepaired = 3/5 − 2/5 = 1/5 km. (2/5 is the repaired length; 1/3 is the un-done fraction, not the length.)

**58. (A)** 1/2 ÷ 1/8 = 1/2 × 8 = 4. Checking the others: (B) 1/2 × 4 = 2; (C) 8 ÷ 1/2 = 8 × 2 = 16; (D) 1/8 ÷ 1/2 = 1/8 × 2 = 1/4. Only (A) equals 4. (The trap is thinking division must shrink the value — dividing by 1/8 multiplies by 8.)

**59. (C)** Sugar per person = 2/3 ÷ 4 = 2/3 × 1/4 = 2/12 = 1/6 cup. For 6 people: 6 × 1/6 = 1 cup. (4/9 scales by 2/3 instead of unitary; 1/2 under-scales.)

**60. (B)** Monday: read 1/5, leaving 4/5. Tuesday: 1/4 of the remaining 4/5 = 1/4 × 4/5 = 4/20 = 1/5. Total read = 1/5 + 1/5 = 2/5. (9/20 mis-adds; 3/5 over-counts.)
