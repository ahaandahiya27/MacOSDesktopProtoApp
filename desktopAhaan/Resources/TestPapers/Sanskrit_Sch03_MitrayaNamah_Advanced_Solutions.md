# Sanskrit — Chapter 3: मित्राय नमः · Paper 2 (Advanced) Solutions

**1. (C)** नमः governs the dative, so 'salutation to Mitra' is **मित्राय नमः**.

**2. (A)** The dative (चतुर्थी) generally means **'to / for'**.

**3. (D)** The dative ending that turns the a-stem मित्र into मित्राय is **-आय**.

**4. (B)** नमः सूर्यः is wrong (nominative); the correct dative is **सूर्याय**.

**5. (B)** 'Salutation to Ravi' (i-stem) is **रवये नमः**.

**6. (D)** 'Salutation to the guru' (u-stem) is **गुरवे नमः**.

**7. (A)** The dative is -आय for a-stems, -ये for रवि, and **-वे (गुरवे)** for गुरु.

**8. (C)** 'Salutation to the teacher (आचार्य)' is **आचार्याय नमः**.

**9. (A)** स्वस्ति also takes the **dative** ('स्वस्ति प्रजाभ्यः' — well-being TO the people).

**10. (C)** स्वाहा governs the **dative** (अग्नये स्वाहा, 'to Agni').

**11. (D)** नमः, स्वस्ति and स्वाहा all **govern the dative case**.

**12. (B)** The dative मित्राय answers **'to whom (is the salutation)?'**.

**13. (D)** सूर्यनमस्कारः (one compound) is distinguished from the two-word phrase **सूर्याय नमस्कारः**.

**14. (B)** In the compound सूर्यनमस्कारः, the case-relation is **hidden inside the compound** (no separate ending shown).

**15. (C)** नमस्कारः = नमस् + कारः = **'a making of salutation' (a bow / act of homage)**.

**16. (A)** The twelve Sun-names are **synonyms (एकार्थकशब्दाः — many words, one meaning)**.

**17. (C)** एकार्थकशब्दाः (एक + अर्थक + शब्दाः) means words **of one meaning (synonyms)**.

**18. (A)** The opposite of synonyms is antonyms, in Sanskrit **विलोमशब्दाः**.

**19. (B)** **रविः, भानुः, आदित्यः, अर्कः** are all synonyms for the Sun.

**20. (D)** **पर्वतराजः (the Himalaya)** is NOT a name of the Sun.

**21. (A)** मित्र as a Sun-name means the Sun as the **'friend' (of all beings)**.

**22. (D)** सूर्यनमस्कारः has **twelve** postures, mirrored by **twelve** Sun-names.

**23. (B)** द्वादश means **twelve**.

**24. (C)** आचार्या (final -आ) means **a female teacher**.

**25. (B)** आचार्यः (final -अः) means **a male teacher**.

**26. (C)** आचार्यः / आचार्या differ in **gender (masculine vs feminine)**.

**27. (A)** छात्र ('male student') → feminine **छात्रा** ('female student').

**28. (D)** बालक ('boy') → feminine **बालिका** ('girl').

**29. (D)** देव ('god') → feminine **देवी** ('goddess').

**30. (A)** The feminine markers in आचार्या, बालिका, देवी are **-आ, -इका, -ई** respectively.

**31. (C)** 'Salutation to the goddess' (देवी) in the dative is **देव्यै नमः**.

**32. (B)** Daily सूर्यनमस्कारः is said to bring **health, vigour, brilliance and wisdom**.

**33. (C)** स्वस्थं शरीरम् means **a healthy body**.

**34. (B)** Each posture is paired with **a mantra saluting the Sun by one of its twelve names**.

**35. (D)** The first mantra is **मित्राय नमः**.

**36. (A)** 'अग्नये नमः' uses the dative of अग्नि (i-stem): **अग्नये**.

**37. (A)** मित्राय (not मित्रम्) follows नमः because नमः requires **the dative, not the accusative**.

**38. (B)** 'For the Sun' and 'to the Sun' are both the **dative (सूर्याय)**.

**39. (D)** सूर्यनमस्कारः packs into one word what सूर्याय नमस्कारः says in **two words (with the dative shown)**.

**40. (C)** Antonyms are illustrated by **मित्र (friend) / शत्रु (enemy)**.

**41. (D)** Swapping रवि for भानु keeps the meaning **'the Sun'** (synonyms).

**42. (C)** The correct pair: **नमः = salutation; मित्र (here) = the Sun**.

**43. (A)** The correct case rule: **नमः governs the dative (चतुर्थी), e.g. मित्राय**.

**44. (B)** The correct datives: **रवि → रवये; गुरु → गुरवे**.

**45. (B)** The correct gender pair: **आचार्यः (male) / आचार्या (female)**.

**46. (D)** A set of synonyms: **सूर्यः, रविः, भानुः, आदित्यः** (all 'Sun').

**47. (C)** A pair of antonyms: **मित्र (friend) / शत्रु (enemy)**.

**48. (A)** सूर्यनमस्कारः being a समास means it is **one compound word**.

**49. (A)** Both the Sun-names and postures number **twelve (द्वादश)**.

**50. (D)** With a female teacher (आचार्या → dative आचार्यायै): **आचार्यायै नमः**.

**51. (C)** The dative follows नमः because a salutation is offered **TO** someone.

**52. (B)** छात्राय in 'छात्राय पुस्तकं ददाति' is the **recipient ('to the student')**.

**53. (C)** The twelve names + postures link सूर्यनमस्कारः to the twelve **months of the year**.

**54. (D)** नमस्कारः and नमः share नमस्, meaning **bowing / homage**.

**55. (B)** 'Salutation to Mitra, to Ravi, and to Surya' is **मित्राय नमः, रवये नमः, सूर्याय नमः**.

**56. (A)** The chapter pairs the Sun Salutation with lessons on **the dative (चतुर्थी), synonyms/antonyms, the compound समास, and feminine formation**.

**57. (B)** The deeper value is **physical and mental well-being through daily discipline**.

**58. (A)** मित्राय नमः glosses as **'salutation to the Sun (as the friend, Mitra)'**.
