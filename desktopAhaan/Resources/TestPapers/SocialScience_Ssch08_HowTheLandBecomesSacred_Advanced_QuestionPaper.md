**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: what "sacredness" means, the tradition of pilgrimage and tīrthas, how sacred sites are spread across India in networks, the sacred ecology of rivers, confluences and mountains, and sacred trees and groves. Reason about how a whole land comes to be seen as holy. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
