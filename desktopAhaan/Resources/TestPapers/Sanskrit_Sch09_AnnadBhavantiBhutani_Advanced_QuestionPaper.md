**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This Advanced paper goes deeper than Paper 1: it tests the full creation-wheel in order (ब्रह्म → कर्म → यज्ञ → पर्जन्य 'rain' → अन्न 'food' → भूतानि 'beings'), the ABLATIVE case (-आत्, 'arise from', source not maker) with more examples, consonant SANDHI (त् → द् before a voiced sound, अन्नात् + भवन्ति → अन्नाद्भवन्ति), the OPTATIVE (विधिलिङ्: जानीयात् 'one should know', कुर्यात् 'one should do'), and the gender-distinction ब्रह्म (neuter Absolute) vs ब्रह्मा (creator-god) vs ब्रह्मन् (priest). Remember भूतानि = 'beings' (root भू), not 'ghosts'. Sanskrit is in Devanagari. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
