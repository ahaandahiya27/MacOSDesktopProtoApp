**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Apex traps: a maritime neighbour is no 'lesser' neighbour (the sea joins coasts as a land boundary joins lands); an open border (India-Nepal) is a real, recognised boundary that needs cooperation, not merger; landlocked Nepal and Bhutan still depend on the sea via Indian ports; Indian culture reached Thailand, Cambodia, Indonesia PEACEFULLY by trade, travel and religion (Ramakien is Thailand's own retelling), never conquest; direction/side and link-type mix-ups (Pakistan/Afghanistan west-northwest, Bangladesh/Myanmar east, Sri Lanka/Maldives south; China land not maritime; Iran/Oman western sea-trade neighbours); figures land boundary ~15,100 km and coastline ~11,100 km; SAARC (1985) is cooperation not conquest or merger.
