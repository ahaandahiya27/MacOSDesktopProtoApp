**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: the rise of the Guptas, their great rulers and inscriptions, their system of governance and trade, the science, literature and art that made this a "classical age," and the empire's decline alongside the southern kingdoms. Reason from evidence — coins, inscriptions and texts. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
