**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. This paper goes deeper than Paper 1: reason from cause to effect across the dynasties that filled the space left by the Mauryas — Shungas, Satavahanas, Indo-Greeks, Shakas and Kushanas — and the cultural blending of the age (Gandhara vs Mathura art, the Silk Route, the southern Sangam world and the Roman trade). Keep the order of dynasties and their regions straight, and never confuse the Gandhara and Mathura schools. This paper does not repeat any question from Paper 1.
