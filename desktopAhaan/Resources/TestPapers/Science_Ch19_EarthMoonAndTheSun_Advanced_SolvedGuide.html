Set 1 · Q1–Q10

Q1

The spinning of the Earth on its own axis is called:

  • Arevolution
  • Beclipse
  • Crotation✓
  • Dtide

Working

✓

The spinning of the Earth on its own axis is **rotation**.

Q2

The Earth completes one rotation on its axis in about:

  • A24 hours✓
  • B27.3 days
  • C365.25 days
  • Done month

Working

✓

Earth completes one rotation in about **24 hours**, giving one day-night cycle.

Q3

Day and night are caused mainly by Earth's:

  • Arevolution around the Sun
  • Btilt alone
  • Cdistance from the Moon
  • Drotation on its axis✓

Working

✓

Day and night arise mainly from Earth's **rotation on its axis** — the lit half faces the Sun.

Q4

The movement of the Earth around the Sun is called its:

  • Arotation
  • Brevolution✓
  • Cphase
  • Declipse

Working

✓

Earth's journey around the Sun is its **revolution**.

Q5

The Earth takes about ____ to complete one revolution around the Sun.

  • A24 hours
  • B365.25 days✓
  • C27.3 days
  • D12 hours

Working

✓

One revolution around the Sun takes about **365.25 days** — one year.

Q6

The extra quarter-day (0.25) in each year's revolution is the reason we add an extra day every four years in a:

  • Alunar month
  • Bsolar eclipse
  • Cnew moon
  • Dleap year✓

Working

✓

The leftover quarter-day each year is collected into an extra day every four years in a **leap year**.

Q7

Earth's axis is not upright but tilted at about:

  • A23.5°✓
  • B0°
  • C45°
  • D90°

Working

✓

Earth's axis is tilted at about **23.5°** from the upright.

Q8

The main cause of the seasons is:

  • AEarth moving closer to and farther from the Sun
  • Bthe Moon blocking the Sun
  • Cthe tilt of Earth's axis as it revolves around the Sun✓
  • Dchanges in Earth's rotation speed

Working

✓

The seasons are caused by **the tilt of Earth's axis as it revolves around the Sun**, not by changing distance.

Q9

It is summer in the part of Earth that is:

  • Atilted toward the Sun, receiving more direct sunlight✓
  • Btilted away from the Sun
  • Cfarthest from the Moon
  • Din shadow

Working

✓

It is summer where the hemisphere is **tilted toward the Sun, receiving more direct sunlight**.

Q10

When the Northern Hemisphere has summer, the Southern Hemisphere has:

  • Asummer too
  • Bno season
  • Cwinter✓
  • Dan eclipse

Working

✓

When the Northern Hemisphere has summer, the Southern Hemisphere has **winter** — they are opposite.

Q15

The phase when the Moon's lit side faces fully toward Earth, appearing as a complete disc, is the:

  • Anew moon
  • Bfirst quarter
  • Cfull moon✓
  • Dcrescent

Working

✓

When the Moon's lit side fully faces Earth as a complete disc, it is the **full moon**.

Q16

The phase when the side of the Moon facing Earth is unlit, so the Moon is nearly invisible, is the:

  • Anew moon✓
  • Bfull moon
  • Cgibbous
  • Dhalf moon

Working

✓

When the Earth-facing side is unlit and the Moon is nearly invisible, it is the **new moon**.

Q17

A solar eclipse happens when:

  • Athe Earth comes between the Sun and the Moon
  • Bthe Sun comes between the Earth and the Moon
  • Cthe Moon comes between the Sun and the Earth, casting its shadow on Earth✓
  • Dthe Earth stops rotating

Working

✓

A solar eclipse occurs when **the Moon comes between the Sun and the Earth, casting its shadow on Earth**.

Q18

A solar eclipse can occur only at the time of a:

  • Anew moon✓
  • Bfull moon
  • Cfirst quarter
  • Dcrescent

Working

✓

Because the Moon must sit between Sun and Earth, a solar eclipse happens only at a **new moon**.

Q19

A lunar eclipse happens when:

  • Athe Moon comes between the Sun and Earth
  • Bthe Earth comes between the Sun and the Moon, casting its shadow on the Moon✓
  • Cthe Sun blocks the Earth
  • Dthe Moon stops orbiting

Working

✓

A lunar eclipse occurs when **the Earth comes between the Sun and the Moon, casting its shadow on the Moon**.

Q20

A lunar eclipse can occur only at the time of a:

  • Anew moon
  • Bcrescent
  • Cfirst quarter
  • Dfull moon✓

Working

✓

Because Earth must sit between Sun and Moon, a lunar eclipse happens only at a **full moon**.
