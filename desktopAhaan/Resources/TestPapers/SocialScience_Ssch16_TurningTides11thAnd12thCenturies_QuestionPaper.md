**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Watch the classic traps: Mahmud of Ghazni only RAIDED for plunder and withdrew (about 17 times), he did NOT rule India - lasting rule began later with the Ghurids; raiding for loot and conquering to govern are different; NOT all of India fell (the south - Cholas, Kakatiyas, Hoysalas - and much of the north stayed free); the age was NOT only war (al-Biruni, Bhaskaracharya, Ramanuja, Basava, great temples); and al-Biruni studied India deeply (two-way learning). Keep the Tarain battles straight: Ghuri lost the First (1191), won the Second (1192). Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
