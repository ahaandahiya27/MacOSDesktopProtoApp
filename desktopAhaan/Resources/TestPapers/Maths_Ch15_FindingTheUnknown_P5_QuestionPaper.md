|---|---|

**Marking scheme:** **+4** for each correct answer · **−1** for each wrong answer · **0** if unattempted.

**Instructions:** Each question has four options (A), (B), (C), (D); exactly one is correct. Every item reduces to ONE linear equation in one unknown, but the modelling is layered: deeply nested brackets, fractions-of-remainders, multi-condition age/digit/mixture set-ups, and solve-then-evaluate questions where the asked quantity is NOT x itself — watch sign flips inside nested braces, never average two rates, and re-read what is actually asked. Do not write on this paper — mark answers on your answer sheet.
