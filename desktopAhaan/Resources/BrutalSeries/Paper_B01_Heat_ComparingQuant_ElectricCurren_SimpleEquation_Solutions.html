1. At dawn a laboratory thermometer reads -8 °C. By noon it had risen 23 °C, and by night it fell 9 °C from the noon reading. What does it read at night? Heat

(A) 24 °C
(B) 14 °C
(C) -24 °C

The ideaTemperature changes add and subtract like signed numbers along the scale.
Step by step
  1. Start: -8 °C.
