1. A rover moves at a steady 12.5 m/s. How far does it travel in 4 minutes? Motion & Time

(A) 50 m
(B) 300 m
(C) 3000 km
(D) 30 km
(E) 750 m
(F) 3 km ✔
(G) 1.5 km
(H) 0.05 km
The ideaDistance = speed x time, but the units must match — change minutes to seconds first.
Step by step
  1. Speed = 12.5 m/s, time = 4 minutes.
  2. Change time to seconds: 4 x 60 = 240 s.
  3. Distance = speed x time = 12.5 x 240 = 3000 m.
  4. Change to km: 3000 / 1000 = 3 km.
AnswerOption (F) — 3 km
Watch out50 m comes from 12.5 x 4 (forgot to turn minutes into seconds).
Where you would use itThe same maths tells you how far a car travels in a few minutes on a highway.
