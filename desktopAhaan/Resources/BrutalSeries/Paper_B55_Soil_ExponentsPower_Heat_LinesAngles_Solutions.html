1. A farmer pours 240 mL of water into a pipe on sandy soil and it fully soaks in 16 minutes; the same 240 mL on clayey soil takes 48 minutes. How many times faster is the sandy soil's percolation rate? Soil

The ideaPercolation rate = volume / time; comparing two rates means dividing the larger by the smaller.
