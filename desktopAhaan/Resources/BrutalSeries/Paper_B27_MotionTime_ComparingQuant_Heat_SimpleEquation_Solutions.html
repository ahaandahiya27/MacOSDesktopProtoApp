1. A cyclist rides 90 km out at 45 km/h, then the same 90 km back at 90 km/h. What is her average speed for the whole trip? Motion & Time

(A) 45 km/h
(B) 55 km/h
(C) 90 km/h
(D) 67.5 km/h
(E) 65 km/h
(F) 72 km/h
(G) 60 km/h ✔
The ideaAverage speed is total distance divided by total time, NOT the average of the two speeds.
Step by step
  1. Time going = 90 / 45 = 2 h
  2. Time back = 90 / 90 = 1 h
  3. Total distance = 90 + 90 = 180 km
  4. Total time = 2 + 1 = 3 h
  5. Average speed = 180 / 3 = 60 km/h
AnswerOption (G) — 60 km/h
Watch out67.5 km/h is the mean of 45 and 90 — wrong, because she spends more time at the slower speed.
Where you would use itUsed to estimate realistic travel time for a there-and-back commute.
