1. A class tests 40 household liquids. 45% turn blue litmus red, 30% turn red litmus blue, and the rest change neither paper. How many liquids are neutral, and what do neutral liquids do to litmus? Acids, Bases & Salts

The ideaAcids redden blue litmus, bases blue red litmus, and neutral liquids change neither.
Step by step
