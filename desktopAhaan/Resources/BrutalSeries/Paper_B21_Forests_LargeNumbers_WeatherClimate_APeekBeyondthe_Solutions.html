(B) To escape from the gravity of their home region
(C) To make their long-distance flying muscles weaker
(D) Because they cannot see in their home region's sunlight
(E) To avoid drinking any water during the year
(F) To reach places where the weather is suitable and food is plentiful for feeding and breeding ✔
(G) Because they are pushed along helplessly by all winds
The ideaMigration is an adaptation that lets birds follow good weather and food supplies across seasons.
Step by step
  1. As seasons change, some regions become cold or short of food.
  2. Staying would mean facing harsh weather and hunger.
  3. So birds fly to regions with milder weather and more food.
  4. There they can feed well and raise their young.
AnswerOption (F) — To reach places where the weather is suitable and food is plentiful for feeding and breeding
Watch out'To make their muscles weaker' is backwards - migration is a demanding feat that follows food and good weather.

96. A car uses 5.5 litres of petrol to travel 100 km. How much petrol does it need to travel 240 km at the same rate? A Peek Beyond the Point

(A) 11.0 litres
(B) 132 litres
(C) 12.2 litres
(D) 13.2 litres ✔
(E) 13.0 litres
(F) 14.2 litres
The ideaFind how many 100-km units are in 240 km, then multiply the fuel rate by that.
Step by step
  1. 240 km = 2.4 lots of 100 km.
  2. Fuel = 5.5 x 2.4.
  3. 5.5 x 2 = 11.0; 5.5 x 0.4 = 2.2.
  4. Total = 11.0 + 2.2 = 13.2 litres.
AnswerOption (D) — 13.2 litres
Watch out'11.0 litres' uses only 200 km worth of fuel; 240 km needs 2.4 times the 100-km rate.
Where you would use itDrivers estimate how much fuel a trip of a given distance will need.

97. In a food chain grass to deer to tiger, only one-tenth of the food energy passes to the next level each step. To build up 2 kg of tiger, how many kilograms of grass must the food chain start with? Forests

(A) 2 kg
(B) 100 kg
(C) 20 kg
(D) 400 kg
(E) 2000 kg
(F) 22 kg
(G) 200 kg ✔
The ideaGoing backwards down the chain, each step needs ten times as much as the level above it.
Step by step
  1. 2 kg of tiger needs 10 times as much deer.
  2. Deer needed = 2 x 10 = 20 kg.
  3. Deer needs 10 times as much grass.
  4. Grass needed = 20 x 10 = 200 kg.
AnswerOption (G) — 200 kg
Watch out'20 kg' is only the deer needed; the chain must go back one more step to the grass.
Where you would use itExplains why eating plants directly feeds far more people than eating meat does.
