1. An object stands 12 cm in front of a flat (plane) mirror. The mirror is then slid 5 cm straight back, away from the object. By how much does the gap between the object and its image change? Light

(A) 29 cm
(B) 34 cm
(C) 10 cm ✔
(D) 24 cm
(E) 2 cm
(F) 5 cm
(G) 17 cm
The ideaIn a plane mirror the image is as far behind the mirror as the object is in front; moving the mirror by d changes the object-to-image gap by 2d.
Step by step
  1. At first the image is 12 cm behind the mirror, so object-to-image gap = 12 + 12 = 24 cm.
  2. After sliding the mirror 5 cm away, the object is 17 cm in front of it.
  3. New gap = 17 + 17 = 34 cm.
  4. Change in gap = 34 - 24 = 10 cm (which is twice the 5 cm shift).
AnswerOption (C) — 10 cm
Watch out5 cm is the distance the mirror moved; but the object-image gap changes by DOUBLE that, 10 cm.
Where you would use itDesigners of dressing-room and shop mirrors use this doubling rule to plan how far back a mirror should sit.
