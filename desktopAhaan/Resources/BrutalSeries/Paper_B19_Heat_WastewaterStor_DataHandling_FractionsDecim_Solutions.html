1. An immersion rod heats 2 litres of water, raising it by 8 C every minute. The same rod is put into 5 litres of water at 26 C. If the heat it gives each minute is shared over all the water, how many minutes will it take to reach 50 C? Heat

(A) 5 minutes
(B) 7.5 minutes ✔
(C) 3 minutes
(D) 15 minutes
(E) 9.6 minutes
(F) 8 minutes
(G) 12 minutes
The ideaHeat given per minute is fixed, so a larger amount of water warms proportionally slower.
Step by step
  1. Rod warms 2 L by 8 C each minute, so its heat = 2 x 8 = 16 litre-degrees per minute.
  2. For 5 litres, rise per minute = 16 / 5 = 3.2 C.
  3. Rise needed = 50 - 26 = 24 C.
  4. Time = 24 / 3.2 = 7.5 minutes.
AnswerOption (B) — 7.5 minutes
Watch out'3 minutes' uses the 2-litre rate (8 C/min) and ignores that 5 litres heats more slowly.
Where you would use itEstimating how long a geyser takes to heat a full tank versus a half tank.
