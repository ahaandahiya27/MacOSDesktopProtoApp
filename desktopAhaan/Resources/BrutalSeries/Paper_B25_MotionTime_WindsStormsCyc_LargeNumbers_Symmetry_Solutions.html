1. A car covers the first 150 km of a trip in 2 hours and the next 90 km in 1 hour. What is its average speed for the whole journey? Motion & Time

(A) 240 km/h
(B) 120 km/h
(C) 82.5 km/h
(D) 90 km/h
(E) 80 km/h ✔
(F) 70 km/h
The ideaAverage speed of a whole trip is total distance divided by total time, not the average of the two speeds.
Step by step
  1. Total distance = 150 + 90 = 240 km.
  2. Total time = 2 + 1 = 3 hours.
  3. Average speed = 240 / 3 = 80 km/h.
AnswerOption (E) — 80 km/h
Watch out'82.5 km/h' just averages 75 km/h and 90 km/h, which is wrong because the car spent more time on the first leg.
Where you would use itTrip computers in cars use total-distance-over-total-time to show a journey's average speed.
