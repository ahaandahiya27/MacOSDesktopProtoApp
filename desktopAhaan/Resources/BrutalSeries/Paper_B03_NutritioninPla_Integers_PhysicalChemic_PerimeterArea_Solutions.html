1. A leaf makes 6 mg of glucose each hour in bright sun. It gets 7 hours of sun, and the plant uses one-third of that glucose for its own respiration. How much glucose is stored, and which gas did the leaf take in to make it? Nutrition in Plants

(C) 28 mg; oxygen
(D) 14 mg; carbon dioxide
(E) 28 g; carbon dioxide
(F) 42 mg; carbon dioxide

The ideaPhotosynthesis makes glucose using carbon dioxide; subtract the part used in respiration.
Step by step
  1. Glucose made = 6 mg/h x 7 h = 42 mg.
  2. Used in respiration = 1/3 x 42 = 14 mg.
  3. Stored = 42 - 14 = 28 mg.
  4. Photosynthesis takes in carbon dioxide (and gives out oxygen).
AnswerOption (G) — 28 mg; carbon dioxide
Watch out'42 mg; carbon dioxide' forgets that one-third of the glucose is used up in respiration.
