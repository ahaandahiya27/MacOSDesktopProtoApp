(B) It acts at 1 A; melting immediately
(C) It acts at 15 A; so it just survives
(D) It acts at 6 A; so it never melts
(E) It never acts
(F) It acts at 8 A; so it holds at 15 A
(G) It acts at 7 A; but 15 A is below it, so it holds
The ideaA fuse acts at its rated melting current; any current above that makes it melt.
Step by step
  1. The fuse is rated to melt at 7 A.
  2. The fault current is 15 A, which is greater than 7 A.
  3. Since 15 A exceeds the 7 A rating, the fuse melts and cuts the circuit.
AnswerOption (A) — It acts at 7 A; since 15 A is above 7 A, the fuse melts
Watch out'Acts at 15 A so it just survives' confuses the fault current with the rating; the fuse melts at 7 A and 15 A is well past it.

94. A town's population is 8000 and grows 5% in a year. The NEXT year it falls by 5% of the new figure. Is the final population equal to, more than, or less than 8000? Comparing Quantities

(A) Equal to 8000
(B) Equal to 8400
(C) More than 8000 (8020)
(D) Less than 8000 (it is 7980) ✔
(E) More than 8000 (8100)
(F) Less than 8000 (7600)
(G) Less than 8000 (7920)
The ideaA percent rise then an equal percent fall act on different bases, so they do not cancel.
Step by step
  1. After 5% rise: 8000 x 1.05 = 8400.
  2. 5% of 8400 = 420; fall to 7980.
  3. 8400 - 420 = 7980, which is less than 8000.
AnswerOption (D) — Less than 8000 (it is 7980)
Watch out'Equal to 8000' assumes the rise and fall cancel; the 5% fall is on the larger 8400, so the result dips to 7980.
Where you would use itThis 'percentage of a changed base' effect explains why a price cut after a hike rarely returns to the start.

(G) Water blocks current, and copper melts too fast

The ideaWet skin conducts current (shock risk); a thick copper wire will not melt, so it defeats the fuse's purpose.
Step by step
  1. Water makes skin a conductor, so touching a live appliance can pass current through you.

(A) 10 litres
(B) 2 litres
(C) 3 litres
(D) 4 litres
(E) 5 litres
(F) 2.5 litres ✔
The ideaPetrol litres stay fixed; set petrol as 80% of the new total to find the new total, then the ethanol added.
Step by step
  1. Petrol = 90% of 20 = 18 litres (this amount does not change).
  2. If petrol must be 80% of the new total T: 18 = 0.8 x T, so T = 22.5 litres.
