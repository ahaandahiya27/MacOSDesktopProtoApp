Q1Physical & Chemical Changes A change in which no new substance is formed and which can usually be reversed is called a:
(A) chemical change

(C) nuclear change
(D) physical change ✓
Correct answer: D

The idea In a physical change only the look, size or state of a substance shifts; the substance itself stays the same.

  • C: Nuclear change alters the atom's core; everyday melting and folding are simply physical.
