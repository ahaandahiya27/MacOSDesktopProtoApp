Q1Heat Heat always flows on its own from a hotter body to a colder one until both reach the:
(A) colder body's freezing point
(B) same temperature (thermal balance) ✓
(C) hotter body's boiling point
(D) temperature of the Sun
Correct answer: B

The idea Heat moves spontaneously from hot to cold. The flow stops when both bodies share one temperature — a state of thermal balance (equilibrium).

Place a hot spoon in cool water
heat leaves the spoon and enters the water
flow stops when both sit at one common temperature.

Where you meet it in real life A cup of hot tea left on the table slowly cools to room temperature, then stops changing.

Why the other options miss
  • A: Heat does not chase a freezing point; it flows until the two bodies share one temperature.
  • C: Nothing pushes the cooler body up to a boiling point; the two simply meet at a common temperature.
  • D: The Sun is irrelevant to two bodies in a room; they settle at a shared temperature between their starting values.
