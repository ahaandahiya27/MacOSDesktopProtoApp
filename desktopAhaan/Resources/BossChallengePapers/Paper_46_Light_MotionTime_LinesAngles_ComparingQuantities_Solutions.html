Q1Light Light always travels from one point to another along a path that is:
(A) a curved arc
(B) a widening spiral
(C) a straight line ✓
(D) a wavy zig-zag
Correct answer: C

The idea In a uniform medium light travels in straight lines — this is rectilinear propagation, the reason shadows have sharp edges.

Cover and uncover a torch in a dusty room
the beam you see is perfectly straight
so light travels in straight lines.

Where you meet it in real life A laser pointer makes a straight dot on the wall — the beam never bends on its own in air.

Why the other options miss

  • B: A spiral path is not how light moves; the straight-line rule is what lets us aim a torch beam.
  • D: A zig-zag path would blur every shadow; real shadows have crisp edges because light goes straight.

Q99Lines & Angles Two angles forming a linear pair are in the ratio 1 : 2. The larger angle is:
(A) 120° ✓
(B) 90°
(C) 45°
(D) 60°
Correct answer: A

The idea A linear pair adds to 180°. With parts 1 + 2 = 3, one part = 180° ÷ 3 = 60°, so the larger (2 parts) = 120°.

Linear pair sum = 180°
parts 1 + 2 = 3, so one part = 180° ÷ 3 = 60°
larger = 2 × 60° = 120°.

Where you meet it in real life Splitting a straight 180° edge in a 1 : 2 ratio gives angles of 60° and 120°.

Why the other options miss
  • B: 90° would come from an equal 1 : 1 split; a 1 : 2 split of 180° gives 60° and 120°.
  • C: 45° fits a split of 90°, not a linear pair; here the larger angle is 120°.
  • D: 60° is the SMALLER angle (1 part); the larger is 2 parts, i.e. 120°.
Q100Comparing Quantities A glass pane lets through 80% of the light falling on it. If 500 units of light strike it, the amount passing through is:

Correct answer: B

The idea 80% of 500 = 80/100 × 500 = 400 units pass through; the other 20% (100 units) is reflected or absorbed.

Light passing = 80% of 500
= 80/100 × 500
= 400 units.

Where you meet it in real life A clean window passing 80% of daylight lets 400 of every 500 units of sunlight into the room.

Why the other options miss
  • A: 80 is just the percentage figure; 80% of 500 units is 400 units.
  • C: 580 adds 80 to 500; the light that passes is 80% of 500 = 400 units.
  • D: 100 units is the 20% that does NOT pass through; the part that passes is 400 units.
