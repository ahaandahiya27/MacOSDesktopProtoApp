Q1 A liquid turns blue litmus red and has no effect on red litmus. What can you be sure about it?

The idea An acid is a sour substance that makes blue litmus turn red. A base does the opposite (red litmus → blue). A neutral liquid changes neither. Litmus is our quick colour test.

Q2 In a circuit, one bulb glows. You add a second identical cell in the same direction. What happens to the bulb?
(A) It glows dimmer
(B) It glows brighter ✓
(C) It stops glowing
(D) No change at all
Correct answer: B

Q3 Find the value: 12 − 3 × (4 − 6)
(A) 18 ✓
(B) 6
(C) −18
(D) 30
Correct answer: A

The idea BODMAS tells the order: Brackets → Of/Order → Division/Multiplication → Addition/Subtraction. Do brackets and multiplication before subtraction.

12 − 3 × (4 − 6)
Brackets first → (4 − 6) = −2
Multiply → 3 × (−2) = −6
12 − (−6) = 12 + 6 = 18
Why the other options miss
  • B: 6 comes from wrongly doing 12−3 first, then ×, ignoring BODMAS.
  • C: −18 forgets that minus-times-minus is plus.
  • D: 30 mishandles the signs.

Where you meet it in real life A shopkeeper computing '12 items minus 3 boxes of (4−6) returns' must follow the same order to bill correctly.

Q5 Two bulbs are in a single loop (series) with one cell. One bulb's filament breaks. The other bulb:
(A) glows brighter
(B) glows the same
(C) goes off ✓
(D) glows dimmer
Correct answer: C

The idea A series circuit is a single loop — current has only one path. If any part of that loop opens, current stops everywhere.

Reasoning One bulb's filament breaking is like cutting the wire, so the second bulb goes off too.

Why the other options miss
  • A: Brighter needs more current, not a break.
  • B: Same is impossible once the loop is cut.
  • D: Dimmer needs a partial path; here the path is fully broken.

Where you meet it in real life Old-style fairy lights: when one bulb fused, the whole string went dark — that's a series circuit.

Q100 A single circuit has 1 cell and 1 bulb (bulb glows). Three changes are made one at a time: (i) add a second cell in series, (ii) add a second bulb in the same loop, (iii) open the switch. The brightness order of the original bulb, from BRIGHTEST to OFF, is:

Order: (i) → (ii) → (iii)
Why the other options miss
