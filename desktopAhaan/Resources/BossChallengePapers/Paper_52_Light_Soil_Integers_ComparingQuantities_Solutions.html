(B) air and water can reach the roots and roots can spread easily ✓
(C) all the humus is removed from the field
(D) the soil becomes harder for water to enter
Correct answer: B

The idea Ploughing breaks up packed soil, opening air spaces. Loose soil lets air and water reach the seeds and roots, and lets tender roots push through easily.

Packed soil has few air gaps
ploughing loosens it and opens spaces
so air, water and growing roots move through freely.

Where you meet it in real life Gardeners turn over a flower bed with a fork for the same reason before planting.

Why the other options miss
  • A: Soil never becomes bedrock; ploughing loosens it so air, water and roots move freely.
  • C: Ploughing mixes humus in rather than removing it; its purpose is to aerate and loosen the soil.
  • D: Loosening does the OPPOSITE — it makes water soak in more easily, not less.
Q99Integers Subtracting, (-4) - (-9) comes to:
(A) 5 ✓
(B) 13
(C) -13
(D) -5
Correct answer: A

The idea Subtracting a negative is the same as adding its positive: (-4) - (-9) = -4 + 9 = 5.

Subtracting -9 means adding +9
-4 + 9
= 5.

Where you meet it in real life Cancelling a 9-unit debt while 4 units down leaves you 5 units ahead — taking away a negative.

Why the other options miss
  • B: 13 adds the sizes; (-4) - (-9) = -4 + 9 = 5, not 13.
  • C: -13 ADDS the sizes with a negative sign; subtracting a negative adds, giving -4 + 9 = 5.
  • D: -5 has the wrong sign; -4 + 9 lands at +5, not -5.
Q100Comparing Quantities If 20% of a number is 40, then the number itself is:
(A) 60
(B) 200 ✓
(C) 400
(D) 8
Correct answer: B

The idea 20% of the number is 40, so the whole (100%) is found by working back: 40 is one fifth of the number, so the number is 40 x 5 = 200.

20% = 40, so 1% = 40 / 20 = 2
100% = 2 x 100
= 200.

Where you meet it in real life Working back from a percentage like this finds an original price when only the tax or tip amount is known.

Why the other options miss
  • A: 60 adds 20 to 40; to find the whole from 20% you scale up, giving 200.
  • C: 400 doubles too far (treats 40 as 10%); since 40 is 20%, the number is 40 x 5 = 200.
  • D: 8 takes 20% OF 40; here 40 IS the 20%, so the whole is 40 x 5 = 200.
