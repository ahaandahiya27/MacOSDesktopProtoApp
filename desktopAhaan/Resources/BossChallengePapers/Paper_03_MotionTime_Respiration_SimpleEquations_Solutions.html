Q1Motion & Time Speed of a moving body is found by:
(A) distance ÷ time ✓
(B) distance + time
(C) distance × time
(D) time ÷ distance
Correct answer: A

The idea Speed tells how fast something moves: speed = distance ÷ time.

Where you meet it in real life A car going 100 km in 2 hours has speed 100÷2 = 50 km/h.

Why the other options miss
  • B: You can't add distance and time — different things.
  • C: Multiplying gives the wrong quantity, not speed.
  • D: That's upside-down (it gives time per unit distance).
