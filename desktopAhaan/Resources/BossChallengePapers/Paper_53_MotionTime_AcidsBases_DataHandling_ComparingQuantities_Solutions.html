Q1Motion & Time A scooter covers a distance of 90 kilometres in exactly 3 hours of steady riding. Its speed is:
(A) 30 km/h ✓
(B) 270 km/h
(C) 90 km/h
(D) 3 km/h
Correct answer: A

The idea Speed is distance divided by time. Here 90 km / 3 h = 30 km/h — the scooter covers 30 km in each hour.

Speed = distance / time
= 90 km / 3 h
= 30 km/h.

Where you meet it in real life A bus timetable's 'average speed' is worked out exactly this way to predict arrival times.

  • C: 90 km is the whole distance, not the speed; dividing by the 3 hours gives 30 km/h.
  • D: 3 is the number of hours, not the speed; the speed is 90 / 3 = 30 km/h.
