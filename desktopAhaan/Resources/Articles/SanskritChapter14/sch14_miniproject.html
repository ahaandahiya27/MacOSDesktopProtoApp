🛠️ Mini Project — Build Your Own One-Page Case Chart

A hands-on activity that turns the chapter from something you read into something you do.

≈ 20–30 min total · do-it-yourself level

The chapter introduced three declension paradigms. This project lets you make your own chart for a noun you choose.

What you'll need

  • A blank sheet of paper
  • A pencil and eraser
  • The chapter's देव/कवि/गुरु tables for reference
  • 15 minutes

Steps

  1. Pick any masculine अकारान्त noun (बाल, राम, शिक्षक, हस्त — any -अ-ending noun).
  2. Draw a 8x3 grid: rows = the 8 cases (प्रथमा through सम्बोधन); columns = singular/dual/plural.
  3. Fill in all 24 forms using the देव paradigm as your guide.
  4. Compare your chart with देव's chart. Same endings? Yes.
  5. Recite all 24 forms aloud once. Notice the pattern.

🔍 What you should notice

You have just generated 24 correct Sanskrit forms from scratch. The chapter's grammar is now actionable, not just memorisable.

Why it works

Sanskrit grammar BECOMES yours only when you apply it. The chart is a 15-minute proof that you can decline any new noun the textbook throws at you.
