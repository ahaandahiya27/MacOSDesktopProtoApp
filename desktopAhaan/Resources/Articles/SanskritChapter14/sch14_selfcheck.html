Question 1 — The warm-up

How many विभक्तयः (cases) does Sanskrit have (excluding the vocative)?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: seven


• प्रथमा, द्वितीया, तृतीया, चतुर्थी, पञ्चमी, षष्ठी, सप्तमी = SEVEN cases.
• Plus the सम्बोधनम् (vocative) makes eight forms total.

Question 2 — The check

The dative singular (चतुर्थी एकवचनम्) of 'देव' is —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: देवाय


• देव's चतुर्थी एकवचनम् = देवाय (the same -आय ending you saw in मित्राय नमः in Ch.3).
• देवः = nom.; देवस्य = gen.; देवेन = inst.

Question 3 — The stretch

The nominative plural (प्रथमा बहुवचनम्) of 'कवि' is —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: कवयः


• इकारान्त nouns make nominative plural in -अयः: कवयः ('poets').
• Compare हरयः, मुनयः, रवयः — same pattern.

Question 4 — The trap

Which noun stem-type does 'गुरु' belong to?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: उकारान्त (ending in उ)


• गुरु ends in उ — so it is उकारान्त ('-u-ending').
• Same family: शम्भु, विष्णु, साधु, प्रभु.

Question 5 — The finisher

Which case (विभक्ति) marks the SUBJECT (कर्ता) of a sentence?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: प्रथमा (1st)


• प्रथमा (nominative) marks the doer/subject.
• द्वितीया marks the object, षष्ठी 'of', सप्तमी 'in/on/at'.
