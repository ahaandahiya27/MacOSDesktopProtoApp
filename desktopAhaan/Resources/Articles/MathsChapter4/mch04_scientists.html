Mathematics was built by real people, often centuries apart, each adding one idea. Here are the minds behind the ideas in this chapter.

al-Khwarizmi (c. 780 – c. 850 CE)

Persian (worked in Baghdad). His book gave us the word 'algebra'; his name gives us 'algorithm'.

Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi worked at the House of Wisdom in Baghdad. His treatise on solving equations, whose title contained the Arabic word al-jabr ('restoring'), became the foundation of algebra and gave the subject its name. A Latin form of his own name, 'Algoritmi', became our word 'algorithm'.
