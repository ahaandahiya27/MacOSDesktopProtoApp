Expressions Using Letter-Numbers: Worked NCERT Questions

Textbook questions from this chapter, each with a clear worked answer.

These are the kind of questions the NCERT chapter asks. Try each one yourself first, then check the worked answer.

Q1. Shabnam is 3 years older than Aftab. Write an expression for Shabnam's age in terms of Aftab's age a, and find it when a = 23.

Answer: Shabnam's age = a + 3. When a = 23, Shabnam's age = 23 + 3 = 26 years.

Q2. Parthiv makes L-shapes using 2 matchsticks each. Write an expression for the matchsticks needed for n Ls.

Answer: Number of matchsticks = 2 × n = 2n. For example, 45 Ls need 2 × 45 = 90 matchsticks.

Q3. Write formulas for the perimeter of (a) an equilateral triangle, (b) a regular pentagon, (c) a regular hexagon, with side q.

Answer: (a) 3q (three equal sides), (b) 5q (five equal sides), (c) 6q (six equal sides). Each is the number of sides times the side length q.

Q4. Find the value of 5m + 3 when m = 2.

Answer: 5m means 5 × m. When m = 2: 5 × 2 + 3 = 10 + 3 = 13.

Q5. Mind the mistake: someone wrote 'If a = −4, then 10 − a = 6.' Is it correct?

Answer: No. Substituting a = −4: 10 − a = 10 − (−4) = 10 + 4 = 14, not 6. The error is dropping the negative sign of a.

Q6. Simplify the perimeter expression l + b + l + b for a rectangle.

Answer: Reorder to l + l + b + b, then collect like terms: l + l = 2l and b + b = 2b, giving 2l + 2b. This equals the original for all l, b.

Q7. A shop sells pencils at price c and erasers at price d. Over 3 days it sells 5+3+10 pencils and 4+6+1 erasers. Write the simplified total earnings.

Answer: Pencil earnings 5c + 3c + 10c = 18c; eraser earnings 4d + 6d + 1d = 11d. Total = 18c + 11d (unlike terms, left separate).

Q8. Krithika has 3 notes of ₹100, 5 of ₹20 and 6 of ₹5. Write an expression and find her total.

Answer: 3 × 100 + 5 × 20 + 6 × 5 = 300 + 100 + 30 = ₹430.
