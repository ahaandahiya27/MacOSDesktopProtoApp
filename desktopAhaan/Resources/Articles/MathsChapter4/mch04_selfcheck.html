Question 1 — The warm-up

Shabnam is 3 years older than Aftab. Write an expression for Shabnam's age in terms of Aftab's age a, and find it when a = 23.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Shabnam's age = a + 3. When a = 23, Shabnam's age = 23 + 3 = 26 years.

Question 2 — The check

Parthiv makes L-shapes using 2 matchsticks each. Write an expression for the matchsticks needed for n Ls.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Number of matchsticks = 2 × n = 2n. For example, 45 Ls need 2 × 45 = 90 matchsticks.

Question 3 — The stretch

Write formulas for the perimeter of (a) an equilateral triangle, (b) a regular pentagon, (c) a regular hexagon, with side q.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: (a) 3q (three equal sides), (b) 5q (five equal sides), (c) 6q (six equal sides). Each is the number of sides times the side length q.

Question 4 — The trap

Find the value of 5m + 3 when m = 2.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: 5m means 5 × m. When m = 2: 5 × 2 + 3 = 10 + 3 = 13.

Question 5 — The finisher

Mind the mistake: someone wrote 'If a = −4, then 10 − a = 6.' Is it correct?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: No. Substituting a = −4: 10 − a = 10 − (−4) = 10 + 4 = 14, not 6. The error is dropping the negative sign of a.
