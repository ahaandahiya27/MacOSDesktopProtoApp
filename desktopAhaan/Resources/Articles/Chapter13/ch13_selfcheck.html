Question 1 — from Chapter 13

Define speed and write its formula. State its SI unit.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: Speed is the distance covered by an object in unit time. Formula: Speed = Distance / Time. SI unit: metres per second (m/s).

Why: Definition: distance covered per unit time.

Question 4 — from Chapter 13

A pendulum clock runs slightly fast in summer and slow in winter. Explain WHY using the physics of pendulums, and predict what design feature would compensate.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: A pendulum's period depends on its LENGTH (T = 2π√(L/g)). In summer, metal pendulum rod expands → length increases slightly → period increases → clock runs SLOW (each tick longer). Wait — actually the question says fast in summer. Let me reconsider: if pendulum lengthens, period INCREASES, so the clock ticks slower per minute → clock runs SLOW in summer. The original question may be reversed. Either way: temperature changes length → length changes period → clock drifts. Compensation: GRIDIRON PENDULUM — alternating rods of brass and steel that expand opposite directions, cancelling net length change. Used in precision clocks since 1721. The physics is the temperature-induced length change; the engineering is materials that cancel it.

Why: Pendulum period T = 2π√(L/g).

Question 5 — from Chapter 13

GPS satellites have to apply RELATIVITY corrections to their clocks. Explain WHY — what does Einstein's relativity say happens to a clock that's moving fast AND high above Earth's gravity?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: Two relativistic effects on satellite clocks: (1) SPECIAL RELATIVITY: moving clocks tick slower. GPS satellites orbit at ~14,000 km/h — their clocks run ~7 microseconds/day SLOWER than ground clocks. (2) GENERAL RELATIVITY: clocks at lower gravity tick faster. GPS satellites are 20,000 km above Earth in weaker gravity — their clocks run ~45 microseconds/day FASTER than ground clocks. Net effect: clocks run ~38 microseconds/day FASTER. GPS engineers pre-correct the satellite clocks by exactly this amount BEFORE launch — otherwise positions would be off by 10+ km per day. GPS is the most public proof of relativity in daily life.

Why: Two relativistic effects on orbiting clocks: speed slows them (special), low gravity speeds them up (general).
