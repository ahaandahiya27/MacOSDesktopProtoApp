Q1. The basic unit of speed is (a) km/min (b) m/min (c) km/h (d) m/s.

Model answer: (d) m/s. The metre is the SI unit of distance; the second is the SI unit of time. SI unit of speed is metres per second. Other units (km/h, mph) are common in daily life but the scientific 'basic' unit is m/s. Conversion: 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h.

Q2. A car moves with a speed of 40 km/h for 15 minutes and then with a speed of 60 km/h for the next 15 minutes. The total distance covered by the car is (a) 100 km (b) 25 km (c) 15 km (d) 10 km.

Model answer: (b) 25 km. First leg: 40 km/h × 15 min = 40 × 0.25 = 10 km. Second leg: 60 km/h × 15 min = 60 × 0.25 = 15 km. Total = 10 + 15 = 25 km.

Q3. Suppose the two figures (graph A: straight diagonal line; graph B: stepped non-uniform) represent motion of two cars. Which one represents (a) uniform motion (b) non-uniform motion?

Model answer: (a) Uniform motion = straight line on a distance-time graph (constant slope = constant speed). The diagonal line in graph A. (b) Non-uniform motion = curve or steps (slope changes over time). The stepped graph B. A train cruising at constant speed produces a straight line; a Mumbai local with stops produces steps and curves.

Q4. Distance between two stations is 240 km. A train takes 4 hours to cover this distance. Calculate the speed of the train.

Model answer: Speed = Distance ÷ Time = 240 ÷ 4 = 60 km/h. In SI units, this is 60 × (1000 m / 3600 s) = ~16.67 m/s. This is a typical Indian passenger train average — much slower than max because of stops.

Q5. The odometer of a car reads 57,321 km at the start of a trip and 57,605 km at the end of the trip. If the trip took 8 hours, calculate the average speed of the car in km/h and m/s.

Model answer: Distance = 57605 − 57321 = 284 km. Time = 8 hours. Average speed = 284 / 8 = 35.5 km/h. In m/s: 35.5 × (1000/3600) = 35.5/3.6 ≈ 9.86 m/s. Note this is AVERAGE — the car might have driven 100 km/h on highway and 0 km/h at restaurant stops, but averaged 35.5 km/h.

Q6. Salma takes 15 minutes from her house to reach her school on a bicycle. If the bicycle has a speed of 2 m/s, calculate the distance between her house and the school.

Model answer: Time = 15 minutes = 15 × 60 = 900 s. Speed = 2 m/s. Distance = Speed × Time = 2 × 900 = 1800 m = 1.8 km. So the school is 1.8 km from her house.

Q7. Show the shape of the distance-time graph for the motion in the following cases: (i) A car moving with a constant speed. (ii) A car parked on a side road.

Model answer: (i) Straight diagonal line from bottom-left rising upward at constant slope. Constant speed = constant slope. (ii) Horizontal flat line — the car isn't moving, so distance from a fixed reference point doesn't change with time. The line is at some height (its position) but parallel to time axis.

Q8. Which of the following relations is correct? (a) Speed = Distance × Time (b) Speed = Distance/Time (c) Speed = Time/Distance (d) Speed = 1/(Distance × Time).

Model answer: (b) Speed = Distance ÷ Time. A car covers 60 km in 1 hour means its speed is 60 km/h, which is 60 km ÷ 1 h. Speed always has units of distance over time (m/s, km/h, etc.). Option (a) would have units of (distance × time), which is meaningless physically.
