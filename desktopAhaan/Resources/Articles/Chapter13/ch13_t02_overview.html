The Pendulum and Measuring Time

Topic t02 of Chapter 13 · Motion and Time · Class 7 Science

The big idea

A pendulum's swing time (period) depends only on the string LENGTH — not on the mass or how far you pull it. Specifically, T = 2π√(L/g). Galileo discovered this watching a lamp swing in Pisa Cathedral in 1583, and pendulum clocks ruled accurate timekeeping for the next 300 years.

Galileo's Discovery. The √L relationship is also why a longer pendulum looks 'slower' — it isn't slower, it just has a longer period.

Looking ahead

Class 11 Physics 'Oscillations' formalises this as Simple Harmonic Motion. JEE Physics tests T = 2π√(L/g) and the energy + phase analysis every year. NEET asks the basic pendulum formula in their physics section.
