Speed and Motion

Topic t01 of Chapter 13 · Motion and Time · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Speed = distance ÷ time. A cycle covers about 15 km in an hour, a car 80 km, a train 160 km, an airliner 900 km. Motion is uniform when the speed is constant, non-uniform when it changes (a falling apple, a car at a traffic light). Distance-time graphs make this readable: flat line = at rest, straight slanted line = uniform speed, upward curve = speeding up.

Distance, Time, and Graphs. The slope of a distance-time graph is the speed itself — your first encounter with the most important idea in all of calculus.
