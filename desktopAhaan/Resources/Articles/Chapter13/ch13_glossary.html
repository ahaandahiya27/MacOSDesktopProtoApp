A Class 7 Motion and Time Dictionary

The 10 must-know terms from Chapter 13 — in plain English plus the Hindi name, with a one-line meaning a Class 7 student can actually use.

The 10 terms to know

  • Speed · चाल — The distance covered per unit time.
    Example: A cyclist covering 20 km in 1 hour has speed 20 km/h.
  • Average speed · औसत चाल — Total distance covered divided by total time taken, accounting for stops and varying speed.
    Example: A train averages 60 km/h over a 240 km journey even though it touched 110 km/h between stations.
  • Uniform motion · एकसमान गति — Motion in which speed remains constant — equal distances in equal time intervals.
    Example: A satellite orbiting Earth at constant speed.
  • Non-uniform motion · असमान गति — Motion in which speed changes over time.
    Example: A car in city traffic — speeding up, slowing down, stopping.
  • Distance-time graph · दूरी-समय ग्राफ — A graph with time on the x-axis and distance from start on the y-axis; the slope at any point equals the speed at that instant.
    Example: A straight diagonal line indicates uniform motion; a curve indicates non-uniform motion.
  • Simple pendulum · सरल लोलक — A mass hung on a string, swinging back and forth under gravity.
    Example: The chandelier in a Goan church; the bob of a wall clock.
  • Time period (of a pendulum) · आवर्तकाल — The time taken for one complete oscillation (one back-and-forth).
    Example: A 1-metre pendulum on Earth has a time period of ~2 seconds.
  • Sundial · धूप घड़ी — An ancient time-measuring device that uses the position of the Sun's shadow.
    Example: Jantar Mantar in Delhi and Jaipur contains the world's largest sundials.
  • Atomic clock · परमाणु घड़ी — An extremely precise clock that uses vibrations of atoms (like cesium) to measure time.
    Example: The atomic clocks in GPS satellites are accurate to one second in 30 million years.
  • Odometer · ओडोमीटर — An instrument in vehicles that measures total distance travelled.
    Example: A car's odometer might read 57,321 km after 5 years of use.
