Timeline

From the fading of the Gupta Empire (c.600 CE) to about 1200 CE, India had no single empire but many strong regional kingdoms - a creative, not declining, age.

Big changes make more sense as a sequence. Walk this timeline from start to finish to see how one step led to the next.

Key events, 6th to 10th centuries

The main political and cultural milestones of the period, in order.

  • 606 CE: Harsha's reign begins
    Harshavardhana ascends the throne at Kannauj, the north's last great king before the Tripartite Struggle.
  • 630-644 CE: Xuanzang in India
    The Chinese pilgrim travels India, recording its kings and cities and carrying 600+ manuscripts home.
  • ~650 CE: Bhakti begins in the south
    The Tamil Alvars and Nayanars spark the Bhakti tradition, devotion open to all.
  • 712 CE: Conquest of Sindh
    Muhammad bin Qasim defeats King Dahar and conquers Sindh, though Arab rule stays limited.
  • 750 CE: Rise of the Palas
    Gopala is chosen king in Bengal, founding the Pala dynasty and the Tripartite Struggle era.
  • 9th century: Cholas emerge
    Reborn under Vijayalaya at Tanjavur, the Cholas grow into a dominant southern power.
