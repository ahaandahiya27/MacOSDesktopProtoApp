The big idea

Soil is not just solid — it's a mix of mineral grains, organic humus, water and air. Roots breathe through soil air pockets; nutrients dissolve in soil water. Percolation rate (how fast water sinks) decides what crop grows best where — paddy on clay, cotton on black soil, groundnut on sand.

Water, Air and Crops. Soil acts as a slow-release tank: it holds water from the monsoon and feeds it to crops weeks later, evening out the rainfall.

Looking ahead

Class 9 Geography ties soil to agriculture and irrigation. Class 11 Biology covers soil's role in mineral nutrition of plants. NEET asks soil-microbe symbioses and the nitrogen cycle every year.
