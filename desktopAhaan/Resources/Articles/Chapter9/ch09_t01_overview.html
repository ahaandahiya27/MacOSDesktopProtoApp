The Soil Profile and Types

Topic t01 of Chapter 9 · Soil · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Soil is a layered structure — O (organic litter), A (topsoil rich in humus), B (subsoil holding minerals), C (weathered rock), R (bedrock). Soils are classified by particle size: sandy (large grains, fast drainage), clayey (tiny grains, water-holding), and loamy (a balanced mix, ideal for most crops).

Layers Underfoot. Different layers store different things — topsoil has the nutrients and roots; subsoil holds water reserves; bedrock is the slow factory turning stone into new soil over centuries.
