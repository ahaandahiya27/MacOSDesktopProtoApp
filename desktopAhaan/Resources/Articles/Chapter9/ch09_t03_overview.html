Soil Conservation

Topic t03 of Chapter 9 · Soil · Class 7 Science

The big idea

When forests are cut, rain washes away fertile topsoil in months — what nature took centuries to build. Three classic conservation tricks: terracing (flat steps on slopes), cover crops (keep soil clothed), contour ploughing (across the slope, not down it). And nature's free soil engineer is the earthworm — Darwin called it 'the plough'.

Erosion, Earthworms and Restoration. Erosion plus desertification is one of India's biggest environmental challenges; conservation is everyone's job, not just the farmer's.

Looking ahead

Class 10 Geography studies land degradation and conservation policy. Class 11 Biology covers ecological succession (how barren land slowly rebuilds soil + vegetation). NEET asks soil-biology questions on biofertilisers, decomposers, and the nitrogen cycle.
