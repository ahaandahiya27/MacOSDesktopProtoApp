A Class 7 Soil Dictionary

The 10 terms to know

  • Soil · मिट्टी — The top layer of Earth's land surface, formed from broken rock plus organic matter, supporting plant growth.
    Example: Healthy garden soil contains billions of microorganisms per handful.
  • Soil profile · मिट्टी का परिच्छेद — The vertical cross-section showing the soil's layered structure from surface to bedrock.
    Example: A typical profile: leaf litter, topsoil, subsoil, weathered rock, bedrock.
  • Humus · ह्यूमस — Dark organic matter formed by the decomposition of plant and animal remains; the most fertile component of soil.
    Example: Forest soils have 20-30% humus content; depleted farmland may have less than 1%.
  • Sandy soil · बलुई मिट्टी — Soil dominated by large rock particles; drains water quickly, low in nutrients.
    Example: Rajasthan's Thar Desert soil is sandy — great for groundnut and watermelon, poor for rice.
  • Clayey soil · चिकनी मिट्टी — Soil dominated by tiny particles; holds water tightly, sticky when wet, hard when dry.
    Example: Bengal's clay-rich soils are ideal for rice paddy and jute.
  • Loamy soil · दोमट मिट्टी — A balanced mix of sand, silt, and clay with humus — best for most crops.
    Example: Punjab's alluvial loam supports India's wheat economy.
  • Percolation rate · रिसाव दर — How quickly water seeps down through soil; expressed in mL/min or mm/hour.
    Example: Sand: ~10 mL/min. Loam: ~5 mL/min. Clay: ~1 mL/min.
  • Soil erosion · मृदा अपरदन — The wearing away and loss of topsoil by water, wind, or human activity.
    Example: India loses ~5 billion tonnes of topsoil per year — a layer 1 cm thick across the country.
  • Terracing · सीढ़ीनुमा खेती — Cutting steep slopes into stepped flat strips to prevent water runoff and soil loss.
    Example: Sikkim and Himachal Pradesh's rice paddies are terraced into mountain slopes.
  • Cover crop · आवरण फसल — A plant grown specifically to cover bare soil and prevent erosion + add nutrients.
    Example: Farmers in Bihar grow dhaincha (Sesbania) between rice seasons to protect soil + fix nitrogen.
