The turning tides: key events of the 11th-12th centuries

How invasions and achievements unfolded side by side across two pivotal centuries.

  • 985-1014: Rajaraja Chola I
    The Cholas reach their peak as a sea power, conquering the Maldives and northern Sri Lanka and building the Brihadishvara temple.
  • 1008: Mahmud defeats Anandapala
    Mahmud of Ghazni overcomes the Hindu Shahi ruler, gaining easy access to the Indus plains.
  • ~1025: Somnath raid
    Mahmud destroys the Somnath Shiva temple and loots its treasure, then withdraws.
  • 1114: Bhaskaracharya born
    India's great mathematician, author of the Lilavati, is born in the Deccan - science flourishes away from the invaded north.
  • 1191-92: Battles of Tarain
    Prithviraj Chauhan wins the first battle (1191) but is defeated and executed by Muhammad Ghuri in the second (1192).
  • 1205-06: Bakhtiyar Khilji
    Ghuri's commander conquers Bihar and Bengal, destroying Nalanda and Vikramashila; he is then defeated in Kamarupa.
