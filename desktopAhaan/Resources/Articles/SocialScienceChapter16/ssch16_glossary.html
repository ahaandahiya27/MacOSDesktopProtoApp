The 11 terms to know

  • Turkic · तुर्क — Relating to peoples, languages and cultures historically from a vast region across Central Asia to Turkey and Siberia.
    Example: The Ghaznavids and Ghurids were Turkic powers.
  • Sultan · सुल्तान — An Arabic word meaning 'authority' or 'power'; in the Muslim world it came to mean a sovereign ruler.
    Example: Mahmud of Ghazni took the title 'sultan'.
  • Booty · लूट — Valuable goods seized during war or a raid.
    Example: Mahmud returned to Ghazni with huge booty after each campaign.
  • Khyber Pass · खैबर दर्रा — A major pass through the Hindu Kush mountains, long used as both a trade route and an invasion route into India.
    Example: The Hindu Shahis guarded the Khyber Pass.
  • Torana · तोरण — A decorative stone gateway, as built by the Kakatiyas at Warangal.
    Example: The Warangal fort has impressive toranas.
  • Vihara · विहार — A Buddhist monastery.
    Example: Rajaraja allowed a vihara to be built at Nagapattinam by the Srivijaya ruler.
  • Vachana · वचन — A short Kannada poem; Basavanna used vachanas to express his spiritual and ethical vision.
    Example: Basavanna's vachanas teach equality and devotion.
  • Anubhava mandapa · अनुभव मंडप — A hall set up by Basavanna in Kalyani where people of all backgrounds met to exchange ideas.
    Example: The anubhava mandapa welcomed people of every social rank.
  • Vishishtadvaita · विशिष्टाद्वैत — Ramanujacharya's philosophy holding that the world and souls are real, with bhakti and surrender as the path to liberation.
    Example: Ramanuja's vishishtadvaita differed from Shankara's advaita.
  • Lingayat · लिंगायत — The movement founded by Basavanna that rejected caste and ritual, teaching devotion, honest work and equality.
    Example: The Lingayat movement promoted the equal worth of all people.
  • Mount Meru · मेरु पर्वत — The cosmic mountain at the centre of the universe in Hindu mythology, symbolised by Angkor Wat's five towers.
    Example: Angkor Wat's five shikharas represent Mount Meru.
