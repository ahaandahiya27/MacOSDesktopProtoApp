Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

Chandragupta II of the Guptas is the same person as Chandragupta Maurya.

✅ Actually…

They are two different rulers from two different dynasties, centuries apart. Chandragupta Maurya founded the Maurya Empire long before; Chandragupta II ('Vikramaditya') was a Gupta ruler whose name is linked to the Iron Pillar's 'Chandra' inscription.

❌ Many kids think…

The Iron Pillar of Delhi never rusts because iron simply cannot rust.

✅ Actually…

Iron normally rusts very easily. The pillar resists rust only because its special phosphorus-rich iron lets a thin protective layer form on the surface, which seals the metal from oxygen and water.

❌ Many kids think…

Faxian's travelogue describes the whole of Gupta society exactly as it was.

✅ Actually…

Faxian recorded only what he saw, mostly the prosperous cities, from his own viewpoint at one time. He himself also noted the harsh treatment of the chandalas, so historians must check his account against many sources before trusting it.
