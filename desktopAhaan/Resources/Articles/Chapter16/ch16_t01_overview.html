Earth's Water and the Water Table

Topic t01 of Chapter 16 · Water: A Precious Resource · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Earth is 70% water, but ~97% of it is salty ocean and ~2% is frozen as polar ice. Less than 1% is fresh liquid water we can actually drink. Most of THAT is hidden underground in saturated rock layers called aquifers — pumped up through wells. The 'water table' is the depth at which the soil becomes fully saturated.

How Much Is Actually Drinkable. Rain seeps down and tops the water table up; pumps suck it down. If we extract faster than rain replenishes, the table drops and wells go dry.
