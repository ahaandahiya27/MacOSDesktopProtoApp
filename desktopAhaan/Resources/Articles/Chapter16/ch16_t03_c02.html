Why World Water Day Matters — Global and Indian Water Stress

The 22nd-of-March wake-up call · Class 7 Science

The world's water budget

Earth has 1.4 billion km³ of water — but 97% is salty ocean. Of the 3% fresh water, 70% is locked in glaciers and ice caps. Less than 1% of Earth's water is liquid fresh water accessible for drinking, farming and industry.

Water stress map of India

NITI Aayog's Composite Water Management Index (2018) flagged 21 Indian cities — including Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad — as likely to run out of groundwater. Chennai's 'Day Zero' moment in 2019 saw reservoirs at 1% and trucks delivering water to apartments.

World Water Day

Every 22 March the UN highlights a different water theme — groundwater, sanitation, valuing water, etc. The day was first observed in 1993. India runs the Jal Jeevan Mission aiming to deliver tap water to every rural household by 2024.

What individuals can do

  • Fix dripping taps (a drop per second wastes 5L/day).
  • Take shorter showers or use a bucket.
  • Reuse RO-reject water for plants/mopping.
  • Install rainwater harvesting at home.
  • Eat less meat — beef takes ~15,000 L of water per kg, vegetables only 300-500 L.
A vegetarian diet uses about 1/3 the water of an average meat-heavy diet. That single food choice has a bigger water-saving effect than all the tap-closing combined.

Try this at home

Set a 2-minute timer when you brush teeth tomorrow morning. Notice how unnecessary running the tap during brushing is. A 6 LPM tap left running wastes 12 litres in those 2 minutes.
