A Class 7 Water: A Precious Resource Dictionary

The 10 terms to know

  • Water cycle · जल चक्र — The continuous movement of water between the ground, water bodies, atmosphere, and back.
    Example: Rain → ground → river → sea → evaporation → cloud → rain.
  • Evaporation · वाष्पीकरण — The change of liquid water to water vapour.
    Example: Wet clothes drying in the sun; sea water becoming clouds.
  • Condensation · संघनन — The change of water vapour back into liquid water.
    Example: Water droplets on a cold bottle pulled from the fridge; cloud formation.
  • Water table · भूजल स्तर — The upper level of the underground water-saturated zone.
    Example: When the water table drops, wells run dry.
  • Aquifer · जलभृत — An underground layer of rock or sand that holds water.
    Example: Punjab's aquifers are depleting due to heavy tubewell pumping.
  • Groundwater · भूजल — Water present underground in soil or aquifers, accessed by wells and tubewells.
    Example: 60% of India's irrigation and 85% of rural drinking water comes from groundwater.
  • Rainwater harvesting · वर्षा जल संचयन — Collecting and storing rainwater for later use, or recharging it into the ground.
    Example: Rooftop pipes leading to underground tanks or recharge pits.
  • Drip irrigation · बिन्दु सिंचाई — Slow delivery of water to plant roots through pipes with tiny holes, minimising evaporation and runoff.
    Example: Maharashtra grape orchards use drip irrigation, using 30% of the water of flood irrigation.
  • Bawri / baoli / stepwell · बावड़ी — An ancient Indian water structure descending into the ground, with steps to access water at any level.
    Example: Chand Baori in Rajasthan, built 800 CE, is 13 storeys deep with 3500 steps.
  • Tanka / kund · टांका — An underground rainwater storage tank, traditionally used in dry Rajasthan and Gujarat.
    Example: A tanka in Bikaner stores all monsoon rainwater from a small catchment for an entire family for the year.
