Irrigation and Rainwater Harvesting

Topic t02 of Chapter 16 · Water: A Precious Resource · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Three irrigation methods, ranked by efficiency: drip (water dripped right at the roots, near-zero evaporation) > sprinkler (sprayed, some evaporation) > flood (whole field soaked, biggest waste). Rainwater harvesting catches rooftop runoff with gutters and tanks — saving thousands of litres a year per home. Some systems also recharge underground aquifers through soak-pits.

Smart Water for Smart Farming. Drip irrigation, invented in Israel, has revolutionised farming in arid Indian states like Rajasthan and Gujarat.
