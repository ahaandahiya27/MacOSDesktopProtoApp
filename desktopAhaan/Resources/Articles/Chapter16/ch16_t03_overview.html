Water Conservation

Topic t03 of Chapter 16 · Water: A Precious Resource · Class 7 Science

The big idea

For thousands of years, dry-region India built stepwells (bawdis) like Chand Baori and Rani-ki-Vav — some nine storeys deep — to reach the water table even in the driest months. Today's tools are different: low-flow taps, bucket baths, watering plants at dusk, and choosing native drought-resistant crops.

The Bawdi to the Tap. World Water Day (March 22) reminds us that water saved is water available to everyone downstream — and underground for the next generation.

Looking ahead

Class 9 Geography covers water conservation policy. Class 11 Economics studies water as a public good. Civics and environmental science cover sustainable development goals (SDG 6: Clean Water and Sanitation).
