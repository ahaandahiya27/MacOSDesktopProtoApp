The terms below are drawn from the chapter's own concept list. Each carries the canonical meaning the chapter teaches — the same words and meanings you will meet in the chapter's questions and exercises.

Words to Remember

  • श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी — Antyakshari with Shlokas. श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी — the antyakshari game played with Sanskrit shlokas (each new verse begins with the last letter of the previous one).
  • विद्याहीना न शोभन्ते — Without Knowledge, Even the Lovely Do Not Shine. विद्याहीना न शोभन्ते निर्गन्धा इव किंशुकाः — without knowledge even the handsome do not shine; they are like fragranceless किंशुक flowers.
  • विद्या-धन-शक्तिः — The Same Thing in Wicked and Good Hands. विद्या विवादाय धनं मदाय शक्तिः परेषां परिपीडनाय — knowledge, wealth and power, in the wicked, become quarrel, pride and oppression.
  • भारभूताः — A Burden on the Earth. भारभूताः — 'those who are a burden (on the earth)'; the chapter's harsh image for people without virtue.
  • खलः — The Wicked Person. खलः (khalaḥ) — 'a wicked, evil-minded person'.
  • शीलम् — Character
  • लक्ष्मीः — Wealth / Fortune (Personified). लक्ष्मीः (lakṣmīḥ) — 'wealth, fortune, prosperity' (also the goddess of wealth).
  • यशस्विनी — Endowed with Fame. यशस्विनी (yaśasvinī) — 'fame-bearing, renowned (feminine)'.
