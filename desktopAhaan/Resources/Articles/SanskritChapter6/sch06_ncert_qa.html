NCERT Q&A — Model Answers

The chapter's textbook exercise questions, with model answers grounded directly in the chapter's own content.

≈ 8 min · revision-ready

These are the kinds of questions the textbook itself asks at the end of the chapter. Each answer below is derived from the chapter's own text — what the lesson actually says, not what a wider Sanskrit reading would supply. Treat this as a study reference for revision before a test.

Question 1

'श्लोकान्त्याक्षरी' इत्यस्य कोऽर्थः?

Answer: श्लोकानाम् अन्त्याक्षरेण क्रीडा। (a game played with the last letters of shlokas)

💡 Why

श्लोक + अन्त्य ('last') + अक्षर ('letter') + ई (feminine action-noun) = the game in which each verse begins with the last letter of the previous one.

Question 2

विद्याहीनाः केषाम् इव शोभन्ते?

Answer: निर्गन्धाः किंशुकाः इव। (like fragrance-less Flame-of-the-Forest flowers)

💡 Why

The famous verse: विद्याहीना न शोभन्ते निर्गन्धा इव किंशुकाः — without knowledge, even the handsome do not shine; they are like beautiful but scent-less किंशुक flowers.

Question 3

खलस्य विद्या केन प्रयोजनेन भवति?

Answer: विवादाय। (for quarrel / dispute)

💡 Why

The verse contrasts: in the wicked (खल), विद्या → विवाद, धन → मद, शक्ति → परिपीडन. In the good (साधु) the same three serve sharing, giving, and protecting.

Question 4

'भारभूताः' इत्यस्य कोऽर्थः?

Answer: भारकराः। (burdens [on the earth])

💡 Why

Glossary: भारभूताः = भारकराः. भार ('burden') + भूत ('become') = 'burden-having-become'. The chapter applies this to those without शीलम् (good character).

Question 5

शीलम् इति किम्?

Answer: वृत्तम् / आचरणम्। (character / moral conduct)

💡 Why

Glossary: शीलम् = वृत्तम्. The chapter's anchor virtue — what makes a person truly worthy, beyond looks or birth or fortune.
