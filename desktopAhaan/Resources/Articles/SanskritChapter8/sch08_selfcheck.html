Question 1 — The warm-up

What does the chapter's title 'हितं मनोहारि च दुर्लभं वचः' mean?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: speech that is both beneficial and pleasing is rare


• हितम् = beneficial, मनोहारि = pleasing, च = and, दुर्लभम् = rare, वचः = speech.
• The whole thing means it's hard to find speech that's both helpful AND kind.

Question 2 — The check

According to the verse, you should gain विद्यां (knowledge) —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: moment by moment (क्षणशः)


• The verse: क्षणशः कणशो विद्यां अर्थं च साधयेत्.
• Small, steady units add up to great results.

Question 3 — The stretch

'विकत्थनम्' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: boasting


• Glossary: विकत्थनम् = गर्ववाणी ('proud talk') / मिथ्या आत्म-प्रशंसा ('false self-praise').
• Opposite of क्रियावान् (one of action).

Question 4 — The trap

The chapter praises the क्रियावान् person — meaning —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: one who is full of action


• Glossary: क्रियावान् = क्रियाशीलः ('engaged in action').
• -वान् suffix means 'possessing' — क्रिया + वान् = 'action-having'.

Question 5 — The finisher

'सूक्तिः' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: a well-spoken (wise) saying


• Glossary: सूक्तिः = सुन्दरम् उक्तिम् ('a beautiful saying').
• सु ('well') + उक्तिः ('saying'). Close in meaning to सुभाषितम्.
