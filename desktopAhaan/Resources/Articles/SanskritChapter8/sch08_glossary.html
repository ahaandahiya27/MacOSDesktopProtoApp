Words to Remember

  • हितं मनोहारि च दुर्लभं वचः — Rare Is Speech Both Useful and Pleasing. हितं मनोहारि च दुर्लभं वचः — 'rare is speech that is both useful (हितम्) and pleasing (मनोहारि)'.
  • क्षणशः कणशः — Moment by Moment, Particle by Particle. क्षणशः कणशो विद्यां अर्थं च साधयेत् — 'gain knowledge moment by moment, wealth particle by particle'.
  • विकत्थनम् — Boasting
  • क्रियावान् — One of Action. क्रियावान् (kriyāvān) — 'an active, doing person'; the opposite of a boaster.
  • सूक्तिः — A Good Saying. सूक्तिः (sūktiḥ) — 'a good/well-spoken saying'; a synonym of सुभाषितम्.
  • कुतूहलम् — Curiosity
  • भाण्डागारः — Treasure-Trove
  • शीलम् — Character (revisited). शीलम् (śīlam) — 'character, moral conduct' — again the chapter's anchor word.
