🛠️ Mini Project — One Day of the Two-Condition Speech Test

A hands-on activity that turns the chapter from something you read into something you do.

≈ 20–30 min total · do-it-yourself level

The chapter set a near-impossible speech standard. This project tries it for a single day.

What you'll need

  • A normal school/home day
  • Self-honesty
  • Patience with yourself

Steps

  1. At the start of the day, recite the chapter's rule: useful AND pleasant.
  2. Throughout the day, before each significant comment, pause for ONE second to check both boxes.
  3. If a sentence fails either test, say nothing OR re-phrase it.
  4. At day's end, write down ONE time you held back AND ONE time you spoke well.

🔍 What you should notice

You will be silent more often than you expected — most casual speech fails the test. Even ONE held-back unkind sentence is a win. Notice how others react when you ARE useful AND kind.

Why it works

The chapter does not ask for perfection — it asks for ATTENTION. One day's experiment is enough to start changing how you speak forever.
