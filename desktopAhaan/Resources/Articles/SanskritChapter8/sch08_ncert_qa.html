NCERT Q&A — Model Answers

The chapter's textbook exercise questions, with model answers grounded directly in the chapter's own content.

These are the kinds of questions the textbook itself asks at the end of the chapter. Each answer below is derived from the chapter's own text — what the lesson actually says, not what a wider Sanskrit reading would supply. Treat this as a study reference for revision before a test.

Question 1

कीदृशं वचः दुर्लभम् इति पाठः कथयति?

Answer: हितं मनोहारि च। (both useful and pleasing)

💡 Why

The chapter's title states the rule: rare is speech that is BOTH हितम् (beneficial) AND मनोहारि (pleasing). The particle च joins the two — both, not either.

Question 2

'क्षणशः कणशः' किं किम् साधयेत्?

Answer: विद्यां च अर्थं च। (knowledge and wealth)

💡 Why

The verse advises: knowledge moment by moment (क्षणशः), wealth particle by particle (कणशः). The -शस् suffix builds 'one-by-one' adverbs.

Question 3

कीदृशः पुरुषः शोभते — क्रियावान् वा विकत्थनः?

Answer: क्रियावान्। (the action-oriented one)

💡 Why

The chapter elevates क्रियावान् (one of action) over विकत्थन (the boaster). Sanskrit consistently prefers deeds (कर्म) to mere speech (कथन).

Question 4

'सूक्तिः' कीदृशं वचनम्?

Answer: सुन्दरम् उक्तिम्। (a beautiful saying / well-spoken)

💡 Why

Glossary: सूक्तिः = सुन्दरम् उक्तिम्. सु ('well') + उक्ति ('saying'). Synonym of सुभाषितम्. Both name the wisdom-verse genre.

Question 5

कुतूहलम् इति कीदृशी अवस्था?

Answer: ज्ञातुम् इच्छा / कौतुकम्। (curiosity / eagerness to know)

💡 Why

Glossary: कुतूहलम् = कौतुकम्. Close cousin of जिज्ञासा (the wish to know). The chapter holds कुतूहलम् up as a learner's virtue.
