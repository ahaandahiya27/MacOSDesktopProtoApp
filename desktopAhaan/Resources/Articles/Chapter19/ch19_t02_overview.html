Why this topic matters

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite, orbiting us every 27.3 days and showing a changing cycle of phases from New Moon to Full Moon. When the Sun, Moon, and Earth line up perfectly, we get the spectacular events called eclipses. The Moon's gravity also pulls on Earth's oceans, creating the daily rise and fall of tides. Understanding these ideas connects classroom science to real phenomena you see every day — from sunrise to seasons to the changing Moon.
