Forest Layers and Food Webs

Topic t01 of Chapter 17 · Forests: Our Lifeline · Class 7 Science

The big idea

A forest is a layered community. The canopy (tall trees, most sunlight) houses birds, monkeys, leopards. The understory has smaller trees and woody vines. The shrub layer is where deer and rodents browse. The forest floor is dark, damp, and full of decomposers. Energy flows from plants (producers) through herbivores to carnivores to apex predators — a food web.

The Four-Storey Living Building. Remove a top predator and prey populations explode, plants get over-grazed, and the whole forest changes. Forests are systems, not just collections of trees.
