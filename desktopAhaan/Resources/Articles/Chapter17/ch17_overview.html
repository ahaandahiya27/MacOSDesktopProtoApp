Forests: Our Lifeline

Chapter 17 · Class 7 Science

A forest is more than just trees

A forest is a layered, living community where thousands of species depend on each other. From the tall canopy down to the forest floor, every level has a job.

The four storeys of a forest

  • Canopy — tall trees at the top, where most sunlight is captured. Home to birds, monkeys, leopards.
  • Understory — smaller trees and woody vines under the canopy. Filtered light.
  • Shrub layer — bushes and grasses where deer and rodents browse.
  • Forest floor — dark, damp ground covered in leaf litter. Decomposers and insects thrive here.

The decomposer cycle

When leaves and animals die, fungi, bacteria, worms and termites break them down into humus — a dark, spongy material rich in nutrients. The nutrients return to the soil and feed the next generation of plants. Nothing in a forest is wasted.

The food web

A forest's energy starts with plants (the producers) and flows up:

  • Plants → grass, shrubs, fruits.
  • Herbivores → deer, rabbits, insects.
  • Carnivores → fox, snake.
  • Apex predators → tiger, eagle.

If you remove a top predator, prey populations explode, plants get over-grazed, and the whole forest changes.

Forests as Earth's lungs

Trees absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen through photosynthesis. They also remove airborne dust and pollutants. Cutting forests adds CO₂ to the atmosphere — and removes the system that cleans it up.

Forests also act as living water tanks. Tree canopies break the force of rain, roots hold soil, and water seeps slowly into the ground rather than flooding away.

Deforestation — a cascade of problems

Cutting forests sets off a chain reaction: less local rain, eroded soil, downstream floods, rising temperatures, lost wildlife homes. The damage often hurts communities thousands of kilometres away.

How to help

India celebrates Van Mahotsav — the national tree-planting festival — every July. Started in 1950 by Dr K M Munshi, it has planted millions of trees. Plant a sapling, adopt a tree at school, take part in the drive.
