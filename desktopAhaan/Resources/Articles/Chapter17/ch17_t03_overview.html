Deforestation and Conservation

Topic t03 of Chapter 17 · Forests: Our Lifeline · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Cutting forests sets off a chain reaction: less local rain, eroded soil, downstream floods, rising temperatures, lost wildlife homes. India's Van Mahotsav (national tree-planting festival, every July) was started in 1950 by Dr K M Munshi and has planted millions of trees. Modern conservation strategies include protected reserves, agroforestry, and community forest management.

What We Lose, How We Restore. The Chipko movement of the 1970s in Uttarakhand showed that ordinary people can stop deforestation just by hugging trees.
