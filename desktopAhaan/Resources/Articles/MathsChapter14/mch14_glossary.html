Constructions and Tilings: Key Words

Every term in this chapter, with a one-line meaning and an example.

&#8776; quick reference &middot; 10 terms

Knowing the exact words makes the maths click. Here is every key term from the chapter, defined plainly with an example you can picture.

Construction

Drawing an exact figure using only a compass and straightedge, without measuring.

Example: Constructing a perpendicular bisector.

In Hindi: रचना

Perpendicular bisector

A line that cuts a segment in half at a right angle.

Example: Found by equal arcs from both ends.

In Hindi: लंब समद्विभाजक

Angle bisector

A ray that divides an angle into two equal angles.

Example: Bisecting 60° gives two 30° angles.

In Hindi: कोण समद्विभाजक

Equidistant

At the same distance from two or more points.

Example: Points on a perpendicular bisector are equidistant from the segment's ends.

Tiling (tessellation)

Covering a region with shapes leaving no gaps or overlaps.

Example: Square floor tiles.

Interior angle

The angle inside a polygon at a vertex.

Example: A square's interior angle is 90°.

In Hindi: अंतःकोण

Regular polygon

A polygon with all sides and all angles equal.

Example: A regular hexagon.

In Hindi: सम बहुभुज

Vertex (tiling)

A point where tile corners meet; angles there sum to 360°.

Example: Four square corners meet at a vertex.

Tangram

A Chinese puzzle of 7 pieces cut from a square, rearranged into shapes.

Example: Forming an arrow from tangram pieces.

Straightedge

An unmarked ruler used only to draw straight lines in constructions.

Example: Used with a compass for exact figures.
