Minds Behind Constructions and Tilings

Real mathematicians whose work lies underneath this chapter.

≈ 7 min read · for curious 12-year-olds

Mathematics was built by real people, often centuries apart, each adding one idea. Here are the minds behind the ideas in this chapter.

Euclid (c. 300 BCE)

Greek (Alexandria). Built geometry on compass-and-straightedge constructions.

Euclid's geometry was built on figures drawn with only a compass and an unmarked straightedge. For over two thousand years people tried to use these tools to trisect any angle, double a cube and 'square the circle'. In the 1800s mathematicians finally proved that all three are impossible with those tools — the puzzles were not merely hard, they could not be done.

💡 What they gave us

Proving something is impossible can be as great an achievement as solving it.
