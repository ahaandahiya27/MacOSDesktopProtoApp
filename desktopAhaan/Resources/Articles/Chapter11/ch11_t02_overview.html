Excretion and the Kidneys

Topic t02 of Chapter 11 · Transportation in Animals and Plants · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Two bean-shaped kidneys filter about 180 litres of blood per day. They remove urea (the waste from protein breakdown), excess salt, and excess water — sending them to the bladder as urine. Useful nutrients are kept and returned to the blood.

The Body's Filtration Plant. Healthy kidneys are why your body's internal salt and water balance stays nearly constant despite huge daily variations in what you drink and eat.
