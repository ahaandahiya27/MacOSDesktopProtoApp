The Human Circulatory System

Topic t01 of Chapter 11 · Transportation in Animals and Plants · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Your heart is a 4-chambered pump (two atria + two ventricles) that beats about 80 times a minute — over 100,000 beats a day. The right side sends blood to the lungs to grab oxygen; the left side pumps oxygen-rich blood to every cell in the body. Blood travels in three vessel types: thick-walled arteries (away from heart, high pressure), valved veins (back to heart, low pressure), and thin one-cell-wall capillaries (where exchange actually happens).

Heart, Blood, Vessels. Blood itself has four parts — RBCs carry oxygen via haemoglobin, WBCs fight germs, platelets clot wounds, plasma carries everything else dissolved.
