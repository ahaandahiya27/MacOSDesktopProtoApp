Chapter 6 · Topic 3 Overview

Chemical Changes in Everyday Life

From your kitchen to the sky -- chemistry is happening all around you, all the time.

Topic overview · for curious 12-year-olds

You do not need a laboratory to see chemical changes. They happen when you bake a cake, when fireworks light up the night sky, and even inside every leaf on every tree. This topic takes you on a tour of spectacular real-world chemistry.

What This Topic Covers

Topic 3 has three concept articles. Each one focuses on a different area of everyday chemistry.

  1. Baking Soda and Vinegar -- Chemistry in Your Kitchen Why does this combo fizz? Explore acid-base reactions you can do at home.
  2. Fireworks and Sparklers -- Spectacular Chemical Reactions The science behind the colours, bangs, and sparkles in the sky.
  3. Photosynthesis and Respiration -- Life's Chemical Engines How plants make food from sunlight and how your body turns food into energy.

Chemistry is everywhere

Your body runs about 37 thousand billion billion chemical reactions every single second. Without chemistry, there would be no life at all!
