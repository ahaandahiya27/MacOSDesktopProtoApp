Question 1 — from Chapter 6

Classify each of the following as physical or chemical change: (a) Photosynthesis (b) Dissolving sugar in water (c) Burning of coal (d) Melting of wax

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: (a) Photosynthesis — chemical change (CO₂ and water become glucose). (b) Dissolving sugar in water — physical change (sugar can be recovered by evaporation). (c) Burning of coal — chemical change (carbon + oxygen → CO₂ + heat). (d) Melting of wax — physical change (wax can be re-solidified by cooling).

Why: Apply the rule: chemical change → new substance formed, usually irreversible. Physical change → no new substance, usually reversible.

Question 2 — from Chapter 6

Which is NOT a sign of a chemical change?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: The substance is moved to a new container

Why: Heat/light, gas, permanent colour change, formation of a new solid — all signs of chemical change.

Question 3 — from Chapter 6

True or false: A chemical change is always reversible.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: False

Why: Many chemical changes are essentially irreversible (burning wood → ash; can't unburn).

Question 4 — from Chapter 6

Why is crystallization better than simple evaporation for purification?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: Simple evaporation leaves behind all dissolved substances — pure and impure. Crystallization gives only the pure solid as crystals, leaving impurities in solution.

Why: Evaporation: heat until all water leaves → leaves a mixed solid.

Question 5 — from Chapter 6

Photosynthesis and respiration are best described as:

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: Opposite (one builds glucose, the other burns it)

Why: Photosynthesis: 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + sunlight → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂ (builds glucose, uses energy).
