NCERT Q&A — Model Answers

The chapter's textbook exercise questions, with model answers grounded directly in the chapter's own content.

≈ 8 min · revision-ready

These are the kinds of questions the textbook itself asks at the end of the chapter. Each answer below is derived from the chapter's own text — what the lesson actually says, not what a wider Sanskrit reading would supply. Treat this as a study reference for revision before a test.

Question 1

नरः कतिभिः वकारैः पूजितः भवति?

Answer: पञ्चभिः। (by five)

💡 Why

The famous verse lists five 'va' qualities — विद्यया, वपुषा, वाचा, वस्त्रेण, विनयेन — by which a person becomes honoured. The instrumental plural पञ्चभिः is the answer to कतिभिः.

Question 2

पुरुषेण कति दोषाः हातव्याः?

Answer: षड्। (six)

💡 Why

The verse names exactly six faults: निद्रा, तन्द्रा, भयम्, क्रोधः, आलस्यम्, दीर्घसूत्रता — to be discarded by one who desires prosperity (भूतिम् इच्छता).

Question 3

बुद्धिः केन शुध्यति?

Answer: ज्ञानेन। (by knowledge)

💡 Why

The purification verse pairs: body-water, mind-truth, soul-austerity, intellect-KNOWLEDGE. The intellect's cleanser is specifically ज्ञानम्.

Question 4

जलबिन्दुनिपातेन क्रमशः कः पूर्यते?

Answer: घटः। (the pot)

💡 Why

The verse: जलबिन्दुनिपातेन क्रमशः पूर्यते घटः — drop by falling drop, the pot gradually fills. The image is then extended to विद्या, धर्म, धनम्.

Question 5

आलस्यं केषां महान् रिपुः अस्ति?

Answer: मनुष्याणाम् / पुरुषाणाम् / कार्यार्थिनाम्। (of human beings / those seeking achievement)

💡 Why

The chapter frames laziness as an inner enemy (रिपुः) of those who would accomplish anything. The genitive plural answers केषाम्.

Try answering each question in your own words FIRST. Then read the model answer. The 'Why' line shows you the place in the chapter the answer comes from — so you can re-read that paragraph if your answer didn't match.
