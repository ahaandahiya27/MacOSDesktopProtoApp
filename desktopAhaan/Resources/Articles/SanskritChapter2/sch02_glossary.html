The terms below are drawn from the chapter's own concept list. Each carries the canonical meaning the chapter teaches — the same words and meanings you will meet in the chapter's questions and exercises.

Words to Remember

  • सुभाषितम् — A Wise Saying. सुभाषितम् (subhāṣitam) — a 'well-spoken' verse: a short saying full of wisdom.
  • पञ्च वकाराः — The Five 'Va' Qualities. A person is honoured by five qualities beginning with 'व': विद्या, वपुस्, वाणी, वस्त्र, विनय.
  • षड् दोषाः — The Six Faults to Give Up. One who wants prosperity must give up six faults: sleep, drowsiness, fear, anger, laziness, procrastination.
  • शुद्धि — How Different Things Are Purified. Body is cleaned by water, the mind by truth, the soul by learning and austerity, the intellect by knowledge.
  • जलबिन्दुनिपातेन — Drop by Drop the Pot Fills. जलबिन्दुनिपातेन क्रमशः पूर्यते घटः — 'drop by falling drop, the pot gradually fills'.
  • प्रेरकः — One Who Inspires
  • दीर्घसूत्रता — Procrastination
  • रिपुः — Enemy
  • सङ्गतिः — Company / Association. सङ्गतिः (saṅgatiḥ) — 'association, the company one keeps'.
