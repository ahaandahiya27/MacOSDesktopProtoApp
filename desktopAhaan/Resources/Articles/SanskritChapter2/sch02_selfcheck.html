Question 1 — The warm-up

By how many 'व' (va) qualities does a person become honoured (पूजितः)?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: पञ्च (five)


• The verse lists विद्या, वपुस्, वाणी, वस्त्र, विनय — all starting with व.
• The exercise answer to 'कतिभिः वकारैः?' is पञ्चभिः (five).

Question 2 — The check

How many faults (दोषाः) should a person desiring prosperity give up?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: षड् (six)


• The verse names six: निद्रा, तन्द्रा, भयम्, क्रोधः, आलस्यम्, दीर्घसूत्रता.
• The exercise answer to 'कति दोषाः हातव्याः?' is षड् (six).

Question 3 — The stretch

According to the verse, the intellect (बुद्धिः) is purified by —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: ज्ञानेन (knowledge)


• The verse: बुद्धिर्ज्ञानेन शुध्यति — the intellect is purified by knowledge.
• Water cleans the body, truth the mind, knowledge the intellect.

Question 4 — The trap

'जलबिन्दुनिपातेन क्रमशः पूर्यते ___' — what gets filled?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: घटः (a pot)


• The verse: जलबिन्दुनिपातेन क्रमशः पूर्यते घटः — drop by drop the pot fills.
• It teaches that small steady efforts accumulate.

Question 5 — The finisher

What does 'सुभाषितम्' mean?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: a wise, well-spoken saying


• सु ('good/well') + भाषित ('spoken') = 'well-said' — a wisdom verse.
• The chapter calls them सुवचनानि, good sayings of the wise.
