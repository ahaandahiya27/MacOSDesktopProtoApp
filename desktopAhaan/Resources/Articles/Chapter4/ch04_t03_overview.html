Heat in Bodies, Climates & the Future

From sweating to space rockets — heat in the bigger picture

≈ 4 min read · for curious 12-year-olds

Heat is not just a physics idea on a chalkboard — it is deeply woven into your body, your planet, and the technology of tomorrow. Your body maintains a steady 37 °C no matter if it is freezing outside or blazing hot. Earth's climate depends on a careful balance of heat coming in from the Sun and heat escaping back to space.

In this final topic, you will explore how your body manages heat, how humans are accidentally warming the planet, and how scientists use extreme cold — cryogenics — to preserve cells, fuel rockets, and push the boundaries of physics.

Concepts in this topic

  1. Heat in the Human Body Sweating, shivering, goosebumps — your body has a whole toolkit for temperature control.
  2. Heat and Climate Change Greenhouse gases trap heat in Earth's atmosphere. Understand the science behind global warming.
  3. Cryogenics Liquid nitrogen at −196 °C, superconductors, and rocket fuel — welcome to the world of extreme cold.
