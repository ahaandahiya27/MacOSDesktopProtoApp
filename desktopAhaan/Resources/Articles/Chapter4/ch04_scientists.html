Anders Celsius (with India context)

1701–1744 · Swedish

🎧 In one line

Defined the temperature scale that every Indian weather forecast and laboratory still uses 280 years later.

In 1742, the Swedish astronomer Anders Celsius proposed a temperature scale where water froze at 0 and boiled at 100. He chose 100 divisions because the decimal system was easier than the Fahrenheit scale used elsewhere. The Celsius scale spread worldwide because of its simplicity — pick any two stable points in nature, divide by 100, and you have a usable thermometer. India officially adopted Celsius after independence (the colonial Fahrenheit lingered briefly in railway timetables). Every IMD weather report, every WHO fever guideline, every Class 7 textbook in India uses his scale. His insight wasn't just a number system — it was the decision to anchor measurement on universal natural events (water phase changes) rather than human bodies (Fahrenheit was originally tied to brine and body heat).
