Heat, Temperature & Thermometers

Measuring the invisible energy around us

≈ 5 min read · for curious 12-year-olds

Temperature is something you feel every day — the chill of an ice cube, the warmth of sunlight on your face. But how do scientists put a number on "hot" and "cold"? That is where thermometers and temperature scales come in.

In this topic you will learn the difference between heat and temperature, explore how thermometers work, compare Celsius and Kelvin scales, and discover why a huge lake and a tiny cup of water at the same temperature hold vastly different amounts of heat energy.

Concepts in this topic

  1. Heat vs Temperature They sound the same but mean very different things. Learn the crucial distinction.
  2. How a Thermometer Works Mercury expands when heated — and that simple fact gave us one of science's most useful tools.
  3. Celsius and Kelvin Two scales, one universe. Why scientists prefer Kelvin while your weather app uses Celsius.
  4. Newton's Law of Cooling Why does hot tea cool quickly at first and then slow down? Newton figured it out.
  5. Temperature vs Heat — the Surprise Two cups at the same temperature can carry wildly different amounts of heat energy.
  6. Mercury vs Digital Thermometers Old-school glass tubes vs modern sensors — which is better and why?
  7. Specific Heat Capacity Water takes ages to heat up. Metal heats in seconds. The secret is specific heat capacity.
