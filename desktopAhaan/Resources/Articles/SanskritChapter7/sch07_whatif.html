"What If?" — ईशावास्यम् इदं सर्वम्

Hypothetical questions with no single right answer. Think hard before you read the discussion.

≈ 7 min read · brain-stretcher level

The textbook teaches what IS. Real understanding comes from asking what IF? Pick a question below, pause and think for a minute, then read one possible discussion.

What if 1 — the verse said 'the Lord is NOT in everything'?

The question: Imagine Sanskrit's most famous opening read नास्ति इदं सर्वम् — 'this is not pervaded'.

🧠 Think first

How would Indian thought have developed differently?

One discussion path: Removal of the pervasion doctrine would change Vedānta fundamentally. The idea that the divine is everywhere underlies everything from temple-worship of ordinary objects (tree, river, fire) to ethical claims that all beings deserve respect. A 'not in everything' opening would push Indian thought toward a stricter separation of sacred and profane — closer to many Western religious traditions.

What if 2 — the entire Īśopaniṣad were just this one verse?

The question: Gandhi said the first verse alone would suffice. Suppose the other 17 had been lost.

🧠 Think first

Is one verse enough?

One discussion path: Gandhi's claim is provocative on purpose. The other verses elaborate, complicate, refine — they discuss action, knowledge, immortality. They are not wasted. But Gandhi's point is that the FIRST line carries a complete teaching: God is everywhere, so do not grasp. If you really lived by that one line, you would not need the rest. Few people can live by it.

What if 3 — the second half of the verse — 'do not covet anyone's wealth' — had been left out?

The question: Imagine the verse as just 'all this is pervaded by the Lord', without the ethical instruction.

🧠 Think first

Would the verse still TEACH anything?

One discussion path: The genius of the verse is the LINK between metaphysics and ethics in two clauses: 'all is pervaded; therefore do not covet'. Without the second half it is a poetic claim about the universe — beautiful, abstract, but not action-guiding. The second half turns metaphysics into a way of life.

📝 Stretch your thinking

None of these has one 'correct' answer — they are for arguing about. Try one on a friend or a parent and see if their discussion path differs from yours.
