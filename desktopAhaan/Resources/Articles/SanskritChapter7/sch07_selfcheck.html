Question 1 — The warm-up

'ईशावास्यम् इदं सर्वम्' is the famous opening line of —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: The Īśopaniṣad


• The line opens the Īśopaniṣad — one of the principal Upaniṣads.
• ईशावास्यम् = 'pervaded by the Lord' (ईश्वरेण व्याप्तम्).

Question 2 — The check

What does 'ईशावास्यम्' mean?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: pervaded by the Lord


• Glossary: ईशावास्यम् = ईश्वरेण व्याप्तम् ('pervaded by God').
• From ईश ('Lord') + आवास्यम् ('to be inhabited').

Question 3 — The stretch

'सर्वशक्तिमान्' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: all-powerful


• Glossary: सर्वशक्तिमान् = सर्वसामर्थ्यवान् ('possessed of all might').
• सर्व + शक्ति + -मान् = 'possessing all power'.

Question 4 — The trap

Which of these is NOT a class of semi-divine beings mentioned in the chapter?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: दानवाः


• The chapter names यक्षाः, गन्धर्वाः, किन्नराः together.
• दानवाः (demons) is a real Sanskrit class but not in this chapter.

Question 5 — The finisher

'आराधनम्' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: worship


• Glossary: आराधनम् = पूजनम् ('worship').
• From the root राध् ('to please') with prefix आ.
• Part of the chapter's vocabulary of devotion.
