Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

The government and the state are the same thing.

✅ Actually…

The government is only one part of the state. Governments change after elections, but the state (its people, territory and sovereignty) remains - like teachers changing while the school stays.

❌ Many kids think…

In a democracy, the majority can do whatever it wants.

✅ Actually…

The Constitution guarantees everyone's fundamental rights, so the majority cannot trample a minority's rights. The rule of law is stronger than the wishes of the majority.

❌ Many kids think…

All democracies are republics.

✅ Actually…

Not so - the UK, Canada and Sweden are democracies but not republics, because they have monarchs. A republic has an elected (non-hereditary) head of state.
