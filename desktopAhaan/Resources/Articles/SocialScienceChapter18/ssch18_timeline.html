How government and citizens connect

From the foundations of the state to the everyday power of citizens, the building blocks of Indian democracy.

  • The state is formed
    People, territory, government and sovereignty come together to make a state.
  • A government is chosen
    People elect representatives who form a government to make and run the laws.
  • Power is divided into three organs
    The legislature makes laws, the executive implements them, and the judiciary ensures they are followed.
  • Power is shared across three tiers
    Union, state and local governments share responsibility - decentralisation keeps decisions close to the people.
  • Citizens hold government accountable
    Through RTI, grievances, media, civil society and voting, citizens keep the government doing its job.
