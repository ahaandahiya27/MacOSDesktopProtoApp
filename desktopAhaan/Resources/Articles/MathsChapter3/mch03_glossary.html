A Peek Beyond the Point: Key Words

Every term in this chapter, with a one-line meaning and an example.

&#8776; quick reference &middot; 10 terms

Knowing the exact words makes the maths click. Here is every key term from the chapter, defined plainly with an example you can picture.

Decimal

A number written with a decimal point to show parts smaller than one.

Example: 2.7 means 2 ones and 7 tenths.

In Hindi: दशमलव

Decimal point

The dot that separates the whole part of a number from its fractional part.

Example: In 2.7 the dot is the decimal point.

Tenth

One of ten equal parts of a unit; 1/10. Ten tenths make one whole.

Example: 0.7 = 7 tenths = 7/10.

In Hindi: दशांश

Hundredth

One of a hundred equal parts of a unit; 1/100. Ten hundredths make a tenth.

Example: 0.05 = 5 hundredths = 5/100.

In Hindi: शतांश

Thousandth

One of a thousand equal parts of a unit; 1/1000.

Example: 0.008 = 8 thousandths.

Decimal place value

The value of a digit based on its position right of the decimal point.

Example: In 0.45, the 4 is 4 tenths and the 5 is 5 hundredths.

Trailing zero

A zero at the right-hand end of a decimal's fractional part; it does not change the value.

Example: 0.2 = 0.20 = 0.200.

Regrouping (carry/borrow)

Trading 10 of one place for 1 of the next-higher place (carry) or the reverse (borrow).

Example: 13 tenths = 1 whole and 3 tenths.

Number line

A straight line on which every number, including decimals, has an exact position.

Example: 1.4 sits 4 tenths past 1.

In Hindi: संख्या रेखा

Place value ladder

The pattern that each place is 10× the place to its right and 1/10 of the place to its left, continuing past the decimal point.

Example: … 100, 10, 1, 1/10, 1/100 …
