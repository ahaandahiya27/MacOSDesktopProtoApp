Question 1 — The warm-up

What is the meaning of the length 2 7/10 cm?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: It means 2 whole centimetres plus 7 tenths of a centimetre. The unit between consecutive whole numbers is divided into 10 equal parts, and 7 of those parts are taken beyond the 2-cm mark. It is read 'two and seven-tenth centimetres' and written 2.7 cm.

Question 2 — The check

How many one-hundredths make one unit, and how many make one tenth?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: 100 hundredths make one unit (since a unit is 10 tenths and each tenth is 10 hundredths, 10 × 10 = 100). 10 hundredths make one tenth.

Question 3 — The stretch

Why do we split a unit into 10 parts every time to make decimals, rather than 4 or 8 parts?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Because 10 is the base of the Indian place value system: each whole-number place is 10 times the one to its right. Splitting by 10 below the units place keeps the same ×10/÷10 ladder going, so one uniform set of arithmetic rules works for both whole numbers and decimals. We could split into 4 or 8, but then the sub-units wouldn't match the place-value system.

Textbook page 58.

Question 4 — The trap

Sonu says 0.2 = 0.20 = 0.200. Zara thinks the extra zeros change the value. Who is right?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Sonu is right. 0.2, 0.20 and 0.200 all represent 2 tenths — the trailing zeros only add 0 hundredths and 0 thousandths, contributing nothing. However, 0.2, 0.02 and 0.002 are all different, because there the 2 moves into smaller places (tenths, hundredths, thousandths).

Textbook page 70.

Question 5 — The finisher

Which of these are equal: 4.5, 4.05, 0.405, 4.050, 4.50, 4.005, 04.50?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: 4.5 = 4.050 = 4.50 = 04.50 (all four and five tenths). The rest are different: 4.05 (four and 5 hundredths), 0.405 (zero point four-oh-five), and 4.005 (four and 5 thousandths).
