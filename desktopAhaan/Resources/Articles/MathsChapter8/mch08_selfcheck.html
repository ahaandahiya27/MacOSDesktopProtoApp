Question 1 — The warm-up

Aaron's tortoise walks 1/4 km in an hour. How far in 3 hours?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: 3 × 1/4 = 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 = 3/4 km. Multiply the whole number by the numerator and keep the denominator.

Question 2 — The check

Tenzin drinks 1/2 glass of milk every day. How many glasses in a week, and in the month of January (31 days)?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Week: 7 × 1/2 = 7/2 = 3 1/2 glasses. January: 31 × 1/2 = 31/2 = 15 1/2 glasses.

Textbook page 175.

Question 3 — The stretch

Multiply 7 × 3/5 and convert to a mixed number.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: 7 × 3/5 = 21/5. As a mixed number, 21 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 1, so 21/5 = 4 1/5.

Textbook page 176.

Question 4 — The trap

What is 1/2 of 1/3?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: 'Of' means multiply: 1/2 × 1/3 = (1×1)/(2×3) = 1/6.

Textbook page 178.

Question 5 — The finisher

Compute 2/3 × 4/5.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Multiply numerators and denominators: (2×4)/(3×5) = 8/15.

Textbook page 179.
