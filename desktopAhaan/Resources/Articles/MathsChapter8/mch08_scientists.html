Real mathematicians whose work lies underneath this chapter.

Mathematics was built by real people, often centuries apart, each adding one idea. Here are the minds behind the ideas in this chapter.

Ahmes (c. 1650 BCE (the scribe))

Egyptian. Copied the Rhind Papyrus, our richest source of ancient Egyptian fractions.

Ahmes was the Egyptian scribe who, around 1650 BCE, copied the mathematical scroll now called the Rhind Papyrus. It is one of the oldest maths documents we have, and it shows the Egyptian habit of writing almost every fraction as a sum of 'unit fractions' (fractions with 1 on top), such as 3/4 = 1/2 + 1/4.
