Working with Fractions: Worked NCERT Questions

Textbook questions from this chapter, each with a clear worked answer.

&#8776; practice &middot; 8 questions

These are the kind of questions the NCERT chapter asks. Try each one yourself first, then check the worked answer.

Q1. Aaron's tortoise walks 1/4 km in an hour. How far in 3 hours?

Answer: 3 × 1/4 = 1/4 + 1/4 + 1/4 = 3/4 km. Multiply the whole number by the numerator and keep the denominator.

Q2. Tenzin drinks 1/2 glass of milk every day. How many glasses in a week, and in the month of January (31 days)?

Answer: Week: 7 × 1/2 = 7/2 = 3 1/2 glasses. January: 31 × 1/2 = 31/2 = 15 1/2 glasses.

Q3. Multiply 7 × 3/5 and convert to a mixed number.

Answer: 7 × 3/5 = 21/5. As a mixed number, 21 ÷ 5 = 4 remainder 1, so 21/5 = 4 1/5.

Q4. What is 1/2 of 1/3?

Answer: 'Of' means multiply: 1/2 × 1/3 = (1×1)/(2×3) = 1/6.

Q5. Compute 2/3 × 4/5.

Answer: Multiply numerators and denominators: (2×4)/(3×5) = 8/15.

Q6. What is 1 ÷ 2/3? Restate it as a multiplication.

Answer: 1 ÷ 2/3 asks '2/3 × ? = 1'. Since 2/3 × 3/2 = 1, the answer is 3/2. Dividing by a fraction means multiplying by its reciprocal.

Q7. Manju shares 5 litres of oil equally among 3 families. How much per family in a week, and over 4 weeks?

Answer: Per week: 5 ÷ 3 = 5/3 litres. Over 4 weeks: 4 × 5/3 = 20/3 = 6 2/3 litres.

Q8. How many 1/2-cup servings are there in 3 cups?

Answer: 3 ÷ 1/2 = 3 × 2 = 6 servings. Dividing by a fraction less than 1 gives a larger number.
