Working with Fractions: Key Words

Every term in this chapter, with a one-line meaning and an example.

&#8776; quick reference &middot; 10 terms

Knowing the exact words makes the maths click. Here is every key term from the chapter, defined plainly with an example you can picture.

Fraction

A number written a/b representing a parts out of b equal parts of a whole.

Example: 3/4 is three quarters.

In Hindi: भिन्न

Numerator

The top number of a fraction; how many parts are taken.

Example: In 3/4, the numerator is 3.

In Hindi: अंश

Denominator

The bottom number of a fraction; how many equal parts the whole is divided into.

Example: In 3/4, the denominator is 4.

In Hindi: हर

Of (as multiplication)

In fraction problems, 'of' means multiply.

Example: 1/2 of 1/3 = 1/2 × 1/3 = 1/6.

Improper fraction

A fraction whose numerator is greater than or equal to its denominator.

Example: 21/5 is improper (= 4 1/5).

Mixed number

A whole number together with a proper fraction.

Example: 4 1/5.

In Hindi: मिश्र संख्या

Reciprocal

The fraction obtained by swapping numerator and denominator; a number times its reciprocal is 1.

Example: The reciprocal of 2/3 is 3/2.

In Hindi: व्युत्क्रम

Dividend / Divisor / Quotient

In a ÷ b = c, a is the dividend, b the divisor, c the quotient.

Example: 12 ÷ 4 = 3: dividend 12, divisor 4, quotient 3.

Proper fraction

A fraction less than 1 (numerator smaller than denominator).

Example: 2/3 is a proper fraction.

Product of fractions

The result of multiplying fractions: tops multiply, bottoms multiply.

Example: 2/3 × 4/5 = 8/15.
