Story Mode — Working with Fractions in Real Life

The same ideas from this chapter, but out in the world — in shops, on playgrounds, and in everyday choices.

≈ 6 min · see-it-everywhere level

Maths can feel like it lives only inside the textbook. It doesn't. Here are real situations where the ideas of "Working with Fractions" quietly do the work.

Sharing oil among families

Manju and two neighbours share 5 litres of oil equally among 3 families: each gets 5 ÷ 3 = 5/3 litres a week. Over 4 weeks one family gets 4 × 5/3 = 20/3 = 6 2/3 litres. Real sharing problems are fraction multiplication and division in disguise.

Discount on a discount

A jacket at half price, with a further 'pay 3/4' festive offer, costs 3/4 × 1/2 = 3/8 of the original. Shoppers who think the discounts 'add up' are surprised — fractions of fractions multiply, and the result is a smaller fraction of the original.

How many servings?

A cook with 3 cups of batter making 1/2-cup idlis gets 3 ÷ 1/2 = 6 idlis. Dividing by a small fraction gives a big count — the everyday meaning of 'how many small pieces fit'.
