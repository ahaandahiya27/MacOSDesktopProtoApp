मित्राय नमः · NCERT Q&A

NCERT Q&A — Model Answers

The chapter's textbook exercise questions, with model answers grounded directly in the chapter's own content.

≈ 8 min · revision-ready

These are the kinds of questions the textbook itself asks at the end of the chapter. Each answer below is derived from the chapter's own text — what the lesson actually says, not what a wider Sanskrit reading would supply. Treat this as a study reference for revision before a test.

Question 1

सूर्यनमस्कारः कतीनाम् आसनानां समाहारः अस्ति?

Answer: द्वादशानाम्। (of twelve)

💡 Why

Surya Namaskar is traditionally a समाहारः (combination) of twelve postures (आसनानि) — paired one-to-one with the twelve Sun-name mantras the chapter lists.

Question 2

सूर्यनमस्कारेण जनाः कीदृशं शरीरं प्राप्नुवन्ति?

Answer: स्वस्थम्। (a healthy [body])

💡 Why

Glossary: स्वस्थम् = नीरोगम् ('disease-free'). The chapter says one attains both स्वस्थं शरीरम् (healthy body) and स्वस्थं मनः (healthy mind) through daily practice.

Question 3

सूर्यनमस्कारस्य प्रथमः मन्त्रः कः?

Answer: मित्राय नमः।

💡 Why

The chapter's title IS the first mantra. The dative मित्राय ('to Mitra/the Sun') + नमः ('salutation') = 'salutation to the Sun-as-friend'.

Question 4

'नमः' पदस्य योगे का विभक्तिः प्रयुज्यते?

Answer: चतुर्थी विभक्तिः। (the dative)

💡 Why

नमः idiomatically governs the चतुर्थी विभक्ति — the one saluted goes into the dative ('to ___'). The chapter teaches the dative paradigm of छात्र (छात्राय) so students see WHY मित्राय appears in the mantras.

Question 5

सूर्यस्य कानिचन नामानि लिखत।

Answer: मित्रः, रवि, सूर्यः, भानुः, खगः, पूषा, हिरण्यगर्भः, मरीचिः, सविता, अर्कः, भास्करः, आदित्यः।

💡 Why

The chapter teaches these as synonyms (एकार्थकशब्दाः) for the Sun — twelve in the tradition, paired with the twelve Sun-Salutation postures.
