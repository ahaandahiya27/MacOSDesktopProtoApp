Question 1 — The warm-up

What is the first mantra of सूर्यनमस्कार?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: मित्राय नमः


• The chapter states सूर्यनमस्कारस्य प्रथमः मन्त्रः = मित्राय नमः.
• मित्राय is the dative of मित्र ('the Sun/friend').

Question 2 — The check

In 'मित्राय नमः', why is it मित्राय and not मित्रः?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: नमः takes the dative (चतुर्थी) — 'salutation TO'


• नमः means 'salutation TO' — the one saluted goes into the चतुर्थी विभक्ति (dative).
• मित्र → मित्राय, just like छात्र → छात्राय in the chapter's table.

Question 3 — The stretch

Which of these is NOT used in the chapter as a name of the Sun?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: रिपुः


• भास्करः, सविता, पूषा are all names of the Sun listed in the chapter.
• रिपुः means 'enemy' (from Chapter 2) — not a Sun-name.

Question 4 — The trap

Doing सूर्यनमस्कार daily gives people a ___ body.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: स्वस्थम् (healthy)


• The chapter says one attains a स्वस्थं शरीरम् ('healthy body') and स्वस्थं मनः ('healthy mind').
• स्वस्थम् is glossed नीरोगम् ('disease-free').

Question 5 — The finisher

The 'चतुर्थी विभक्ति' (fourth case) is used to express —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: 'to / for' (the recipient)


• The dative (चतुर्थी) marks the one TO/FOR whom — e.g. छात्राय 'to a student'.
• नमः + dative = 'salutation TO ___'.
