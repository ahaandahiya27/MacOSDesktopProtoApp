The terms below are drawn from the chapter's own concept list. Each carries the canonical meaning the chapter teaches — the same words and meanings you will meet in the chapter's questions and exercises.

Words to Remember

  • सूर्यनमस्कारः — The Sun Salutation. सूर्यनमस्कारः (sūrya-namaskāraḥ) — 'salutation to the Sun', a set of yoga postures.
  • मित्राय नमः — The First Mantra (and the Dative Case). मित्राय नमः (mitrāya namaḥ) — 'salutation to Mitra (the Sun)'; नमः takes the dative case.
  • सूर्यस्य द्वादश नामानि — The Twelve Names of the Sun. The Sun has twelve names: मित्रः, रविः, सूर्यः, भानुः, खगः, पूषा, हिरण्यगर्भः, मरीचिः, आदित्यः, सविता, अर्कः, भास्करः.
  • स्वस्थं शरीरम् — A Healthy Body and Mind
  • चतुर्थी विभक्तिः — The Dative ('to / for') Case. चतुर्थी विभक्तिः — the dative case, the 'to/for' form; अकारान्त nouns add -आय (छात्र → छात्राय).
  • प्रज्ञा — Wisdom / Intelligence. प्रज्ञा (prajñā) — 'wisdom, deep intelligence'.
  • तेजः — Brilliance / Radiance. तेजः (tejaḥ) — 'brilliance, radiant energy, splendour'.
  • वीर्यम् — Vigour / Valour. वीर्यम् (vīryam) — 'vigour, strength, valour'.
