"What If?" — मित्राय नमः

Hypothetical questions with no single right answer. Think hard before you read the discussion.

≈ 7 min read · brain-stretcher level

The textbook teaches what IS. Real understanding comes from asking what IF? Pick a question below, pause and think for a minute, then read one possible discussion.

What if 1 — the Sun had just ONE name in Sanskrit?

The question: Sanskrit gives the Sun twelve names. What if it had just one — say सूर्यः?

🧠 Think first

Could you still have a 12-posture mantra sequence?

One discussion path: You could pair all twelve postures with the same name — repetitive. The variety of names invites variety of attention: each posture reaches toward a different aspect of the Sun. The richness of synonym shapes the practice.

What if 2 — the सूर्यनमस्कार had no mantras attached — just postures?

The question: Modern yoga sometimes drops the mantras. What changes?

🧠 Think first

Is a 'mantra-less Sun Salutation' the same practice?

One discussion path: Without the mantras, the practice becomes physical exercise, not devotion. The mantras are how the body's salute becomes a mind's address. In the original conception they were not optional — they were what MADE the postures a salutation.

What if 3 — you faced any direction other than east at sunrise?

The question: The tradition prescribes facing east (toward the rising sun). Suppose you faced west.

🧠 Think first

Does the direction change the practice's meaning? Its effect?

One discussion path: Facing east physically aligns you with the Sun's light at dawn — practical and symbolic. Facing west would still be exercise, but it would no longer be a salutation TO the Sun arriving; it would be one toward the Sun departing. Geography shapes worship.

📝 Stretch your thinking

None of these has one 'correct' answer — they are for arguing about. Try one on a friend or a parent and see if their discussion path differs from yours.
