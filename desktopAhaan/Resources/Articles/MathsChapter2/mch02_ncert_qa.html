Arithmetic Expressions: Worked NCERT Questions

Textbook questions from this chapter, each with a clear worked answer.

These are the kind of questions the NCERT chapter asks. Try each one yourself first, then check the worked answer.

Q1. Fill in the blank to make both sides equal: 13 + 4 = ____ + 6.

Answer: 11. Since 13 + 4 = 17 and the right side is ? + 6, we need ? = 17 − 6 = 11.

Q2. Arrange in ascending order of value: 67 − 19, 67 − 20, 35 + 25, 5 × 11, 120 ÷ 3.

Answer: Values: 48, 47, 60, 55, 40. Ascending: 120÷3 (40) < 67−20 (47) < 67−19 (48) < 5×11 (55) < 35+25 (60).

Q3. Find the value of 28 − 7 + 8 by writing the terms.

Answer: Terms: 28, −7, 8. Sum = 28 − 7 + 8 = 29.

Q4. A metro ticket is ₹40 for an adult and ₹20 for a child. Write and evaluate the cost for four adults and three children.

Answer: Expression: 4 × 40 + 3 × 20. Terms: 4×40 = 160 and 3×20 = 60. Total = 160 + 60 = ₹220.

Q5. Queen Alia gave 100 gold coins each to Elsa and Anna. Elsa doubled hers; Anna has half left. Write an expression for their combined coins and evaluate.

Answer: Expression: 2 × 100 + 100 ÷ 2 = 200 + 50 = 250 gold coins together.

Q6. Is 1023 + 125 greater or less than 1022 + 128?

Answer: Less. The left first number is 1 more, but its second number is 3 less, so the left side is 2 smaller: 1023 + 125 < 1022 + 128.

Q7. Remove the brackets: 100 − (15 + 56).

Answer: 100 − 15 − 56 = 29. The minus before the bracket flips both inner signs.

Q8. Write the arrangement described by both 2 × (5 + 3) and 5 × 2 + 3 × 2, and give the value.

Answer: Both describe 2 rows of (5 + 3) squares = 16 squares. 2 × (5+3) = 16 and 5×2 + 3×2 = 10 + 6 = 16 — equal by the distributive property.
