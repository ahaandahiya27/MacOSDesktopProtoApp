Question 1 — The warm-up

Fill in the blank to make both sides equal: 13 + 4 = ____ + 6.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: 11. Since 13 + 4 = 17 and the right side is ? + 6, we need ? = 17 − 6 = 11.

Question 2 — The check

Arrange in ascending order of value: 67 − 19, 67 − 20, 35 + 25, 5 × 11, 120 ÷ 3.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Values: 48, 47, 60, 55, 40. Ascending: 120÷3 (40) < 67−20 (47) < 67−19 (48) < 5×11 (55) < 35+25 (60).

Question 3 — The stretch

Find the value of 28 − 7 + 8 by writing the terms.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Terms: 28, −7, 8. Sum = 28 − 7 + 8 = 29.

Question 4 — The trap

A metro ticket is ₹40 for an adult and ₹20 for a child. Write and evaluate the cost for four adults and three children.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Expression: 4 × 40 + 3 × 20. Terms: 4×40 = 160 and 3×20 = 60. Total = 160 + 60 = ₹220.

Question 5 — The finisher

Queen Alia gave 100 gold coins each to Elsa and Anna. Elsa doubled hers; Anna has half left. Write an expression for their combined coins and evaluate.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Expression: 2 × 100 + 100 ÷ 2 = 200 + 50 = 250 gold coins together.
