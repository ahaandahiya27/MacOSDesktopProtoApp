🛠️ Mini Project — Conjugate One New Verb You've Never Met

A hands-on activity that turns the chapter from something you read into something you do.

≈ 20–30 min total · do-it-yourself level

The chapter showed conjugations for four roots. This project makes you conjugate a FIFTH — one you haven't seen yet — using the pattern.

What you'll need

  • A blank sheet
  • The chapter's पठ् conjugation table for reference
  • 10 minutes

Steps

  1. Pick any 1st-class (भ्वादिगण) parasmaipada root from any dictionary or hint: गम् (go), वद् (speak), जय् (conquer), नी (lead), क्षम् (forgive). All work.
  2. Draw a 3x3 grid: rows = persons (3rd, 2nd, 1st); columns = numbers (sg, dual, pl).
  3. Apply the पठ् endings to your root's stem: -ति/-तः/-न्ति, -सि/-थः/-थ, -मि/-वः/-मः.
  4. Pronounce all 9 forms aloud.
  5. Optionally: write 'I read', 'he reads', 'they read' in English next to the forms.

🔍 What you should notice

Nine new Sanskrit verb-forms — generated by you from one rule.

Why it works

The chapter showed grammar IS a generator: learn one pattern, write hundreds of words. This exercise makes the generative power real.
