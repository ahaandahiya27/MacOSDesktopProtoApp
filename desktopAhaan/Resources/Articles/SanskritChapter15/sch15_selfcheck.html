Question 1 — The warm-up

'धातुः' in Sanskrit grammar means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: a verb root


• धातु is the 'root' of a verb — its irreducible core.
• Pāṇini lists about 2,000 roots in his धातुपाठ.

Question 2 — The check

Which लकार stands for the PRESENT tense?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: लट्


• लट्-लकार = वर्तमानकालः (present tense).
• लङ् is past, लोट् is imperative, विधिलिङ् is optative.

Question 3 — The stretch

Which set of endings does the root 'पठ्' (read) take?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: परस्मैपदम्


• पठ् → पठति (-ति ending) = परस्मैपद (active, 'for another').
• भाष् → भाषते (-ते ending) would be आत्मनेपद.

Question 4 — The trap

In Sanskrit grammar, 'प्रथमपुरुष' (first person) refers to —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: 'he/she/it' (third party)


• Sanskrit orders persons OPPOSITELY from English: प्रथम = he/she.
• 'I' is उत्तमपुरुष (last in the Sanskrit table).

Question 5 — The finisher

The 3rd-person singular present of the root 'पठ्' (to read) is —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: पठति


• पठ् + ति (3rd-singular parasmaipada present) = पठति.
• Means 'he/she reads'.
