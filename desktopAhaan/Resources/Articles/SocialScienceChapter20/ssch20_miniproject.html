Reading teaches; doing sticks. Try this hands-on activity at home — it turns the chapter's idea into something you can see, build, or trace for yourself.

🪙 See compounding grow your money (about 35 min)

What you need

  • A notebook
  • A calculator

Steps

  1. Start with Rs 100 and an interest rate of 10% per year.
  2. Year 1: add 10% (Rs 10) -> Rs 110. Year 2: add 10% of Rs 110 (Rs 11) -> Rs 121. Continue for 10 years.
  3. Now do the same with SIMPLE interest (always 10% of the original Rs 100 = Rs 10 each year) for 10 years.
  4. Compare the two totals and write what you notice.

👀 What you should see

Compound interest grows faster than simple interest, and the gap widens every year - just like the doubling rice on the chessboard.

🧠 Why it works

Working the numbers yourself makes the abstract 'magic of compounding' real and convincing.

📱 A family finance and safety survey (about 40 min)

What you need

  • A notebook
  • Family or neighbours to ask

Steps

  1. Ask family members how they save money (bank, post office, cash, etc.).
  2. Ask whether they use UPI, ATM or cheques, and which they prefer and why.
  3. Ask if they or anyone they know has faced a digital fraud (like a fake call asking for bank details) and what they did.
  4. Summarise your findings in a table and write one safety rule everyone agreed on.

👀 What you should see

You will learn how real people save and pay, and gather practical wisdom about avoiding fraud.

🧠 Why it works

It connects the chapter to your family's real financial life and reinforces safe digital habits.
