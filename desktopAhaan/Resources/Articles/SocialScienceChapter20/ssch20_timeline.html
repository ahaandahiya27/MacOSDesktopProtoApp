How money moves: from ancient temples to UPI

The evolution of banking and payments in India, from the past to the digital present.

  • Ancient: temples as lenders
    In ancient India, temples acted somewhat like banks, lending money to artisans and traders.
  • 1875: Bombay Stock Exchange
    Asia's oldest stock exchange is founded, where people buy and sell shares in companies.
  • 1935: RBI established
    The Reserve Bank of India is set up; it becomes the 'banker to banks' in 1949.
  • 2014: Jan Dhan Yojana
    A national drive opens bank accounts for crores of Indians, with no minimum balance.
  • 2010s-2020s: UPI revolution
    Instant phone payments transform India and begin spreading to other countries.
