Common Mistakes

Financial infrastructure - banks, payment systems, stock markets and other institutions - moves and manages money, complementing physical infrastructure. Banks

≈ 10 min · read-mode · 🌏 Social Science

Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

A bank just keeps your money safe in a locker.

✅ Actually…

A bank keeps deposits safe AND lends most of them out as loans to other people and businesses, keeping only some as reserves. That is how it connects savers and borrowers and earns income.

❌ Many kids think…

The RBI is just another bank where people open accounts.

✅ Actually…

The RBI is the central bank - the 'banker to banks'. Ordinary people do not bank there; instead, the banks themselves keep accounts at the RBI, which also prints currency and regulates the system.

❌ Many kids think…

It's fine to share your OTP if the caller says they are from your bank.

✅ Actually…

Never share your OTP, PIN or password with anyone - a genuine bank never asks for them. Anyone who does is trying to commit fraud; report it on helpline 1930.

❌ Many kids think…

Money in a savings account just sits idle in the bank.

✅ Actually…

The bank lends most of your deposit out to others as loans. It pays you some interest and charges borrowers more, keeping the difference. So your savings keep working in the economy — funding homes, businesses and farms — which is exactly how banks earn money.

❌ Many kids think…

Buying shares is just like a savings account — the money is sure to grow.

✅ Actually…

A savings deposit is safe and earns fixed interest. Shares can rise OR fall with the company and the market. Over time they may give higher returns, but they carry real risk — shares are an investment, not a guaranteed saving.
