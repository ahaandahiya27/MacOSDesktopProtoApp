Question 1

स्वरवर्णानां कति वर्णमात्राः सन्ति?

Answer: तिस्रः। (three)

💡 Why

Three: ह्रस्व (अल्पकालिकः, 1 mātrā), दीर्घ (लम्बकालिकः, 2 mātrās), and प्लुत (अतिविलम्बकालिकः, 3 mātrās).

Question 2

'अल्पकालिकः' वर्णस्य अपरं नाम किम्?

Answer: ह्रस्वः।

💡 Why

अल्पकालिकः ('short-time') is the descriptive name; ह्रस्वः ('short') is the classical name. Examples: अ, इ, उ, ऋ, ऌ — each 1 mātrā long.

Question 3

'अतिविलम्बकालिकः' वर्णस्य कति मात्राः?

Answer: तिस्रः। (three)

💡 Why

प्लुत vowels (the third class) are drawn out for three mātrās — three times the duration of a short vowel. Used in chanting and dramatic calls (e.g. 'भो-3 राम!').

Question 4

'कुक्कुटः' इति शब्दः कस्य पक्षिणः नाम?

Answer: कृकवाकोः। (the rooster / cock)

💡 Why

Glossary: कुक्कुटः = कृकवाकुः. The rooster — and an onomatopoeic example used by Pāṇini himself to illustrate vowel-quantity (the rooster's crow naturally spans short, long, prolonged sounds).

Question 5

'चटका' इत्यस्य अर्थः कः?

Answer: गृहनीडः — गौरेया। (the sparrow)

💡 Why

Glossary: चटका = गृहनीडः. The little sparrow. One of several Sanskrit bird-names the chapter introduces — alongside वायसः (crow), शिखी (peacock), कपोतः (pigeon).
