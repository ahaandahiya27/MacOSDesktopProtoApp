Words to Remember

  • वर्णमात्रा — Vowel Duration (Mātrā). वर्णमात्रा (varṇa-mātrā) — the 'time-measure' of a vowel; how long it is pronounced.
  • ह्रस्व, दीर्घ, प्लुत — The Three Names. ह्रस्व (short), दीर्घ (long), प्लुत (prolonged) — Sanskrit's three vowel-quantity classes.
  • कुक्कुटः रौति — The Rooster's Crow (a Phonics Example). कुक्कुटः रौति — 'the rooster crows'; the chapter's classic mātrā illustration.
  • भेदः and उपभेदः — Types and Sub-Types. भेदः (a type/division) + उपभेदः (a sub-type) — useful classification terms.
  • कुक्कुटः, वायसः, शिखी — Sanskrit Bird Names. कुक्कुटः (rooster), वायसः (crow), शिखी (peacock), चटका (sparrow), कपोतः (pigeon), पुंस्कोकिलः (male cuckoo).
  • नकुलः, मूषकः, चाषः — Other Animals. नकुलः (mongoose), मूषकः (mouse), चाषः (Indian Roller bird).
  • रौति / रुवन्ति — Singular and Plural Verbs. रौति (he/it cries) vs रुवन्ति (they cry) — singular vs plural verb forms.
  • ध्यानं निदधतु — 'Pay Attention!' (Imperative). ध्यानं निदधतु — 'Please pay attention!' (polite imperative).
