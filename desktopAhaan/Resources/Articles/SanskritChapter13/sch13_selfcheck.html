Question 1 — The warm-up

How many भेदाः (types) of वर्णमात्रा (vowel duration) does the chapter teach?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: three


• The chapter teaches THREE: अल्पकालिकः (short), लम्बकालिकः (long), अतिविलम्बकालिकः (prolonged).
• Classical names: ह्रस्व, दीर्घ, प्लुत.

Question 2 — The check

Which is the SHORT (अल्पकालिक) vowel quantity, in classical name?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: ह्रस्व


• ह्रस्व = short (1 mātrā), e.g. अ, इ, उ.
• दीर्घ = long, प्लुत = prolonged.

Question 3 — The stretch

What is the chapter's classic illustrative example of varying vowel duration?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: the rooster crowing (कुक्कुटः रौति)


• The chapter uses कुक्कुटः रौति ('the rooster crows') as the illustration.
• Pāṇini's own definition of mātrā uses the rooster's crow.

Question 4 — The trap

'भेदः' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: a kind / division


• Glossary: भेदः = प्रकारः ('kind/type').
• From भिद् ('to split') — 'a division'.

Question 5 — The finisher

Which Sanskrit name means 'peacock'?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: शिखी


• Glossary: शिखी = मयूरः पक्षी (peacock — literally 'the crested one').
• वायसः = crow; कपोतः = pigeon; नकुलः = mongoose.
