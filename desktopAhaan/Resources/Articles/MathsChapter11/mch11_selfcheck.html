Question 1 — The warm-up

Sameeksha tiles a 12 ft by 16 ft room with the largest equal square tiles. What size tile, and why?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: The tile side must divide both 12 and 16 (a common factor); the largest such, the HCF, gives the fewest tiles. Common factors of 12 and 16 are 1, 2, 4; the HCF is 4, so 4 ft tiles.

Question 2 — The check

What is the HCF (GCD) of two numbers?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: It is the greatest number that divides both of them exactly — the highest of their common factors.

Question 3 — The stretch

Lekhana packs 84 kg and 108 kg of rice (separately) into equal largest bags. Find the bag size and number of bags.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Bag size = HCF(84,108) = 12 kg. Bags = 84/12 + 108/12 = 7 + 9 = 16 bags.

Question 4 — The trap

What is the LCM of two numbers? Find LCM(6,8).

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: The LCM is the smallest number that is a multiple of both. Multiples of 6 (6,12,18,24…) and of 8 (8,16,24…) first meet at 24, so LCM(6,8) = 24.

Textbook page 42.

Question 5 — The finisher

Find the HCF and LCM of 12 and 18 by prime factorisation.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: 12 = 2²·3, 18 = 2·3². HCF = common primes to the lowest power = 2·3 = 6. LCM = all primes to the highest power = 2²·3² = 36.
