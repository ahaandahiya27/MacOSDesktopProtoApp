The 10 terms to know

  • Electric current · विद्युत धारा — The flow of electric charge (electrons) through a conductor.
    Example: When you switch on a fan, current flows through its wires and motor.
  • Circuit · परिपथ — A closed path that allows electric current to flow.
    Example: A torch with cell + bulb + switch makes a simple circuit.
  • Voltage / Potential difference · विभवांतर — The push that drives current through a circuit, measured in volts.
    Example: An AA cell is 1.5 V; an Indian home socket is 230 V; a high-voltage line is 132 kV.
  • Resistance · प्रतिरोध — How much a material resists the flow of current; measured in ohms (Ω).
    Example: A tungsten bulb filament has high resistance — that's why it heats up and glows.
  • Fuse · फ्यूज़ — A thin wire that melts when current exceeds a safe limit, breaking the circuit.
    Example: A 5 A fuse protects a circuit by melting if current exceeds 5 amperes.
  • MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker) · एमसीबी — A modern auto-resetting safety device that trips when current exceeds limit.
    Example: Modern Indian homes have an MCB panel near the meter — instead of fuses.
  • Electromagnet · विद्युत चुम्बक — A magnet created by passing current through a coil of wire wound around an iron core.
    Example: Scrap yard cranes lift tonnes of iron with electromagnets.
  • Conductor · चालक — A material that allows current to flow easily — usually metals.
    Example: Copper, aluminium, silver, iron — all are good conductors.
  • Insulator · विद्युतरोधी — A material that resists current flow.
    Example: Rubber, plastic, wood, glass, dry air — all are insulators.
  • Battery · बैटरी — Two or more cells connected together to provide higher voltage.
    Example: A car battery is six 2-volt cells in series, giving 12 V.
