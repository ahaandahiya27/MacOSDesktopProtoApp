🛠️ Mini Project — Write a Letter from Panna Dhai to Future Udai Singh

A hands-on activity that turns the chapter from something you read into something you do.

≈ 20–30 min total · do-it-yourself level

The chapter told one of India's most poignant sacrifice stories. This project makes you imagine the inner life of the heroine.

What you'll need

  • One sheet of paper
  • A pen
  • 20 minutes
  • Imagination + respect

Steps

  1. Imagine Panna Dhai writing a letter to be opened by Udai Singh when he is old enough.
  2. What would she tell him about that night?
  3. What would she NOT tell him? Why?
  4. Would she ask anything of him? Would she ask nothing?
  5. Write the letter in your own words. Sanskrit greeting (वत्स!) optional.

🔍 What you should notice

Your letter will be your interpretation of one of history's hardest moments. Keep it for your future self.

Why it works

Sanskrit class often stays at the level of grammar. This exercise brings the HEART of a story into your hand. Reading history with feeling is a separate skill from reading it for facts.
