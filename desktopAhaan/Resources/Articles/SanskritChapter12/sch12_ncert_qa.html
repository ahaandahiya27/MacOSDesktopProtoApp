NCERT Q&A — Model Answers

The chapter's textbook exercise questions, with model answers grounded directly in the chapter's own content.

These are the kinds of questions the textbook itself asks at the end of the chapter. Each answer below is derived from the chapter's own text — what the lesson actually says, not what a wider Sanskrit reading would supply. Treat this as a study reference for revision before a test.

Question 1

पाठे का वीराङ्गना वर्णिता?

Answer: पन्नाधाया।

💡 Why

The chapter's heroine — पन्ना धाया, the loyal Rajput wet-nurse (धाया, glossed धात्री) of Mewar's prince Udai Singh II in the 16th century.

Question 2

पन्ना कस्य प्रकोष्ठे स्वपुत्रं शायितवती?

Answer: राजपुत्रस्य शयनागारे। (in the prince's bedroom)

💡 Why

When the assassin came मारयितुम् (to kill) the young prince, Panna placed her OWN son in the prince's शयनागारम् (bedroom) in his place — knowing her son would be killed.

Question 3

एषा घटना कस्मिन् शतके अभूत्?

Answer: षोडशे शतके। (in the 16th century)

💡 Why

The chapter specifies षोडशे शतके — the 1500s, the time of Mewar's Maharana lineage. The exact year is around the 1530s.

Question 4

पन्ना कीदृशं त्यागं कृतवती?

Answer: सर्वस्वत्यागम् — स्व-पुत्रस्य प्राणत्यागम्। (sacrifice of one's everything — the life of her own son)

💡 Why

Glossary: सर्वस्वम् = स्वीयं सम्पूर्णम् ('one's complete own'). To save the future king, she gave up the most precious thing she had — her own child's life.

Question 5

कस्याः नाम स्वर्णिमाक्षरैः लिखितम् भारतस्य इतिहासे?

Answer: पन्नाधायायाः / वीराङ्गनानां — विशेषतः पन्नाधायायाः।

💡 Why

The chapter places Panna's name in स्वर्णिमाक्षरैः (golden letters) among India's brave women (वीराङ्गनासु) — a Sanskrit idiom for being permanently honoured by history.
