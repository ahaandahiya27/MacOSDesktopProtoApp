Question 1 — The warm-up

Who was पन्नाधाया?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: the loyal wet-nurse of the Mewar prince


• The chapter names her as धाया (wet-nurse) of Mewar's prince Udai Singh II.
• धाया = wet-nurse (धात्री); she was not the queen herself.

Question 2 — The check

In which century does the chapter place Panna Dhai's story?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: the 16th


• The chapter explicitly says षोडशे शतके ('in the 16th century').
• षोडशम् is the ordinal '16th'.

Question 3 — The stretch

What did Panna Dhai sacrifice to save the prince?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: her own son


• The chapter says she put her OWN son in the prince's bed in his place — सर्वस्वत्याग, ultimate sacrifice.
• Knowing the assassin would kill 'the boy in the bed', she chose to give up her own child to save the future king.

Question 4 — The trap

'स्वर्णिमाक्षरैः लिखितम्' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: written in golden letters (i.e. permanently honoured)


• Glossary: स्वर्णिमाक्षरैः = सुवर्णाक्षरैः.
• Idiom: names of the truly great are 'written in golden letters' in history.

Question 5 — The finisher

'धाया' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: a wet-nurse / foster-mother


• Glossary: धाया = धात्री ('foster-mother').
• From धा ('to nourish') — a woman who nourishes a child not her own.
