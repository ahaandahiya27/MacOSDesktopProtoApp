Words to Remember

  • पन्नाधाया — Panna Dhai, the Brave Wet-Nurse. पन्नाधाया — Panna Dhai, the loyal Rajput धाया (wet-nurse) honoured as a वीराङ्गना (brave woman).
  • सर्वस्वत्यागः — Sacrificing One's Everything. सर्वस्वम् (one's all) + त्यागः (sacrifice) — Panna Dhai gave her own son to save the prince.
  • षोडशे शतके — In the Sixteenth Century
  • स्वर्णिमाक्षरैः — Names Written in Golden Letters. स्वर्णिमाक्षरैः लिखितम् — '(her name is) written in golden letters' (in the history of India's brave women).
  • धाया — Wet-Nurse / Foster-Mother. धाया (dhāyā) — 'a wet-nurse, foster-mother'; the role title at the heart of Panna's story.
  • छलेन + कुतन्त्रम् — By Deception, an Evil Plot. छलेन (by deception) + कुतन्त्रम् (evil plot) — the villain's tools in the Panna Dhai story.
  • मारयितुम् — To Kill (Infinitive). मारयितुम् (mārayitum) — 'to kill'; the infinitive of the causative of मृ.
  • शयनागारम् — Bedroom
