NCERT Q&A — Model Answers

The chapter's textbook exercise questions, with model answers grounded directly in the chapter's own content.

≈ 8 min · revision-ready

These are the kinds of questions the textbook itself asks at the end of the chapter. Each answer below is derived from the chapter's own text — what the lesson actually says, not what a wider Sanskrit reading would supply. Treat this as a study reference for revision before a test.

Question 1

कति बालकाः स्नातुं गताः?

Answer: दश। (ten)

💡 Why

The chapter's opening line: 'दश बालकाः नदीं तीर्त्वा पारं गताः' — ten boys crossed the river to bathe. The whole parable depends on the number being TEN.

Question 2

कतमः बालकः नद्यां मग्नः इति निश्चयम् अकुर्वन्?

Answer: दशमः। (the tenth)

💡 Why

Each boy counted only नव ('nine') and concluded the dasama (tenth) had drowned. But each boy was forgetting to count HIMSELF — each had been the tenth.

Question 3

दशमः कः आसीत्?

Answer: नायकः / स्वयं गणकः। (the leader himself — the counter who kept forgetting to count himself)

💡 Why

The traveller's revelation 'दशमः त्वम् असि' ('you are the tenth') points at the counter himself. The Self that was being searched for was the Self that was searching.

Question 4

कः सम्यक्‌रूपेण बालकान्‌ गणितवान्?

Answer: पथिकः। (the wayfarer / traveller)

💡 Why

Only the OUTSIDE viewer — the पथिकः (मार्गगन्ता) — counted properly. Each boy was blinded by trusting his own count without checking it from outside.

Question 5

बालकाः कां तीर्त्वा पारं गताः?

Answer: नदीम्। (a river)

💡 Why

The chapter specifies a नदी (river), crossed by तरण (swimming). The गерund तीर्त्वा ('having crossed') marks the action that sets the parable in motion.
