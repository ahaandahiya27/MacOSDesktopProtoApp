🛠️ Mini Project — Find Your Own 'Tenth' Moment This Month

A hands-on activity that turns the chapter from something you read into something you do.

≈ 20–30 min total · do-it-yourself level

The chapter's parable taught that we search for what we already have. This project asks you to notice it happening to you.

What you'll need

  • A small notebook or note-app
  • One month of awareness
  • Willingness to ask for help

Steps

  1. For one month, watch for moments where you were searching for something you already had — keys in your pocket, a skill you already used, a friend who was always there.
  2. Each time you catch yourself, write a one-line note. Just the situation.
  3. Optionally: tell a friend about one of these moments. Sometimes the 'traveller' role of the parable is just talking to someone.

🔍 What you should notice

By month's end you will have 5–10 'tenth' moments documented. The parable becomes a lens; you start using it on yourself.

Why it works

Vedānta's claim was that the deepest 'lost' thing — the Self — is the one always at hand. This exercise practices that pattern at smaller scales until it becomes habitual.
