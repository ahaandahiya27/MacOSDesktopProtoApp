Words to Remember

  • दशमः कः? — Who Is the Tenth?
  • पथिकः — The Traveler / Teacher. पथिकः (pathikaḥ) — 'a traveler, wayfarer'; in the parable, he plays the teacher's role.
  • मग्नः — Submerged / Drowned. मग्नः (magnaḥ) — 'submerged, drowned, immersed'.
  • विषण्णाः → प्रहृष्टाः — From Sad to Delighted. विषण्णाः (saddened) → प्रहृष्टाः (delighted) — the parable's emotional arc.
  • प्रथमः, द्वितीयः, तृतीयः … दशमः — Ordinal Numbers
  • तीर्त्वा — Having Swum (Across). तीर्त्वा (tīrtvā) — 'having swum/crossed' (a gerund).
  • अगणयत् — He Counted (Imperfect Past). अगणयत् (agaṇayat) — 'he counted'; the imperfect-past tense.
  • कतमः vs कतरः — 'Which?' (of many vs of two). कतमः (katamaḥ) — 'which one (of many)'; distinct from कतरः ('which of two').
