Question 1 — The warm-up

How many boys went to bathe in the river?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: ten (दश)


• The exercise: कति बालकाः स्नातुं गताः? Answer: दश.
• The whole parable depends on the number being TEN.

Question 2 — The check

Who counted the boys correctly?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: a passing traveler (पथिकः)


• The exercise: कः सम्यक्‌रूपेण बालकान्‌ गणितवान्‌? Answer: पथिकः.
• Glossary: पथिकः = मार्गगन्ता ('traveler').
• Each boy missed himself; only the OUTSIDE viewer (पथिकः) counted right.

Question 3 — The stretch

Who turned out to BE the tenth?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: the counter himself (नायकः)


• The traveler revealed: दशमः त्वम् असि — 'you are the tenth'.
• Each counter had forgotten to count HIMSELF.
• Exercise: दशमः कः आसीत्‌? Answer: नायकः.

Question 4 — The trap

What body of water did the boys cross?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: a river (नदी)


• The chapter says they तीर्त्वा (having swum) पारं गताः (crossed) the नदीम् (the river).
• Exercise: बालकाः कां तीर्त्वा पारं गताः? Answer: नदीम्.

Question 5 — The finisher

The Sanskrit ordinal for 'tenth' is —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: दशमः


• दश (cardinal 'ten') → दशमः (ordinal 'tenth', with -म suffix).
• Masculine; feminine दशमा, neuter दशमम्.
