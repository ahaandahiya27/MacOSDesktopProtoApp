Finding the Unknown · Mini Project

⚖️ Mini Project — Build a Balance, Solve an Equation

A hands-on activity that turns this chapter into something you actually do, not just read.

≈ 40 min · do-it-yourself level

Reading about maths and doing maths are two different things. This short project lets you see the ideas of "Finding the Unknown" at work with your own hands.

What you'll need

  • A ruler balanced on a pencil (a simple see-saw), or a drawing of a balance scale
  • Small identical coins/weights
  • A mystery item (e.g. a sealed envelope with hidden coins)

Steps

  1. Set up the balance with a mystery item plus some known coins on one side, and known coins on the other, so it balances.
  2. Write the equation: mystery + (known) = (known), e.g. m + 3 = 8.
  3. Remove the SAME number of coins from both sides and watch it stay balanced — until the mystery item is alone.
  4. Read off the mystery weight (the solution), e.g. m = 5.
  5. Now write a 'two-step' equation like 2m + 1 = 11, and solve it systematically (subtract 1 from both sides, then divide by 2), checking by substitution.

🔍 What you should notice

Removing equal amounts from both sides keeps the balance level, and peeling them away leaves the mystery weight alone — exactly mirroring how subtracting and dividing both sides of an equation isolates the unknown.

Why it works

An equation is a balanced scale: both sides are equal. Any operation done equally to both sides preserves that equality, so inverse operations (subtract to undo add, divide to undo multiply) strip away everything around the unknown without ever tipping the balance — leaving the solution.
