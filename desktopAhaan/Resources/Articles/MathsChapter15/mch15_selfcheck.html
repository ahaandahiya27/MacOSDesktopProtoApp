Question 1 — The warm-up

A balanced scale has a sack of weight w plus 5 kg on the left and 12 kg on the right. Write and solve the equation.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: w + 5 = 12. Remove 5 kg from both pans (subtract 5 from both sides): w = 7 kg.

Question 2 — The check

Solve 2n + 1 = 99 by trial and error, then check.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Testing values: n=40 gives 81, n=50 gives 101, n=49 gives 99. So n = 49. Trial and error works but is slow.

Question 3 — The stretch

If LHS = RHS, will the equation still hold after adding the same number to both sides?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Yes. Since both sides have the same value, performing the same operation (adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing by the same number) on both keeps them equal.

Question 4 — The trap

Solve 2n + 1 = 99 systematically.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Subtract 1 from both sides: 2n = 98. Divide both sides by 2: n = 49. Check: 2×49+1 = 99.

Textbook page 169.

Question 5 — The finisher

Solve 5x − 4 = 7.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: Add 4 to both sides: 5x = 11. Divide by 5: x = 11/5 = 2.2. Check: 5×2.2 − 4 = 7.
