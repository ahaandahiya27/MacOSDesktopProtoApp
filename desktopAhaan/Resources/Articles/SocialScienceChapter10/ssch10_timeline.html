Making the Constitution of India, 1946–1950

The key milestones in how India's Constitution was written and brought into force.

  • Constituent Assembly formed
    On 9 December 1946, the Constituent Assembly is formed to write India's Constitution, initially with 389 members.
  • Partition reduces members
    After the Partition of India in 1947, the Assembly's membership is reduced to 299, of which 15 are women.
  • Drafting Committee at work
    A Drafting Committee, chaired by Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, prepares the initial text while Dr. Rajendra Prasad chairs the full Assembly.
  • Constitution adopted
    After almost three years of debate, the Constitution of India is adopted on 26 November 1949 (now Constitution Day).
  • Constitution comes into effect
    On 26 January 1950 the Constitution comes into effect, making India a republic — celebrated every year as Republic Day.
