Connecting the Dots: Key Words

Every term in this chapter, with a one-line meaning and an example.

&#8776; quick reference &middot; 10 terms

Knowing the exact words makes the maths click. Here is every key term from the chapter, defined plainly with an example you can picture.

Data

A collection of numbers or facts gathered for study.

Example: Runs scored across matches.

In Hindi: आँकड़े

Mean (average)

The total of all values divided by how many there are.

Example: (0+17+21+90)/4 = 32.

In Hindi: माध्य

Median

The middle value of sorted data (average of the two middle if the count is even).

Example: Median of 0,17,21,90 is 19.

In Hindi: माध्यिका

Mode

The value that occurs most often.

Example: Mode of 5,3,5,7,5 is 5.

In Hindi: बहुलक

Representative value

A single number summarising a data set (mean, median, or mode).

Example: Mean run-rate represents a batter's series.

Range

The difference between the largest and smallest values; a measure of spread.

Example: Range of 0..90 is 90.

In Hindi: परिसर

Bar graph

A chart showing data as bars whose heights represent the values.

Example: Runs per player as bars.

In Hindi: दंड आलेख

Scale (of a graph)

How much each unit of length on the axis represents.

Example: 1 cm = 10 runs.

Outlier

A value far from most others, which can distort the mean.

Example: A score of 90 among 0,17,21.

Statistical statement

A claim about likely patterns in data, carrying uncertainty.

Example: 'She will likely score a century.'
