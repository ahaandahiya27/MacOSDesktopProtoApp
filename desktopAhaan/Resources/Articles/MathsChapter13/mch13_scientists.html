Real mathematicians whose work lies underneath this chapter.

Mathematics was built by real people, often centuries apart, each adding one idea. Here are the minds behind the ideas in this chapter.

Prasanta Chandra Mahalanobis (1893 – 1972)

Indian. Founder of the Indian Statistical Institute and pioneer of large-scale statistical surveys in India.

P. C. Mahalanobis was the architect of modern statistics in India. He founded the Indian Statistical Institute in Kolkata in 1931 and developed large-scale sample-survey methods that shaped India's economic planning after independence. The 'Mahalanobis distance', still used worldwide in data science and machine learning, bears his name. He showed that careful sampling and representative values could describe an entire nation's agriculture, industry and population from a manageable amount of data — exactly the statistical thinking this chapter introduces. India's National Statistics Day falls on his birthday, 29 June.
