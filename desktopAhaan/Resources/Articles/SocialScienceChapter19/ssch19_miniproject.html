🛣️ Map the infrastructure of a journey (about 40 min)

What you need

  • Paper and pens
  • Knowledge of a real trip you have taken

Steps

  1. Pick a journey you have made (home to a relative's town, or a school trip).
  2. List every piece of infrastructure you used: roads, bridges, metro, train, airport, phone/internet.
  3. Mark which were physical and which were social.
  4. Then imagine one piece was missing and write how the journey would change.

👀 What you should see

You will see how many connected pieces of infrastructure even a simple journey needs - and how missing one (like a bridge) can break the whole trip.

🧠 Why it works

Tracing a real journey makes the 'pieces of a puzzle' idea concrete and personal.

♻️ Make your school greener and more inclusive (about 40 min)

What you need

  • A notebook
  • A walk around your school

Steps

  1. Walk around your school and note its infrastructure (water, electricity, ramps, footpaths, dustbins).
  2. List two ways to make it more sustainable (e.g. solar panels, less litter, rainwater harvesting).
  3. List two ways to make it more inclusive (e.g. ramps for wheelchairs, clear signs, accessible toilets).
  4. Present your ideas as a 'Community Responsibility Pact' poster.

👀 What you should see

You will discover practical ways infrastructure can be both eco-friendly and welcoming to everyone, including children, the elderly and the disabled.

🧠 Why it works

It turns the chapter's ideas of sustainability and inclusion into real changes you can champion.
