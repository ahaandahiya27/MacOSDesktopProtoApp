Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

Infrastructure means only roads and buildings.

✅ Actually…

Infrastructure also includes social infrastructure (schools, hospitals, courts, parks) and invisible systems like power grids, water pipelines and communication networks.

❌ Many kids think…

The British built railways in India to help Indians.

✅ Actually…

They built them in 1853 mainly for their own benefit - to export India's raw materials, sell British goods, and move troops to control India. The railways only later became India's lifeline.

❌ Many kids think…

Only the government is responsible for public infrastructure.

✅ Actually…

Caring for public infrastructure is a collective responsibility - citizens must use it well, not damage it, and report problems, while the government builds and maintains it.
