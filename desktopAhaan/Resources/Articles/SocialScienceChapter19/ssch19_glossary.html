The 11 terms to know

  • Infrastructure · अवसंरचना — The basic facilities and systems a country needs to function, of two types: physical and social.
    Example: Roads, schools and power grids are all infrastructure.
  • Physical infrastructure · भौतिक अवसंरचना — Transport, utilities, communication and energy systems that carry goods, people, power and information.
    Example: Highways, railways and telecom towers are physical infrastructure.
  • Social infrastructure · सामाजिक अवसंरचना — Facilities like schools, hospitals, courts and parks that support people's wellbeing and development.
    Example: A hospital is part of social infrastructure.
  • Expressway · द्रुतगामी मार्ग — A high-speed national road that joins cities across states, built by the central government.
    Example: The Delhi-Meerut Expressway speeds up travel.
  • Golden Quadrilateral · स्वर्णिम चतुर्भुज — A highway network connecting India's four major cities - Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai and Kolkata.
    Example: The Golden Quadrilateral links the four metros by road.
  • Metro · मेट्रो — A fast urban rail system (often underground or elevated) that helps city commuters avoid road traffic.
    Example: Rishabh took the metro to skip Delhi's traffic.
  • TEU · टीईयू — Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit - a measure of container-ship and port capacity (how many standard containers).
    Example: A port's trade is measured in TEUs.
  • Coherer · कोहिरर — An early device, improved by J.C. Bose, that decoded transmitted wireless signals.
    Example: Bose invented a new type of coherer for wireless systems.
  • Patent · पेटेंट — A right granted by the government to be the only one who can make, sell or use an invention for a number of years.
    Example: J.C. Bose rarely took out patents for his inventions.
  • Sustainable infrastructure · संधारणीय अवसंरचना — Infrastructure built with clean energy and eco-friendly materials to minimise pollution and harm to biodiversity.
    Example: Using solar panels on buildings makes infrastructure more sustainable.
  • Living root bridge · जीवित जड़ पुल — A natural bridge grown by guiding tree roots across a stream, made by the Khasi and Jaintia tribes of Meghalaya.
    Example: Living root bridges can last for hundreds of years.
