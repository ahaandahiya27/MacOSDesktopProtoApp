How India's infrastructure grew

From ancient road rules to a digital, electrified nation - milestones in India's infrastructure.

  • Ancient: the Arthashastra
    Kautilya's Arthashastra sets out road widths and penalties for damaging public works - over 2,000 years ago.
  • 1853: First railways
    The British introduce railways in India, mainly for colonial trade and control.
  • 1895: J.C. Bose's wireless
    J.C. Bose demonstrates wireless transmission through a wall in Calcutta, pioneering radio technology.
  • 1990s-2000s: Telecom boom
    Mobile phones spread; costs fall from Rs 17/minute toward some of the world's cheapest rates.
  • 2025: A connected, electrifying India
    ~150,000 km of national highways, 159 airports, ~1.16 billion wireless subscribers, and trains going fully electric.
