Aerobic vs Anaerobic Respiration

Topic t02 of Chapter 10 · Respiration in Organisms · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Most cells respire aerobically — using oxygen to fully release the energy in glucose (yielding CO₂ + water + lots of ATP). Some cells switch to anaerobic respiration when oxygen runs low: yeast ferments sugar into alcohol + CO₂ (the basis of bread and brewing), and sprinting muscles briefly make lactic acid (the basis of cramps).

With Oxygen vs Without. Anaerobic respiration releases far less energy per glucose molecule — that's why you can sprint anaerobically only briefly before you have to slow down and breathe deeply to repay the oxygen debt.

Looking ahead
