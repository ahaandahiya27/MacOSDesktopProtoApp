Words to Remember

  • शृगालः — The Fox. शृगालः (śṛgālaḥ) — 'a fox / jackal', the main character of the story.
  • द्राक्षालता — The Grape-Vine. द्राक्षालता (drākṣā-latā) — 'a grape-vine / grape creeper'.
  • उत्पतति — He Jumps Up. उत्पतति (utpatati) — 'jumps up, leaps upward'.
  • आम्लम् — 'Sour!' (the fable's moral). आम्लम् (āmlam) — 'sour'; the fox declares the grapes sour because he can't reach them.
  • तसिल् दिशाशब्दाः — Direction Words (-तः). वामतः, दक्षिणतः, अग्रतः, पृष्ठतः — 'on the left, on the right, in front, behind' (the -तः suffix).
  • पिपासा / तृषा — Thirst
  • श्रान्तः — Tired
