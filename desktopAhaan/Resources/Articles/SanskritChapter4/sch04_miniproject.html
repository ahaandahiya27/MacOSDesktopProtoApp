🛠️ Mini Project — Catch One 'Sour Grapes' Moment Each Day for a Week

A hands-on activity that turns the chapter from something you read into something you do.

≈ 20–30 min total · do-it-yourself level

The chapter's fable named a universal habit. This project makes you a quiet observer of it — in yourself and others.

What you'll need

  • A small notebook or phone-notes app
  • Curiosity (no judgement)
  • One week of paying attention

Steps

  1. For seven days, watch for moments when someone (including you) belittles something they could not get.
  2. Write a one-line note when it happens. Just the situation, no name-calling.
  3. After three days, look at your notes. Notice patterns.
  4. Optionally: try the OPPOSITE response once — admit something is actually wonderful that you didn't get. Notice how it feels.

🔍 What you should notice

By week's end you will have 5–10 notes. The pattern of 'sour grapes' will become visible like a watermark on paper — once you see it, you can't unsee it.

Why it works

The fable was told to make you notice the move. Once noticed, the move is much harder to do unconsciously. That is the whole point of the parable.
