NCERT Q&A — Model Answers

The chapter's textbook exercise questions, with model answers grounded directly in the chapter's own content.

These are the kinds of questions the textbook itself asks at the end of the chapter. Each answer below is derived from the chapter's own text — what the lesson actually says, not what a wider Sanskrit reading would supply. Treat this as a study reference for revision before a test.

Question 1

कस्य कथा अस्मिन् पाठे वर्ण्यते?

Answer: शृगालस्य। (of the fox / jackal)

💡 Why

The chapter's protagonist (शृगालः, glossed लोमशा) is the wandering fox. He is the central character; the grape-vine and the moral are around him.

Question 2

शृगालः कीदृशः सन् वने भ्रमति?

Answer: श्रान्तः, खिन्नः, पिपासायुक्तश्च। (tired, sad, and thirsty)

💡 Why

The chapter's opening describes the fox as श्रान्तः (क्लान्तः, weary), खिन्नः (दुःखितः, sad), and full of पिपासा / तृषा (thirst, glossed पातुम् इच्छा).

Question 3

शृगालः कति वारं उत्पतति?

Answer: पुनः पुनः। (again and again)

💡 Why

The fox jumps एकवारम्, द्विवारम्, then पुनः पुनः ('once, twice, again and again'), unable to reach the grapes.

Question 4

अप्राप्तं द्राक्षाफलं शृगालः कीदृशम् इति वदति?

Answer: आम्लम्। (sour)

💡 Why

The chapter's title states the fable's punch-line: 'न लभ्यते चेत् आम्लं द्राक्षाफलम्' — if not obtained, the grape-fruit is (declared) sour. आम्लम् is the fox's verdict.

Question 5

'वामतः' इति शब्दस्य अर्थः कः? कीदृशः प्रत्ययः प्रयुक्तः?

Answer: वामतः = 'on the left'. The तसिल् suffix (-तः) is used.

💡 Why

The chapter teaches four direction adverbs in this family: वामतः (left), दक्षिणतः (right), अग्रतः (in front), पृष्ठतः (behind). All formed by adding -तः to the base.
