Five Wrong Answers Class 7 Students Give

The five places almost every Class 7 student trips up on Chapter 7 — and the one-line fix for each.

≈ 5 min read · revision-tier

Examiners say the same wrong answers come back year after year. None of these mistakes are silly — they are almost right. That is exactly what makes them dangerous. Read each one, cover the answer, and try to spot what is off before peeking.

1. “Weather and climate mean the same thing.”

🚫 The mistake

A common belief that's almost right — but misses the key detail.

The fix: Weather is what's happening now — today's rain, this hour's temperature, this morning's wind. Climate is the LONG-term pattern over 30+ years. Today's weather in Delhi might be unusually cool, but Delhi's climate is hot-and-dry. Climate is what you EXPECT; weather is what you GET. Same place, very different concepts.

2. “Animals near the equator are bigger because there's more food.”

🚫 The mistake

A common belief that's almost right — but misses the key detail.

The fix: Opposite is generally true: cold-climate animals tend to be LARGER (Bergmann's rule). Bigger bodies have lower surface-area-to-volume ratio, so they lose heat more slowly. Polar bears are the largest land carnivores; tropical leopards are smaller than tigers. India's smallest deer (mouse deer) lives in tropical Western Ghats; the larger sambar lives in cooler hills.

3. “Migration is just travelling for fun.”

🚫 The mistake

A common belief that's almost right — but misses the key detail.

The fix: Migration is a high-cost survival strategy. Birds lose 25-50% of body weight during migration. Many die — exhausted, starved, attacked. The reward is access to two seasonal feeding/breeding grounds. Bharatpur's Siberian cranes have BOTH a Siberian summer (long days, abundant insects to feed chicks) AND an Indian winter (open wetlands when Siberia freezes). Without migration, they'd lose half the year.

4. “Climate change just means hotter summers.”

🚫 The mistake

A common belief that's almost right — but misses the key detail.

The fix: Climate change disrupts ALL weather patterns. India's monsoon is becoming more erratic — heavy rains in fewer days, longer dry spells in between. Winters are warmer in some places, colder snaps elsewhere. Cyclone frequency on the east coast has increased. Glaciers in Himalayas are retreating, affecting river flows. 'Just hotter' is the surface story; instability is the deeper one.

5. “Coral reefs are plants underwater.”

🚫 The mistake

A common belief that's almost right — but misses the key detail.

The fix: Coral is a colony of tiny animals (polyps) that build calcium carbonate skeletons. The colourful colour comes from microscopic algae (zooxanthellae) living symbiotically inside the polyps. When water warms, the algae are expelled, and the coral turns white — bleaching. Coral is animal + algae living together, an entire underwater civilisation built by symbiosis.

📝 Stretch your thinking

Pick any two of these and explain them to a younger sibling using a one-line analogy each. If you can compress a mistake into one sentence with one image, you have understood it.
