Parallel and Intersecting Lines: Worked NCERT Questions

Textbook questions from this chapter, each with a clear worked answer.

&#8776; practice &middot; 8 questions

These are the kind of questions the NCERT chapter asks. Try each one yourself first, then check the worked answer.

Q1. Two lines intersect and ∠a = 120°. Find ∠b, ∠c and ∠d.

Answer: ∠c (vertically opposite a) = 120°. ∠b and ∠d each form a linear pair with a: 180 − 120 = 60°. So ∠b = ∠d = 60°, ∠c = 120°.

Q2. If two intersecting lines form four equal angles, what is each angle?

Answer: Each is 90°. The four angles sum to 360°, and being equal, each is 360 ÷ 4 = 90°. The lines are perpendicular.

Q3. How would you describe the opposite edges and adjacent edges of a square sheet of paper?

Answer: Opposite edges are parallel (never meet); adjacent edges are perpendicular (meet at 90° at the corners).

Q4. When a transversal crosses two lines, how many angles form, and how many distinct measures are possible?

Answer: 8 angles form (4 at each crossing). Because vertically opposite angles are equal, there are at most 4 distinct measures — and exactly 2 when the two lines are parallel.

Q5. In Fig. 5.14, ∠1 and ∠5 are corresponding angles. If the lines are parallel and ∠1 = 70°, what is ∠5?

Answer: ∠5 = 70°. Corresponding angles are equal when the two lines cut by the transversal are parallel.

Q6. In Fig. 5.25, if ∠f = 120°, find its alternate angle ∠d, justifying without measuring.

Answer: ∠f = ∠b = 120° (corresponding angles, parallel lines). ∠b = ∠d = 120° (vertically opposite). Therefore ∠f = ∠d = 120°. Alternate angles are equal.

Q7. Why are alternate angles equal when lines are parallel? Give the two-step reason.

Answer: An alternate angle equals a corresponding angle (equal because the lines are parallel), which equals its vertically opposite angle (always equal). Chaining the two equalities shows the alternate angles are equal.

Q8. A transversal cuts two parallel lines. One angle is 65°. Give a corresponding, an alternate, and a co-interior angle to it.

Answer: Corresponding = 65° (equal), alternate = 65° (equal), co-interior = 115° (supplementary, 180 − 65).
