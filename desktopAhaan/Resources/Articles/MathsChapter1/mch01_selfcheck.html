Question 1 — The warm-up

Write the following numbers in Indian place value notation: (a) One crore one lakh one thousand ten (b) One billion one million one thousand one

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before you read on — no peeking.

Answer: (a) 1,01,01,010 (b) 1,00,10,01,001

Question 2 — The check

🧠 Answer in your head first

Answer: 30 thousand < 3 lakhs (since 30,000 < 3,00,000).

Question 3 — The stretch

Answer: 500 lakhs > 5 million. (500 lakhs = 5 crore = 50 million.)
