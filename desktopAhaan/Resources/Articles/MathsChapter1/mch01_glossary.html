Large Numbers Around Us: Key Words

Every term in this chapter, with a one-line meaning and an example.

&#8776; quick reference &middot; 12 terms

Knowing the exact words makes the maths click. Here is every key term from the chapter, defined plainly with an example you can picture.

Lakh

The Indian-system unit equal to 1,00,000 (one hundred thousand, or 10^5).

Example: ‘One lakh varieties of rice once existed in India.’

In Hindi: लाख

Crore

The Indian-system unit equal to 1,00,00,000 (ten million, or 10^7).

Example: ‘Mumbai’s population is over 1.2 crore.’

In Hindi: करोड़

Arab

The Indian-system unit equal to 1,00,00,00,000 (10^9), also known as one hundred crore or one billion.

Example: ‘India’s GDP is around 200 lakh crore (20 arab? 2,000 arab?).’

In Hindi: अरब

Million

International-system unit equal to 1,000,000 (10^6), also known as ten lakh.

Example: ‘A small city of about 5 million people.’

Billion

International (short-scale) unit equal to 1,000,000,000 (10^9), equal to one arab or 100 crore.

Example: ‘Earth has a population of about 8 billion.’

Place value

The value a digit takes from its position in a number — ones, tens, hundreds, thousands, and so on.

Example: In 5,072 the digit 5 has place value 5,000.

In Hindi: स्थानीय मान

Decomposition

Writing a number as a sum of place-value pieces (or other parts).

Example: 5,072 = 5 × 1000 + 7 × 10 + 2 × 1.

Approximation

A value close enough to the true number for the purpose at hand, but not exact.

Example: Population about 75,000 (true value 76,068).

In Hindi: सन्निकट

Rounding

Replacing a number with a nearby simpler value, usually a multiple of 10, 100, 1000, etc.

Example: 76,068 rounded to nearest thousand is 76,000.

Nearest neighbour

The closest round number at a given precision (nearest 10, 100, etc.).

Example: Nearest lakh to 6,72,85,183 is 6,73,00,000.

Estimation

Making a reasoned guess at the size of a number, often by rounding inputs first.

Example: Estimate 4,63,128 + 4,19,682 ≈ 4,60,000 + 4,20,000 = 8,80,000.

Order of magnitude

A rough size description in powers of ten — e.g. ‘in the thousands’ or ‘in the lakhs’.

Example: India’s population is in the order of 10^9 (a few hundred crore).
