Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

A market is always a physical place you can walk into.

✅ Actually…

A market is any arrangement where buying and selling happen. It can be a physical haat or mall, an online app or website, or even the stock market — what makes it a market is the exchange, not the building.

❌ Many kids think…

The lower the price, the better — a seller should always sell as cheap as possible.

✅ Actually…

A price that is too low means the seller makes no profit, or even a loss, and may stop selling. A 'just right' price is high enough for the seller to profit and low enough for the buyer to be willing to pay.

❌ Many kids think…

Middlemen like wholesalers and retailers are useless and just make things expensive.

✅ Actually…

Each middleman adds real value: wholesalers buy in bulk and store goods, distributors reach far-off shops, and retailers split bulk into small quantities sold near your home. They make goods cheaper to move and more convenient to buy, which is why the chain exists.
