Common Mistakes

Farming, or agriculture, is one of humankind's oldest occupations and the backbone of the Indian economy. This chapter follows the long story of Indian farming

≈ 6 min · read-mode · 🌏 Social Science

Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

A farmer is almost always a man.

✅ Actually…

In rural India more than 75% of the people working in agriculture are women (2025). They do most of the farm operations — sowing, harvesting and threshing — so the typical farmer is more likely to be a woman than a man.

❌ Many kids think…

The Green Revolution was only a good thing because it gave us more food.

✅ Actually…

It did make India self-sufficient in food, but over time it also impoverished the soil, depleted groundwater, polluted soil and water with chemicals, and harmed human and animal health. Its record is mixed, which is why scientists now seek more sustainable methods.

❌ Many kids think…

Plants can only grow in soil.

✅ Actually…

Hydroponics lets plants grow in nutrient-rich water with no soil at all — minerals are given directly to the roots. It is used in controlled environments and is an option for urban farming and places with poor soil.
