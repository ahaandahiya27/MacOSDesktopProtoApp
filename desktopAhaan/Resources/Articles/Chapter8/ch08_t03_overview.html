Cyclones, Storms and Safety

Topic t03 of Chapter 8 · Winds, Storms and Cyclones · Class 7 Science

The big idea

A cyclone is a giant low-pressure storm where winds spiral inward and rise. The eye in the centre is strangely calm; the wall of clouds around it carries the fastest, most destructive winds. India's Meteorological Department warns coastal towns in four staged warnings — pre-cyclone watch (72 hrs) through post-landfall.

From Calm Eye to Devastating Wall. Thunderstorm safety follows the same physics: lightning takes the easiest path to the ground, so trees, metal poles and plumbing are dangerous. Shelter in a pucca building, crouch low in the open, avoid metal contacts.

Looking ahead

Class 9 Geography studies cyclones by region (Bay of Bengal vs Arabian Sea). Class 11 Physics gives the Coriolis force that decides their rotation direction. JEE rarely asks cyclone questions directly, but the underlying fluid-dynamics ideas show up in rotating-frame mechanics.
