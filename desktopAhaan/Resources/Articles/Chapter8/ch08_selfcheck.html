Question 1 — from Chapter 8

We have to fill air in a football to inflate it. Explain why.

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: An empty football has only atmospheric pressure inside it, equal to the pressure outside. To make the football firm and bouncy, we need to push more air molecules inside, raising the internal pressure ABOVE atmospheric pressure. The compressed air inside pushes outward against the football's leather skin, making it taut.

Why: Empty football: pressure inside = pressure outside = 1 atmosphere. The leather is loose.

Question 4 — from Chapter 8

The 'eye' of a cyclone is:

🧠 Answer in your head first

Don't peek. Speak the answer out loud, then check.

Answer: A calm region at the centre

Why: At the very centre of a cyclone, air sinks rather than rises.
