Sea Breeze and Land Breeze

Topic t02 of Chapter 8 · Winds, Storms and Cyclones · Class 7 Science

The big idea

By day, land heats faster than the sea, so air over the land rises and a cool sea breeze flows in from the water. By night, the land cools faster and the wind reverses into a land breeze. The same uneven-heating principle drives both.

Local Coastal Winds. These daily wind cycles are why beach towns are pleasant in the afternoon — and why fishing fleets in Kerala, Goa and Gujarat plan their departures and returns around them.

Looking ahead

Class 9 Geography expands this into the monsoon (a continental-scale land-sea breeze). Class 11 Physics introduces convection currents formally. JEE Physics applies them in heat-transfer problems; NEET uses the analogy for blood-pressure-driven flow.
