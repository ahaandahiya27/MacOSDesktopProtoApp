Why Wind Exists

Topic t01 of Chapter 8 · Winds, Storms and Cyclones · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Wind exists because the Sun heats different parts of the Earth by different amounts. Hot air rises and leaves behind low pressure; cooler, denser, higher-pressure air rushes in to take its place. That moving air is wind.

Uneven Heating & Pressure. Air has weight, and pressure differences are what drive everything from a gentle breeze to a destructive cyclone. The bigger the temperature gap between two regions, the stronger the wind that connects them.

Looking ahead

Class 9 Geography studies the global wind belts (trade winds, westerlies). Class 11 Physics formalises pressure as P = F/A and air density relations. JEE Physics applies these in Bernoulli's principle and fluid statics; NEET asks the basic gas laws (Boyle, Charles).
