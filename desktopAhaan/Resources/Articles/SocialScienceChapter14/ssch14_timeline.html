Buddhism travels from India to her neighbours

How an Indian religion spread peacefully across Asia over a thousand years, carried by monks, traders and pilgrims.

  • 3rd century BCE: Sri Lanka
    Emperor Ashoka's children Mahendra and Sanghamitra carry Buddhism south to Sri Lanka; Theravada takes root.
  • 1st century CE: China
    Buddhism reaches China via trade and pilgrimage; monks like Faxian and Xuanzang later travel to India to study.
  • 8th–9th century CE: Indonesia
    The mandala-shaped Borobudur Stupa, the world's largest Buddhist monument, is built in Java.
  • 8th century CE: Bhutan
    Guru Padmasambhava introduces the Vajrayana school to Bhutan, shaping its religious identity.

Modern milestones in India's neighbourhood

Key dates that shaped India's relations with her neighbours in modern times.

  • 1947: Partition
    Pakistan is created from part of India; Afghanistan loses its direct land link to India.
  • 1950: India–Nepal Treaty
    The Treaty of Peace and Friendship establishes the open border and free movement of people and goods.
  • 1971: Bangladesh is born
    After a war between India and Pakistan, Bangladesh becomes an independent nation.
  • 1985: SAARC formed
    Eight South Asian nations create SAARC to cooperate in science, education and health.
  • 2019: Kartarpur Corridor
    A visa-free pilgrimage corridor opens for Guru Nanak's 550th birth anniversary.
