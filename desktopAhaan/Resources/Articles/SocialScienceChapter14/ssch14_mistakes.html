Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

A neighbour must share a land border with us.

✅ Actually…

A neighbour can also be a maritime neighbour, linked by a shared sea. Sri Lanka and the Maldives have no land border with India but are among her closest neighbours.

❌ Many kids think…

India once ruled Thailand and Indonesia because they have so much Indian culture.

✅ Actually…

India did not conquer them. Indian culture spread peacefully through trade, pilgrimage and shared religion, which is why Thai kings are named Rama and Indonesia has the Borobudur stupa without ever being an Indian colony.

❌ Many kids think…

The 'open border' between India and Nepal means there is no border at all.

✅ Actually…

The border exists; it is simply 'open', meaning people cross without a visa or passport. Both countries still cooperate to keep it safe and prevent misuse.
