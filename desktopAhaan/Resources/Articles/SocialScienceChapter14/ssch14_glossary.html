The 11 terms to know

  • Maritime neighbour · समुद्री पड़ोसी — A country connected to another by a shared sea or ocean, even without a land border.
    Example: Sri Lanka and the Maldives are India's maritime neighbours.
  • Open border · खुली सीमा — A border that people can cross without a visa or passport.
    Example: The India–Nepal border is open, so citizens cross freely.
  • Regionalism · क्षेत्रवाद — Neighbouring countries working together for peace, stability and shared progress.
    Example: SAARC is an expression of regionalism in South Asia.
  • Archipelago · द्वीपसमूह — An extensive group or chain of islands.
    Example: Indonesia is an archipelago of over 17,000 islands.
  • Landlocked · स्थलरुद्ध — A country with no coastline, surrounded entirely by land.
    Example: Afghanistan, Nepal and Bhutan are landlocked.
  • SAARC · दक्षेस — The South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation, formed in 1985 to promote cooperation among eight South Asian nations.
    Example: India, Nepal and Sri Lanka are SAARC members.
  • Ingot · धातु पिंड — A lump or block of metal shaped for convenient transport and reworking.
    Example: Harappan traders brought copper ingots from Oman.
  • Gross National Happiness · सकल राष्ट्रीय प्रसन्नता — Bhutan's holistic measure of progress, covering wellbeing, sustainability, governance and culture rather than money alone.
    Example: Bhutan uses GNH instead of relying only on GDP.
  • Stupa · स्तूप — A dome-shaped Buddhist shrine that keeps sacred relics of the Buddha, monks or revered figures.
    Example: The Borobudur Stupa in Indonesia is the world's largest.
  • Uttarapatha · उत्तरापथ — The ancient northern trade route linking the Ganga plains to Central Asia via Afghanistan.
    Example: Goods and Buddhism travelled along the Uttarapatha.
  • Transboundary river · सीमापार नदी — A river that flows across the border of two or more countries.
    Example: The Ganga and Brahmaputra are transboundary rivers shared by India and Bangladesh.
