Question 1 — The warm-up

Which set of islands does this chapter describe as 'द्वीपेषु रम्यः'?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: The Andaman & Nicobar Islands


• The chapter's title literally names it: 'द्वीपेषु रम्यः द्वीपोऽण्डमानः'.
• The Andamans are India's eastern island territory.

Question 2 — The check

'कालापानी' was another name for —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: the Cellular Jail in the Andamans


• The chapter says: अस्य कारागारस्य अपरं नाम 'कालापानी' इत्यपि अस्ति.
• 'Black waters' = the dread of being shipped to permanent island exile.

Question 3 — The stretch

According to the chapter, Veer Savarkar bore how many years of imprisonment in the Cellular Jail?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: ten (दश)


• The chapter explicitly states: दश वर्षाणि यावत् कल्पनातीतं घोरं कष्टं सोढवान्.
• Ten years (दश वर्षाणि) of suffering for the Motherland.

Question 4 — The trap

Which of these is NOT one of the four indigenous tribes the chapter names on the Andamans?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: कन्नडिगाः


• The chapter names: अण्डमानी, ओङ्गी, जारवा, सेण्टिनली.
• कन्नडिगाः would be Kannada-speakers — not an Andaman tribe.

Question 5 — The finisher

'द्वीपः' literally comes from —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: द्वि ('two') + अप् ('water') — 'two-watered'


• द्वीप = द्वि (two) + अप् (water) — 'water on (both) sides'.
• An island IS literally land surrounded by water.
