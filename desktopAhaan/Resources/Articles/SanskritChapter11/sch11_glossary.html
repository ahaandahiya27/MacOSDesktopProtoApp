Words to Remember

  • द्वीपोऽण्डमानः — The Andaman Islands. द्वीपोऽण्डमानः — 'the Andaman island(s)' — the most beautiful of India's islands.
  • सेल्युलर कारागारम् — The Cellular Jail (Kālāpāni). सेल्युलर कारागारम् / कालापानी — the colonial-era prison on Port Blair where India's freedom-fighters were jailed.
  • वीर सावरकर — A Hero of Freedom. स्वातन्त्र्यवीरः सावरकरः — Veer Savarkar, who bore ten years' घोरम् कष्टम् (terrible suffering) in the Cellular Jail.
  • जनजातयः — The Indigenous Tribes. अण्डमानी, ओङ्गी, जारवा, सेण्टिनली — the four indigenous जनजातयः (tribes) named in the chapter.
  • द्वीपः + प्रवालप्रस्तरैः — Island & Coral Reefs. द्वीपः (island) + प्रवालप्रस्तरैः (by coral reefs) — the chapter's geographical vocabulary.
  • कारागारम् + त्रितलात्मकम् — Prison & Three-Storied. कारागारम् (prison) + त्रितलात्मकम् (three-storied) — naming the Cellular Jail's structure.
  • सोढवान् — He Bore (Past Active Participle). सोढवान् (soḍhavān) — '(he) bore, endured' (past active participle).
  • जनजातिः — A Tribal Community
