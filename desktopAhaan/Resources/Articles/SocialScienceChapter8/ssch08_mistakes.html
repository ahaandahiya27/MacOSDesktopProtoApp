Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

Only buildings like temples or mosques can be sacred.

✅ Actually…

In India, sacredness extends far beyond buildings: a journey (pilgrimage), the route it takes, rivers, mountains, forests, trees and even the whole land can be sacred. The land itself becomes a 'sacred geography'.

❌ Many kids think…

Pilgrimage and trade were completely separate things in ancient India.

✅ Actually…

Pilgrimage routes and trade routes often overlapped. Pilgrims needed goods, traders supplied them, and some traders doubled as pilgrims — so the same roads carried both devotion and commerce, helping integrate India.

❌ Many kids think…

Sacred groves are protected by government laws.

✅ Actually…

Sacred groves are protected mainly by belief — communities see them as the abodes of deities, so harming them is disrespect to a god. This belief has shielded their biodiversity and water for centuries, often without any formal law.
