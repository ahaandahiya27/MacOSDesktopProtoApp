Real mathematicians whose work lies underneath this chapter.

Mathematics was built by real people, often centuries apart, each adding one idea. Here are the minds behind the ideas in this chapter.

Euclid (c. 300 BCE)

Greek (Alexandria). Set out the rules for when two figures are exact copies.

Euclid's Elements states the conditions under which two triangles must be congruent — exact copies — using only a few measurements, such as three matching sides. These criteria, proved more than 2,000 years ago, are exactly the SSS, SAS and ASA rules you meet in this chapter.
