A Class 7 Reproduction in Plants Dictionary

The 10 must-know terms from Chapter 12 — in plain English plus the Hindi name, with a one-line meaning a Class 7 student can actually use.

≈ 5 min read · revision-ready

Reading the chapter once isn't enough. Knowing the words is what makes you confident. Glance through this deck every Sunday for four weeks and these terms will be yours.

The 10 terms to know

  • Pollination · परागण — The transfer of pollen from anther to stigma.
    Example: Mango flowers are bee-pollinated; rice flowers are wind-pollinated.
  • Fertilisation · निषेचन — The fusion of male and female gametes to form a zygote.
    Example: After pollen tube reaches the ovule, the male gamete fuses with the egg.
  • Stamen · पुंकेसर — The male part of a flower, with filament and anther; produces pollen.
    Example: A hibiscus flower has 5 stamens visible inside.
  • Pistil / Carpel · स्त्रीकेसर — The female part of a flower — stigma, style, and ovary together.
    Example: A mustard flower's pistil sits in the centre, with stamens around it.
  • Ovule · बीजांड — The structure inside an ovary that contains the female gamete; becomes a seed after fertilisation.
    Example: A pea pod is a fruit; the peas inside are seeds, each from one ovule.
  • Vegetative propagation · कायिक प्रवर्धन — Production of new plants from vegetative parts (root, stem, leaf) without seeds.
    Example: Banana, potato, sugarcane, and rose are all grown this way commercially.
  • Asexual reproduction · अलैंगिक प्रजनन — Reproduction involving only one parent; offspring are clones.
    Example: Amoeba splits in two; yeast buds off; bryophyllum grows new plantlets on leaf edges.
  • Pollen tube · परागनली — A long tube that grows from a pollen grain, through the style, to deliver the male gamete to the ovule.
    Example: A pollen tube can be many times longer than the pollen grain itself.
  • Seed dispersal · बीज प्रकीर्णन — Movement of seeds away from the parent plant by wind, water, animals, or explosion.
    Example: A coconut floats across the Indian Ocean to start a new tree.
  • Hybridisation · संकरण — Cross-pollination of two different varieties to produce offspring with combined traits.
    Example: The Green Revolution wheat varieties were hybrids of Indian and Mexican wheat strains.
