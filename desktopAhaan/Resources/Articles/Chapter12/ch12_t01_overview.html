Flowers and Pollination

Topic t01 of Chapter 12 · Reproduction in Plants · Class 7 Science

The big idea

A flower is the plant's reproductive organ. Sepals (green, protective), petals (colourful, attractive), stamens (male — anther + filament, makes pollen), and pistils (female — stigma + style + ovary, holds eggs). Since plants can't walk, they hire pollinators: wind for grasses, insects for hibiscus, water for water lilies, birds for banana flowers.

How Plants Find Mates Without Walking. Pollination is just DELIVERY — pollen lands on stigma. Fertilisation happens later, when the pollen tube grows down to the egg.
