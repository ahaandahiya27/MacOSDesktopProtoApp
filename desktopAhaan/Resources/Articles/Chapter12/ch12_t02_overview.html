Fertilisation and Seed Formation

Topic t02 of Chapter 12 · Reproduction in Plants · Class 7 Science

The big idea

After pollination, the pollen grain germinates on the stigma and grows a tube down the style. Inside the ovary, the male nucleus fuses with the egg — that's fertilisation. The fertilised ovule then matures into a SEED, and the surrounding ovary swells into a FRUIT.

From Pollen Grain to Fruit. When the seed lands somewhere new, it can germinate into a new plant — and the cycle starts over. Dispersal mechanisms (wind, water, animals, explosion) spread seeds far from the parent.
