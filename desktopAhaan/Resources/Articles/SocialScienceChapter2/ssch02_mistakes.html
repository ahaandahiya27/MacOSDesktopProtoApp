Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

Weather and climate are the same thing.

✅ Actually…

Weather is the state of the atmosphere at a particular time and place — what it is like right now. Climate is the average pattern of weather over a long time. Today's rain is weather; the fact that a place is rainy most years is climate.

❌ Many kids think…

Atmospheric pressure must be higher on a mountain because you are closer to the sky.

✅ Actually…

Pressure is the weight of the air ABOVE you. As you climb, more air is below you and less is above, so pressure is LOWER on a mountain — only about 650 mb at Khardung La compared with 1013 mb at the coast.

❌ Many kids think…

Meteorologists just look at the sky and guess the weather.

✅ Actually…

They use special instruments to measure each element precisely, collect data from many places over time, and apply scientific methods to predict the weather hours, days or even weeks ahead. It is a science called meteorology, not guesswork.
