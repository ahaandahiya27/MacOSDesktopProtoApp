From a guess to a forecast: how a weather prediction is made

The steps that turn the changing air into a weather warning you can act on.

  • The atmosphere changes
    The Sun heats the Earth unevenly, so temperature, pressure, wind, humidity and precipitation are always changing in the troposphere.
  • Instruments measure it
    Thermometers, rain gauges, barometers, wind vanes, anemometers and hygrometers measure each element precisely.
  • Weather stations gather data
    Weather stations and Automated Weather Stations bring the instruments together and record readings at regular intervals.
  • Meteorologists study the data
    Meteorologists collect data over time and apply scientific methods to work out how the weather will behave.
  • A forecast and warning are issued
    The India Meteorological Department issues forecasts and colour-coded warning maps (green, yellow, orange, red).
  • People prepare and stay safe
    Fishermen are warned off the sea, coasts are evacuated before cyclones, and governments mobilise resources for disasters.
