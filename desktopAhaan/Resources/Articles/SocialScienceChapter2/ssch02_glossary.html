The 18 terms to know

  • Weather · मौसम — The state of the Earth's atmosphere at a particular time and place.
    Example: The weather today is sunny, warm and dry.
  • Atmosphere · वायुमंडल — The layer of gases (called 'air') that surrounds the Earth.
    Example: Almost all weather happens in the lowest part of the atmosphere.
  • Troposphere · क्षोभमंडल — The lowest layer of the atmosphere, closest to the ground, where we live and where almost all weather occurs.
    Example: The troposphere extends about 6 to 18 km above the ground.
  • Precipitation · वर्षण — Any form of water — rain, snow, sleet or hail — that falls from the sky.
    Example: Rain and hail are both kinds of precipitation.
  • Atmospheric pressure · वायुमंडलीय दाब — The pressure exerted by the weight of the air above and around us, felt on the Earth's surface.
    Example: Normal pressure at the sea coast is about 1013 millibars.
  • Humidity · आर्द्रता — The amount of water vapour present in the air, usually measured as relative humidity (a percentage).
    Example: Coastal Kochi has higher humidity than inland Jaipur.
  • Water vapour · जलवाष्प — Water in its gaseous form, instead of liquid.
    Example: Humidity measures how much water vapour is in the air.
  • Forecast · पूर्वानुमान — To predict or find out in advance — in this case, the weather.
    Example: The weather forecast predicted heavy rain tomorrow.
  • Meteorology · मौसम विज्ञान — The systematic study of weather and its evolution, which is the basis for weather forecasting.
    Example: Meteorology lets scientists predict the weather days ahead.
  • Depression (low-pressure system) · निम्न दाब क्षेत्र — A region where atmospheric pressure drops dramatically (below about 1000 mb), which can develop into a storm or cyclone.
    Example: A pressure below 1000 mb indicates a depression.
  • Anemometer · वायुवेगमापी — An instrument with rotating cups that measures wind speed (and direction).
    Example: The faster the anemometer's cups spin, the stronger the wind.
  • Thermometer · तापमापी / थर्मामीटर — An instrument that measures temperature; from Greek thérmē ('heat') + métron ('measure').
    Example: A thermometer showed 6.5°C as the day's maximum in Srinagar.
  • Barometer · वायुदाबमापी / बैरोमीटर — An instrument that measures atmospheric pressure; from Greek báros ('weight' of the air) + métron ('measure').
    Example: When the barometer falls quickly, a storm may be on the way.
  • Hygrometer · आर्द्रतामापी / हाइग्रोमीटर — An instrument that measures humidity; from Greek hygrós ('moist') + métron ('measure').
    Example: The hygrometer read 84% relative humidity in Mumbai.
  • Rain gauge · वर्षामापी — An open-mouthed funnel-and-scale instrument that measures precipitation; a 'gauge' is any tool for measuring an amount.
    Example: The rain gauge collected 12 mm of rain overnight.
  • Condensation · संघनन — The change of water vapour (gas) into liquid water droplets as the air cools; from Latin condensare ('to make dense').
    Example: Condensation of vapour high in the sky forms clouds.
  • Dew point · ओसांक — The temperature at which the air becomes saturated (full of vapour) so that water begins to condense into dew.
    Example: When the night air cools to its dew point, dew settles on the grass.
  • Wind vane · वातदिक्सूचक / पवन दिशा सूचक — A free-turning pointer, often arrow-shaped, that shows the direction FROM which the wind is blowing; 'vane' comes from Old English fana ('flag').
    Example: The wind vane swung to point west, so a west wind was blowing.
