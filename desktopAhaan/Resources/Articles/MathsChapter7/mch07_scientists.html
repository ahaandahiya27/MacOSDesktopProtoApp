Real mathematicians whose work lies underneath this chapter.

Mathematics was built by real people, often centuries apart, each adding one idea. Here are the minds behind the ideas in this chapter.

Euclid (c. 300 BCE)

Greek (Alexandria). Proved the foundational truths of triangles in The Elements.

In The Elements, Euclid proved the truths about triangles this chapter explores — including that the three angles of any triangle add to two right angles (180°), and the exact conditions under which two triangles must be identical. His proof that the base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal was the first tricky one for beginners, earning the nickname the 'bridge of asses'.
