These are the kind of questions the NCERT chapter asks. Try each one yourself first, then check the worked answer.

Q1. What are the vertices, sides and angles of triangle ∆ABC?

Answer: Vertices: the corner points A, B, C. Sides: the segments AB, BC, CA joining them. Angles: ∠A, ∠B, ∠C (i.e. ∠CAB, ∠ABC, ∠BCA) at the vertices.

Q2. Does the set of lengths 10, 15, 30 satisfy the triangle inequality?

Answer: No. 10 + 15 = 25, which is less than 30, so the sum of the two shorter sides does not exceed the longest. The inequality fails and no triangle can be formed.

Q3. State the triangle inequality and give one example that satisfies it and one that does not.

Answer: The sum of any two sides of a triangle must be greater than the third side. 3, 4, 5 satisfies it (3+4>5, etc.). 10, 15, 30 does not (10+15 < 30).

Q4. Describe the construction of ∆ABC given AB = 4 cm, AC = 5 cm, BC = 6 cm.

Answer: Draw base AB = 4 cm. From A, draw an arc of radius 5 cm. From B, draw an arc of radius 6 cm intersecting the first. The intersection point is C (it is 5 cm from A and 6 cm from B). Join AC and BC.

Q5. What is an altitude of a triangle?

Answer: An altitude is the perpendicular segment drawn from a vertex to the opposite side (or its extension). It is the triangle's height relative to that side, and there are three altitudes, one from each vertex.

Q6. Name the three types of triangle classified by sides, and by angles.

Answer: By sides: equilateral (all sides equal), isosceles (two equal), scalene (all different). By angles: acute (all angles < 90°), right (one 90° angle), obtuse (one angle > 90°).

Q7. Why can an acute triangle not be defined as a triangle with one acute angle?

Answer: Because every triangle already has at least two acute angles (at most one angle can be 90° or more). So 'has one acute angle' is true of all triangles. An acute triangle is defined as having ALL three angles acute.

Q8. Two sides of a triangle are 8 cm and 5 cm. What are the possible lengths of the third side?

Answer: The third side must be greater than 8 − 5 = 3 cm and less than 8 + 5 = 13 cm. So 3 cm < third side < 13 cm.
