Minds Behind Another Peek Beyond the Point

Real mathematicians whose work lies underneath this chapter.

≈ 7 min read · for curious 12-year-olds

Mathematics was built by real people, often centuries apart, each adding one idea. Here are the minds behind the ideas in this chapter.

Simon Stevin (1548 – 1620)

Flemish (worked in the Dutch Republic). Argued for a fully decimal system of measures and money.

As well as popularising decimal fractions, Stevin argued for decimal weights, measures and coins — an idea finally realised when France created the metric system in the 1790s. Because every metric unit is ten or a hundred or a thousand of another, converting between them is just sliding the decimal point, exactly the arithmetic of this chapter.

💡 What they gave us

Choosing a base-ten system on purpose makes the maths easy for everyone.
