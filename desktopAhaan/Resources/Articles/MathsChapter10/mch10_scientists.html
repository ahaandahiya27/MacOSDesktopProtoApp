Real mathematicians whose work lies underneath this chapter.

Mathematics was built by real people, often centuries apart, each adding one idea. Here are the minds behind the ideas in this chapter.

Brahmagupta (c. 598 – 668 CE)

Indian. Wrote the first clear rules for calculating with negative numbers and zero.

Brahmagupta, working in 7th-century Rajasthan, gave the first systematic rules for arithmetic with negative numbers and zero. He pictured positives as 'fortunes' and negatives as 'debts', and his rules — a debt subtracted from a fortune, a fortune times a debt, and so on — are exactly the integer rules in this chapter, written down nearly 1,400 years ago.
