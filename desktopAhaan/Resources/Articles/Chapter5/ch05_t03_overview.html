Acids and Bases in Industry and Nature

From factory floors to coral reefs — acids and bases shape our world

≈ 3 min read · for curious 12-year-olds

Acids and bases are not only found in your kitchen or science lab — they drive massive industries, affect your dental health, and are even changing the chemistry of the world’s oceans. In this topic, you will see how the same reactions you learned about in Topics 1 and 2 play out on a grand scale.

What you will explore

  1. Acids in Industry — From Batteries to Fertilisers Sulphuric acid, hydrochloric acid, and nitric acid power factories around the world.
  2. The Chemistry of Tooth Decay How acids produced by bacteria in your mouth attack tooth enamel, and what you can do about it.
  3. Ocean Acidification — Acids in the Sea How carbon dioxide from the air is making the oceans more acidic and threatening marine life.

A staggering number

The world produces over 250 million tonnes of sulphuric acid every year — more than any other manufactured chemical. It is used to make fertilisers, clean metals, refine petroleum, and much more.
