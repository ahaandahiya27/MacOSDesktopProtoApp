Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

Money has always existed; people have always used coins and notes.

✅ Actually…

Barter — directly swapping goods for goods — came first and was the earliest form of exchange. Money was invented later to solve barter's problems, and coins, paper and digital money each appeared at different points in history.

❌ Many kids think…

A note is valuable because the paper itself is precious.

✅ Actually…

A ₹500 note is just printed paper. Its value comes from everyone agreeing to accept it and from the Reserve Bank of India backing it. Modern money rests on trust, not on the worth of the material it is made of.

❌ Many kids think…

Barter just means trading, so any time you buy something with money it's barter.

✅ Actually…

Barter is specifically the exchange of goods or services for OTHER goods or services, with NO money involved. The moment money is used as a medium of exchange, it is no longer barter.
