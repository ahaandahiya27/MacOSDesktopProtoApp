India shelters the world, across the ages

How wave after wave of communities found a home in India, from ancient times to the present.

  • ~175 BCE: The Bene Israel
    An ancient Jewish community is said to reach the Konkan coast after a shipwreck.
  • 4th century CE: Syriac Christians
    Persecuted Syriac Christians travel along trade routes to Kerala's Malabar coast.
  • 7th century CE onward: Arab merchants
    Arab traders settle peacefully on the west coast, forming the Mappila community.
  • 8th-10th centuries: The Parsis
    Zoroastrians fleeing Persia reach Gujarat; the milk-and-sugar legend is born.
  • 1942-46: Polish children
    The Good Maharaja of Nawanagar shelters around a thousand orphaned Polish children.
  • 1959: Tibetan refugees
    The 14th Dalai Lama and many Tibetans take refuge in India after China's annexation of Tibet.
