Every topic has traps that catch careful students. Here are the most common ones for this chapter, paired with what is actually true, so you can sidestep them.

❌ Many kids think…

Islam came to India only through invasions.

✅ Actually…

Islam first came peacefully to India's west coast through Arab merchants from the 7th century, who built India's oldest mosque - long before and separately from the later military invasions.

❌ Many kids think…

All the communities came to India to trade and prosper.

✅ Actually…

Many came fleeing persecution (Jews, Syriac Christians, Parsis, Baha'is), and the Siddis were brought enslaved - not everyone came freely or for trade.

❌ Many kids think…

Refugee communities lost their own identity in India.

✅ Actually…

They kept their traditions - synagogues, fire temples, churches, languages - while also becoming part of Indian society. India allowed identity-preserving integration.
