The terms below are drawn from the chapter's own concept list. Each carries the canonical meaning the chapter teaches — the same words and meanings you will meet in the chapter's questions and exercises.

Words to Remember

  • अन्नाद् भवन्ति भूतानि — From Food, Beings Come To Be. अन्नाद् भवन्ति भूतानि — 'from food, beings come into being'; the chapter's title and the opening line of Gītā 3.14.
  • ब्रह्म — The Omnipresent Source. ब्रह्म (brahma) — 'the all-pervading Cosmic Power; the ultimate source of all'.
  • रसायनशास्त्रम् — The Modern Touch (Chemistry). रसायनशास्त्रम् (rasāyana-śāstram) — 'chemistry'; the chapter explicitly mentions modern chemistry.
  • जानीयात् — '(One) Should Know' (the Optative). जानीयात् (jānīyāt) — 'one should know'; the optative case used for advice/duty.
  • भूतानि — All Living Beings
  • जिज्ञासा — Curiosity (returns!). जिज्ञासा (jijñāsā) — 'the wish to know' (returns from Chapter 1 — same word, same meaning).
  • उत्पत्तिक्रमम् — The Order of Origin. उत्पत्तिक्रमम् (utpatti-kramam) — 'the order/sequence in which things come into being'.
  • ओषधीनाम् — Of Herbs. ओषधीनाम् (oṣadhīnām) — 'of the herbs (medicinal plants)'.
