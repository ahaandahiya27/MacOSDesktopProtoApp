Question 1 — The warm-up

'अन्नाद् भवन्ति भूतानि' is from which great Sanskrit work?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: The Bhagavad Gītā


• This is the opening of Bhagavad Gītā 3.14.
• The full verse builds a chain: food/rain/yajña/action.

Question 2 — The check

According to the chapter's verse, 'भूतानि' (beings) come from —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: food (अन्नात्)


• The verse: अन्नाद् भवन्ति भूतानि — 'from food, beings come to be'.
• अन्नात् is the ablative ('from food').

Question 3 — The stretch

'ब्रह्म' in this chapter refers to —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: the all-pervading Cosmic Power


• Glossary: ब्रह्म = सर्वव्याप्तः परमेश्वरः ('all-pervading Supreme Power').
• Neuter ब्रह्म is the impersonal Absolute, distinct from masculine ब्रह्मा.

Question 4 — The trap

'रसायनशास्त्रम्' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: chemistry


• Glossary: रसायनशास्त्रम् = रसशास्त्रम् ('science of rasas/essences') = chemistry.
• An ancient word reused for the modern subject.

Question 5 — The finisher

'भूतानि' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: all living beings


• Glossary: भूतानि = प्राणिनः ('living ones').
• Past-participle of भू, 'beings/those that have become'.
