Question 1

पर्वतराजः कः?

Answer: हिमालयः। (the Himalaya)

💡 Why

पर्वतराजः literally means 'king of mountains' (पर्वतानां राजा). In Sanskrit poetry this title traditionally refers to the Himalaya, which crowns Bharat 'as a crown' (मुकुटरूपेण), as the chapter's poem describes.

Question 2

समुद्रः कस्याः चरणौ प्रक्षालयति?

Answer: भारतमातुः। (the Motherland's)

💡 Why

The chapter's poem says the ocean (समुद्रः, रत्नाकरः) washes the Motherland's feet — कस्याः answers with the genitive (षष्ठी) of भारतमाता.

Question 3

कृषकबान्धवाः भारतभूमिं कैः सिञ्चन्ति?

Answer: स्वेदबिन्दुभिः। (with drops of sweat)

💡 Why

The chapter honours farmers (कृषकबान्धवाः) for watering the Motherland with their own sweat (instrumental plural — स्वेदबिन्दुभिः, glossed घर्मस्य जलकणैः).

Question 4

'वन्दे मातरम्' इत्यस्य कः अर्थः?

Answer: अहं मातुः वन्दनं करोमि। ('I offer salutation to the Mother.')

💡 Why

वन्दे (1st-person sg, from वन्द् 'salute') + मातरम् (accusative of मातृ) = 'I bow to the Mother'. The chapter glosses the full sense as 'I salute the Mother'.

Question 5

'वन्दे मातरम्' इति गीतं कः लिखितवान्?

Answer: बङ्किमचन्द्रः चट्टोपाध्यायः।

💡 Why

The chapter explicitly names बङ्किमचन्द्रः चट्टोपाध्यायः as the author, and dates the novel आनन्दमठः (in which the song appears) to १८८२।
