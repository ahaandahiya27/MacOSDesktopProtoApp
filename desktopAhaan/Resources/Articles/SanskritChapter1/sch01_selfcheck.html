Question 1 — The warm-up

What does 'वन्दे मातरम्' mean?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: I bow to the Mother


• The chapter explains वन्दे मातरम् as 'अहं मातुः वन्दनं करोमि' — 'I offer salutation to the Mother'.
• वन्दे = 'I praise/greet'; मातरम् = 'Mother' (here, the Motherland).

Question 2 — The check

Who wrote the song 'वन्दे मातरम्'?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: बङ्किमचन्द्रः चट्टोपाध्यायः


• The chapter names the great patriot बङ्किमचन्द्रः चट्टोपाध्यायः as the author.
• He placed the song inside his novel आनन्दमठः.

Question 3 — The stretch

In which year did Bankim Chandra write the novel आनन्दमठः?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: १८८२


• The chapter states '१८८२ तमे वर्षे' — in the year 1882.
• १८८२ in Devanagari numerals is 1882.

Question 4 — The trap

What does 'जिज्ञासा' mean?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: curiosity (wish to know)


• The chapter glossary gives जिज्ञासा = ज्ञातुम् इच्छा, 'the wish to know'.
• It comes from the root ज्ञा, 'to know'.

Question 5 — The finisher

'पर्वतराजः' (the king of mountains) refers to which mountain?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: हिमालयः


• पर्वतराजः = पर्वतानां राजा, 'king of mountains'.
• The poem pictures it as the crown (मुकुट) of Bharat — the Himālaya.
