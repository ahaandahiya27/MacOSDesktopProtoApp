🛠️ Mini Project — Five Days of One Quiet Sevā Per Day

A hands-on activity that turns the chapter from something you read into something you do.

≈ 20–30 min total · do-it-yourself level

The chapter held up service as the highest duty. This project gives you a structured way to practice it for five days — without anyone praising you.

What you'll need

  • Awareness
  • Five days
  • The willingness to NOT mention what you did

Steps

  1. Each day for five days, do one small act of help — quietly, unasked, unannounced.
  2. Examples: rinse a sibling's lunchbox, water a plant that's drooping, carry a heavy bag for a neighbour, help with homework without being asked.
  3. Do NOT tell anyone you did it. Don't mention it at dinner. Don't post about it.
  4. At the end of five days, sit quietly for two minutes. Notice how it feels.

🔍 What you should notice

By day five, you will have done five quiet acts of सेवा. The point is the QUIETNESS — सेवा without applause is what the chapter held up. Many people find this surprisingly grounding.

Why it works

The chapter's word for service implies presence without performance. The five-day exercise teaches the muscle of helping without needing acknowledgement — the difference between सेवा and showing off.
