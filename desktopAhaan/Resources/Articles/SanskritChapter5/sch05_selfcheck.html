Question 1 — The warm-up

What does the chapter's title 'सेवा हि परमो धर्मः' mean?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: service is indeed the highest duty


• सेवा = service, हि = indeed, परमः धर्मः = the highest duty.
• The whole lesson illustrates this thesis.

Question 2 — The check

In the story, what does the skilled person choose to do?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: serve the sick devotedly


• The चिकित्सकः chooses to serve रोगिणः (the sick) over pursuing status.
• This choice proves सेवा हि परमो धर्मः.

Question 3 — The stretch

'यन्त्रवत्' describes the doctor's service as —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: tireless, like a machine


• यन्त्रवत् = यन्त्रम् इव ('like a machine') — working steadily and tirelessly.
• The -वत् suffix means 'like'.

Question 4 — The trap

'चिकित्सकः' means —

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: a physician / doctor


• Glossary: चिकित्सकः = भिषक् ('healer/physician').
• From चिकित्स् ('to wish to cure').

Question 5 — The finisher

What does 'उपाधिः' mean?

🧠 Answer in your head first

Work it out before reading on — no peeking.

Answer: a degree / title


• Glossary: उपाधिः = नामचिह्नम् ('a title/name-mark') = an academic degree.
• The chapter contrasts a degree with real service.
