NCERT Q&A — Model Answers

The chapter's textbook exercise questions, with model answers grounded directly in the chapter's own content.

These are the kinds of questions the textbook itself asks at the end of the chapter. Each answer below is derived from the chapter's own text — what the lesson actually says, not what a wider Sanskrit reading would supply. Treat this as a study reference for revision before a test.

Question 1

पाठस्य शीर्षकम् अनुसृत्य परमो धर्मः कः?

Answer: सेवा। (selfless service)

💡 Why

The chapter's title is its thesis: सेवा हि परमो धर्मः — service (सेवा) IS the highest duty. The particle हि is emphatic ('verily').

Question 2

चिकित्सकः कान् सेवते?

Answer: रोगिणः। (the sick)

💡 Why

The chapter's example is a doctor who chooses to serve sick people (रोगिणः) tirelessly — embodying the chapter's thesis in his daily practice.

Question 3

'यन्त्रवत्' इति पदस्य कोऽर्थः?

Answer: यन्त्रम् इव। (like a machine)

💡 Why

Glossary: यन्त्रवत् = यन्त्रम् इव. The -वत् suffix means 'like', so यन्त्रवत् = 'machine-like' — describing tireless, steady, automatic motion.

Question 4

'मानवीयाः' इति शब्दस्य कोऽर्थः?

Answer: मानवे भवितव्याः। (humane / what should be in a human)

💡 Why

Glossary: मानवीयाः = मानवे भवितव्याः. From मानव (human) + -ईय ('befitting'). The chapter values humane qualities — not titles or wealth — as what truly makes a person.

Question 5

कषायम् किम्?

Answer: क्वाथः। (a herbal decoction)

💡 Why

Glossary: कषायम् = क्वाथम्. An Āyurvedic herbal medicine made by boiling herbs. The chapter's doctor prepares कषायम् for the sick — service in concrete form.
