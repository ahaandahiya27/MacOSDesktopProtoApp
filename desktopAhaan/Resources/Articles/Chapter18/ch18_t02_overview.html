Treatment Plant Stages

Topic t02 of Chapter 18 · Wastewater Story · Class 7 Science

The big idea

A modern wastewater treatment plant works in stages: bar screen (catches rags and plastic), grit chamber (sand and gravel settle), aeration tank (microbes eat dissolved organic waste — air is bubbled in to feed them), clarifier (microbes settle as sludge), sludge digester (sludge produces biogas methane).

How Dirty Water Becomes Clean. Each stage targets a different kind of pollutant — solids first, organics second, sludge last.
