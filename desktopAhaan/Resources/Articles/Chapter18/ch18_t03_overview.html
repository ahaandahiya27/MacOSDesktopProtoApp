Sanitation at Home

Topic t03 of Chapter 18 · Wastewater Story · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Open drains breed mosquitoes and spread cholera, dysentery and typhoid — closed sewers are far healthier. At home, low-cost sanitation includes pit latrines (rural), septic tanks (no-sewer suburbs), bio-toilets (trains), and soak-pits for greywater. Composting kitchen waste at home halves the load on sewer pipes and produces free fertiliser.

Open Drains, Soak Pits, Composting. India's Swachh Bharat Mission has built over 100 million toilets since 2014 — drastically cutting open defecation and water-borne disease.

Looking ahead
