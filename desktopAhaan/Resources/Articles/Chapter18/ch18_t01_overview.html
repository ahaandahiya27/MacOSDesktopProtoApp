Where Wastewater Goes

Topic t01 of Chapter 18 · Wastewater Story · Class 7 Science

The big idea

Every time you wash dishes, take a bath, flush a toilet, or empty a kitchen sink, you create wastewater (sewage). It contains organic matter, chemicals, and sometimes germs. It travels through house drains → sewer pipes → wastewater treatment plants → clean water released back to rivers or the sea.

From Sink to River. Wastewater is half the urban water cycle. Treating it well is what keeps cities habitable and rivers alive.

Looking ahead

Class 9 Geography covers urban sanitation. Class 11 Biology covers water-borne diseases and their prevention. Class 12 / NEET tests water and air pollution control, biological oxygen demand (BOD), and chemical oxygen demand (COD).
