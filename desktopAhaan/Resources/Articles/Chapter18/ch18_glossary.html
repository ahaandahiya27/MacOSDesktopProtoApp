The 10 terms to know

  • Sewage · मलजल — Wastewater from homes, offices, factories, and hospitals — a mix of liquid waste and dissolved solids.
    Example: Delhi produces 3000 million litres of sewage every day.
  • Sludge · गाद — The settled solid matter collected at the bottom of sewage treatment tanks.
    Example: Dried sludge is a rich fertiliser, full of nitrogen and phosphorus.
  • Aeration tank · वायुसंचार टैंक — A tank in a WWTP where air is pumped to encourage bacteria that digest organic waste.
    Example: An aeration tank smells like wet earth, not sewage.
  • Sludge digester · गाद पाचक — A closed tank where anaerobic bacteria digest sludge, producing biogas.
    Example: A WWTP near Mumbai's Bhandup produces enough biogas to run its own electricity.
  • Septic tank · सेप्टिक टैंक — An underground tank where wastewater from a house collects; solids settle and bacteria partly digest waste.
    Example: Indian villages without sewer connections use septic tanks; need cleaning every 3-5 years.
  • Bio-toilet · बायो-टॉयलेट — A toilet with an anaerobic bacterial chamber that digests waste into water + CO₂ + methane.
    Example: All Indian Railways coaches now have bio-toilets, replacing track-pollution toilets.
  • Biogas · बायोगैस — A mixture of methane and CO₂ produced by anaerobic bacteria digesting organic waste.
    Example: Cow dung biogas plants in rural Maharashtra provide cooking gas to thousands of households.
  • Open defecation · खुले में शौच — Defecating in fields, forests, or open areas instead of using a toilet.
    Example: Swachh Bharat Mission reduced India's rural open defecation from 60% to <5% in 5 years.
  • Manhole · मैनहोल — A vertical access point to underground sewer pipes for maintenance and cleaning.
    Example: Every 50-100 m along a city sewer, you'll find a manhole cover.
  • Swachh Bharat Mission · स्वच्छ भारत मिशन — India's flagship sanitation campaign launched 2014 to eliminate open defecation.
    Example: Built 11 crore toilets in 5 years; cut diarrhoea child deaths by 60%.
